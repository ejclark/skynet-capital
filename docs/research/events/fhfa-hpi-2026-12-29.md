Re-derived independently from the 271-row archive, the record matches: **221 listed releases across 221

**What is new is the exposure.** `cr-expiry-2026-12-11` and `government-funding-deadline-2026-12-11`
sit **eighteen days** before this print. The sibling's date, 2026-11-24, is *before* the deadline, so

That is the difference between a statutory argument and a natural experiment, and it is the reason this
edition earns a ledger of its own rather than a pulse row on the sibling's.

Two prior natural experiments already exist and both went FHFA's way — **2018-12-27 and 2019-01-23

year-end compression is the base case *conditional on the funding question being settled*, and forward

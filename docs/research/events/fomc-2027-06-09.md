## Initial research

### The question, plainly

Four 2027 FOMC ledgers already exist. [`fomc-2027-01-27`](fomc-2027-01-27.md) established the
roster and that January carries no SEP; [`fomc-2027-03-17`](fomc-2027-03-17.md) established that
March is the year's first *pure-revision* SEP and deployed a corridor rule — *"treat 2027-03-17 →
2027-03-19 as one event window"* — built on the March FOMC landing two sessions before triple
witching; [`fomc-2027-04-28`](fomc-2027-04-28.md) established that April acts on the March dots and
cannot revise them *"until 06-08/09"*. And [`boj-decision-2027-06-11`](boj-decision-2027-06-11.md),
which discovered this entry, measured the sequence from the Japanese side.

So this document's question is narrow and inherited: **the siblings have named this meeting three
times without researching it — does the March corridor framing transfer to June, and is "the second
SEP of 2027" a distinction or a label?**

**One-line verdict.** The framing does **not** transfer, and the reason is a calendar anomaly no
sibling looked for: **2027's June meeting is eight days earlier than the mechanical continuation of
2026's, where every other 2027 meeting is exactly one day earlier** — which pulls it clear of the
June expiration for only the third time in fourteen years, and leaves it with a one-event corridor
instead of a five-event one. Around that sits a measured seasonal result that also cuts against a
sibling: **June is the quietest FOMC month on record, and the projection set is not what makes it
so.** No rate call is made at D-277 and none is implied.

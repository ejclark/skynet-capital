## Initial research

### The question, plainly

What should we expect from the November 2026 ISM Manufacturing PMI on 2026-12-01, is the date right,
and — the question three sibling ledgers have now each pushed one step further — **does this release
move anything, or was the 2026 result an artifact of one year and one ETF's dividend schedule?** And
given that it opens a five-event December corridor from inside an FOMC communications blackout, what
should a paper-trading book holding long-duration tech (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN)
do about it?

**One-line verdict:** out of sample the release-day effect is **gone** — the bond leg is the TLT
monthly distribution and the equity leg is the turn of the month — so the stand-aside is no longer a
2026 finding but an 11.7-year one; what is genuinely specific to **12-01** is that it is the only
2026 ISM Manufacturing print nobody at the Fed is permitted to talk about, and it is superseded by
payrolls three days later.

### Method

Macro-print mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — `symbols: []`, so no
symbol-keyed instrument applies; `earnings-cycle.mjs` / `intraday-edges.mjs` were not run, but their
caches were busted anyway (2026-09-02) because this session re-fetched bars through the same

**(a) An out-of-sample price measurement run this session.** Yahoo daily bars for SPY, QQQ, TLT and

### What the conditions support

Nothing directional, and the output of this session is a **refutation that hardens a refusal**.
Concretely: (a) **retire the ISM-release-day bond story permanently** — on the correct basis it is a
51.4% up-rate against a 51.1% baseline over 140 releases, and the version that looked loud is the TLT
distribution; (b) **do not read SPY's 62.1% up-rate as an ISM edge** — it is turn-of-the-month, its
p-value is beaten by a placebo offset with no story, and it has decayed in each of the last two
complete years; (c) **never structure options around 12-01**, because the December contract spans the
dot plot, CPI and the funding cliff and none of those is this print; (d) **read it, from inside the
blackout, for Prices and Employment specifically** — nobody at the Fed can contextualise it before
12-09, which raises what a careful reader gets out of it without raising what a trader could; and (e)
**watch two dated things** — the 10-01 release, because ISM's own chain is what will name 12-01 on
11-02, and the CR signature, because the 12-11 expiry is the corridor's live variable. None of that
licenses an entry.

### Honest limits

**The proxy.** Release dates are the first *trading* day of each month, not a fetched ISM calendar
(SSO-gated, leg 1). It reproduces 2026 **9/9**, but 2015–2025 is unverified against ISM's own
schedule; a month where ISM's business day and the market's trading day diverge would be silently
misdated. Good Friday is the plausible such case and never fell on a month's first day in this
window, but that is a check, not a proof. **The statistics.** n = 140 spans COVID and two rate
regimes — much better than one year, still one country, one publication convention, and one
overlapping calendar effect this design *cannot* separate from ISM, because every first trading day
carries both. No multiple-comparison correction was applied to the headline table either; 21 tests
were run in the placebo alone, and that argument is used **against** SPY's p = 0.046 rather than for
anything. The December subsample is **n = 11** and settles nothing on its own. TLT's dividend
adjustment is Yahoo's, not recomputed from distribution records. **The vol leg has no measured
number.** vixcentral.com now 301s to volchart.io, which served no numerical data on fetch, so **no
VIX futures curve was obtained this session**; the December contract's **2026-12-16** settlement is
**rule-derived** (Cboe's published rule — the Wednesday 30 days before the third Friday of the
following month; third Friday of Jan 2027 = 01-15) and corroborated only by a secondary summary,
because Cboe's own 2026 settlement-calendar PDF was fetched and its content streams inflated but is a
legend-plus-grid layout whose day cells cannot be attributed from the text layer. The quarter-horizon
call therefore rests on the **structure** of that contract, not on a measured spread, and the 11-02
sibling's ~4.4pt election premium is deliberately **not** carried forward as a number. Spot VIX
**16.34** is a 2026-09-01 Yahoo close, not an intraday read. **The facts most likely to move first:**
the CR signature is unverified (leg 6), and no November consensus exists (leg 7). And the whole
document assumes ISM publishes on schedule.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`, ISM first-business-day cadence re-checked 2026-09-02 against a still-gated
primary, cross-checked statutorily via the 11-03 midterm date, and now carrying a dated promotion
path through ISM's own release chain).** Treat 2026-12-01 10:00 ET as a **high-impact known-date read
with no tradeable edge — and, as of this session, no measurable release-day reaction at all**: no
position opened, closed or sized off it, no house playbook targets it, and per leg 2 the bond finding
that made the 11-02 sibling interesting does **not** survive out of 2026 on a dividend-adjusted basis
(51.4% up over 140 releases vs a 51.1% baseline), while the surviving equity tilt is the turn of the
month and is decaying. Specific to this date: **avoid any options structure keyed to it**, because
the December contract that would price one spans the 12-09 dot plot, the 12-10 CPI and the 12-11
funding cliff. What makes it worth `high` is **position, not power** — it is the only 2026 ISM
Manufacturing print inside an FOMC blackout (11-28 → 12-10, `confirmed`), so it is read without any
Fed voice able to frame it until 12-09, and it opens a corridor where payrolls supersede it in three
days. Base case for the November reading (**estimate**-labelled, **Low** confidence, trend
extrapolation only at D-90): continued expansion in the low-to-mid 50s consistent with 2026's
52.4–55.6 range, with Prices elevated near 71.

**Kill switches:**

- **The 2026-10-01 release does not occur on 10-01, or its text does not name 2026-11-02** — ISM's own
  release chain (leg 1) is broken, the cadence this date is derived from is no longer prospectively
  confirmed, and the `estimate` widens instead of narrowing. Registered as **FT-48**, scored
  **2026-10-02**.
- **The 2026-11-02 release names a December date other than 2026-12-01** — the date is wrong and this
  document is re-dated, not patched.
- **A dividend-adjusted TLT release-day series turns significant on the long sample** — e.g. a
  re-run through 2027 pushing it past ~55% up on n > 150 — leg 2's refutation reopens and the bond
  question goes back to live.
- **The CR is signed before 2026-09-30** — the near-term funding branch closes; the December cliff
  becomes the only live one and leg 6's downstream-exposure framing is the whole of it.
- **No CR signature by 2026-09-30** — the inverse: the funding question re-enters the December
  corridor ahead of this print rather than behind it, and the risk to manage becomes the vacuum.
- **ISM prints sub-50 on 10-01, 11-02 or 12-01** — the 2026 expansion streak breaks, leg 7's base
  rates stop being the right prior, and this doc is re-read from the national tape.
- **The Fed stops being inflation-anchored** — a payroll or CPI print repricing the question from
  hold-vs-hike to hold-vs-cut — at which point the headline reclaims primacy over the Prices line.
- **A published November consensus emerges** — resolves leg 7; re-run the framing against a real
  number instead of trend extrapolation.
- **The December FOMC moves off 12-08/09** — leg 4's blackout window shifts and 12-01 may fall
  outside it, removing this print's only structural distinction from its siblings.

**¥650bn** reopening of *existing* off-the-run bonds with **5 to 11 years** left to run. **Nothing here is
ours to trade**, and this ledger does not pretend otherwise. What it is for is that this is the **only
high-n instrument in the JGB family** — **32 prints** on MOF's own result pages against **n=3** at the

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This entry was proposed on 2026-09-09 by the
[`jgb-liquidity-enhancement-11-39y-2026-10-27`](jgb-liquidity-enhancement-11-39y-2026-10-27.md) adjacency
sweep, on one specific claim: that it is **the direct falsifier** for that ledger's headline finding —
three super-long auctions running that reopened **zero** 20-year issues. That sibling wrote its own limit
plainly: *"The paper may simply be clearing in the neighbouring 5–11y zone rather than failing to clear at
all… until it is read, 'segmentation' and 'demand failure' are observationally equivalent here."*

So this ledger has three jobs, and only one is about 2026-10-22:

**does the proposed falsifier actually work, what does this zone's own record say, and does it change
what the family believes?**

**One-line verdict:** the falsifier **does not work as posed** — MOF's own announcements show the two
zones share **no bonds at any date**, so refused 20-year paper cannot clear next door — but the April 2026
redefinition supplied a **better, controlled** test that the sibling had in its own data and did not run:
it moved a **0.47%-coupon 20-year block** and a **2.26%-coupon 30-year block** into the same auction at
**overlapping remaining maturities**, and the auction bought one and refused the other, three times. The
family's "zero 20-year" is a **coupon/vintage** result, not a tenor result; this zone's 32 prints confirm
the ordering monotonically; and the super-long's cover trap does **not** transfer, because MOF raised this
auction while cutting that one.

### Conviction legs, tested

**1. This is the family's only high-n JGB instrument, and its cadence is the calendar's most regular —
SUPPORTED, and it is what licenses every calibration below.** MOF's 35 published monthly slates
2024-01 → 2026-11 carry **exactly two** Liquidity Enhancement Auctions per month, every month, with no
exception:

| Zone | Months present | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **5-11y (was 5-15.5y)** | **35 of 35** | **every month, no exception** |
| 11-39y (was 15.5-39y) | 18 of 35 | even months only |
| 1-5y | 17 of 35 | odd months only |

The lower bound moved **15.5 → 11 years** with the April 2026 slate (last 5–15.5y print 2026-03-09, first
5–11y print 2026-04-16), and the upper bound of the super-long zone moved with it. **The redefinition did
not interrupt the cadence and did not change the size.** Thirty-two of these auctions have printed, which
is the number the sibling ledger did not have: it wrote *"there is no distribution to compute"* at n=3.
Here there is one, and it is stated in **Signals & conditions** with its n attached.

**2. MOF moved the two zones in OPPOSITE directions, which is why the super-long's cover trap does not
transfer — SUPPORTED.** From MOF's own announcement pages, the offering amount for this zone:

| Window | Offering | Auctions |
|---|---|---|
| 2024-01-22 → 2024-03-21 | ¥500bn | 3 |
| 2024-04-16 → 2024-07-16 | ¥600bn | 4 |
| **2024-08-22 → 2026-08-26** | **¥650bn** | **25 consecutive** |

Over the same window the super-long auction was cut **¥450bn → ¥250bn**. So total Liquidity Enhancement
capacity was **reallocated toward the short end**, +30% here against −44% there — which is a materially
different reading of the super-long series than "demand weakened," and one that ledger could not make from
its own zone alone. The measurable consequence is that the sibling's trap is a property of a **shrinking**
auction and not a general law:

| Statistic | This zone (n=32) | Super-long (n=12, sibling) |
|---|---|---|
| corr(cover, offering size) | **−0.438, t = −2.67** (entirely the 2024 ramp) | −0.648, t = −2.69 |
| corr(gross bids, offering size) | **+0.149, t = +0.83** — insignificant | **+0.734** |
| corr(excess bids, sequence) | **−0.128, t = −0.71** — no trend | −0.624, t = −2.52 |
| corr(spread tail, cover) | **−0.626, t = −4.40** | −0.786, t = −4.02 |

Read together: here the size has been constant for **25 prints**, gross bids do **not** track it, and
excess bids are **flat** — so cover is a readable metric at this auction in a way it is not at the
super-long. Tail and cover agree in sign at both, so the 30-year's "report both, they can disagree" stays
a 30-year rule.

**4. THE CONTROLLED TEST THE REDEFINITION HANDED US — SUPPORTED, and it is the finding of this ledger.**
The April 2026 move did not just relabel a band. It **transferred two specific blocks** out of this zone
and into the super-long auction, and those two blocks are a near-perfect matched pair:

| Transferred block | Outstanding | Remaining maturity at 2026-10-22 | Weighted-avg coupon |
|---|---|---|---|
| **20-Year No.161-177** (17 issues) | **¥5.7tn** | **10.66 – 14.66 y** | **0.47%** |
| **30-Year No.27-34** (8 issues) | **¥1.6tn** | **10.91 – 14.41 y** | **2.26%** |

Same auction. Same day. Same bidder base. Same ¥250bn constraint. **Overlapping remaining maturities.**
The only material difference is the coupon — and the 20-year block is **3.6× larger**, so any
size-crowd-out story predicts the opposite of what happened. From the three post-redefinition

candidates — it is not the curve at 20 years (the 30-year block sits at the same years) and it is not
crowd-out by a smaller auction (the small block won). Registered as
`FT-jgb-liquidity-enhancement-5-11y-2026-10-22-3`.

"seasoned/small-float." Both are a *bond-characteristic* story rather than a *curve-location* story, which
is the claim being made; the finer split is not available at this n and is stated as a limit.

### What plays the conditions support

**None.** No tracked symbol has yen-rates exposure and no house playbook is rates-keyed. The refusal is
inherited rather than re-tested — the 30-year sibling ran the JGB→US-long-end spillover test in its
cleanest available form and it died under control, and a mid-curve reopening auction has a weaker claim on
that channel than the 30-year did, not a stronger one.

What this entry is *for* is that it is the **evidence base** the JGB family did not have: 32 prints where
the siblings have 3 and 12, the only place where a calibrated distribution exists, and the only reading
that explains the family's sharpest finding without appealing to a curve segmentation that the 5–11y
curve does not show.

### Honest limits

- **The controlled test is a natural experiment, not a randomised one.** MOF chose the boundary; the two
  transferred blocks differ in size (¥5.7tn vs ¥1.6tn), in vintage (2017–21 vs 2007–10) and in coupon, and
  only maturity, venue and date are actually held fixed. The size difference cuts *against* the observed
  result, which is what makes it worth reporting; vintage does not, and cannot be separated from coupon.
- **Coupon and issue age are collinear throughout.** Every result in legs 4–7 is consistent with "bidders
  prefer seasoned, small-float, high-coupon paper" and this ledger cannot say which of those three is
  doing the work. What it *can* say is that none of them is "the curve at 20 years."
- **The 30-year overweight rests on a ¥1.1tn denominator.** The ordering is robust across 32 prints; the
  5.05× magnitude is not, and should never be quoted without its 2.01 series standard deviation.
- **The 2025 regime break was dated after the fact.** The 2025-onward mean stands alone (t = +5.20, 20
  prints); the break statistic does not.
- **Eligible stock is computed from the workbook's cumulative issued amounts**, which stop at the 2026-07
  auctions, and it counts **face issued**, not float — it ignores BoJ holdings, buy-backs and anything
  else removed from the tradeable stock. At the 10-year point that matters most, since BoJ ownership is
  heaviest there; the direction of the bias is toward **understating** the 10-year overweight.
- **The spread tail is not a coupon tail.** As at the super-long, this auction is bid as a spread to a
  per-issue reference rate, so "highest minus average accepted spread" measures dispersion across a
  **basket of different bonds**. The n=32 distribution here is internally consistent and transfers to
  **nothing** — not to the 20-year, not to the 30-year, not to the 40-year.
- **The auction result does not exist**, and neither does its size or its issue list. Everything here is
  prior.
- **VIX is Yahoo, i.e. secondary**, and is a US reading with no mechanical bearing on a JGB auction; it is
  carried because the probe-ref contract asks for it.
- **The 1-5y zone is untracked entirely.** It runs 17 of 35 months on MOF's calendars and this family has
  never read one. The coupon ordering predicts it should show the same monotonicity; that is an untested
  prediction, deliberately not proposed here rather than filed as calendar padding.
- **The date and the size are both `estimate`.** MOF is a primary and the date is not in doubt; the label
  reflects an unannounced size, an unpublished issue list, and a gap in `market-events-data.ts`'s prefix
  taxonomy, which has no slot for a non-US sovereign debt office. Estimates widen caution and license
  nothing.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (2026-09-09, D-43; date and size both `estimate`).** **Stand aside, permanently.** No entry, exit
or hedge is keyed to the 2026-10-22 auction or to the 10-22 session. The entry is kept for four diagnostic
jobs, in descending order of value:

1. **It corrects the family's headline reading from tenor to coupon.** The April 2026 redefinition moved a
   **0.47%-coupon 20-year block** and a **2.26%-coupon 30-year block** into the same ¥250bn auction at
   **overlapping 10.7–14.7y remaining maturity**; three prints later the 30-year block has taken **¥234.7bn**
   and the 20-year block **¥0**. "Zero 20-year" is a statement about the bonds, not about the curve.
2. **It is the family's only calibrated distribution.** Thirty-two prints against n=3 at the super-long and
   n=12 across its whole predecessor zone. Cover, excess bids and spread tail all have real percentiles
   here, stated in **Signals & conditions** — and they transfer to no other JGB entry.
3. **It kills the transplant of the super-long's cover trap.** MOF **raised** this auction 30% while
   cutting that one 44%, and has held it at **¥650bn for 25 prints**; gross bids do not track size
   (t = +0.83) and excess bids show no trend (t = −0.71). Cover is readable here.
4. **It bounds the curve story.** 5s10s sits at the **62.3rd percentile** while 20s25s is 93.5th and 25s30s
   1.2nd. Whatever is happening to the JGB curve is happening past 20 years, not here.

Three reading rules attach to the day itself:

- **Cite the cross-sector control, not the within-sector one.** Inside a single tenor, coupon and remaining
  maturity are the same variable; across tenors at matched maturity, they are not.
- **Read the "Issues Re-opened" page, not the headline.** As at the super-long, the headline row is nearly
  uninformative; the coupon ordering is on the second page.
- **August broke ranks and one print is not a regime.** The 2026-08-26 20-year take went to the *lowest*
  coupons. Say so when quoting the ordering, and require two consecutive reversals before abandoning it.

**Kill switches.**

1. **The no-price-channel premise breaks** — a tracked name (NVDA/AVGO/MRVL/CRWV) moves **>2%** on a session
   between **2026-09-09** and **2026-10-22** that the tape attributes to a JGB or yen headline. This
   document is rebuilt, not patched.
2. **The instrument is redefined or resized** — MOF's ~2026-10-15 announcement names a band other than
   **5-11 years**, or an offering other than **about ¥650bn** (which would end a 25-auction run and remove
   the constant-size premise that makes cover readable here)
   (`FT-jgb-liquidity-enhancement-5-11y-2026-10-22-1`).
3. **The coupon ordering breaks in this zone** — the 2026-10-22 "Issues Re-opened" page shows the 30-year
   overweight **below 2.0×** its 4.2% share of eligible stock, or the ordering 30Y > 20Y > 10Y inverted
   anywhere (`FT-jgb-liquidity-enhancement-5-11y-2026-10-22-2`).
4. **The controlled test breaks at the super-long** — the 2026-10-27 11–39y "Issues Re-opened" page takes
   **20-year paper at ≥5%** of accepted while taking **30Y No.27-34 at under 5%**. That is the reverse
   ordering under the same control, and leg 4 does not survive it
   (`FT-jgb-liquidity-enhancement-5-11y-2026-10-22-3`).
5. **The coupon regime breaks** — the coupon-weighted overweight prints negative on **2026-10-22**, making
   it two consecutive negatives after 2026-08-26's −0.030pp
   (`FT-jgb-liquidity-enhancement-5-11y-2026-10-22-4`).
6. **The cadence breaks** — MOF's December 2026 calendar (`2612e.htm`, currently 404) publishes without a
   5–11y row, ending a 35-month unbroken run. Leg 1's "the calendar's most regular instrument" would be
   wrong and every n-based calibration would need re-dating.
7. **The eligible-stock benchmark moves under the ratios** — MOF's ~2026-10-15 announcement names issue
   ranges that shift any security's share of eligible stock by more than **5pp** from the Aug-2026 basis
   (10Y 60.0% / 20Y 35.8% / 30Y 4.2%). The overweight denominators in leg 5 are recomputed before the
   ordering is read.

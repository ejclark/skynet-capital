   SUPPORTED, and it is why the entry stays `estimate`. France's *"Provisional Agenda of Work of the
   Security Council – September 2026"* ("as of" 31 August 2026, published 2 September 2026),
   re-fetched direct by this lane on 2026-09-15 independently of the proposal, reads verbatim for
   **Monday, 28 September**: *"Briefing (A.M.) - Middle East [2334]"*, *"Consultations (A.M.) -
   Middle East [2334]"*, *"OPCW report due."* The cadence corroborates it arithmetically: the 2334
   quarterly implementation briefings ran **2025-12-16**, **2026-03-24** (37th report, covering
   2025-12-03 → 2026-03-13) and **2026-06-29** (38th report, covering 2026-03-14 → 2026-06-12), so
   the **39th** falls in late September 2026. Against that, `securitycouncilreport.org`'s own
   September-2026 overview carries only *"the monthly meeting, with the possibility of additional
   meetings depending on developments"* — **no date, no mention of 2334**. So: one primary, one
   arithmetic corroboration, one non-corroboration, a document captioned *provisional*, and no UN or
   diplomatic slot in `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` (`scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs`). That is an estimate.

2. **What happens on 09-28 is a briefing, not a vote — and the Council is blocked from making it

   Council that cannot agree a *press statement* is not about to agree a resolution. So the event's
   product is a Secretary-General's report read into the record, which the Secretariat circulates in
   writing in any case.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (the event is `estimate`-dated; nothing here is date-keyed action).** Stand aside on the
09-28 briefing, and decline it as an event class rather than as one instance. No position, no hedge,
no sizing — `symbols: []`. What is scheduled is a quarterly report read into the record by a Council
blocked from acting on it, and across eleven observations the most exposed listed instrument moves
*less* on that day than on an ordinary one (EIS 0.63% vs 0.82%, 0.84× local regime, 1.97% max). The
corridor's attention belongs to **PCE 09-30**, **ISM 10-01** and **jobs 10-02** (all confirmed,
high), with **Trump–Xi 09-24** and the **funding deadline 09-30** as the `estimate`-dated wildcards.

**Kill switches — any one of these ends the stance and earns its own ledger row:**

- **The Council adopts a resolution or presidential statement on settlements or West Bank annexation before 2026-12-31** — leg 2 collapses and the agenda item becomes a decision venue rather than a reporting one.
- **EIS's 2026-09-28 close-to-close absolute move exceeds 1.97%** — the eleven-briefing sample maximum — **and the move is separable** from strikes, Hormuz transits and the quarter-end macro block. Separability is the bar, not size.
- **The 39th Secretary-General report on 2334 implementation is not circulated before 2026-09-28** — the date is then wrong, not the stance, and the entry needs re-dating rather than re-calling.
- **VIX ≥ 20 before 2026-09-28 on a headline sourced to the Council's Israel/Palestine track** — leg 6 would break, and it is the leg that says the equity book is ignoring the theatre entirely.
- **ITA or XAR reclaiming their 21-session highs on a UN-track headline** — the defense complex is at 60-session lows into a live war; it starting to trade diplomacy would be a regime this stance does not model.

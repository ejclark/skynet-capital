## At a glance

**TL;DR.** **Stand aside; this is a restatement of the series, not a trade.** On Tuesday 2026-11-24 (`estimate` — the Board "plans to issue" it and has not yet posted a time) the Federal Reserve restates every industrial-production and capacity-utilization number back to 1972 on a **2022 base year**, folding in Census manufacturing benchmarks for **2023 and 2024**. It lands seven days after the October print on 11-17 and the day before PCE and the Q3 GDP second estimate, so the October level and every percentile quoted in the 11-17 ledger are redrawn in between. The size of the redraw is a coin flip by the Board's own record: **4 of the 9 revisions that state a size called the net revision small** (2017, 2019, 2023, 2024), the other five moved growth or utilization visibly, and the last one (2025-11-24) turned five years of manufacturing output from a rise into a decline of more than 1-1/2%. ### Leg 1 — the Board plans this revision for 2026-11-24 · **SUPPORTED**, on one Board primary, kept `estimate`

The feed item dated **2026-09-18**, titled "G.17 Annual Revision Planned to be Released on November 24, 2026", states the date, the **2022** base year, **2023 and 2024** Census manufacturing benchmarks, monthly-indicator and seasonal-factor revisions for each industry, possible estimation-method changes that "will affect the index from 1972 to the present", and capacity revised through Q4-2025 from the Census Quarterly Survey of Plant Capacity Utilization. It supersedes the 2026-05-15 item ("autumn of 2026", 2023 benchmark only), so the 2024 benchmark was added between the two notices. The newest feed item is the 2026-10-16 September-data release; nothing since. Verb "plans to issue", no time: kept `estimate`, with the honest note that the Board's own pattern is to post a second, time-bearing item.

### Leg 2 — the time is noon, if past form holds · **SUPPORTED** on the base rate, **OPEN** on this year

Eleven revisions since 2014. Notices that state a time: **2015-07-21 at 10:00 a.m. EDT**, then **noon** for 2017-03-31, 2021-05-28, 2022-06-28, 2023-03-28 and 2025-11-24. 2014, 2016, 2018, 2019 and 2024 carry no stated time in the feed (so no claim is made for them). None of the eleven was folded into a scheduled monthly; 2018, 2019 and 2024 each superseded a monthly published days earlier.

The Board's own summary sentence, as published on release day:

| Revision | Net effect, in the Board's words |
|---|---|
| 2016-04-01 | total IP growth lower, "especially 2014 and 2015"; return to pre-recession peak six months later |
| 2017-03-31 | "revisions were small"; 2015 decline 2-3/4% vs 1-3/4% |
| 2018-03-23 | total IP revisions "negative", 0.4 to 0.7pp lower in each of 2015–17 |
| 2019-03-27 | "small and positive" |
| 2021-05-28 | slower manufacturing growth, "somewhat higher" utilization |
| 2022-06-28 | "little revision" to manufacturing growth; higher utilization |
| 2023-03-28 | no more than 0.6pp in any year; utilization "little changed" |
| 2024-06-28 | total IP growth "little changed", 2020 up 0.1pp, 2023 down 0.1pp |

Four of nine small/little (2017, 2019, 2023, 2024), five with a named direction. The big two (2018, 2025) both followed new Census benchmark data for manufacturing — which is exactly what 2026 incorporates (two ASM years, 2023 and 2024). The prior is therefore roughly even and the registration below is low-confidence on purpose.

### Leg 4 — the tape on revision days · **MIXED**, thin and confounded

SPY, 11 revision sessions since 2014 against 3,163 others:

| Measure | Revision median | Baseline median | Baseline p90 | Revision days above p90 | Permutation p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High–low range (% prior close) | 1.285 | 0.871 | 2.041 | 2 | 0.051 |
| Close-to-close absolute move | 0.493 | 0.489 | 1.583 | 2 | 0.495 |

| Open-to-close absolute move | 0.519 | 0.378 | 1.232 | 3 | 0.212 |

Range is elevated at the edge of significance; net move and the overnight gap are not. The overnight gap being *lower* than baseline fits a noon release (nothing prints before the open). The two sessions above p90 were **2018-03-23** (range 2.545%, −2.13% close-to-close) and **2022-06-28** (3.250%, −2.04%); neither is attributed to the G.17, because each carried other market news and the range figure uses a daily bar that cannot isolate a noon print. Five of the 11 dates fall within the last three sessions of a month (2014-03-28, 2017-03-31, 2021-05-28, 2022-06-28, 2024-06-28), which carries rebalancing volume of its own and was not controlled for. So the honest read is *elevated range, unattributed, n=11*.

**Stance (date is `estimate`).** Stand aside on 2026-11-24; the position is the absence of one.

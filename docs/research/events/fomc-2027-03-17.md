## Initial research

### The question, plainly

The [January 2027 ledger](fomc-2027-01-27.md) answered the standing questions for the 2027 Fed for
this book: the roster rotates on 2027-01-01 and drops the hawkish dissent bloc, January is the
organizational meeting, and the January venue carries **no SEP** so it *"can act on the December dots
but cannot revise them."* That sentence names this meeting without researching it. And the
[BoJ 03-18 ledger](boj-decision-2027-03-18.md), which discovered this entry three days ago, measured
the sequence from the Japanese side and explicitly handed the global-duration channel back:
*"an externally-pressured normalization is a global duration story… which the FOMC ledgers, not this
one, would own."*

So this document's question is narrow: **what does the March venue carry that January cannot, and is
"the first SEP of 2027" a real distinction or a label?**

**One-line verdict.** It is real, and it is a *format* fact rather than a rate fact: by the Fed's own
published rule, **March is the year's first pure-revision SEP** — the same forward years December
publishes, no new terminal year, no completed year to absorb — which makes its change-in-projections
table the cleanest December-to-March dot comparison the annual cycle produces. Around that sit two
measured structures the January ledger had no reason to look for: **a decision-day distribution that
is materially more bimodal than January's**, and a **triple-witching corridor** the March FOMC has
landed inside in 7 of 11 completed years. No rate call is made at D-193 and none is implied.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event was filed on 2026-09-05 by the [`treasury-5y-note-2026-10-27`](treasury-5y-note-2026-10-27.md)
adjacency sweep, which already answered the obvious cross-question — *does the 1:40pm buyback bid for

nowhere near 10/28/2046–10/27/2056). Repeating that would add nothing.

The [10-08 sibling](treasury-buyback-20y30y-2026-10-08.md), written the same day, spent its session
on the corridor's most-repeated caution — *"the cap is a ceiling, not a purchase"* — and produced a

auction**, 2 of 5 vs 0 of 21, Fisher **p = 0.031**) and a CUSIP-level shape test. That work is
excellent and is reproduced here from primary, independently, to the digit.

So this session asked the question that a same-day sibling is uniquely placed to ask and a later
pulse would be too invested to: **is the window real, or is it something else wearing the window's
clothes?** 2026-10-27 is the one date in this corridor whose defining feature is not an auction at
all — it is **day 1 of an FOMC meeting**, settling on the decision itself.

**One-line verdict:** the window is **confounded on half its evidence** — its stronger positive,
2026-03-19, is also the only post-FOMC-decision operation on record — and **2026-10-27 sits in a
recurring, mechanically-generated slot that is 3 for 3 on full fill and cannot break the tie.**

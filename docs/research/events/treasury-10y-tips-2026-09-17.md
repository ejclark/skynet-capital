## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the 10-year TIPS reopening (1:00pm ET, confirmed) is a real-yield supply
event landing the day after the September FOMC. It reopens the July new issue that cleared at a
17-year-high real yield on soft demand. What is priced going in, how has TIPS demand been trending,
what does the breakeven read tell us about whether the long-end selloff is a real-rate or an
inflation-expectations story, and which tracked names carry exposure?

**One-line verdict:** the load-bearing signal is the breakeven, not the headline real yield — the
long-end selloff has been a real-rate / term-premium story (nominals to multi-year highs while
breakevens stayed roughly flat), so this TIPS stop is the cleanest single test of whether that read
still holds, and it lands immediately after a two-sided FOMC. Medium impact, no symbols; the auction
licenses monitoring, not entries — its role is a conditioning input on the long-duration-tech rate
read, sharper on inflation-expectations than the nominal auctions are.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date, time, and confirmed status are right — SUPPORTED.** treasury.gov's tentative
   auction schedule places the 10-year TIPS reopening at 1:00pm ET on Thursday 2026-09-17, matching
   the checked-in calendar entry (`TSY:`, formal announce ~6bd prior, checked 2026-08-18) exactly.
   The reopening size is ~$19B for the Aug–Oct quarter per the refunding schedule (July new issue was
   $21B); formal announcement ~6 business days ahead (~2026-09-11). No date discrepancy found.

2. **The July predecessor cleared at a 17-year-high real yield on soft demand — SUPPORTED.** The
   July 2026 10-year TIPS **new issue** (CUSIP 91282CRE3, 2.375% coupon, $21B) generated a real
   yield of **2.438% — the highest at auction for this term since October 2008** — versus a
   when-issued ~2.41% (i.e. tailed ~2.8bp, weaker demand than priced), on a **lukewarm bid-to-cover
   of 2.30** (tipswatch.com, 2026-07-23). "A great result for investors" (high real yield to buyers)
   but soft on demand. The prior 10Y TIPS (May 2026) cleared 2.169% "to soft demand" (tipswatch.com,
   2026-05-21) — two consecutive soft real-yield prints. **This auction reopens the July issue.**

3. **The long-end selloff is a real-rate / term-premium story, not an inflation-expectations story
   — SUPPORTED, and this is why the breakeven is the load-bearing signal.** Per
   [`treasury-30y-tips-2026-08-20.md`](treasury-30y-tips-2026-08-20.md): nominal long yields hit
   multi-year highs (30Y 19-year high ~5.33% on 8/18) while **breakevens stayed roughly flat** — the
   move is real-rate/term-premium driven, not the market repricing inflation higher. A TIPS auction
   isolates exactly this: the real-yield stop plus the breakeven move is the cleanest read on whether
   that decomposition still holds.

4. **The load-bearing adjacency is the prior-day FOMC — SUPPORTED as a structural fact.** The
   reopening lands D+1 to the Sep-16 FOMC (statement + SEP/dot plot). Per
   [`fomc-2026-09-16.md`](fomc-2026-09-16.md), that is two-sided (hawkish hold ~60–70%, hike ~30–40%,
   cut 0%). So this TIPS stop and its breakeven are the first supply-side read *after* the Fed's
   inflation/rate signal — a hawkish FOMC that lifts real yields could show up directly in this
   auction's stop the next day.

5. **Real-yield / duration sensitivity of the tracked names — SUPPORTED via the mechanism.** Rising
   *real* yields (the TIPS story) compress high-multiple growth valuations directly — the same
   mechanism that drove CRWV −12.1% and NVDA −2.4% on 8/18. Sensitivity tier is unchanged: **CRWV**
   highest (debt-financed capex), then **NVDA/AVGO/MRVL** (high-multiple semis), then mega-cap
   MSFT/GOOG/META, least direct AAPL/AMZN. A real-yield-driven selloff hits the same names as a
   nominal one; the TIPS auction adds the inflation-expectations dimension the nominals don't isolate.

6. **This is a medium-impact, no-symbol conditioning event, not a market-mover on its own —
   SUPPORTED.** Medium impact, no tracked tickers; a $19B real-yield reopening is a second-order
   signal versus the FOMC it follows and the OpEx (9/18) it precedes. Its value is the breakeven
   read, not a standalone tape event.

**Sharpened at D-15 (2026-09-02; receipt: the row below) — the stance is unchanged, the tape is

strikes) is live. Watch-only still: medium impact, no tracked symbols, no terms until ~9/11.

- **Weak-demand stop** (real yield tailing well above when-issued, bid-to-cover below ~2.30) — real-
  rate compensation demand still rising; consistent with the term-premium stance, mild caution up.
- **The Sep-16 FOMC** (hawkish surprise / hike) the day before would lift real yields directly into
  this auction — the meeting and the stop are a linked pair, read together.
- **A >5% single-session move in CRWV or another high-duration name** around auction day confirms the
  real-yield reaction-function read live in the current cycle.

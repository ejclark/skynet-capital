## Initial research

### The question, plainly

PJM's one-time Reliability Backstop Procurement (RBP) — filed at FERC on 2026-07-31 in docket
**ER26-3380-000** against the 6,831 MW shortfall left by the 2028/29 Base Residual Auction — is
scheduled to open its central bid window on **2026-09-30**. What actually happens that day, is any
of it observable, and does it touch the nine tracked names?

### One-line verdict

**Nothing tradable happens on 2026-09-30** (estimate): the day is a *gate* on the December sequence,
not a print — and the December print it gates will not be the clearing price the proposing ledger
expects, because the RBP is pay-as-bid under a MW-weighted-average willingness to pay.

### Legs tested

1. **"The window opens 2026-09-30."** MIXED — stays an **estimate**, but better sourced than the
   proposal had it. Three independent sources give the same window: Utility Dive (2026-08-03,
   "September 30 to October 21"), Sysotechnologies (2026-08-21, "Sept. 30 – Oct. 21, 2026"), APPA
   (2026-07-31, "commencing a Reliability Backstop Procurement (RBP) on Sept. 30, 2026"). PJM's own
   posted RPM page carries no RBP schedule (fetched 2026-09-08 HTTP 200 by the proposing lane), so
   the confirmed `PJM:` prefix does not apply and the date stays `NEWS:`/estimate. It is also
   expressly conditional: PJM intends to open on 09-30 *only* if the framework is accepted by
   **2026-09-29**.

2. **"No FERC order found ⇒ the window is at risk."** REFUTED as an inference — this is the leg
   that most changes the read. The **Federal Register** (govinfo.gov, FR-2026-08-06, notice
   2026-15979, fetched direct 2026-09-08 HTTP 200) lists the filing verbatim as a
   **"205(d) Rate Filing"** by "PJM Interconnection, L.L.C.", filed **7/31/26**, accession
   **20260731-5214**, **effective date 9/29/2026**, comments due **5 p.m. ET 8/21/26**. Under the
   Federal Power Act's §205 default, a 205(d) rate filing takes effect on its requested date unless
   the Commission acts to reject, suspend, or set it for hearing — 2026-09-29 is exactly 60 days
   after 2026-07-31, the statutory notice period. So the *absence* of an order as of 2026-09-08 is
   the modal path to the window opening, not evidence against it. (Stated as this lane's reading of
   the statute, not as a quote from a source; no source found says it outright.) **The honest
   counterweight:** FERC routinely acts on protested filings, and this one is protested — consumer
   advocates from **Delaware, DC, Illinois, Maryland and New Jersey** filed jointly, arguing PJM has
   not shown the RBP is necessary and does not protect existing customers if the forecast data
   centers are delayed, cancelled or sited elsewhere (reported by Inside Climate News, 2026-09-01;
   the article itself returned 403 and is in `probe-ref.blocked`, so this is a search-relayed
   reading). A deficiency letter, a suspension, a hearing order, or an acceptance conditioned on
   redesign all still slip or reshape 09-30.

3. **"$555/MW-day price cap."** REFUTED as stated. It is a **MW-weighted average** maximum
   willingness to pay across all selected offers, not a per-resource ceiling: APPA (2026-07-31) —
   "a maximum willingness to pay equal to a MW-weighted average of $555/MW-day"; Sysotechnologies
   (2026-08-21) — "The RBP is subject to a MW-weighted average cap of $555/MW-day across all
   selected offers"; KilowattLogic — a "volume-weighted maximum willingness to pay." **The
   consequence:** an individual resource can be committed *above* $555/MW-day so long as the
   selected portfolio averages at or under it. Every downstream claim that treats $555 as a ceiling
   on any single commitment is overstated.

4. **"The RBP result is the first observable market price for new PJM supply in the collar era"**
   (the proposing ledger's 2026-09-08 stance amendment). REFUTED as stated. The central procurement
   is **pay-as-bid**: "Unlike the 'pay as clear' structure of the RPM, where all cleared resources
   receive the same clearing price for their respective LDA, the RBP will be structured as a 'pay as
   bid' procurement" (Sysotechnologies, 2026-08-21), corroborated by RenewableEnergyWorld's April
   design write-up ("resources will get paid based on their offers/bids") and by the same source's
   note that settlement runs as a contract-for-differences against RPM clearing prices. A pay-as-bid
   procurement has **no clearing price**. Whatever lands on 2026-12-02 is committed MW plus, at
   best, an average or a total cost — not a number comparable to the $325/$333/$329 RPM prints. The
   *direction* of the proposing ledger's amendment survives (this is the first PJM procurement able
   to pay above the collar); its *unit* does not.

5. **"Something is published on 2026-09-30."** REFUTED on the evidence available. No source found
   says PJM publishes anything at window open; bids are confidential by construction. Worse for the
   information case, the **bilateral target-adjustment window runs ~2026-09-10 to ~2026-10-09** —
   past the bid-window open — and qualifying bilateral contracts and self-supply *reduce* the
   central target ("the initial procurement target will be decreased by the sum of qualifying new
   resources that are the subject of executed bilateral contracts or otherwise bring new capacity to
   the system", APPA 2026-07-31). So on 09-30 neither the price nor the final quantity exists. The
   only observable is binary: did PJM open the window.

6. **Reaction function — how do IPP equities trade a *window open*?** No precedent, and none should
   be inferred. The RBP has never been run. The proposing ledger's reaction data (n=3: CEG +4.6%/VST
   +6% on 2025-07-22; TLN +3.7%/CEG +2.7%/VST +1.6% on 2025-12-17; no surge on 2026-07-14) is
   entirely **results-day** moves; RPM offer-window openings have never been reported as equity
   events. Compounding it, 2026-09-30 is one of the densest macro days on this calendar — **PCE**
   and the **government funding deadline** (both `high`), plus GDP Q2 third, ADP, Chicago PMI, the
   Russell style capping and the S&P select-sector reweight all land the same day. Any IPP move on
   09-30 is unattributable to the RBP absent coverage saying so.

7. **Do the nine tracked names carry sensitivity?** REFUTED as a direct play, on the proposing
   ledger's own grounds — none of NVDA/MRVL/AVGO/CRWV/MSFT/GOOG/META/AAPL/AMZN is an IPP, and the
   exposed names (VST, CEG, TLN, NRG) live in [`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md)
   rather than on the tracked roster. **CRWV** remains the most exposed of the nine as a leveraged
   PJM-footprint datacenter operator, and its own event
   [`crwv-fully-connected-2026-09-29`](crwv-fully-connected-2026-09-29.md) lands the day before this
   one — but that read belongs to that ledger, not this one. Context tape at 2026-09-08 close
   (Yahoo daily bars, not a tracked-symbol probe): VST 153.96, CEG 301.61, TLN 327.07, NRG 121.39,
   CRWV 102.17; **VIX 15.27**.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date estimate — no date-keyed action is licensed regardless).** **Stand aside.** No action
on any tracked name and none contemplated: 2026-09-30 is a confidential bid-window opening that
publishes no price and no final quantity, and its only observable is binary. Base case (estimate):
**FERC takes no adverse action in ER26-3380 by 2026-09-29, the 205(d) filing takes effect on its
requested date, and PJM opens the central bid window on 2026-09-30** — a non-event for the tape.
This ledger carries forward the proposing ledger's watch-only posture on the AI-energy sleeve and
**corrects two of its claims** (both estimate): $555/MW-day is a MW-weighted average across selected
offers rather than a per-resource cap, and the procurement is pay-as-bid rather than pay-as-clear, so
**2026-12-02 will not produce an RPM-comparable clearing price**. VST's Tier-2 gate in
[`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md) therefore stays **closed** on this event's
account, and stays closed on 2026-12-02's account too — a pay-as-bid emergency procurement is
willingness-to-pay evidence, not the RPM cap sunsetting the gate names. Registered as
`FT-pjm-reliability-backstop-procurement-2026-09-30-1` through `-3` so the tape settles it.

**Kill switches.**

- **The gate leg** dies if FERC issues a deficiency letter, a suspension, a hearing order, or a
  conditioned acceptance in **ER26-3380 on or before 2026-09-29** — the "silence is acceptance"
  reading in Leg 2 is then wrong on its face and the window slips or is reshaped.
- **The date leg** dies if PJM posts an RBP schedule on pjm.com naming a window-open date other than
  **2026-09-30** → amend `src/domain/market-events/pjm-reliability-backstop-procurement-2026-09-30.json`
  and re-band the cadence.
- **The "gate, not a print" verdict** dies if PJM publishes anything quantitative on **2026-09-30** —
  a procurement-target MW, an offer count, a bilateral-offset figure → this event upgrades toward a
  genuine information event and likely a higher impact tier.
- **The pay-as-bid correction (Leg 4)** dies if PJM's accepted tariff or its 2026-12-02 publication
  reports a single uniform RBP clearing price → the proposing ledger's original framing is restored
  and the sleeve's re-rate case reopens.
- **The weighted-average correction (Leg 3)** dies if a primary reading of the ER26-3380 tariff
  sheets shows $555/MW-day binding per resource rather than across the selected portfolio.
- **Escalation to an Eric-facing item** happens on none of the above alone — only if credible RPM
  cap-sunset signalling appears, which is VST's named gate and is not this event.

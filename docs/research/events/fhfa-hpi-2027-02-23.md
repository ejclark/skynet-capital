### Leg 3 — **THE FINDING: the February FHFA print is FOMC-free by arithmetic** · **SUPPORTED**

The proposal observed that "the 2027 FOMC calendar has no February meeting at all (Jan 26-27, then
Mar 16-17)." True, and it understates the case. Three measurements turn it from a 2027 observation
into a structural one.

**First, February is empty across the whole posted calendar.** Of 56 meeting pairs, the day-one month
distribution is Jan 7 · Mar 7 · Apr 4 · May 3 · Jun 7 · Jul 7 · Sep 7 · Oct 4 · Nov 3 · Dec 7 —
**zero in February**. Decision days add exactly **one** February entry ever, 2023-02-01, the tail of a
meeting that opened 2023-01-31. **No FOMC day of any kind falls after February 10.** The Committee's
eight-meeting year runs January → March; February is the trough.

**Second, the distance is not merely large, it is pinned.** The last Tuesday of February to the March
meeting's day one:

| Year | February FHFA release | March FOMC day 1 | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 2021-02-23 | 2021-03-16 | **21 days** |
| 2022 | 2022-02-22 | 2022-03-15 | **21 days** |
| 2023 | 2023-02-28 | 2023-03-21 | **21 days** |
| 2024 | 2024-02-27 | 2024-03-19 | **21 days** |
| 2025 | 2025-02-25 | 2025-03-18 | **21 days** |
| 2026 | 2026-02-24 | 2026-03-17 | **21 days** |
| **2027** | **2027-02-23** | **2027-03-16** (posted) | **21 days** |

**Third — and this is why it is a finding rather than a run — the 21 is an identity.** The FOMC's
March meeting has opened on the **third Tuesday of March** in **7 of 7** posted years. And
`lastTue(February) + 21 days = thirdTue(March)` is arithmetically **always** true: the last Tuesday
of February falls Feb 22–29, and adding three weeks lands Mar 15–21, which is exactly the third
Tuesday's range. Tested over **1990–2060: 71 years, zero exceptions.** So as long as FHFA publishes
on last Tuesdays and the FOMC opens March on the third Tuesday, the February FHFA print sits three
weeks ahead of the Fed — not sometimes, always.

**What that buys, expressed against the full distribution.** Nearest-FOMC-day distance across all 68
FHFA release days since 2021: **median 13, min 0, max 21**. The six February releases are all at
**21** — the *maximum the distribution reaches*. Blackout exposure (second Saturday before a meeting
through the Thursday after): **23 of 68** releases overall, **0 of 6** Februaries. And 2027-02-23 is
**27 days past** the Jan 26–27 decision, with `fomc-blackout-start-2027-03-06` landing **11 days
after** it.

**This is the exact mirror of the January sibling.** `fhfa-hpi-2027-01-26` found the January print
pinned to **FOMC day one, 8 of 8**, because the FOMC opens January on the last Tuesday (**7 of 7** on
this parse) and FHFA publishes on last Tuesdays. The same two rules put the February print at the
opposite extreme of the same ruler. One publisher pair, two systematic outcomes, three weeks apart.

**Two honest limits.** The FOMC data begins **2021-01-26** on the page as fetched, so the 7-of-7
third-Tuesday claim is measured over 2021+ only; the identity is arithmetic and needs no sample. And
the Board calls every 2027 date *tentative until confirmed at the preceding meeting* — `fomc-2026-12-09`
is what confirms Mar 16–17, which is precisely what `-5`'s kill switch names. **A dated forward
caveat for whichever lane researches the 2028 February print:** the Jan→Feb last-Tuesday gap is 28
days in **68 of 71** years 1990–2060, but **35** in 2000, **2028** and 2056, the leap years whose Feb
29 is a Tuesday. The 27-day post-decision gap measured here is 7 of 7 but not an identity.

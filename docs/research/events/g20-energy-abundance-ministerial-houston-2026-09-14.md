**The rule for the next session, stated so it generalises past this case: write the falsifier as a
test on the outcome class, and let the channel be whatever it turns out to be.** The first should
have read *"any US federal instrument with a Federal Register or docket number, announced from the

[`opec-momr-2026-09-10`](opec-momr-2026-09-10.md) used to score its own window `unscoreable` rather
than claim it.

The tell that a falsifier has this defect is cheap to spot: **if it contains the words "rather than"
or a parenthetical list of examples, it is enumerating channels.** Convert the list into a property
the outcome must have.

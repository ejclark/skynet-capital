**Stance addendum 2026-08-31 (D-44) — the call is unchanged; the mechanism is amended and three
structural facts are added.** Receipt: the D-44 ledger row below.

1. **This print has a statutory floor.** The 2027 Social Security COLA is announced **2026-10-14**
   off the Q3 CPI-W average (Jul/Aug/**Sep**) — this release supplies the third input. In the 2025
   lapse BLS recalled furloughed staff to publish the September CPI on **2025-10-24**, nine days

   risk — a materially different exposure from `jobs-2026-10-02`, which has no such tether. This
   *narrows* the blackout scenario for this event and widens the timing uncertainty around it.
2. **The headline has a consequence here that it does not have elsewhere in this doc.** The
   "energy-carried headline heat is discounted" leg was derived from *equity* reaction functions and
   remains right for equities. It does not extend to the COLA channel, which is headline-CPI-W
   arithmetic with a 2027 fiscal-transfer outcome. Two channels, one print; the doc previously
   modelled only the first.

   it was at D-58.** Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote (08-28) pinned the hawkish case on **PCE** — 3.7%

   asymmetric the *other* way from the initial read. This print sits **14 days before the 10-28
   FOMC** and `pce-2026-10-29` publishes the morning *after* that statement, so this is the last
   major inflation reading that meeting sees — decision-relevance rises even as the
   September-specific asymmetry (initial-research leg 5) weakens; direction left unresolved rather
   than guessed.

   this print's surprise power. Warsh instead **formally abandoned forward guidance** ("a
   discipline, not a decision"). With no guidance to anchor to, each incoming print carries *more*

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** On **2026-09-30 at 17:00 JST** the Bank of Japan publishes its **October–December 2026**
JGB purchase schedule. **The headline number is already public and has been for three months**: the
June 16 2026 MPM's own plan attachment prints *"October-December 2026 | About 2.3 trillion yen"*,
and the ¥200bn-a-quarter pace behind it was fixed at the **June 2025** MPM, ~15 months out. So this
release is **not** the "first read on whether a post-hike BoJ trims its buying" the proposal called
it. The only genuinely new content is **which of the six maturity buckets absorbs the ~¥200bn/month
cut**, plus October's auction dates. On that question the record is unanimous and measured this
session from five consecutive schedules: the **">25 years" bucket has been frozen at ¥75bn per
auction / ¥150bn a month in every one**, while the monthly total fell **¥3.655tn → ¥2.47tn
(−32.4%)** — four cuts, ¥1.185tn removed, **zero of it from the super-long end**, whose *share* of
BoJ buying therefore rose **4.10% → 6.07%**. **Nothing here is ours to trade** (`symbols: []`, no
yen-rates exposure anywhere on the book). The one thing that could make 09-30 informative is the
**BoJ meeting on 09-18**, because the June plan reserves the right to amend the pace at an MPM and
rules out any other review. Date is `estimate` on a schema gap, not on doubt; it licenses nothing.

### The question, plainly

This entry was proposed on 2026-09-15 by the
[`jgb-40y-auction-2026-09-29`](jgb-40y-auction-2026-09-29.md) D-14 adjacency sweep, which had just
sourced the BoJ purchase number its own initial research had flagged as unsourced, and framed the
successor release as *"the first scheduled read on whether a post-hike BoJ trims that bucket
further."* That framing contains a testable assumption — that the release is a **read**, i.e. that
it resolves something. So the question is:

**how much information does the 09-30 release actually carry, and where in it does that information
sit?**

**One-line verdict:** the headline carries **none** — the Oct–Dec monthly total was published on
**2026-06-16** and its pace fixed on **2025-06-17** — and the residual information is confined to
the **maturity-bucket allocation**, where a five-schedule record says the super-long bucket will
again take none of the cut.

### Conviction legs, tested

**1. The Oct–Dec headline is already published, and the pace was fixed 15 months ago — SUPPORTED,
and it is the finding that demotes this event.** The June 16 2026 MPM's plan carries an attachment
titled *Amount of Monthly Outright Purchases of Japanese Government Bonds*:

| Quarter | Monthly purchase amount |
|---|---|
| April–June 2026 | About **2.7** trillion yen |
| July–September 2026 | About **2.5** trillion yen |
| **October–December 2026** | **About 2.3 trillion yen** |
| January–March 2027 | About **2.1** trillion yen |
| From April 2027 onward | About **2** trillion yen |

Its note states that the schedule *"including the purchase size per auction by residual maturity
and dates, will continue to be announced in the 'Quarterly Schedule of Outright Purchases …'"* —
i.e. the BoJ itself partitions the two documents exactly as this leg does: **plan = amount,
schedule = composition**. And the pace predates even that: the June **2025** MPM plan reads *"From
April-June 2026 to January-March 2027: about 200 billion yen"* per calendar quarter. The Oct–Dec
2026 figure has therefore been determinate since **2025-06-17**.

Point 3 of the June 2026 plan closes the last door: *"The Bank will not conduct interim assessments
of the plan for the outright purchases of JGBs in the future. However, the Bank is prepared to
amend the pace of its JGB purchases at the MPMs, if deemed necessary."* Passed **7–1** (against:
TAMURA Naoki, who wanted the ¥200bn/quarter cuts extended a further year to Jan–Mar 2028; UEDA
Kazuo absent). So the schedule release is a *publication*, and the **MPM is the only amendment
venue** — which is why the "this week" call points at 09-18 rather than at 09-30.

**2. The four cuts to date have taken nothing out of the super-long bucket — SUPPORTED, 5 of 5, and
this is the row to read on the day.** Monthly purchase amounts, ¥100mn, every coupon bucket of
every schedule since Jul–Sep 2025:

| More than 1y, up to 3y | 9,750 | 9,000 | 8,100 | 7,650 | 7,100 | −2,650 |
| More than 3y, up to 5y | 9,750 | 8,400 | 7,350 | 6,900 | 6,400 | −3,350 |
| More than 5y, up to 10y | 10,500 | 9,150 | 7,800 | 7,200 | 6,700 | −3,800 |
| More than 10y, up to 25y | 4,050 | 3,450 | 2,850 | 2,400 | 2,000 | −2,050 |

That total is **¥3.655tn → ¥2.47tn**, −32.4%, and it reconciles to the plan's own headline at every
step (about 3.7 / 3.3 / 2.9 / 2.7 / 2.5) — which is itself the cleanest evidence that these

−¥390bn, −¥195bn, −¥195bn**, i.e. the June 2025 plan's ¥400bn pace and then its ¥200bn pace, to the
rounding the word "about" allows.

**The super-long row never moves.** Its per-auction size is **750** (¥75bn) in all five schedules
and its frequency is **twice a month** in all five. The consequence is a rising share: >25y is
**4.10%** of BoJ monthly buying in Jul–Sep 2025 and **6.07%** in Jul–Sep 2026 — a **48% increase in
relative weight** with no policy decision to that effect, purely because the taper is being taken
entirely out of the shorter zones. At an Oct–Dec total of ~22,750 with >25y unchanged, the share
goes to **~6.6%**. Registered as `FT-boj-jgb-purchase-schedule-q4-2026-09-30-1` and `-2`.

**None.** The entry's value is diagnostic and, like its parent, largely **corrective**: it stops the

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (2026-09-15, D-15; date `estimate`).** **Stand aside, permanently.** No entry, exit or
hedge is keyed to the 2026-09-30 release or to the 09-30 session. The entry is kept for three
diagnostic jobs, in descending order of value:

1. **It corrects the premise it was proposed under.** This release does not *decide* the Oct–Dec
   purchase amount; the **June 16 2026 MPM** did, and the **June 2025 MPM** fixed the pace that
   produced it. Any future assessment describing 09-30 as "the BoJ cutting purchases" is describing
   a fifteen-month-old decision.
2. **It names the one row that carries information, and gives it a base rate.** *"More than 25
   years"* — ¥75bn/auction, ¥150bn/month, **5 of 5** schedules, through a −32.4% cut to the total.
3. **It banks the second-order number.** The super-long **share** of BoJ buying is rising
   mechanically (4.10% → 6.07%, and ~6.6% if 09-30 changes nothing), which is the quantity that
   actually matters for net super-long supply and which nobody announces.

Three reading rules attach to the release itself:

- **Read the composition, ignore the total.** The total is arithmetic off a published plan.
- **A change to the ">25 years" row is the surprise**, and it would be the first in five quarters —
  in either direction. A *cut* there would be a genuine tightening of super-long net supply; an
  *increase* would be the "nimble response" point 2 of the plan reserves for a rapid rise in
  long-term rates.
- **Credit MOF, not the Bank, for the Q4 tightening.** Absorption rises to ~25% in October because
  there is no 40-year that month, not because the BoJ bought more.

**Kill switches.**

1. **The "headline already published" premise breaks** — the **2026-09-18** MPM amends the monthly
   purchase pace, or the 09-30 release prints a summed coupon-JGB monthly total outside
   **¥2.225–2.325tn**. This document is rebuilt, not patched
   (`FT-boj-jgb-purchase-schedule-q4-2026-09-30-2`).
2. **The protected-super-long reading breaks** — the 09-30 release prints a *"More than 25 years"*
   monthly figure **other than 1,500** (¥100mn) or a per-auction size other than **750**
   (`FT-boj-jgb-purchase-schedule-q4-2026-09-30-1`).
3. **The frequency grid moves again** — any coupon zone's Oct–Dec monthly-to-per-auction ratio
   differs from its Jul–Sep value (`FT-boj-jgb-purchase-schedule-q4-2026-09-30-3`).
4. **The no-price-channel premise breaks** — a tracked name (NVDA/AVGO/MRVL/CRWV) moves **>2%** on a
   session between **2026-09-15** and **2026-10-01** *while USD/JPY strengthens ≥1% the same
   session*, i.e. the co-movement carries the yen sign a Japanese-rates story requires. The sibling's
   D-14 row is the precedent for requiring that second leg.
5. **The Q4 supply arithmetic breaks** — MOF's December 2026 calendar (due ~**2026-10-31**) carries
   no 30-year, or an announced 30-year size other than ¥600bn, or MOF changes the 40-year cadence
   away from bi-monthly.
6. **The release slips** — the Oct–Dec schedule is not published at 17:00 JST on **2026-09-30**. Two
   BoJ primaries three months apart name that date and time; a slip would mean the Bank changed a
   cadence it has run to the last business day of the quarter for at least five quarters.

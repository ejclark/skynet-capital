## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** what will the 2026-09-02 Beige Book likely say, does a qualitative
all-districts report move the tape at all inside a live *hike* debate, and does it change how we
sit in the tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN) into the Sep-16 FOMC?

**One-line verdict:** the last institutional Fed read before the blackout, and genuinely the
district-level test of the Warsh framing that turned 9/16 into a coin flip — but the only measured
evidence says it stops mattering once other macro data is in the window, and this window is packed
with it, so the honest call is *stand aside and read two counts*.

2. **The measured reaction function is weak, and weakest precisely in a week like this one —
   SUPPORTED.** Zavodny & Ginther, *"Does the Beige Book Move Financial Markets?"*, **Southern
   Economic Journal 2005, 72(1), 138–151** (read from the authors' source PDF, fetched 2026-08-30).
   They build a quantitative index of the qualitative reports and find: more positive Beige Book
   reports on economic growth are associated with **increases in interest rates, particularly
   intermediate- and long-term rates — but not after controlling for several other macroeconomic
   indicators.** Their own reading: markets treat it as *a summary indicator of economic growth,
   but not of monetary policy moves in the near term.* That control result is the load-bearing part
   here, because the 9/2 report does not arrive alone — ISM Manufacturing and JOLTS print 9/1, ADP
   at 08:15 the same morning, ISM Services 9/3, payrolls 9/4. The one condition under which the
   Beige Book historically shows an effect (being the only thing on the tape) is absent.

3. **…but the Warsh regime is the strongest available argument *against* leg 2 — MIXED, and
   labelled as theory, not measurement.** Warsh has withdrawn the two things that normally carry the
   Fed's pre-meeting signal. From the Board's own transcript of the **2026-08-28** Jackson Hole
   keynote: *"In normal times, the role of forward guidance should be limited and circumscribed,"*
   and he opposes mechanical reaction functions because *"our knowledge just doesn't extend that
   far."* Press coverage of his first meeting as chair (June 2026) reports he declined to submit his
   own fed-funds dot and called individual projections "not helpful in the conduct of policy"
   (press-sourced, not primary). With guidance gone and the dot plot devalued, the surviving
   Fed-published inputs carry proportionally more of the signal — so the 2005 finding is being
   applied out of sample, to a Fed that communicates differently than any in that study's window.
   That is a real caveat and it is **why the stance is "read it" rather than "ignore it"** — but an
   unmeasured mechanism does not upgrade a stand-aside into a position.

4. **It is one of the last three Fed-sourced inputs before the blackout — SUPPORTED.** The FOMC

## Stance & kill switches

**Stand aside. Read the report as evidence into 9/16, take no position sized to it.** The date is
`estimate`-labelled (federalreserve.gov, checked 2026-08-30), which widens caution and licenses
nothing. Concretely: no new risk opened for the 14:00 ET release, no adjustment to any tracked name
on its content alone, and the two counts (price 9/2/1, labor 5/7 as of July 15) are logged for the
next pulse to diff.

**Kill switches:**

- **Four or more districts describing "robust" or "strong" price growth on 2026-09-02** (vs two in
  July) — the district-side corroboration of Warsh's breadth claim, landing on a market already at
  ~55–59% hike. Kills "informationally inert," not the stand-aside; the next pulse re-reads the
  hike-odds channel.
- **Nine or more districts reporting little-to-no change or outright declines in employment, or
  explicit layoff language** — contradicts "quite stable … consistent with full employment" two days
  before payrolls, and makes the 9/4 print the adjudicator between the report and the chair.
- **A measurable tape move in the 2026-09-02 14:00–15:00 ET hour with the release as the named
  driver** — falsifies leg 2 for this regime and forces the reaction function to be rebuilt from
  scratch rather than patched.
- **The 9/2 release slipping or the time changing** — the entry is `estimate`; a schedule change is
  a calendar correction, not a market signal.

**No forward test registered.** The stance takes no position sized to any branch, so there is
nothing to score in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md); the kill switches above carry the
falsification, each with its date.

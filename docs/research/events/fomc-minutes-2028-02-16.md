The minutes of the January 25–26, 2028 FOMC should publish at 14:00 ET on **2028-02-16**. Nine sibling

Two things are open. First, the

and if so, is it luck or structure?** A perfect record with no mechanism behind it is a coincidence waiting
to end.

Second, the [12-29](fomc-minutes-2027-12-29.md) lane closed by showing the family's headline vol number was a
**calendar-window artifact** for its date, and [11-17](fomc-minutes-2027-11-17.md) had earlier shown it was a

lane could test the other's. **This date can test both at once** — if it collides with settlement, which
nobody had checked.

**One-line verdict:** the safety is **structural, not statistical** — a Monday-anchored closure and a
Wednesday-anchored release make the displacement mechanism *unable to fire* on this sub-class, which is why
it is 18-for-18 — and the tape question resolves harder than either sibling's: **2028-02-16 is a settlement
Wednesday, the January class is the family's most collided sub-class at 13 of 18, and once matched on both
window and settlement status the class is a perfect null.** Date `estimate`; no directional call at D−525.

   days from a Monday**, so the gap is quantised to exactly two values, and the record confirms it — across
   all 18 cases the gap is **+2 (fifteen times)** or **−5 (three times: 2011, 2012, 2022)** and nothing else.

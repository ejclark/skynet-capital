### Kill switches — one fired, one fired in substance and not in form, three did not

4. **Channel kill** (a 2026-09-16 FOMC moving the S&P ≥1.5% on a <5bp 2y move) — **not yet
   adjudicable**; it scores on 09-16 and belongs to the FOMC ledger. But 09-03 supplies evidence

5. **Guidance-restoration kill** — **fired in substance, and was refuted in form by the speaker in
   the same breath.** This is the most interesting result in the close-out. The switch read:
   *"Waller publishing an explicit reaction function or rate path on 9/3 would contradict Warsh's
   8/28 framework."* He published an explicit reaction function — keyed to a **dated** release, the
   09-11 CPI — and then spent half the speech explaining why that is **not** forward guidance. His
   own taxonomy, verbatim: he communicates (i) *"why I have taken my current policy position"*, (ii)
   *"how future economic data will shape my future monetary policy decisions—in short, I try to
   describe my 'reaction function'"*, and (iii) forward guidance, *"which specifies a path for the
   policy rate that is essentially independent of incoming data."* Then: *"I agree with Chairman
   Warsh that forward guidance isn't appropriate now or in many other situations."* **Our kill switch
   conflated categories (ii) and (iii) that Waller himself separates, and the speech is titled after
   the distinction** — "…and Some Comments on My Policy Communication." So the switch is **not**
   scored as fired: the Board has not restored guidance, and the correct reading of 09-03 is that a
   data-contingent reaction function is now the *sanctioned* form of Fed communication under Warsh,
   not a breach of it. **The repair, for every future Fed-speak event in this calendar:** stop
   watching for "guidance"; watch for a **reaction function with a dated trigger**, because that is
   what this Board will give and — on the evidence of 12 points in minutes — it is what the tape

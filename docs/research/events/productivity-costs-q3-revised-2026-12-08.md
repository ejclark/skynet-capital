## Initial research

### The question, plainly

The calendar entry for this event asserts that the **12-08 revision**, not the 11-05 preliminary, is
the Q3 productivity vintage the December FOMC actually reads. Granting that: **how much does the
revision move, what does it move, and can it change any conclusion the preliminary already
supported?**

**One-line verdict.** The revision is materially large — nonfarm-business ULC has moved up to 1.6
percentage points of quarterly SAAR between vintages — but a quarterly revision passes into the
4-quarter rate at exactly one quarter's weight, so on the central case it cannot carry unit labor
costs from below the Fed's 2% objective to at or above it; **the 12-08 release is custody of a
number whose meaning was fixed on 11-05**, and the one observation that would invert that is a
preliminary ULC print of 1.81% SAAR or higher.

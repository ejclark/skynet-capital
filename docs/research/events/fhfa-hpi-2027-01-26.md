### Leg 3 — **THE FINDING: the January FHFA print is structurally FOMC day one** · **SUPPORTED**

Neither sibling looked at the Fed's calendar against FHFA's. Doing so produces a mechanical result
rather than a coincidence, and it turns on two published scheduling rules colliding:

- **FOMC meetings run Tuesday–Wednesday.** All 56 date-pairs on `fomccalendars.htm` do.
- **FHFA publishes on the last Tuesday** — 68 of 68 since 2021.

Two consequences follow with no statistics at all:

1. **An FHFA release can never fall on an FOMC decision day**, because a decision day is always a
   Wednesday. Measured: **0 of 68**.
2. **An FHFA release falls on FOMC day one** exactly when the meeting opens on a last Tuesday.
   Measured: **17 of 68** — 2021-01-26, 2021-04-27, 2021-07-27, 2022-01-25, 2022-07-26, 2023-01-31,
   2023-07-25, 2023-10-31, 2024-01-30, 2024-04-30, 2024-07-30, 2025-01-28, 2025-07-29, 2025-10-28,
   2026-01-27, 2026-04-28, 2026-07-28. **A quarter of all FHFA release days sit inside an FOMC
   meeting.**

**In January it is not occasional — it is every year:**

| Year | FHFA January release | FOMC January meeting | Day one? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 2021-01-26 | Jan 26–27 | **yes** |
| 2022 | 2022-01-25 | Jan 25–26 | **yes** |
| 2023 | 2023-01-31 | Jan 31–Feb 1 | **yes** |
| 2024 | 2024-01-30 | Jan 30–31 | **yes** |

| 2028 | not yet posted | Jan 25–26 (posted); last Tuesday of Jan 2028 **is** Jan 25 | **yes, if FHFA holds its rule** |

**8 of 8**, using the Fed's own posted dates in both forward years. The mechanism is that both

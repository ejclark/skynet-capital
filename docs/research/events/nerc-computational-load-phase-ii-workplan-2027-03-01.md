## Initial research

### The question, plainly

The [nerc-computational-load-standards-2026-12-31](nerc-computational-load-standards-2026-12-31.md)
research found this deadline while establishing that 12-31 is a filing date rather than an effective
one, and proposed it to the calendar as the successor federal instrument on the data-center-power
theme. So: **is the 2027-03-01 date real and correctly described, what would the Phase II work plan
actually contain, and does anything about it reach a book holding NVDA / MRVL / AVGO / CRWV / MSFT /
GOOG / META / AAPL / AMZN plus the AI-energy sleeve?**

**One-line verdict.** Real, correctly described as an *informational* filing on five independent
reads — and the least tradeable instrument on this calendar, because the thing due on 03-01 is a
description of future work whose content NERC already published in July 2026.

**Method.** Sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) (sector

### Legs tested

1. **"FERC's order requires a NERC filing by 2027-03-01."** **SUPPORTED, on five independent
   secondary reads that agree on the date and, more importantly, on its *character*.** Troutman
   (2026-07-22): NERC must submit "a more detailed **informational filing** describing its workplan
   regarding Reliability Standards relating to computational load integration." Snell & Wilmer
   (2026-07-23): "a March 1, 2027, informational filing describing the next phase of standards
   development." AMPYX Cyber (2026-07-21): "submit a Phase II workplan by March 1, 2027," and
   explicitly "informational in nature rather than a directive requiring immediate standards
   development." Willkie Farr (2026-07-30) and POWER (2026-07-16) carry the same date. Still no
   primary read — `ferc.gov` 403s and eLibrary is unreachable headlessly, now a **third** consecutive
   failure — so the entry stays `NEWS:`/`estimate` however well corroborated.
2. **"NERC has a Phase II work plan, and we can see it before FERC does."** **SUPPORTED, and this is
   the session's finding — primary, from nerc.com fetched today.** The LLWG's *Final Computational
   Loads Standard Authorization Request Roadmap* (**July 2026**) states its own purpose verbatim: it
   "establishes a structured and phased approach," consolidating the LLWG Gap Assessment white paper
   into "a multiyear sequence of SAR/SIR activities, progressing from high-priority reliability risks
   (**Project 2026-02 Phase 1**) in the near term to more complex and emerging issues **extending into
   2027 and beyond**." It carries a per-gap table with an **"Estimated SAR/SIR Submission By"** column
   whose values are **Q2 2026 (Project 2026-02)**, **Q4 2026**, **Q1–Q3 2027** and **Q4 2027**, across
   eight categories: interconnection requirements · interconnection and planning studies · data
   sharing and modeling · protection, disturbance performance and stability · disturbance monitoring
   and reporting · real-time communication · balancing and operations · cyber & physical security.
3. **"Phase II is where the consequential requirements live."** **REFUTED on NERC's own grading.**
   The roadmap's **Priority** column puts the **High** items in Phase 1 (Q2 2026 — MOD-032, IRO-010,
   PRC-028, COM-002 analogues) and Q4 2026 (PRC-024/PRC-029 ride-through, PRC-002, TPL-001/FAC-002
   oscillatory work, CIP-004/005/006/007/008/014 security). What is left for **Q1–Q3 2027** is graded
   **Medium** throughout — MOD-026/MOD-032 dynamic model quality, PRC-027, BAL-002/BAL-003 largest-
   credible-load-loss, IRO-008 near-term studies, IRO-017 outage assessment, PER-005/PER-006 operator
   training, TPL-001/FAC-002 co-located-load performance — and **Q4 2027** is graded **Low**: EOP-011
   manual load shedding and PRC-012 remedial action schemes. Phase II is the residual tier, by the
   authoring body's own priority scoring.
4. **"The Phase II work plan commits NERC to anything."** **REFUTED.** The roadmap disclaims its own
   bindingness twice, verbatim: "the RSTC subgroup must obtain RSTC approval to add the SAR to its
   work plan. Ultimately, the final SAR purpose and scope will be determined by that subgroup," and
   the technical-work items "are ultimately up to the discretion of the relevant subgroups and the
   RSTC." Its conclusion adds that subgroups "should assess whether Project 2026-02 has fully closed
   the gaps mentioned. If gaps are not fully closed, additional SAR/SIRs may be necessary." A work
   plan of discretionary recommendations, filed for information, is roughly the weakest regulatory
   instrument that can carry a federal deadline.
5. **"Any of this reaches a tracked name."** **REFUTED, and by a wider margin than the sibling.**
   The 12-31 ledger already measured the channel and found it absent for silicon and landing on
   facility owners for the sleeve, with Texas's NOGRR282 (PUCT 5–0, 2026-07-09, ≥75 MW) as the only
   costed precedent at a secondary-sourced $0.5–1M/MW at some facilities. Phase II is strictly
   further from any obligation than Phase 1: it is a plan for SARs that would become standards that
   would need FERC approval that would then run implementation plans. Every link in that chain is
   undated past "Q1–Q3 2027," and undated is not a position.
6. **"The corridor around 2027-03-01 is contaminated."** **REFUTED — and it is the one structural
   improvement over the sibling.** No tracked event falls within 5 days; the nearest are
   `fomc-2027-01-27` (33 days prior) and `ftc-v-amazon-antitrust-trial-2027-03-29` (28 days after).
   Where 12-31 shares its date with three other tracked events and is therefore unattributable, an
   early-March power-complex move would be cleanly attributable. There is simply nothing here
   expected to move anything.

## Stance & kill switches

expect to keep standing aside. 2027-03-01 is an informational filing deadline for a work plan whose
substance NERC's own Large Loads Working Group published in July 2026, covering the **Medium**- and
**Low**-priority residue of a gap review whose High-priority items went into Phase 1. It stays `low`
impact and `symbols: []`. This ledger exists to track one documentary question — whether the filed
work plan is the roadmap — and to carry the theme forward after the 12-31 sibling closes out.

**Kill switches.**

- **The "pre-answered" core claim dies** if RSTC or the Standards Committee accepts a Phase II
  SAR/SIR whose scope departs from the roadmap's eight recommended categories, **on or before
  2026-10-31** — the roadmap would then not be the work plan.
- **The "quiet until 2027" call dies** if a Phase II SAR is posted to nerc.com's
  standards-under-development list **on or before 2026-09-18**, i.e. well ahead of the roadmap's own
  Q1–Q3 2027 estimate.
- **The `low` impact tier dies** if any Phase II SAR carries a dated compliance obligation landing
  inside a quarter this book trades, or if a tracked name quantifies computational-load compliance as
  a capex or schedule item in a filing **on or before 2026-12-31**.
- **The date itself dies** if NERC requests an extension at FERC, or if the Phase 1 filing misses
  2026-12-31 (tracked as `FT-nerc-computational-load-standards-2026-12-31-1`), **on or before
  2027-01-15** — 03-01 is strictly downstream of 12-31.

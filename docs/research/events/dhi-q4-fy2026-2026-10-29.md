## Initial research

**The question.** D.R. Horton, ITB's largest holding and the country's largest builder by closings,
reports FY Q4 before the open on 2026-10-29. Is anything here tradable for this book, and what does
it tell the six housing ledgers that sit around it (NAR metro prices 10:00 the same morning, Case-Shiller
and new-home-sales on 10-27, housing-vacancies 10-28)?

**One-line verdict.** No — the size of the gap is statistically real, its direction is not, no playbook
covers the name, and the open is shared with GDP and PCE; the print's value is informational.

**Method.** `earnings-cycle.mjs DHI --bench QQQ --peers LEN,PHM,TOL,ITB` (88 prints, 2004-10-07 ..
2026-07-21, release dates from SEC 8-K Item 2.02) and `intraday-edges.mjs DHI` (721 hourly sessions,
2023-11-17 .. 2026-10-16), caches busted first. **Then re-derived the reaction legs by hand**, because
of the instrument limit in Leg 2. Fundamentals from the company's own Q3 8-K exhibit 99.1.

### Leg 1 — the date and the status: **SUPPORTED**, promoted to `confirmed`

The company's own release (`investor.drhorton.com`, fetched direct 2026-10-16, HTTP 200) lists all four
fiscal-2026 dates; the other three (Jan 20, Apr 21, Jul 21) match the 8-K Item 2.02 dates EDGAR holds,
so the schedule is 3/3 on execution this year. It states *"before the market opens"* and an 8:30 a.m. ET
call each morning. The discovering lane could not self-confirm; this owner read the primary itself,
the `lennar-q3-fy2026-2026-09-16` precedent.

### Leg 2 — the instrument reads the wrong day for this issuer: **a finding, and it changes every number**

`earnings-cycle.mjs` treats D (the 8-K date) as **after the close**, so its "GAP" is close D → open D+1.
DHI releases **before the open**, so that window is an ordinary overnight (the instrument prints a modern
mean of **−0.05%**) and the real reaction is **close D−1 → open D**. The same offset contaminates its
RUN-UP windows (close D−5/−20 → close D include the reaction day) and the peer run-ups it quotes. Every
number below is re-derived from the same split- and dividend-adjusted bars with the correct window.

### Leg 3 — "the pre-open gap is big": **SUPPORTED**

Modern era (2023+, n = 15): mean |gap| **5.27%**; ordinary-day |gap| over 951 sessions **0.84%**.
**11 of 15** gaps were ≥ 3% against **30 of 951 (3.15%)** ordinary days; **7 of 15** were ≥ 5%.
The fiscal-Q4 gaps in particular are the fattest: **2024-10-29 −15.07%**, **2025-10-28 −6.84%**.

### Leg 4 — "the gap has a direction": **REFUTED**

Gaps since 2023: +1.5 +5.5 +3.1 +0.9 −5.8 +5.0 +6.2 −15.1 +2.9 +3.3 +10.2 −6.8 −3.7 +8.9 +0.2 (%).
**11 up of 15** gives P(≥11 | n = 15, 53% ordinary up-rate) = **0.092**; mean **+1.07%**, median
**+2.88%**, sd **6.62%**, **t = 0.63**. The median is positive and the mean is dragged by a −15% tail;
neither survives its own dispersion. Contrast LEN (3 up in 14, p = 0.029) — DHI has no such asymmetry.

### Leg 5 — "the pop fades through the session" (S3-shaped): **REFUTED**

Reaction-day session (open D → close D), modern: mean **+0.30%**, win **47%**, sd 4.27% — no fade
(the instrument's "−0.44% fade" was measured on the wrong day). Whole-day move D−1 close → D close:
mean +1.28%, |move| **4.87%**, win 53%. S3 is scoped to NVDA/MSFT/GOOG and blocked anyway.

### Leg 6 — "there is a pre-print run-up" (S1-shaped): **MIXED, leaning not-significant**

Clean windows, excluding the reaction day: D−6 → D−1 mean **+2.69%** (win 60%) against **+0.36%** (51%)
for an ordinary 6-day window, **t ≈ 1.79**; D−21 → D−1 **+2.25%** vs **+1.50%** ordinary. Suggestive on the
short window, not significant on either, and the sweep's kill list already records **S1-beyond-NVDA**
as killed — this is a confirmation on another symbol, not a re-proposal. Post-reaction D → D+5 is
**+0.30%** vs +0.32% ordinary: nothing to own.

### Leg 7 — E1 and the intraday tape: **SUPPORTED, and nothing tradable**

First-hour share of daily range **31.3%** (inside E1's band), drift **+0.056%**/hour-1 (not significant).
Every intraday strategy loses to buy-and-hold net of 5 bps/side; best net is the opening-range breakout
at **−0.050%/day** on a **2.0 bps** break-even — the sub-5-bps signature the instrument warns about.

### Leg 8 — the fundamental setup: **SUPPORTED, margin-led and order-book-soft**

Company Q3 FY2026 8-K (exhibit 99.1, fetched direct 2026-10-16; quarter ended 2026-06-30): EPS
**$3.20**, revenue **$9.2B**, home sales gross margin **20.7%**, **23,983** closings (+4%), net orders
**23,084 / $8.4B**, cancellation rate **20% vs 17%**, backlog **15,983 homes / $6.179B**, **23,300** unsold
homes of 38,000 in inventory (7,600 completed). FY2026 guide: revenue **$32.5–33.0B**, closings
**83,800–84,300**, gross margin **20.5–21.0%**. On incentives the company said it *"expect[s] sales
incentives to remain elevated during the fourth quarter"*. The stock rose ~1.5% pre-market on 2026-07-21
(consensus EPS $2.97, search-sourced) — the bar's recorded gap was **+0.15%**. Q4 guidance was deferred to
the call (transcript not read); **no Q4 consensus was found**, so none is claimed here.

### The collision

2026-10-28 FOMC → **2026-10-29 08:30 ET** DHI release + call, GDP Q3 advance and PCE → ECB decision
→ **10:00 ET** NAR metro prices → AAPL and AMZN after the close. The open prices all of it at once, so no
move in DHI that morning is attributable to the print alone.

### Honest limits

15 modern prints is a small sample for a distribution with a −15% tail. Peers were not re-derived on the
corrected window. Q4 consensus, the whisper and the implied move were not found. PHM's 2026-10-22 date is
search-sourced. No cited source failed.

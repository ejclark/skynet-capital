### Leg 1 — the date: SUPPORTED
The Underwriting section defines one "Restricted Period" of 180 days after the date of the prospectus (the cover reads "Prospectus Dated June 9, 2026") for the company and for "our directors and executive officers and the holders of substantially all of our common stock, stock options, or any securities convertible into or exercisable or exchangeable for ... common stock, including ER Holdings membership interests". 180 days after 06-09 is Sunday 2026-12-06, so Monday 12-07 is the lockup study's day 0. The earlier estimate saw "30, 180": the 30 is the underwriters' 30-day option to buy 4,186,046 more shares, which was never exercised (Class A outstanding 48,174,023 on 2026-08-07 = 20,267,046 pre-IPO + 27,906,977 sold). The exceptions are gifts, estate planning, affiliate transfers, tax withholding on vesting and 10b5-1 plans that sell nothing in the period. No earnings, price or blackout trigger. The only early path is Morgan Stanley's written consent. The Q2 10-Q mentions no change to it and EDGAR lists no 8-K that touches it. One drafting point matters for timing: holders may not make a registration demand or file a draft registration statement during the period, except clause (H), which lets the company submit one for stockholders whose restrictions have expired or been waived, so a registered sale cannot go public before 12-07 without a waiver.

### Leg 2 — supply: MIXED (6.9x the float, an Up-C, a single holder at 47%)
Float: 27,906,977 shares, all primary, none from selling holders, about $185M at $6.64. Locked: 20,267,046 Class A shares plus 171,226,057 Class B shares paired with Class B Units of Enchanted Rock Holdings, exchangeable one for one into Class A, so **191.5M locked, 6.9x the float, about 60 days of the 20-day average volume (3.21M) or 73 days of the median (2.63M)**. A further 57,065,274 Class M (profits-interest) units exist, convertible into Class B Units at a ratio set by the value above their threshold (52.4M Class A shares reserved for exchange of the units issued on conversion); only a sliver is vested (the sponsor's 111,545). Energy Impact Fund (FT-B and FT-D, managed by Energy Impact Partners) holds 19,350,897 Class A and 83,355,210 Class B, **102.7M, about 47% of the 219.4M Class A plus Class B shares** (13D filed 2026-06-18; the 78.1% on its cover counts only its own units as exchanged). The McAndrew group holds about 22.6M (13G/A filed 2026-09-04). To sell units a holder must exchange to Class A. As an affiliate the sponsor may sell without registration, in any three months, the greater of 1% of Class A (0.48M) or the four-week average weekly volume reported through the NYSE (about 16.0M, my arithmetic: the 20-day average times 5). Only shares count toward it, and the sponsor already holds 19.35M as Class A, so up to about 16M, 57% of the free float, could be sold in a quarter without a registered offering. A larger sale needs a registered offering, and the company is not S-3 eligible until about June 2027 (12 months of reporting; my reading), so a deal needs a full S-1. The sponsor's cost basis is not in the filings I read; at $6.64 every investor in the IPO is down 69%. Cash is $626.6M (10-Q, 2026-06-30), so the company has no need to sell stock.

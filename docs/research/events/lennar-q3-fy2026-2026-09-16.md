## Initial research

**The question.** Lennar reports Q3 FY2026 after the close on 2026-09-16. Is there anything here we
can act on, and what does the print tell the six house-price ledgers whose instrument tables run on
LEN/DHI/PHM/TOL/ITB/XHB but which have never had a builder demand print on the board?

**One-line verdict.** No — the only significant statistic is an overnight gap asymmetry our own
universal rule (S2) forbids capturing and our short-side playbook (S3) is blocked from trading, and
the 2026-09-17 tape is triple-confounded; the print's value here is informational, as a read on
whether the builder breakdown the 09-29 corridor measured is a demand story or a margin story.

**Method.** Instruments re-run live this session, not recalled: `earnings-cycle.mjs LEN --bench QQQ
--peers DHI,PHM,TOL,ITB` (79 prints, 2004-09-22 .. 2026-06-11; release dates from SEC 8-K Item 2.02;
price history through 2026-09-15) and `intraday-edges.mjs LEN` (723 hourly-bar sessions,
2023-10-17 .. 2026-09-15). Per-print gap detail re-derived directly from the same split- and
dividend-adjusted bars. Fundamentals from the company's own releases; corridor from
`src/domain/market-events/`.

### Leg 2 — "the overnight gap is a fat-tailed coin flip" (S2's stated basis): **REFUTED in LEN's favour, and it strengthens S2 rather than weakening it**

S2 is justified in `trade-playbooks.md` on the grounds that *"every print gap tested is a fat-tailed
coin flip."* On LEN it is fat-tailed but **not** a coin flip — it is a **loaded** one, and loaded the
wrong way for anyone long into the print. Modern era (2023+, n=14): **mean −2.45%, median −2.24%,
win 21%, p10 −6.12%, p90 +3.00%**. The full 79-print history is milder (mean −0.46%, win 44%), so
this is a regime, not a permanent property.

| 2023-09-14 | −6.41% | 2025-06-17 | −1.75% |

**The control the instrument does not run, run here.** `intraday-edges.mjs` measures LEN's *ordinary*
overnight move over 723 sessions covering the same era: **mean −0.007%, win 52%**. So the null is a
near-exact coin flip, and 3 up in 14 gives **P(≤3 | n=14, p=0.50) = 0.0287**, or **0.0202** against
the measured 52%. One-sample t on the gaps: **t = −2.82** (sd 3.26). This is the opposite of what
happened to the run-up leg below — it clears its control rather than dying to it.

**Sharper still, and the reason the stance is a refusal rather than a shrug:** across the **last 10**
prints the *full reaction day* (close D → close D+1) averaged **−3.94%** and was red **9 times**
(P(≤1 up | n=10, p=0.5) = **0.011**), including an unbroken **8-print** red-gap run from 2024-03 to
2025-12.

### Leg 3 — "so fade the reaction-day open" (S3-shaped): **REFUTED — the asymmetry is in the gap, not the session**

The obvious next move is the wrong one. LEN's reaction-day *session* (open D+1 → close D+1) is
modern-era **mean −0.50%, median −0.38%, win 43%, n=14** — a weak, unsizeable tilt, and the 2020-2022
era ran **+2.77%** the other way. S3 is explicitly scoped in `trade-playbooks.md` to NVDA/MSFT/GOOG
as a *mega-cap class effect* ("absent on semis + AAPL"); LEN supplies a third class where it is
absent, which is a useful out-of-sample negative for S3's scope line and not a licence to extend it.
S3 is in any case **blocked**, needing shorting and Eric's sign-off.

### Leg 4 — "there is a pre-print positioning bid" (S1-shaped): **REFUTED, and it fails the exact control S1 was built to pass**

Modern era D-20 → D: **−0.40% mean, −1.41% median, win 43%** against a non-earnings baseline of
**+2.14%** — **−2.55% excess**. The instrument's own control: *every* 20-day window in the era means
+0.24% at 53% win; the pre-earnings windows mean −0.40% at 43%, and **P(6/14 positive | base rate) =
0.8394 → NOT SIGNIFICANT**. S1 needed P=0.004 and 14/14 to earn NVDA-only status; LEN is not close.

**The out-of-sample control kills the sector-seasonal rescue too.** Over **LEN's own** pre-print
windows the peers run up *more* than LEN does — DHI **+1.30%**, PHM **+2.27%**, TOL **+3.18%**, ITB
**+0.76%**. A builder-seasonal effect would show there and not in LEN; a LEN-specific effect would
show the reverse. The data says neither is tradable here, and the sweep's kill list already records
**S1-beyond-NVDA** as killed — this is not a re-proposal, it is a confirmation on a 10th symbol.

### Leg 5 — E1 ("don't trade the open") out-of-sample: **MIXED, and the mixed half is the interesting one**

E1 is universal across 9 symbols plus QQQ on the claim that the first hour carries **28–34% of daily
volatility at ~zero drift**. On LEN the **range** framing reproduces cleanly — the 09:30 hour is
**31.1% of daily range**, dead centre of the band, at **|move| 0.942%** vs 0.275% for the 15:30 hour
— while the **vol-share** framing does not: 09:30 is only **19.7%** of variance because LEN's
**15:30 bar carries 21.9%**, the largest single share of the day. So on this name the close, not the
open, is where variance concentrates, while the open still owns the range. Drift is absent as E1
claims: first hour **+0.047%** mean, sd 1.26, **t ≈ 1.00** over 723 sessions — not significant. E1's
conclusion holds on LEN; one of its two justifications does not, and that distinction belongs in the
playbook's evidence column rather than in a trade.

**And nothing else on this name is tradable either.** Every strategy `intraday-edges.mjs` tests loses
to buy-and-hold net of 5bps/side — and **buy-and-hold is itself −11.0% net over the 723 sessions**
(Sharpe −0.11). Best net is the opening-range breakout at **−0.044%/day** on a **2.4bps** break-even,
which is precisely the sub-5bps "someone else's infrastructure" signature the instrument warns about
and which the playbooks already killed.

### Leg 6 — the fundamental setup: **SUPPORTED, and it is a compression story, not a demand story**

Lennar's Q2 FY2026 release (2026-06-11, company primary): EPS **$1.24** ($1.31 ex mark-to-market),
revenue **$7.9B**, new orders **21,749 homes / $8.2B**, deliveries **20,519 / $7.6B**, backlog
**16,818 homes / $6.6B**, ASP **$371,000**, homebuilding gross margin **15.6%**. Management's own
framing of the mechanism, verbatim: **"approximately 12.9% in incentives, along with base price
adjustments necessary to sustain volume"**, against **"normalized levels of 4% to 6%"**, in a market
of **"persistently elevated mortgage rates, constrained affordability, and cautious consumer
sentiment"** — and the company's read on the other side, also verbatim: **"Demand is real, deferred,
and building."**

Q3 guidance from the same release: deliveries **20,500–21,500**, new orders **21,000–22,000**, ASP
**$375,000–$380,000**, gross margin **~16%**, SG&A **8.8–9.0%**. Street consensus into the print
(aggregator-sourced, and labelled as such): EPS **~$1.30**, **−35% y/y** from $2.00, on revenue
**~$8.37B**, **−5% y/y**; the Zacks consensus drifted **$1.31 → $1.29** in the seven days to
2026-09-15. **So volume is guided flat-to-up and earnings are guided down a third — the entire
delta is price and incentive.** That is what makes the orders-and-incentives pair, not EPS, the line
worth reading.

Corroborating sector read: the **NAHB/Wells Fargo HMI for August 2026 was 35**, up one point, with
current sales conditions **39**, six-month expectations **43**, and **traffic of prospective buyers
at 23** — the September edition (`nahb-hmi-2026-09-16`, confirmed) prints this morning, hours before
Lennar's release.

### Honest limits

1. **No implied move.** This lane has no options-chain source, so the market's priced expectation is
   unknown and nothing here is compared against it. Every distribution above is **realized**, not
   implied. A ledger that quoted an implied move from an aggregator would be borrowing a number it
   cannot check; this one does not.
2. **Consensus is aggregator-sourced.** The $1.30 EPS / $8.37B revenue / $1.31→$1.29 drift figures
   come from financial media, not a primary filing, and are labelled so at every use. The
   **guidance** numbers are the company's own.
3. **The regime hazard the kill list names is live here.** The sweep's most recent kill is *"do not
   register an execution base rate from a sub-sample when another test in the same document predicts
   moving the variable that defines the sub-sample."* The 11-of-14 down-gap record is a
   **margin-compression-regime** sample, and FOMC that same afternoon is exactly the variable that
   could end that regime. The forward test below is therefore stated as a **joint region**
   (regime-conditional), never as a marginal direction call — which is what the kill demands.
4. **The 09-17 gap will be uninterpretable as an earnings signal** whatever it does, because three
   dated prints land inside it. The forward test scores the *mechanism*, not the direction alone.
5. **Prices are 2026-09-15 closes.** Today's session was live while this was written; nothing here
   reads 09-16 intraday.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance: stand aside, on every horizon — and specifically, refuse the one edge we found.** The
gap asymmetry (3-up-in-14, p=0.029, **confirmed** event) is real and unmonetizable under this house's
own rules: capturing it long means holding the print (S2 forbids universally), capturing it short
means S3 (blocked, and scoped to mega-caps where LEN is not). Nothing keys to LEN in the playbooks,
every intraday variant loses net of costs, and buy-and-hold is negative over the whole 723-session
window. **In a compounding book this refusal is the P&L.** What the print *is* good for is
informational: it is the first builder demand read this calendar has carried, 13 days ahead of
`case-shiller-hpi-2026-09-29`, and the orders-versus-incentives pair tells that family whether the
builder tape's **−4.30% ITB** drawdown is pricing a demand break or a margin squeeze.

**Kill switches — what would change this stance, each dated:**

- **The gap flips with the mechanism intact** — a **positive** 2026-09-17 open gap while reported
  homebuilding gross margin is **≤16%** and incentives **≥12.9%**. That kills the regime explanation,
  not just the direction, and the asymmetry would have to be re-derived from scratch.
- **Compression bottoms** — reported gross margin **>16%** *and* incentives **<12.9%** on the
  2026-09-16 release. The thermometer read for 09-29 inverts: price is no longer the clearing
  mechanism.
- **Demand breaks instead** — Q3 new orders **below 21,000** (the guidance floor) or cancellations
  materially above the Q2 run-rate. That would make the builder tape a demand story and would matter
  to the 09-29 family far more than to LEN.
- **A playbook comes to key on builders** — any addition to `trade-playbooks.md` scoping a playbook
  to LEN/DHI/PHM/TOL/ITB/XHB. Until then "avoid as a trade" is a statement about our toolkit, not
  about the name.
- **Mortgage transmission moves** — `MORTGAGE30US` outside **[6.25%, 7.00%]** on the 2026-10-01
  `pmms-2026-10-01` survey, following today's FOMC.

**Forward tests registered:** two, both in
[`forward-tests/lennar-q3-fy2026-2026-09-16.md`](../forward-tests/lennar-q3-fy2026-2026-09-16.md),
both scoring by **2026-09-18** — inside this event's `closeOutWithinDays: 6` window, so its own
close-out can score them.

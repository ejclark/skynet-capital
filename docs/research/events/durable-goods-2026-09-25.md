## Initial research

### The question, plainly

What is the Advance Durable Goods report, what should we expect from the August-2026 edition on
2026-09-25, does the release move the market, does it survive a 10-01 funding lapse, and does it actually
give this book (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN) the AI-capex read its calendar entry
claimed?

**One-line verdict:** no — the entry's own rationale fails on the primary's explanatory notes, because
**new orders exclude semiconductor manufacturing**; what survives is a narrow reading order (the
ex-transportation core, whose dispersion is a tenth of the headline's, and the nondefense capital-goods
shipments line that feeds Q3 GDP), a **measured** stand-aside on the morning itself, and one genuinely
consequential finding — the lapse risk belongs to the **successor** edition on 10-27, not to this one.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `confirmed`, two Census primaries fetched 2026-09-01).** Treat 2026-09-25 08:30 ET as a
**reading exercise, never an event**: no position is opened, closed or sized off it, no house playbook
targets it, and the refusal is **measured** — across 12 sourced releases the open gap is statistically
indistinguishable from an ordinary overnight (median percentile 46 SPY / 45 QQQ against a null of 50;
6/12 and 5/12 above median). The standing instruction is a reading order: **ex-transportation first,
nondefense capital-goods shipments second, headline last**, because the headline's dispersion is 10.9×
the core's and its mean forecast miss 7.5×, while the core has printed positive in all seven 2026 months
with an identical σ of 0.34 in 2025 and 2026. **This release is not an AI-capex read** — its new-orders
figures exclude semiconductor manufacturing outright, which is a definitional fact from the primary, not
a data-quality caveat. The event this calendar should actually watch is the **successor**,
`durable-goods-2026-10-27` (`estimate`, filed in this PR): it prints on the first morning of the Oct 27–28
FOMC and, on this series' own 2025 lapse precedent (~27–29 days), would slip past the vote if FY27 funding
lapses on 2026-09-30.

**Kill switches:**

- **An open gap above the 90th percentile on 2026-09-25 with a session wrap naming this release as the
  cause** — leg 5's null breaks and the `medium` impact tier is understated. 09-25 is the sample's first
  clean attribution window (leg 6), so this is scoreable rather than arguable; re-run the leg-5
  measurement with the new observation before repeating any of it.
- **Ex-transportation printing negative on 2026-09-25** — the first negative core month since at least
  January 2025 would end the "slow persistent drift" reading and make this release a genuine cycle signal
  rather than context; the reading order survives but the impact tier goes up.
- **The headline and the core moving together by a similar magnitude on 2026-09-25** — the 10.9×
  dispersion split that justifies ignoring the headline stops holding, and the reading order collapses to
  one number.
- **A CR signed on or before 2026-09-30** — leg 7's delay branch closes, `durable-goods-2026-10-27`
  becomes an ordinary print, and this doc's only consequential forward claim retires.
- **A lapse on 2026-10-01** — the trigger to re-date `durable-goods-2026-10-27` (~27–29 days on precedent)
  and to re-check whether the **2026-10-02 full M3 report** and the **2026-10-29 Q3 GDP advance** slip with
  it, since leg 8's channel runs through both.
- **Census begins publishing semiconductor new orders** (a change to the M3 explanatory note, or a
  restored order panel) — leg 2's refutation of the AI-proxy rationale would no longer hold and this

## Initial research

**The question.** Will the October 27–28 FOMC — the third decision of the Warsh-chaired Fed, a
no-SEP meeting landing in the middle of the mega-cap earnings cluster — cut, hold, or hike, and
what does its reaction function mean for the nine names we track?

**One-line verdict.** Base case is a hawkish hold with live hike risk (a regime that has already
flipped from "when do they cut" to "will they hike"), and the meeting's real danger to this book
is not the decision but the collision: the statement drops at 14:00 ET on the estimated GOOG/META
print day, one day after MSFT's estimated print and one day before AMZN/AAPL's — Oct 27–29 is one
compound-variance window, not three separate events.

### Leg 3 — the reaction function punishes hawkish surprises and relief-rallies on in-line — SUPPORTED

The last three scheduled macro reads, most recent first:

1. **2026-08-12 CPI (July data):** 0.1% m/m, 3.4% y/y headline, 2.5% core — in line. Relief
   rally: Nasdaq +0.9%, S&P +0.5% at open, yields down, September hike odds cut to ~42% (CNBC,
   Reuters "market shrugs off in-line July CPI"). In-line = up, because the priced fear is
   hawkish.
2. **2026-07-29 FOMC:** hawkish hold (3 hike dissents, no forward guidance). Dow ~−1.5%,
   S&P/Nasdaq ~−0.6% within the hour (CNBC). A *hold* sold off on composition alone — the
   asymmetry in one data point.
3. **2026-06-17 FOMC:** unanimous hold but hawkish SEP (9 hike dots, inflation forecasts raised);
   yields rose, equity reaction muted-negative, and the subsequent minutes (2026-07-08) landed on
   negative futures with yields rising (CNBC).

Pattern: the market is positioned long-duration and hawkish-fragile. Dovish *surprise* (any cut
signal) is the large-upside tail; hawkish surprise (a hike, or a hike-signaling statement) is the
repeatedly demonstrated downside. Magnitude has been index-level ~0.5–1.5% same-day — real, but
an order smaller than a mega-cap print gap.

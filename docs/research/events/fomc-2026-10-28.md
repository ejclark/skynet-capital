## Initial research

**The question.** Will the October 27–28 FOMC — the third decision of the Warsh-chaired Fed, a
no-SEP meeting landing in the middle of the mega-cap earnings cluster — cut, hold, or hike, and
what does its reaction function mean for the nine names we track?

**One-line verdict.** Base case is a hawkish hold with live hike risk (a regime that has already
flipped from "when do they cut" to "will they hike"), and the meeting's real danger to this book
is not the decision but the collision: the statement drops at 14:00 ET on the estimated GOOG/META
print day, one day after MSFT's estimated print and one day before AMZN/AAPL's — Oct 27–29 is one
compound-variance window, not three separate events.

**Method.** Sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md)
macro-print protocol — primary sources over aggregators, every source carrying its check date
(2026-08-17 throughout unless noted). No price instruments run: this is a macro print with no
symbol key. The genre model is [`nvda-2026-08-26-print.md`](nvda-2026-08-26-print.md).

### Leg 3 — the reaction function punishes hawkish surprises and relief-rallies on in-line — SUPPORTED

The last three scheduled macro reads, most recent first:

1. **2026-08-12 CPI (July data):** 0.1% m/m, 3.4% y/y headline, 2.5% core — in line. Relief
   rally: Nasdaq +0.9%, S&P +0.5% at open, yields down, September hike odds cut to ~42% (CNBC,
   Reuters "market shrugs off in-line July CPI"). In-line = up, because the priced fear is
   hawkish.
2. **2026-07-29 FOMC:** hawkish hold (3 hike dissents, no forward guidance). Dow ~−1.5%,
   S&P/Nasdaq ~−0.6% within the hour (CNBC). A *hold* sold off on composition alone — the
   asymmetry in one data point.
3. **2026-06-17 FOMC:** unanimous hold but hawkish SEP (9 hike dots, inflation forecasts raised);
   yields rose, equity reaction muted-negative, and the subsequent minutes (2026-07-08) landed on
   negative futures with yields rising (CNBC).

Pattern: the market is positioned long-duration and hawkish-fragile. Dovish *surprise* (any cut
signal) is the large-upside tail; hawkish surprise (a hike, or a hike-signaling statement) is the
repeatedly demonstrated downside. Magnitude has been index-level ~0.5–1.5% same-day — real, but
an order smaller than a mega-cap print gap.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (event date confirmed, FED primary):** treat fomc-2026-10-28 as a **two-sided hold/hike
coin flip** with a near-zero cut tail; no directional macro position now or planned. The
load-bearing read is the **compound-risk window Oct 27–29** — FOMC statement stacked on the
estimated MSFT/GOOG/META/AMZN/AAPL prints (**estimate** dates) — where the existing S2/E1 guards
are the whole playbook: flat tracked-name prints by their D-1, no new entries into the cluster.

**Stance change 2026-08-29 (receipt: the D-60 ledger row below).** Two revisions, both from the
2026-08-28 Jackson Hole keynote and its tape:

1. **The hike stops being a tail.** "Hawkish hold with a live hike tail" was written when September
   priced ~31–36% hike. Warsh's keynote moved that to **59.5%** on CME FedWatch in one session and
   produced the first October-specific read this doc has ever found, **~50%** (single source — see
   the row's honesty note). October is now a coin flip whose distribution is largely determined by
   September: a September hike makes this meeting a hold-after-hike, a September hold makes it the
   live one. Cut stays ~0%.
2. **The equity-transmission leg is downgraded; the rates leg is upgraded.** Leg 3 asserted hawkish
   surprises cost the index "~0.5–1.5% same-day." On 8/28 hike odds near-doubled and the S&P moved
   **−0.13% to −0.25%** while **VIX closed at 14.43, its 2026 low**. The 2y moved **+8bp**. This
   doc adopts the sibling close-out's finding as its operative reaction function: on this chair a
   hawkish surprise prices into the **front end**, not into equity vol. Consequence for the book —
   the Oct 27–29 guards stay exactly as written, but they are earning their keep against the
   **earnings prints**, not against the statement, and equity-index vol is not a Fed hedge here.

Neither revision licenses a position. Both are Medium confidence on n=1 reaction day, and the
falsifier is dated and stated in the header.

**Stance change 2026-09-05 (receipt: the D-53 ledger row below).** The 8/29 revision #1 is
**reversed by its own falsifier** — the one it wrote for the "this week" horizon, *"a second
independent source pinning October materially off ~50% in either direction."* Two independent

   The genuine October-specific risk is second-order — a September *hold* that pushes the live

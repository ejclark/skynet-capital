## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** what is August JOLTS likely to show, how does the market react to a JOLTS
surprise inside whatever Fed regime the Sep-16 FOMC leaves behind, and does it change how we sit in
the tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN) around 2026-09-29?

**One-line verdict:** a second-tier labor-slack read whose reaction function is *contingent on the
Sep-16 FOMC outcome not yet known at D-41* — it opens the short pre-Oct-FOMC corridor into a
projection-less meeting where hard data does more work, but on its own it moves markets less than the
jobs report; the house answer is guard-shaped, not directional, and this ledger's job at D-41 is to
set structure and defer the reading to a post-9/16 pulse.

**Method.** Sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md)
(macro-print mode: no price instruments). Primary sources over aggregators — the BLS release schedule
(bls.gov/schedule/news_release/jolts.htm, fetched 2026-08-19) cross-checked against the data file's
`09_sched_list.htm` citation; the reaction-function evidence is the same July labor tape and press
narration the sibling labor docs carry, each claim dated. All figures checked 2026-08-19.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and the fast turnaround are right — SUPPORTED.** The BLS JOLTS schedule
   (bls.gov/schedule/news_release/jolts.htm, fetched 2026-08-19) lists "August 2026 data:
   Sep. 29, 2026, 10:00 AM" ET — matching the calendar entry exactly, and confirming that July-data
   JOLTS (Sep. 01) and August-data JOLTS (Sep. 29) genuinely fall inside the same month. The
   calendar note's "fast turnaround per BLS's own schedule" is accurate, not an error — two BLS
   primary pages agree.
2. **This print sits in a *different* corridor than its Sep-01 sibling — SUPPORTED.** It lands
   after the Sep-16 FOMC (SEP + dots) and opens the run into the **Oct 27-28 FOMC**, which per
   `market-events-data.ts` (checked 2026-08-19) carries **no SEP** — the calendar's own note on
   `jobs-2026-10-02` flags it as "the last major payroll input between the Sep dots and the Oct
   vote." The corridor is JOLTS 9/29 → `jobs-2026-10-02` → `cpi-2026-10-14` → `fomc-2026-10-28`.
3. **A no-SEP FOMC leans harder on hard data — SUPPORTED, mechanism.** Without an updated dot plot
   or projections to anchor expectations, the market and the committee read the incoming hard data
   (JOLTS, jobs, CPI) more directly between the September and December projection meetings. That
   *raises* the marginal weight on this JOLTS relative to a JOLTS that precedes a SEP meeting — a
   structural point that holds regardless of the September outcome.
4. **The reaction sign is contingent on the Sep-16 FOMC — MIXED, and honestly unresolvable at
   D-41.** The current regime is a live *hike* debate in which weak labor is bought as hike relief
   (the 8/7 tape). But the [`FOMC doc`](fomc-2026-09-16.md) makes explicit that a second jobs
   collapse on 9/4 could reprice the question to hold-vs-cut *before* this print — in which case weak
   labor would read as recession confirmation, not relief. Whether "soft JOLTS" is dovish-supportive
   or recession-negative on 9/29 depends on which regime the 9/4 jobs print and 9/16 FOMC leave
   behind. This leg is MIXED by construction — the honest read is *defer*, and the stance says so.
5. **JOLTS moves markets less than the jobs report — SUPPORTED.** As with its Sep-01 sibling, the
   Employment Situation draws the largest labor-print reaction; JOLTS is a slower slack-signal read
   (openings, the quits rate as a wage-pressure lead, openings-per-unemployed as the Fed's preferred
   ratio) that matters over time rather than as a single-print volatility event — and it is
   overshadowed here by the jobs report three sessions later.

   trade as long-duration assets on the rate path. Most sensitive to a JOLTS-driven hike/cut-odds
   move: **CRWV** (debt-financed AI-infra — discount rate *and* cost of capital; see

   META** (valuation duration, fortress balance sheets mute financing); and **AAPL / AMZN** with the

   ([`multi-symbol-sweep.md`](../multi-symbol-sweep.md)).

**What the conditions support.** Nothing directional, and *less* than even the Sep-01 JOLTS supports
— because the reaction sign is contingent on an unresolved prior event (leg 4). No house playbook is
macro-keyed; what travels is discipline plus a note that the whole Oct corridor's reading has to be
re-derived after the 9/16 FOMC. No new rate-duration paper entries sized around 9/29 without a reason
that survives whatever regime September leaves behind.

**Honest limits.** D-41: no consensus exists, and — more than the usual "forms ~1wk out" caveat —
the entire reaction-function reading is downstream of the 9/4 jobs print and 9/16 FOMC, neither of
which has happened. This ledger is deliberately structural: it fixes the date, the corridor, and the
no-SEP weighting, and explicitly defers the directional read to a post-9/16 pulse. JOLTS is a lagging,
heavily-revised series. No price instruments run (macro-print mode carries none); reaction evidence is
press narration of the July tape, thin and under a new chair.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BLS primary re-checked 2026-08-19).** Treat 2026-09-29 10:00 ET as a
medium-impact, second-tier labor-slack read opening the short pre-Oct-FOMC corridor into a
projection-less (no-SEP) meeting — real regime information, not a trading event, and one whose
reaction sign cannot be fixed at D-41 because it depends on what the 9/4 jobs print and 9/16 FOMC
leave behind. No directional macro position; no new rate-duration paper entries sized around the
release. Base case (estimate-labeled — no consensus, trend extrapolation): openings and quits
continue softening in line with the cooling regime; whether that reads dovish-supportive or
recession-negative is deferred to the post-9/16 pulse. The structural point that *does* hold now: a
no-SEP FOMC raises the marginal weight on this hard-data print.

**Update, 2026-08-30 (receipt: the D-30 ledger row).** The stance *level* is unchanged — still
guard-shaped, still nothing sized, still no directional macro position. Two things underneath it
moved. **(1) The deferral in leg 4 partly resolved, and not by the 9/16 FOMC.** Warsh's 8/28
Jackson Hole keynote characterised labor as "quite stable" at full employment with the last payroll
print negative, abandoned forward guidance, and repriced Sep hike odds ~35% → 57-59%; the chair has
rhetorically pre-committed to reading labor as fine, so "soft JOLTS = hike relief" is downgraded
from base case to **contested** ahead of 9/16, mirroring the same re-basing the
[`jobs-2026-09-04`](jobs-2026-09-04.md) doc recorded on 8/29. **(2) A conditional weight upgrade.**
Per the [`funding-deadline`](government-funding-deadline-2026-09-30.md) doc and BLS's own lapse
precedent (Oct-2025 jobs report cancelled; Sep-2025 JOLTS never published as its own release), a
10/01 lapse deletes jobs 10/2 and CPI 10/14 while this pre-lapse print survives — making it
plausibly the last on-schedule federal labor read before the no-SEP 10-28 FOMC. That leg is
**`estimate`**-labeled and widens caution only; it licenses no entry, and a CR before 9/30 removes it.

**Kill switches:**

- **A 2026-09-30 funding lapse occurs** — this print becomes the last on-schedule federal labor read
  before the 10-28 no-SEP FOMC; elevate it from second-tier to a dedicated read. Conversely a CR
  enacted before 9/30 retires this switch and the weight upgrade with it.
- **The Sep-16 FOMC / 9/4 jobs print flips the debate to hold-vs-cut** — re-bases this print's
  reaction function from "weak = hike relief" to "weak = recession confirmation"; re-derive the
  reading at the first pulse after 9/16, don't carry the pre-September framing forward. **Partly
  fired early, in the opposite direction** — see the 2026-08-30 update above.
- **Openings re-firm / quits rise** — a hawkish surprise that lands harder than usual because the
  Oct FOMC has no dot plot to absorb it; elevate to a dedicated read ahead of the 10/2 jobs print.
- **Openings-per-unemployed below ~1.0** — crosses the Fed's slack threshold; sharpens the
  labor-loosening narrative feeding the October decision.
- **A published August JOLTS consensus emerging closer in** — resolves the biggest honest limit;
  re-run the "surprise" framing against it rather than trend extrapolation.

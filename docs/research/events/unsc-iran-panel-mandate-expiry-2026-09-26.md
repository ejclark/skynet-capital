## Initial research

panel-of-experts mandate — the hard deadline behind the wire-reported 09-17 renewal vote, proposed
2026-09-04 by that event's own initial research. Its sibling ledger already established the
mechanism (a veto lifts no sanction) and the market null. So the only questions left for *this*
document are the ones the vote ledger could not reach: **is the date right, does the calendar's own
corridor around it contain something that matters more, and does a deadline that falls on a closed
tape create a Monday-gap risk worth pricing?**

either way, the corridor contains a strictly larger Iran event two days earlier, and the weekend-gap
channel is real but already fully priced by the war — so the honest position is the same stand-aside
the sibling reached, reached by three independent routes it did not take.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is 09-26 by the committee's web page and 09-27 by the Secretariat's mandate-cycle
   document — a one-day discrepancy that changes nothing on the tape and confirms `estimate`.**
   SUPPORTED. `main.un.org/.../panel-of-experts/work-and-mandate` states verbatim: *"The Panel
   comprises up to eight experts. Its current mandate ends on 26 September 2026."* Reuters (via
   spokesman.com, 2026-09-01) also reports 26 September. But **"Security Council Reporting and
   mandate cycles"** — the Secretariat's own reference document, linked from the Council's
   Programme-of-Work page, review date 31 July 2026 — carries the row *"Non-proliferation (Iran):
   Sanctions: Panel of Experts mandate | For an initial period of one year (**27 September 2026**)"*,
   citing resolution 1929 (2010) para. 29 and resolution 2231 (2015) paras. 11 and 12. The 27th is
   the clean one-year anniversary of re-application: the committee page states the re-applied
   resolutions took effect *"on 27 September at 8 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time"* in 2025. **Why it does
   not matter for any call:** 09-26 is a Saturday and 09-27 a Sunday — the market-relevant window is
   the same Friday-09-25-close to Monday-09-28-open either way. **Why it matters for the entry:** two
   UN primaries disagreeing by a day is a better reason to hold `estimate` than the schema gap alone,
   and `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` in `scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs` still carries no UN or diplomatic
   slot — the **twelfth** ledger to bank that gap.

2. **What lapses is a mandate to look; the measures are live and enumerated.** SUPPORTED, and read
   off the primary rather than inferred. The 1737 Committee page states that following the
   *"re-applied"* resolutions **1696, 1737, 1747, 1803, 1835 and 1929** effective 27 September 2025,
   *"the 1737 Sanctions List was re-established. Currently, there are a total of 43 individuals and
   78 entities on the 1737 Sanctions List."* A list with 121 live entries is not what expires on
   09-26; the Panel's mandate is. The same page states *"The Chair of the Committee has not yet been
   elected by the Security Council"* — eleven months on — which is a fact about the Committee's
   officers, not about the measures' force.

3. **The Panel's own final report was due 28 August 2026 and was not filed — the deadline is
   already missed, not forecast.** SUPPORTED, and this is the leg the new document adds. The
   Secretariat's cycle document carries the row *"Panel of Experts final report | No later than 30
   days prior to the termination of PoE mandate (**28 August 2026**)"* with **no delivery date
   recorded**, and the Panel's Reports page (read direct 2026-09-04) still lists exactly four
   documents — **S/2015/401 (2 June 2015)**, S/2014/394, S/2013/331, S/2012/395 — and nothing after.
   The interim-report row (*"No later than 90 days after"* appointment) likewise records nothing,
   consistent with no post-snapback appointment. So the sibling ledger's inference is now a **dated,
   already-elapsed** obligation rather than a prediction: the body whose mandate expires on 09-26 has
   missed its own final-report deadline by 7 days as of today.

4. **Split the body: the Committee reports on cadence, the Panel does not exist.** SUPPORTED, and it
   is a **refinement of** — not a contradiction of — the sibling's "eleven years dark" reading, which
   was about the Panel. The cycle document's row *"Non-proliferation (Iran): Sanctions: 1737
   Committee reports | Every 90 days (March, June, September, December)"* records occurrences on
   **12 March 2026** and **9 June 2026** — i.e. the Committee has reported twice since snapback,
   without an elected chair. The next 90-day slot is **September**, the same month as the expiry.
   Against that, the **Subsidiary Organs programme of work for September 2026 (as of 01/09/2026)**
   schedules **no 1737 Committee meeting at all** for the month — its only entries are 2653 Committee
   informal consultations (09-01) and four Military Staff Committee formal meetings. **Honest
   limit:** that programme is captioned tentative and provisional, and a 90-day committee report can
   be transmitted in writing without a meeting, so this is a tension to watch, not a finding.

5. **The corridor contains a strictly larger Iran event, and it is not on the Council's calendar.**
   SUPPORTED. Twenty-one tracked events sit within ±5 days of 09-26, including three `high` ones —
   the **FY2027 funding deadline 09-30**, **PCE 09-30** and **ISM 10-01** — plus the 2Y/5Y/7Y
   auctions, durable goods and UMich final on 09-25, and the **Trump–Xi summit 09-24** (`estimate`,
   `high`). That last one is the point: the enforcement that actually binds Iranian barrels is US
   secondary sanctions on Chinese buyers — the sibling ledger's own sourced count puts **87.35%** of
   Iranian exports going to China — so a US–China principals meeting two days *before* the expiry has
   a channel to export volumes that a lapsed monitoring mandate does not. Both are `estimate`-dated,
   so neither licenses date-keyed action; the ranking is about where to point attention, not where to
   put capital.

## Stance & kill switches

**Kill switches — any one of these ends the stance and earns its own ledger row:**

- **A renewal resolution is adopted on or before 2026-09-27** (either candidate date) — including a technical rollover. Russia and China's stated legal position gave way, and the channel re-rates.

- **VIX ≥ 20 or ITA reclaiming its 21-session high before 2026-09-26** — the equity tape starting to price the Iran theatre would break leg 6(c), which is why no hedge is held.
- **Hormuz commodity transits falling below ~5/day on a sustained basis** — an escalation this stance does not model.

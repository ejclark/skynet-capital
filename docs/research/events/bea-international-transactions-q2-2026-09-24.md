## Initial research

### The question, plainly

Two lanes proposed this event on the same day, ten days after a third had explicitly declined it.
Both proposals argue the same thing: that the 09-24 current account is a **pre-release read** on the
net-export line of the **09-30** GDP third estimate — the channel the Q1 2026 third estimate moved
**+0.5pp** through ("primarily reflecting a downward revision to imports"). **Is that read real? And
is this entry a real event, or a second copy of one?**

**One-line verdict:** the read is not real — the print is computable in advance and sits on the
wrong vintage — and the entry is the **fourth** same-release double-slug in a 704-event calendar.

### Leg 3 — "a pre-release read on the 09-30 revision's net-export channel" · **REFUTED**, three independent ways

**(a) The number is computable before it prints.** The current account decomposes into a
goods-and-services balance and net primary + secondary income. The first is fully published:

| Q2 2026 month | Goods & services balance |
|---|---|
| April | **−$52.9B** |
| May | **−$75.8B** |
| June | **−$71.2B** |
| **Q2 total** | **−$199.8B** |

against Q1 2026's **−$158.8B** — a **$41.0B (25.8%) widening**. The income residual (current account
minus goods-and-services, both from BEA's own published series) has been remarkably contained:

| | 2024Q2 | 2024Q3 | 2024Q4 | 2025Q1 | 2025Q2 | 2025Q3 | 2025Q4 | 2026Q1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Income residual ($B) | −70.4 | **−99.2** | −73.8 | −63.5 | −69.1 | −68.8 | **−43.7** | −68.0 |

Median **−$69.0B**, range −$43.7B to −$99.2B. So:

- **Point estimate: −$199.8B − $69.0B = −$268.8B**, ≈ **3.31% of GDP** (Q2 SAAR $32,486.1B) against
  Q1's 2.85%.
- **Band: −$243.5B … −$299.0B**, the residual's own eight-quarter range.

Goods and services was **70.0%** of the Q1 balance. A release whose dominant term is already public
and whose residual has a known band is not information; it is bookkeeping.

**(b) The two releases are on different vintages.** The 06-24 release was titled "…**1st Quarter
2026 and Annual Update**" — the ITA annual revision already ran. The 09-24 row carries no such
suffix. The 09-30 release *is* NIPA's annual update, and its own lane's ledger describes it as
rewriting "GDP, GDI, corporate profits and the PCE price index from Q1 2021 through Q1 2026." A
pre-update vintage cannot pre-reveal a post-update one, and the revision's entire content is the
rewrite the 09-24 print does not contain. **The proposing lane's own note says this** — "the ITA
annual update already ran in June and is NOT part of the 09-30 national-accounts update — conflating
the two would be the mistake this note exists to prevent" — and then proposes the event as "the one
pre-release read on this revision's most-cited channel." The note refutes the rationale it travels
with.

**(c) The new content is the half GDP does not consume.** NIPA net exports is a goods-and-services
concept. The ITA's incremental content over the three published FT-900s is precisely the **primary
and secondary income accounts** — investment income, compensation, transfers — which do not enter
GDP at all (they are the bridge from GDP to GNI). The one number this release produces that nothing
else on the calendar produces is the current-account balance itself, and no tracked instrument
consumes it.

### Honest limits

- **The income-residual band is the whole forecast uncertainty, and it is not tight.** 2024Q3 moved
  it $29B in one quarter. "±$25B" is an eight-quarter empirical range, not a confidence interval,
  and a tariff- or repatriation-driven shift in investment income would break it. Medium confidence
  is doing real work in the table above.
- **Basis is asserted, not reconciled line by line.** `BOPGSTB` (SA, BoP basis) and `IEABC` are both
  BEA series and reproduce Q1 2026's −$226.8B exactly, so the decomposition holds at the quarters
  checked; this lane did not verify that BEA applies no further coverage adjustment between the
  monthly and quarterly vintages.
- **No published analyst consensus exists for this release**, and none was found. The ITA is not a
  consensus-surveyed print. Every content statement here is a last-published reading or an explicit
  arithmetic projection, never a survey.
- **Leg 6's tape measurement is carried from a sibling ledger, not re-run.** The n=94 sample and its
  QQQ control belong to `intl-transactions-q3-2026-12-18`; nothing about this date alters it, but it

- **The archive audit is a spot check with a strong control, not a completeness proof.** Three

- **The duplicate scan is title-based.** Jaccard ≥ 0.50 on same-date titles found four real pairs

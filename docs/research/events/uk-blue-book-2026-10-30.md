### Honest limits

**First, n = 12 is a small sample and every ratio here is a daily close-to-close move.** Twelve publication
days cannot distinguish "the compendium is a non-event" from "twelve quiet Octobers," and the ex-Budget
subset is n = 10. IGLT.L stops trading at 16:30 London, so a 09:30 release's signature is diluted by a full
session; it is also an **all-maturity** gilt index and under-weights the long end where UK fiscal stress
shows. GBPUSD=X has only **10** usable Blue Book bars and its daily-close timing is not dependable — its
0.80x is reported but carries no weight.

**Second, "no new aggregates" is a claim about headline national-accounts totals, not about the whole
book.** The compendium genuinely adds detail the September QNA does not publish — sector and financial
accounts, industrial analyses, supply-and-use tables, the national balance sheet, flow of funds,
environmental accounts. Leg 2 establishes that the **aggregates** are consistent with 09-30 by the
publisher's own policy; it does not establish that nothing in the volume is new to anyone. The claim being
registered as FT-…-1 is the narrow one.

**Third, leg 4's outlier attributions are of two different qualities.** The **2024-10-31 = Budget D+1** and

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event was filed on 2026-09-05 by the [`treasury-30y-bond-2026-10-08`](treasury-30y-bond-2026-10-08.md)
adjacency sweep, which had already answered the obvious question about it — *does the 1:40pm buyback
support the 30-year bond auctioned at 1:00pm the same day?* — with a documented **no**, on eligibility
rather than maturity range, and registered the prediction. Restating that would add nothing.

So this session asked the one question every ledger in this corridor raises and none has measured:
**"the cap is a ceiling, not a purchase" — how often, and is it predictable?** Every sibling carries
the caution and the same lone example. The 09-24 doc calls the 2026-03-19 under-fill *"a demonstrated
possibility with an unknown trigger, not a predictable one."* That is a hypothesis, and the operation
history is a primary dataset. It has not been tested.

**One-line verdict:** the caution is right and its evidence base was wrong — there are **two**
under-fills in this sector, not one, and once both are on the table they stop looking random: they
share a **dated structural feature this event does not have**, and a CUSIP-level signature that
separates them from all 24 full fills without overlap.

**3. Full fill is a property of the long end specifically, not of the buyback programme —
SUPPORTED, and it explains why the error was easy to make.** Under-fills across all **131**
liquidity-support operations on record, by bucket:

|---|---|---|

The belly essentially never fills — 7Y-10Y is **9 of 9** under-filled, including **1.4%** of cap on
2026-03-12 and **0.0%** on 2024-07-24 — while the two long-end nominal buckets fill **49 of 51**
times. So the long end is **cap-constrained** and the belly is **price-constrained**, and a reader
who generalises "Treasury often takes less than the cap" from programme-wide behaviour into this

reaching for.

|---|---|---|

Fisher exact, two-tailed, D+1..2 versus everything else: **p = 0.031**. **2026-10-08 is D+0** — the
auction schedule PDF puts the **30-Year BOND R** at *Thursday, October 08, 2026* (announce 10/01,

operation — and the next 20-Year is not until **10-21**. The two D+0 precedents are **2024-11-20**
(20Y new issue, $16B) and **2026-04-09** (30Y reopening, $22B), and the latter is this event's exact
structural twin: the sector's **largest offer book on record, $36.47B**, filled to the full $2.00B.

*A methodological trap worth recording:* fiscaldata files 30-Year **TIPS** under `security_type:
Bond`. A naive filter puts 2025-02-20, 2025-08-21 and 2026-02-19 into the nominal set and corrupts
the gap table. They are separable by `cpi_base_reference_period`/`index_ratio_on_issue_date` being
non-null, and by inspection — a 30Y "auction" clearing at **2.65%** in August 2025 is a real yield.
The table above is nominal-only.

**5. The mechanism is visible at CUSIP level, and it separates the two groups completely —
SUPPORTED, and this is the session's most usable finding.** From `buybacks_security_details`, the
largest single-CUSIP par amount accepted in each of the 26 operations:

- **All 24 full fills: largest clip ≥ $312M**, median **$1,042M**, top-1 share median ~52% and as
  high as 100% (2025-05-29 took $1,998M of $2,000M in one line).
- **Both under-fills: largest clip ≤ $150M.** 2025-11-20 took 10 issues, biggest **$150M**;
  2026-03-19 took 5 issues, biggest **$100M**.

No overlap, and a **2× gap** between the groups. So an under-fill is not Treasury rejecting the
market wholesale — on 2025-11-20 it accepted *more lines* than the median full fill. It is Treasury
finding **no single line worth size** at its relative-value marks and nibbling instead. Read against
the FAQ's stated rule (acceptance on *"proximity to prevailing market prices … as well as measures of
relative value"*), that is exactly what a price test looks like when the sector has just repriced and
no block is mispriced enough to clear. It also converts the "cap ≠ purchase" caution from a warning
into a **test**: watch the largest clip, not the headline total.

### What the conditions support

Nothing directional — the standard answer for a `symbols: []`, `estimate`-dated rates event. What
the conditions support is a **replacement for a caution this calendar repeats in every rates ledger**.

"The cap is a ceiling, not a purchase" was carried with one example and no rate. It now has three
things it lacked: a **base rate** (2 of 26 in this sector; ~0 in 10-20Y; near-certainty in the
belly), a **dated risk window** (D+1..2 after a nominal long-end auction, 2 of 5 versus 0 of 21), and
a **shape test that separates the two outcomes without overlap** (largest accepted clip ≥ $312M
versus ≤ $150M). Any future ledger row reading "$4B of support" without checking
`total_par_amt_accepted` is still repeating the original error — but a row that checks only the total
and not the clip distribution is now leaving the diagnostic on the table.

And the checks are sequenced so this doc learns before its own event: **09-09** (announced maximum) →

**10-08** → **10-27** (D+6, the second test) → **11-04**, where `sb0607` expires.

### Honest limits

**The window finding is post-hoc and thin, and is presented as suggestive rather than established.**
Two under-fills is the whole positive class; the D+1..2 partition was found by searching the data,
so **p = 0.031 is uncorrected** for the several slicings tried (offer volume, month, prior-auction
tenor, reopening-vs-new), and one counter-example on 09-24 or 10-27 collapses it. The safer statement
is the weaker one: *the two under-fills share a dated feature that 2026-10-08 does not have.*
**The D+0 base rate is n = 2.** **Both under-fills happened to follow a 20-Year auction**, not a
30-Year — with 2 of 4 versus 0 of 1, this session cannot tell whether tenor matters, and if it does,
10-08's 30-Year prior reads slightly safer than the headline claim. **Neither under-fill has a
published explanation** — two targeted searches this session returned only the schedule and the
`sb0607` coverage, so the price-test rule explains *how* they happen, not *why* on those days.
**The clip-separation is descriptive**, drawn from the same 26 observations, and has not been tested
out of sample. **Controls that were run and came back null, recorded so they are not re-run:** offer
volume does not predict fill (2026-04-09 drew the largest book ever, $36.47B, and filled; 2026-03-19
drew $36.00B and did not); month does not; reopening-versus-new-issue does not. **Nothing about
10-08 has happened** — the announced maximum is unknown until **10-07**, `sb0607` says *"at least $4
billion"* with an open ceiling, and the schedule's $2B is superseded and must not be quoted as the
expectation. **`sb0607`, the buyback FAQ and the ~1bp BofA figure are quoted from text read into this
repo on 2026-09-02/05 and from press narration**, not re-fetched today. **The eligibility leg is
wholly inherited** from the same-day 30Y sibling.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date and cap `estimate`-labeled — tentative schedule, published cap superseded, confirming
primary is the 10-07 announcement).** The 2026-10-08 20-30Y liquidity-support buyback is a **data
release, not a catalyst**: no position keyed to it, no attribution of that day's tape to it. Base
case (**estimate**-labeled): the 10-07 announcement prints a maximum of **at least $4B**, the eligible
list excludes CUSIP 912810UW6, Treasury **accepts the full announced maximum** — the D+0 record is
2 of 2 and the sector's overall record 24 of 26 — and it does so from **at least one clip ≥ $312M**,
with the operation passing without a tape effect distinguishable from the 30Y auction 40 minutes
earlier, the ECB account, or the previous evening's FOMC minutes.

**What this initial research changes for the corridor, and what it inherits.** It inherits the
read-not-trade verdict and the eligibility finding unchanged. What it changes is the **evidence base
under the corridor's most-repeated caution**: the claim that 2026-03-19 is the only under-fill in this
sector is **false** — 2025-11-20 took $0.785B of $2.000B — and the corrected pair is structured, not
random. The [10-15 sibling](treasury-buyback-10y20y-2026-10-15.md) carries the "only one" wording in
its signal list and its stance; **its next pulse should correct it there** rather than this lane
editing another event's document, and the corrected base rate is recorded in this event's
`market-events.ts` note so the next session in the corridor meets it without reading this file.

**Two forward tests registered** in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md):

- **`FT-treasury-buyback-20y30y-2026-10-08-1`**, scoreable **2026-10-09** — the 2026-10-08 operation
  **accepts the full announced maximum**, on a 24-of-26 sector base rate and a 2-of-2 D+0 record,
  with the D+1..2 risk window explicitly absent on this date.
- **`FT-treasury-buyback-20y30y-2026-10-08-2`**, scoreable **2026-10-09** — the **shape test**: if
  the operation fills to its cap, its largest single-CUSIP accepted clip is **≥ $312M**; if it
  under-fills, the largest clip is **≤ $150M**. Complete separation across 26 prior operations, and
  a structural claim with no directional leg.

**Deliberate non-proposal.** No new `market-events.ts` entries are proposed by this sweep. The dated
items surfaced — the 09-24, 10-15, 10-27 and 11-04 buyback operations and the 09-15/10-21 20-Year
auctions — are either already tracked or are exactly the ~1bp operations this corridor has ruled not
worth a calendar slot (the 09-10 sibling's rule: a specific warrant earns an entry, sector cadence
alone does not). Ten tracked events already sit inside this event's ±5-day corridor.

**Kill switches:**

- **The 2026-09-24 or 2026-10-27 operation accepting materially less than its announced maximum** —
  both are outside the D+1..2 window (D+9 and D+6), so either would refute the window model directly
  and return the under-fill risk to "unknown trigger." This is the cheapest test and it comes first.
- **The 2026-10-08 operation under-filling with a largest accepted clip above $150M, or filling to
  the cap with every clip below $312M** — kills `FT-…-2`; the clip separation was an artifact of 26
  observations and the shape test does not travel.
- **The 2026-09-09 or 2026-10-07 announcement printing a maximum below $4B, or the operation being
  pulled or moved** — `sb0607`'s supersession premise fails, and the
  [09-09 increase sibling](treasury-buyback-increase-2026-09-09.md) carries the same premise.
- **The 2026-10-07 preliminary eligible list containing CUSIP 912810UW6** — the FAQ's on-the-run and
  first-coupon clauses are not applied as written; this doc's leg 6 and the
  [30Y sibling](treasury-30y-bond-2026-10-08.md)'s registered prediction both fail together.

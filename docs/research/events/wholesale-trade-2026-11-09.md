## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event exists because the `mtis-2026-11-17` lane's adjacency sweep found a hole and argued it was
load-bearing on two grounds: that this print is **34.5%** of the 11-17 aggregate by inventory weight,
and that it is **"the first ordinary monthly wholesale print carried on the restated AIES basis"**,
eight days before the MTIS edition Census names as where the *retail* restatement is reflected.
**Are both grounds true, does the 12-09 sibling's nowcast framing transfer to the September edition,
and what does this print actually add over the advance report that precedes it?**

**One-line verdict:** the weight claim reproduces exactly, the restated-basis claim is true only of
*full monthly reports* and hides the release that matters more (10-28), the sibling's framing transfers
only after its two classifiers are collapsed into the one variable they both proxy — and the answer to
the third question is that this print adds a median **0.10pp** and has never added more than 0.30pp.

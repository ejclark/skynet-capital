Q3 guide: core revenue $214–216M, core gross margin 38–40%, core operating margin -25% to -23%; FY core gross margin 41–43%, operating margin -19% to -17%. Arithmetic (mine): H1 core = $401.2M (191.3 + 209.9), so Q4 is implied at $262.8–274.8M, a ~25% step up from the Q3 midpoint. The Q2 raise took the FY midpoint up $25M while the Q2 beat alone was $15.9M, so the second-half guide rose by only ~$9M: a beat-and-roughly-pass-through, not a re-rate. One guide history (n=1) says the company sandbags; it does not say Q3 will beat by the same margin.

### Leg 3 — the reaction function: REFUTED as "beat means up"
Both prints sold off hard despite beating the company's own core guide. Aggregator feeds compared GAAP numbers to a street figure that tracked the core guide, so Q2 was published as a revenue "miss" ($180.1M vs $194M), with GAAP gross margin 14% vs 26.3% expected and hardware revenue light, while cloud beat ($126.0M vs $116.3M, Newsquawk). Core excludes pass-through data-center revenue and customer-warrant amortization and adds back stock comp (8-K definitions), which is why the GAAP-to-core gap is ~$30M a quarter. Inference, not sourced: part of the selling was headline mechanics plus margin, not demand. After the prints the path is mixed: 5 sessions after the first, +21%; after the second, -9%; no drift to lean on.

### Leg 4 — demand and capacity: MIXED
RPO $25.4B at 2026-06-30 (8-K and 10-Q); the 10-Q puts about 22% of it in the 24 months to June 2028, so ~$5.6B or ~$2.8B a year on average, against ~$0.885B of 2026 core revenue guided. Contracted demand is not the constraint; delivery is (the company says it plans to raise manufacturing capacity more than tenfold in 2026 and has 600+ MW live or contracted by end-2027). Cash was $8.6B at 2026-06-30 after the IPO, so a financing event is not a near-term driver. Concentration is the risk: three customers were 34%, 32% and 10% of Q2 revenue (76% of receivables sat with two). The 10-Q table as read does not name them, and one search summary assigned the 34% to MBZUAI where the fetch inferred OpenAI, so the names are unresolved here. A search summary quoting "$1.3B of RPO" contradicts both filings and is not used.

### Leg 5 — what the options charge: MIXED
Cboe 2026-10-20 (spot 125.31, iv30 88.3%): ATM straddle 15.3% of spot to 11-06 (17 days), 19.4% to 11-13 (24 days), 20.8% to 11-20. The week 11-06→11-13 adds variance equal to about a 15% one-sigma move; net of a normal week at ~4.7% a day it is a 10–11% extra one-sigma move, shared by four things: the 11-09 lock-up step, CPI 11-10 (confirmed), the CoreWeave print 11-10 (est.) and this print. The earnings-only share is therefore below that bundle, i.e. roughly 9–10% or less, against realized next-session moves of -19.1% and -12.5% vs SMH. Weekly open interest is thin, so these are quotes, not trades. Realized vol: 20-day ~71%.

### Leg 6 — tape going in: MIXED

Over the same five sessions CRWV -18%, AMD -10%, SMH -6% (sibling ledger's read; the sector is selling off with CBRS at the high-beta end). Unlike the first two prints, this one comes after a large drawdown rather than a run-up, so "sell the news off a high" does not map cleanly onto it.

### What plays the conditions support

Long needs a repeatable edge and there is no history to find one in; short faces ~90% IV and a name already -60% from its first close; an option structure pays the bundle's implied move (Leg 5) and the sibling found no separate lock-up premium, so nothing is cheap.

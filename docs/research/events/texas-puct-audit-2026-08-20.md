## Initial research

**The question, plainly.** Is Aug 20, 2026 really when a PUCT "audit" process begins, what's
actually on the agenda, and why does the [`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md)
dashboard treat this as a Vistra (VST) watch item?

**One-line verdict.** The date is real and well-corroborated but not confirmable via the cited
primary source; the underlying substance is a routine PUCT open-meeting agenda item (ERCOT's
good-cause-exception request) inside a much larger multi-month audit, and Vistra's own management
has publicly welcomed that audit rather than warned against it — so the "watch item" is a
queue-and-rules-clarity story for VST's ERCOT demand thesis, not a live regulatory threat.

   "good cause exception" to delay its Batch Zero large-load classification notifications
   (originally due Aug 7), filed to accommodate Gov. Abbott's Aug 3 audit directive. The audit
   itself is executed mainly by ERCOT over "several months, but less than nine" (ERCOT's own
   Aug 14 open-meeting statement, via Houston Public Media and Utility Dive coverage) — it is not
   structured as a recurring "PUCT audit meeting" series. The calendar title is a reasonable
   shorthand but slightly overstates what Aug 20 itself is.
3. **Why this is a Vistra watch item.** SUPPORTED. Vistra is ERCOT's largest competitive
   generator; the audit targets ~250–300 interconnection-queue projects (mostly data centers)
   against an estimated 474 GW of total ERCOT interconnection requests (Utility Dive,
   2026-08-1x) — a queue Vistra's own CEO called inflated relative to the company's demand
   forecast. The audit is the mechanism that will either validate or deflate the ERCOT-side
   demand-growth leg of the merchant-scarcity-rent thesis that gates VST's Tier-2 entry condition
   in `ai-energy-constraint.md`.
4. **Does the audit read as a threat to Vistra specifically?** REFUTED as currently framed.
   Vistra's Q2 2026 earnings call (2026-08-14, per Motley Fool/Globe and Mail transcripts) has
   CEO Jim Burke and CSO Stacey Doré on record supporting the pause — Burke: *"I'd like to see the
   queues culled, at the end of the day"* and *"We support the conversation the governor is
   wanting to have"*; Doré frames it as aligned with "thin the queue and ensure responsible
   development." Both say Vistra does not expect the pause to affect already-studied baseload
   projects, specifically the 1.2 GW Amazon/Comanche Peak deal (still targeted for energization
   2027–2028). No source found ties Vistra's existing generation fleet to any compliance concern
   the audit is investigating (water/power usage, incentives) — that scrutiny is aimed at
   data-center developers in the queue, not incumbent generators.
5. **Is Aug 20 itself a plausible near-term catalyst for VST?** REFUTED. The agenda item is a

## Outcome

**Assessed 2026-08-24** (sector kind carries no price/options instrument — sourced web research
only, per the initial doc). Direct fetch of `puc.texas.gov`'s own filing document
(`interchange.puc.texas.gov/Documents/55999_116_1491473.PDF`) failed in this session (TLS/cert
error, not a data limitation) — same honest-limits caveat the initial doc carried; outcome is
corroborated via law-firm coverage (Baker Botts, Beatty & Wozniak) plus the ERCOT market notice
this doc already cited, not the regulator's own posted minutes.

**The agenda item.** PUCT approved ERCOT's Project 59142 good-cause-exception request at (or
around) the Aug 20 open meeting: (1) conditional Base/Studied/Batch-0-Ineligible classification of
large loads pushed to Aug 31, 2026; (2) conditionally-classified base loads still included in the
Aug 1/Nov 1 QSA; (3) a 24-day cure window for large-load developers with dynamic-data deficiencies
found after the Aug 7 deadline. All three requested exceptions were granted — a routine
scheduling/procedural approval, exactly as leg 2 of the initial doc framed the agenda item, not a
ruling on audit substance.

**Market reaction.** VST closed **-2.63%** on 8/20 (~$138.94) against a broad market selloff — S&P
500 -0.87%, Nasdaq -1.00% the same session (Treasury buyback relief fading, oil up on
US-Iran tension, per the sibling `treasury-20y-bond-2026-08-19.md` doc's Aug-20 follow-through
row). VST's move is directionally worse than the index but well within single-name noise for a
power/utility name in a broad risk-off tape — no source ties the move specifically to the PUCT
agenda item, and Q2 guidance (reaffirmed 8/7: adjusted EBITDA $6.8–7.6B FY26) was already public.

  ERCOT/developers, not a finding or order naming Vistra's fleet or its contracted projects.

  back Aug-14 comfort"** — **none fired.** No source found ties this approval to Vistra's existing

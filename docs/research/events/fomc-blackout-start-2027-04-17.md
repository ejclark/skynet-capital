## Initial research

### The question, plainly

Five blackout ledgers precede this one and no two reached the same conclusion.
[September](fomc-blackout-start-2026-09-05.md) called a no-short-vol window because top-tier US data
was trapped inside the gate; [October](fomc-blackout-start-2026-10-17.md) refused that because every
October print landed *before* the gate; [November](fomc-blackout-start-2026-11-28.md) found the gate
outlives the decision by a session with the November CPI in that overhang;
[January](fomc-blackout-start-2027-01-16.md) found the same geometry filled with this book's Q4
earnings and turned it into a **sizing rule** for one session; and
[March](fomc-blackout-start-2027-03-06.md) measured that rule and **refused** it, on the stated ground
that March's overhang has no earnings in it. The decision this gate wraps,
[`fomc-2027-04-28`](fomc-2027-04-28.md), already owns the corridor, the Kalshi book and the loaded
**2027-04-30** session — and it is the ledger that proposed this entry, from its own decode of the
Board's shading.

So the question is not "which shape is April" but something sharper, because April is the first gate
that lets March's refusal be tested on its own terms: **March refused January's rule for want of the
mechanism. April has the mechanism. Does the rule come back?**

**One-line verdict.** No — and the negative result is the document. The Q1 mega-cap cluster sits
inside this gate in **8 of 8** prior years, which is the exact condition January's rule was built on,
and the overhang still measures at its own base rate. Establishing that required supplying the one
thing no gate ledger in this family has used — **a control for the statistic each of them counted** —
and that control reaches back and re-reads two siblings. Date `estimate`; no directional call, no size.

### Honest limits

**Every number here is n=7 and the strongest results are negative ones, which are the easiest thing

earnings cluster,"* never *"the session will be quiet."* Leg 4's control is an unconditional base rate

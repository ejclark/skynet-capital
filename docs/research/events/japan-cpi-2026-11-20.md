**TL;DR.** **Stand aside — and keep this row, but for a sharper reason than its title gives.** The
entry is tracked as *"the last national print before the December BoJ board convenes,"* which is
true and under-specified: it says the print is last without saying what being last is worth. Parsing
the Bank's own meeting table against the Bureau's release schedule, this session found a structure
nobody here had measured — **the BoJ's calendar has two CPI-input regimes.** An Outlook-Report
meeting (Apr / Jul / Oct / Jan) convenes **3–6 days** after a national CPI print; a non-Outlook
meeting (Mar / Jun / Sep / Dec) convenes **24–27 days** after one. December 2026 is a non-Outlook
meeting, so **this print is the only national observation the board holds when it convenes on
2026-12-17, and the first one the October Outlook Report never saw** — between the 2026-10-30 and
2027-01-22 Outlooks there are 84 days and exactly two national prints, and only this one lands
before the board sits. **Then the second finding cuts the first back down:** this is the most

national September print (**2026-10-23**) and the Tokyo October flash (**2026-10-30**) — has landed

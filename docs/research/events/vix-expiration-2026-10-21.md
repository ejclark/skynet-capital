## At a glance

**TL;DR.** On the morning of **Wed 2026-10-21** — a date this PR moves from `estimate` to **confirmed** off three Cboe primary surfaces — every October VIX future and option settles to a **Special Opening Quotation (VRO)**, a one-shot batch of SPX opening-auction prints nobody can trade against once struck. Two things make this settlement different from its September sibling, and **only the second one survives measurement**. *The geometry inverts:* consecutive third Fridays are 28 or 35 days apart, so a VIX settlement either **leads** its own month's equity expiration by two sessions (September) or **trails** it by three (this one, 10-16 → 10-21). September's ledger built a "the hedging cushion leaves early" framing on the lead shape; a purpose-built house instrument says the inversion is a **calendar fact, not a market effect** — settlement sessions are not distinguishable from ordinary sessions on VIX movement (median |ΔVIX| 0.795 vs 0.680, n=248 vs 5,235, permutation **P = 0.061**), and lead-vs-trail separates on nothing (|ΔVIX| **P = 0.647**, basket volume **P = 0.088** raw and **0.206** in 2013+, with trail the *lower* side). *The strip is genuinely extreme:* the VRO is struck off the single **2026-11-20** SPX series, so this settlement fixes a 30-day reading of **10-21 → 11-20** — the only monthly VX window of the cycle that contains the **US midterms (11-03)**, alongside FOMC 10-28, PCE + Q3 GDP 10-29, jobs 11-06, CPI 11-10, the US–China tariff-truce expiry 11-10 and the 11-20 expiration itself. **The curve does not pay for it.** Fitting the nine listed VX settlements on `log(dte)` puts `VX/V6` **0.25 points below** the line — but adding one curvature term takes r² from 0.964 to **0.990** and flips V6 to **+0.08**, so the apparent discount is misspecification, not a signal. Net: a **confirmed low-impact microstructure marker**, one execution guard on the 10-21 opening auction, and no entry.

## Initial research

### The question

What settles on 2026-10-21, is the date real, and does a VIX settlement that **trails** its own month's equity expiration — striking a 30-day strip that contains the midterms and an FOMC decision, from inside the Fed's communications blackout — create anything this repo should act on, or only something it should avoid?

**One-line verdict:** the date is now primary-confirmed and the placement is genuinely unusual, but the two claims that placement invites — that the lead/trail geometry matters, and that an event-dense strip carries a visible premium — are both **measured and both fail**, so the output is the same single execution guard September reached, arrived at by a different and stronger route.

### Method

Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md): the symbol-keyed instruments (`earnings-cycle.mjs`, `intraday-edges.mjs`) do not apply to an event with no symbols. Primary sources only for the date and the prices — Cboe's VIX-futures product page, the contract specification, the daily settlement CSV and the exchange's own index history files, each fetched direct on **2026-09-09** with its HTTP status recorded. Two measurements were run rather than cited:

- **`scripts/research/vix-settlement-geometry.mjs`** — built in this PR, in the trap-detector shape of `fomc-expiration-proximity.mjs`, reusing `expiration-displacement.mjs`'s basket and relative-volume definition rather than restating them. It reproduces the settlement rule cold, classifies every monthly settlement 2006+ as lead or trail, and permutation-tests the two cohorts against each other and against an all-session baseline. All instrument caches were busted first (`--fresh`) per the cache-discipline rule.
- **A curve fit on the nine listed `VX` settlements**, linear and quadratic in `log(days-to-expiry)`, on two settlement dates (2026-09-04 and 2026-09-08) — the same residual method the sibling [`vix-expiration-2027-01-20`](vix-expiration-2027-01-20.md) and [`vix-expiration-2027-05-18`](vix-expiration-2027-05-18.md) ledgers use, with a misspecification control added.

Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md` for playbook fit, and against the calendar for the corridor and the contents of the strip's own 30-day window.

### Conviction legs, tested

**1. The date is right, and it is now primary-confirmed — SUPPORTED (status flipped in this PR).** The [`opex-2026-10-16`](opex-2026-10-16.md) lane discovered this date in its 2026-09-09 adjacency sweep and correctly declined to self-confirm it, filing `proposals/vix-expiration-2026-10-21.from-opex-2026-10-16.json` as an `estimate`. Three Cboe surfaces answered today: the **VIX-futures product page** (HTTP 200, 578,599 bytes) renders the market-data row `VX/V6 | 10/21/2026 | 18.41`; the **contract specification** (HTTP 200, 461,361 bytes) states the rule verbatim — final settlement is *"the Wednesday that is 30 days prior to the third Friday of the calendar month immediately following the month in which the contract expires"*; the **daily settlement CSV** for `dt=2026-09-08` returns `VX,VX/V6,2026-10-21,18.402`. The rule re-derives independently: November 2026's third Friday is **2026-11-20** (11-01 is a Sunday, so the Fridays are the 6th, 13th, 20th, 27th), minus 30 days is **Wednesday 2026-10-21**, and neither that Wednesday nor 2026-11-20 is a Cboe Options holiday, so the specification's fallback clause does not fire. Applying that rule cold in the new instrument reproduces **five of five** listed monthly dates (U6 09-16, V6 10-21, X6 11-18, Z6 12-16, F7 2027-01-20). This event's own initial research is the sanctioned venue for the flip, so the entry moves to `confirmed` with an `OCC:` prefix.

**2. The lead/trail geometry is deterministic, and this is a trail — SUPPORTED (mechanical).** Consecutive third Fridays are **28 or 35 days apart**, so the settlement Wednesday lands on one of exactly two shapes against its own month's expiration and never anywhere else: **LEAD** (28-day step — the Wednesday two calendar days *before* the third Friday) or **TRAIL** (35-day step — five calendar days, three sessions, *after*). 2026-09-16 was a lead; **2026-10-21 is a trail**, as is 2027-01-20; 2026-11-18 and 2026-12-16 revert to leads. September's ledger built its headline framing on the lead shape — *"the hedging cushion starts leaving two sessions before the witching"* — which is a sentence about calendar geometry that reads as a sentence about the market.

**3. That inversion has no measurable consequence — REFUTED as an effect (house-measured, 2026-09-09).** `vix-settlement-geometry.mjs`, run `--fresh` on Cboe's own VIX history plus the expiration-displacement basket, 2006+ (VIX **options** listed February 2006; before that a settlement is a futures-only event and a different animal): **n=248, lead 162, trail 86**. On absolute close-to-close VIX movement, lead median **0.825** vs trail **0.745**, permutation **P = 0.647**; in 2013+ alone, **0.830 vs 0.620, P = 0.341**. On basket relative volume, lead **1.087** vs trail **1.028**, **P = 0.088** raw — the only reading that flirts with anything, and it dies under the era control (**1.063 vs 0.996, P = 0.211**) *and* points the wrong way for a distortion story, since the trail cohort is the **quieter** side. Nothing here supports treating this settlement differently from September's because of where it sits relative to opex.

**4. The settlement session is not distinguishable from an ordinary session either — MIXED, read as a null.** Before comparing the cohorts to each other, the instrument compares them to everything: settlement sessions carry median |ΔVIX| **0.795** against an all-session 2006+ baseline of **0.680** (n=248 vs 5,235), permutation **P = 0.061**. That is a *marginal* reading in the direction one would expect and it does not clear a conventional bar — reported first precisely so the lead/trail null in leg 3 is read as "no difference between two things that are themselves unremarkable," not as "a small difference inside a known effect." Trail-cohort dispersion, banked here for the forward test: **p75 = 1.320**, **p90 = 2.360**.

**5. The strip is the most event-dense window on this calendar's board, and uniquely so — SUPPORTED (mechanical).** The VRO is computed from the opening-auction prices of a **single** SPX/SPXW series exactly 30 days out — here **2026-11-20** — so this settlement fixes a 30-day reading of **2026-10-21 → 2026-11-20**. That window contains, from the calendar: **fomc-2026-10-28** (high, confirmed), **gdp-q3-2026-advance** and **pce-2026-10-29** (both high, confirmed), **eci-q3-2026-10-30** (high, confirmed), **ecb-decision-2026-10-29** and **boj-decision-2026-10-30**, **ism-manufacturing-2026-11-02**, **midterm-elections-2026-11-03** (high, `estimate`), **ism-services-2026-11-04**, **jobs-2026-11-06** (high, confirmed), **cpi-2026-11-10** (high, confirmed), **us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10** (high, `estimate`), **ppi-2026-11-13**, **retail-sales-2026-11-17** (high, confirmed), **fomc-minutes-2026-11-18** and **opex-2026-11-20** itself. Among monthly VX contracts it is the **only** one whose measurement window contains the midterms: a contract settling on date D prices D → D+30, so the midterms fall inside a monthly strip only for a settlement between 10-04 and 11-03, and 10-21 is the sole monthly in that range.

**6. And the curve does not price that density — REFUTED, with a misspecification control that is the finding.** Fitting the nine listed `VX` daily settlements on `log(days-to-expiry)`, `VX/V6` sits **−0.2516** below the line on 2026-09-08 (**−0.0991** on 09-04), which reads as an event *discount* — the opposite of a premium, and interesting enough to be a trap. It is one. Adding a single quadratic term in `log(dte)` lifts r² from **0.964 to 0.990** (09-04: 0.974 → 0.990) and flips V6's residual to **+0.0810** (09-04: **+0.1470**). Under the curvature-controlled fit, exactly one contract survives as an outlier — **`VX/Z6` at −0.359 / −0.371**, the two-holiday year-end strip the sibling `FT-vix-expiration-2027-01-20-2` is built on. So the honest statement is: **the most event-dense strip on the board carries neither a measurable premium nor a measurable discount**, and the linear-residual "signal" was the shape of the curve, not the shape of the calendar. *Carried for the sibling lanes, not scored here:* `FT-vix-expiration-2027-01-20-1`'s kill switch is a **±0.20 band on a linear `log(dte)` residual**, and the linear-to-quadratic residual shifts measured today run **0.09–0.34 points** — i.e. that band is at or inside the misspecification scale of the fit it is stated on. That is an observation for the 2027-01-20 lane's own next pulse, not an edit to its file.

### What the conditions support

Nothing directional, and no sizing. One execution guard: **no paper entry, exit or resize on the 2026-10-21 opening auction**, and the vol complex's first prints that morning read as auction artifacts. One correction carried back to the family of documents: the "cushion leaves early / settlement corridor" framing is **geometry, not effect** — a trail settlement is not a different animal from a lead one, and the next `vix-expiration` ledger should say so rather than re-deriving the corridor story from its own calendar. And one thing genuinely worth watching without acting on it: this is the only monthly VIX contract whose 30-day strip contains the midterms, and as of 2026-09-08 the curve prices it flat.

### Honest limits

`impact: low` is deliberate — a confirmed date that widens caution about one 30-minute window licenses no entry. The settlement prices are Cboe **daily settlements** as of 2026-09-08, not live quotes, and the 09-04 pair is quoted only to show the residual is stable across two dates, not as a time series. The curve fit is **n=9** on one instrument's listed board; a quadratic on nine points is a misspecification *control*, not a term-structure model, and no confidence interval is attached to any residual. The geometry study measures **VIX closes and equity volume only** — no VRO history, no intraday, no open interest, no GEX — so it can say the settlement session is unremarkable in those two dimensions and nothing about the auction print itself, which is where the settlement-distortion literature (Griffin & Shams, *RFS* 2018; contested by Cboe, and unverified post-2018) actually lives. Two `estimate`-labelled events inside the strip do real work in leg 5 — **midterm-elections-2026-11-03** and **us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10** — and estimates widen caution only. The 09-07 anomaly in leg 9 is reported, not diagnosed. Educational, paper-standard throughout.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `confirmed`, Cboe-sourced 2026-09-09).** Treat **2026-10-21, 09:30 ET** as a known-date, **low-impact microstructure marker** and never as a tradeable event. No position is opened, closed or sized because of it. One guard applies: nothing executes on that day's **opening auction**, on any name. Two structural facts are carried forward rather than acted on — the settlement is struck from inside the confirmed **10-17 → 10-29 FOMC blackout**, five sessions before the 10-28 decision, and its **2026-11-20** strip is the only monthly VIX window containing the midterms. Both are placement, not edge: the lead/trail geometry that makes this settlement look different from September's is **house-measured to make no difference** (|ΔVIX| P = 0.647, volume P = 0.088 raw / 0.206 modern-era), the settlement session is not distinguishable from an ordinary session (P = 0.061 against baseline), and the strip's event density does not show up in the curve once curvature is controlled (V6 residual **+0.08**, 2026-09-08). The document's contribution is a null that closes a story, plus timing discipline.

**Kill switches:**

- **Cboe publishes a different final settlement date for `VX/V6`** — the date reverts to `estimate` and this entire document is re-specified; re-check the settlement CSV at every pulse rather than trusting this header.
- **`VX/V6`'s curvature-controlled `log(dte)` residual prints outside ±0.25** on any Cboe settlement date before 2026-10-21 — the strip *is* being repriced against the rest of the curve, leg 6 dies, and the "no event premium" reading has to be withdrawn rather than repeated.
- **`VX/V6` trades below VIX cash before 10-21 (front-month backwardation)** — the market has stopped paying for the strip at all; the contango framing inverts and the guard needs re-reading rather than restating.
- **The 2026-10-28 FOMC decision moves, or the 10-17 → 10-29 blackout is re-dated** — leg 7's placement note dies and the strip's contents change.
- **A settlement-day study with VRO or intraday data gets built** — legs 3, 4 and 6 rest on daily closes and daily volume, which cannot see the auction print itself; a real VRO series could overturn the null in the one dimension this instrument is blind to.
- **`midterm-elections-2026-11-03` or `us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10` moves off the 10-21 → 11-20 window** — leg 5's "uniquely event-dense strip" claim weakens, and the flat-curve finding in leg 6 becomes less interesting rather than more.

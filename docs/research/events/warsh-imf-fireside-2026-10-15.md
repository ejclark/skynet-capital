### Leg 2 — Warsh moves the front end when he speaks: SUPPORTED (n=2, both prepared-text formats)

- **Jackson Hole, 2026-08-28:** "We must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our
  objective, clearly and at sufficient speed. Otherwise, we have work to do." — introduced as
  "an outline … a trail map … just don't call it forward guidance." Two-year yields +11–12bp to
  ~4.34–4.35%, ten-year +5bp to ~4.72%, September-hike odds ~40% → >55%, dollar up, gold down.
- **09-16 press conference:** "This summer's inflation readings do not tell me that underlying
  trends have meaningfully improved." Read as tougher than the 25bp hike itself; the ten-year
  pushed back above 5%.
- Both moves came from a *conditional standard* rather than a decision: the market re-derives odds
  from phrasing. That is the channel to watch on 10-15, and it is why "no forward guidance" does not
  mean "no repricing."

### Leg 3 — a moderated Q&A widens the range of what gets said: MIXED

- A conversation with an IMF head will likely run to the global outlook, the oil/energy shock (Brent
  $104.28 on 10-08), the dollar, sovereign-debt and Treasury-market functioning, and central-bank
  independence — topics where a Fed Chair has latitude to stay off the policy path.
- Against that: Warsh's stated playbook is "saying as little as possible" (investinglive's read of
  Jackson Hole) and a "quieter Fed … more purposeful in its communications." Q&A raises variance
  (a stray sentence about December), but the base rate of a Chair volunteering a meeting-specific
  line at a third-party venue is low. No prior Warsh moderated Q&A exists in this corpus (n=0), so
  the width is a prior, not a measurement.

### Leg 4 — it is conditional on 10-14/10-15 and priced against a hawkish lean: SUPPORTED

- Two asymmetric tails follow. **Dovish hedge** (e.g. a soft CPI/retail run answered with "patient"

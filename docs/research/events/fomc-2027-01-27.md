## Initial research

### The question, plainly

What is the **2027-01-26/27** FOMC actually about, for a paper book holding NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT
GOOG META AAPL AMZN — a meeting 149 days out, on a date the Fed itself calls tentative, with no SEP,

**One-line verdict.** Its distinguishing feature is not the rate decision but the **calendar slot**:
this is the FOMC's annual **organizational** meeting — the venue at which the Committee reaffirms or
revises its governing consensus statement — and it is **Warsh's first**, landing weeks after five
task forces (one of them on communications) are due to conclude. Two things change underneath it that
are already published facts rather than forecasts: the **voting roster rotates on 2027-01-01 and drops
all three of the 2026 hawkish dissenters**, and the meeting carries **no SEP**, so it can act on the
December dots but cannot revise them. The rate call is not this document's to make at D-149 and it
makes none.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

Four questions, in the order they earn their keep. (1) **Which date is right?** The calendar entry
was filed `estimate` *despite* a BLS primary, because a second bls.gov view put this release on
2026-11-30 instead, and a wrong date silently corrupts every window keyed to it. (2) If 12-01 holds,
**what is actually readable at 10:00 ET that morning**, given ISM Manufacturing prints in the same
minute? (3) **What survives from the two sibling JOLTS ledgers**, and what does not? (4) What is
specific to the *October reference month*, as opposed to the July and August instances already
researched?

**One-line verdict:** the date resolves to **2026-12-01 on four agreeing BLS views** and is promoted
to `confirmed` — and the resolution is worth less than the two things it exposes: the print is
structurally unreadable at the release-bar scale (measured, not assumed), and its `confirmed` date
carries a live dated conditional 62 days upstream in the 2026-09-30 funding deadline.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `confirmed`, BLS primary, promoted this session — see leg 1).** Treat **2026-12-01
10:00 ET** as a medium-impact, second-tier labor-slack read that is **not independently readable at
the release-bar scale**, because ISM Manufacturing (high impact) prints in the same minute and the

forecast): the low-hire/low-fire composition persists — hires and quits soft, layoffs contained,
openings roughly flat — with openings-per-unemployed near **1.0–1.05** and as likely to cross on the
unemployment denominator as on openings. Two structural points hold now and are *not* contingent:
**(i)** the December meeting has an SEP and dots, so this print is absorbed rather than read raw —
the 09-29 sibling's no-SEP weight upgrade is refuted for this instance; **(ii)** the print lands
inside the 11-28 → 12-10 blackout (confirmed), so no official voice can reinterpret it before the
decision. One `estimate`-labelled conditional sits upstream of everything: a **2026-10-01** funding
lapse would relocate this release (~12-08 on the 2025 precedent) without deleting the underlying
October data.

**Kill switches:**

- **A 2026-10-01 funding lapse occurs** — re-verify this date immediately; the 2025 precedent is a

  standalone instrumented read. Registered as `FT-jolts-2026-12-01-1`.

  removes the confound entirely and makes the release bar a clean JOLTS read for the first time in
  this series' tracked history.
- **The 12-09 FOMC loses or gains a projection round** — the SEP/dots configuration is the whole of
  leg 3; if it changes, the 09-29 sibling's no-SEP framing becomes the right one after all.
- **A bls.gov view still placing this release on 2026-11-30 at the next pulse** — the residual

  July's rise to 1.05 came from the denominator, not the numerator.
- **A published Oct-data consensus emerging** (expect late November) — resolves the largest honest

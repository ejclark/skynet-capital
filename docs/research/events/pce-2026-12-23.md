## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BEA primary fetched raw 2026-08-31 and promoted from `estimate` this
pass).** Treat 2026-12-23 08:30 ET as **the PCE print with the highest existence risk and the lowest
residual information of the four this calendar tracks** — the mirror image of
[`pce-2026-11-25`](pce-2026-11-25.md), one month and one meeting away. Its content is largely public
before it lands (CPI 12-10, PPI 12-15, import/export 12-17 feed the deflators), it grades the 12-09
dots rather than feeding them, and the blackout that silenced the November print ended on 12-10 — so
none of the three things that made 11-25 exceptional apply here. What *does* apply is a branch and a
trap. The branch is **appropriations**: the House CR expires 12-04 and the Senate's 12-11, both
before this date, and last year this exact release was **canceled** and merged into a 2026-01-22
combined report — so the primary thing to track between now and 12-23 is whether the print will
exist, not what it will say. The trap is the **holiday tape**: the last full session before a 1:00pm
12-24 and a closed 12-25, in a dead week the sibling measures at 45–70% of normal volume, where the
measured slot channel is +0.17% to +1.09% and a **no-print year moved +0.88% anyway**. The operative
content is therefore three rules and no direction: **(a)** check the funding state first on every
pulse and treat a lapse as an *existence* event, not a delay; **(b)** treat a positive 12-23 close as
the null and require ≥1.5% with the print as the named story before calling it information; **(c)**
close out [`pce-2026-11-25`](pce-2026-11-25.md)'s rule (b) by this date — the ~20bp discount is
measured or withdrawn, not carried further. Direction on policy is **not this doc's to call** and is
deferred to [`fomc-2026-12-09`](fomc-2026-12-09.md); **no level is stated** and at D-114 none is
forecastable.

**Kill switches:**

- **BEA moves or removes the 2026-12-23 "Personal Income and Outlays, November 2026" line** — the
  whole doc reverts; re-check the schedule page every pulse, as raw HTML, not a summary (leg 1).
- **A CR is enacted with an expiry after 2026-12-23** — leg 3's exposure closes and the existence
  branch drops to a footnote; rule (a) is retired.
- **A lapse actually begins 12-04 or 12-11 and persists** — switch the premise from "what will it
  say" to "will it exist and will November collection survive," and defer to
  [`government-funding-deadline-2026-09-30`](government-funding-deadline-2026-09-30.md).
- **BEA publishes a Personal Income and Outlays release dated on or before 2027-01-26** — leg 5's
  "last PCE the January FOMC sees" inference is falsified and this print loses its forward role.
- **The 2026-09-30 release lands with revised core y/y within ~5bp of the old series** — the ~20bp
  estimate fails, leg 12 collapses and rule (c) is withdrawn rather than carried to 12-23.
- **The realized revision materially exceeds ~20bp** (say ≥40bp on core y/y) — the opposite failure;
  the basis change, not the level, becomes this print's story.
- **S&P close-to-close on 2026-12-23 moves ≥1.5% with the PCE as the session's named story** — leg 7
  is broken in the only way it can be, rule (b) is wrong, and the print is a repricing venue after all.
- **The 12-08/09 FOMC moves, or loses its projections asterisk** — legs 4 and 5 both rest on it being
  the year's final SEP meeting; re-source federalreserve.gov's calendar on any reported reschedule.
- **The 2027-01-26/27 FOMC date changes** — the Fed calls 2027 tentative; the 34-day vacuum and leg 5
  both re-derive from whatever replaces it.
- **A hike is delivered on 09-16, 10-28 or 12-09** — this print stops grading a hold and starts
  grading a tightening cycle's first move, which is a louder release; reassess off-cycle.
- **The CPI on 12-10 or the PPI on 12-15 surprises by more than ~0.2pp on core** — leg 6's
  "pre-printed" mechanism is the thing that makes those prints informative *for this one*; a large
  upstream surprise raises this print's confirmation value rather than lowering it.

No forward tests are registered in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md) against this event id —
leg 7's channel is too wide (n=3 plus one control) to score informatively, and saying so is the

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** **Stand aside — and stop inheriting this family's "sleepy pre-holiday half-day" prior,
because on this date it is measurably false.** The three SIFMA early closes already on this calendar
all concluded the same thing: a thin tape, nothing to see. **2027-07-02 is a different object.** July 4,
2027 falls on a Sunday, so the holiday is observed Monday July 5 — and NYSE's own 2027 grid, read
first-hand this session, carries **no early-close asterisk** on that row, while the **2028** column
does (`Tuesday, July 4**` → early close Monday, July 3, 2028). So equities run a **full 09:30–16:00 ET
session** on 2027-07-02 while the recommended cash-bond tape shuts at 14:00. Measured: across the
**13 pre-Fourth sessions 1997–2026 where NYSE ran full hours**, SPY's median volume is **0.949×** its

tested (SPY, TLT, IEF, SHY, AGG, LQD) sit **higher** in the full-hours set. **The pre-Fourth ghost town

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event arrived as a proposal from `sifma-bond-market-closure-2027-11-11`'s sweep, which filed it as
a documented hole — one of exactly two 2027 US SIFMA dates the calendar did not carry — and explicitly
left one question open: *"whether NYSE also runs a 1:00 p.m. ET half session that day was not
established."* Two questions follow. **Do the two markets keep different hours on 2027-07-02?** And
**does this family's standing conclusion — that a SIFMA early close produces a thin, unremarkable tape
— actually transfer to this date?**

**One-line verdict:** the hours question resolves cleanly and against the intuition — NYSE runs a full
session because the Fourth falls on a Sunday — and the family's thin-tape finding does **not** transfer,
because that finding was measuring the NYSE half-day all along, and this session is not one. The output
is a correction, a proposed payroll entry, and three forward tests.

### The four measurement sets

Grouping pre-Fourth sessions by **what NYSE actually did**, which is the variable this ledger turns out
to be about:

| Set | Definition | Sessions |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | July 4 on a **Sunday** → Fri **July 2**: NYSE full hours, SIFMA 14:00 bond close, payroll print | 1999, 2004, 2010, 2021 |
| **B** | July 4 on a **weekday** → preceding session: NYSE **13:00 half-day** + SIFMA 14:00 | 17 sessions, 1997–2025 |

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date and the 14:00 ET time reproduce independently — SUPPORTED (and it stays `estimate`).**
   The US 2027 panel card parses as three fields: `U.S. Independence Day` / `Monday, July 5, 2027` /
   `Early Close (2:00 p.m. Eastern Time): Friday, July 2, 2027`. The negative control holds on the same
   list — MLK, Presidents Day and Juneteenth 2027 carry **no** early-close note. The 2026 panel carries
   the parallel card (`U.S. Independence Day (observed)` / `Friday, July 3, 2026` / early close
   **Thursday, July 2, 2026**), which is what establishes that SIFMA flanks the *observed* holiday
   rather than the calendar date. It stays `estimate` on three counts: the prefix taxonomy in
   `market-events-data.ts` has no slot for a trade association's recommended schedule; this lane may
   not self-confirm an in-sweep discovery; and the source is non-binding by its own terms. *(The
   proposing lane read this as card 75 of 119 and this session as card 79 of 121 — a parse-index
   difference between two decoders on identical content. Recorded, not reconciled; nothing turns on it.)*

2. **NYSE runs a FULL session on 2027-07-02 — SUPPORTED, and this closes the question the proposal
   left open.** The grid's 2027 Independence Day row reads `Monday, July 5 (Independence Day observed)`
   with no asterisk. The **2028** column on the same row reads `Tuesday, July 4**`, and that footnote
   says verbatim: *"Each market will close early at 1:00 p.m. (1:15 p.m. for eligible options) on
   Monday, July 3, 2028."* The page carries exactly **three** `close early at` footnotes, naming
   **2026-11-27, 2026-12-24, 2027-11-26, 2028-07-03, 2028-11-24** — and **zero** July 2027 dates. NYSE
   publishes an Independence-Day-eve half-day for 2028 and none for 2027, side by side in one table.
   The mechanism is plain: NYSE shortens the session *preceding the holiday itself*, and in 2027 the
   holiday is observed on a Monday, so the preceding session is a Friday two calendar days after the
   actual Fourth. Each footnote names *NYSE Arca Equities*, where TLT, IEF, SHY, AGG and LQD list.

3. **The pre-Fourth "ghost town" is a property of the NYSE half-day, not of the Fourth — SUPPORTED,
   and it is the finding that corrects this family's prior.** Median volume ÷ trailing-20 median:

   | **TLT** | 0.840× (n=11) | 0.675× (n=13) | 1.24× |
   | **IEF** | 1.245× | 0.753× | 1.65× |
   | **SHY** | 0.895× | 0.763× | 1.17× |
   | **AGG** | 1.058× | 0.676× | 1.57× |
   | **LQD** | 0.725× | 0.612× | 1.18× |

   **Six of six instruments are more active when NYSE runs full hours**, including the four whose cash
   markets are the ones SIFMA actually truncates. The half-day set is the one that looks abandoned
   (SPY 0.591×, 14 of 17 sessions below 1.0×); the full-hours set is close to an ordinary day. Note

   That reading is intact for **its** date. It does not carry here, because the dominant term in the
   pre-Fourth number is the half-day, and 2027-07-02 is not one.

5. **The payroll print landing on 2027-07-02 is a structural identity, not a coincidence — SUPPORTED

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (2026-09-09):** stand aside, permanently and structurally — and **correct the prior this
family would otherwise hand the next session**. Concretely: (a) the SIFMA-recommended 14:00 ET
fixed-income close on 2027-07-02 is confirmed as published (`estimate`). (b) **NYSE runs a full

family has been describing is the half-day, and 2027-07-02 is not one. (d) The thing that actually
characterises this session is the **08:30 ET June payroll print** (`estimate`, derived — leg 5), which
lands on the early-close Friday in every Sunday-July-4 year by construction; on the three analogues the
bond tape moved like an ordinary payroll Friday, not like a half-day. (e) SIFMA's recommendation moves
trading hours, **not settlement**, and covers cash/OTC products only. Every statement carries the
event's **`estimate`** label, and none of this is an entry.

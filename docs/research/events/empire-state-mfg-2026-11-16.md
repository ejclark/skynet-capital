### Leg 1 — the date and time: SUPPORTED, promoted to `confirmed`

The overview page's 2026 release table renders `SEP 15 · OCT 15 · NOV 16 · DEC 15` over "Released at or
shortly after 8:30 a.m." The national economic calendar's November grid lists "Empire State Manufacturing
Survey (08:30)" on the 16th, after "Producer Price Index (PPI)" on the 13th. The Bank's rule (the 15th,
rolled forward off weekends) agrees: 11-15 is a Sunday, as Feb 15 and Aug 15 were. Two primaries and the
rule agree, so the `FRB:` prefix applies, exactly as for the 09-15, 10-15 and 12-15 siblings.

### Leg 2 — the collection window closes before the week's macro: SUPPORTED on pattern, not on a November source

The overview page states the rule: the survey "is sent on the first day of each month… Most are completed
by the tenth, although surveys are accepted until the fifteenth." The published windows were
**Aug 3 – Aug 10** and **Oct 2 – Oct 9** (each starting on the first business day after the 1st;
the Bank posts the window only with the report). 11-01 is a Sunday, so the pattern implies **11-02 → 11-09 or
11-10**. CPI prints 11-10 and PPI 11-13, so the bulk of answers are in before either. That is the
reverse of the Philadelphia Fed's window, which closes on 11-16 itself.

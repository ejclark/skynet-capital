### Leg 4 — **THE YEAR-END AXIS IS A HOLIDAY AXIS: one mechanical count replaces the regime label** · **SUPPORTED**

For every session, count **market closures in the next 9 calendar days** (a weekday inside the sample
span with no SPX close). The ratio sorts monotonically:

| closures in the forward 9 days | n | median VIX9D/VIX |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | 3,110 | **0.9510** |
| **1** | 809 | **0.8993** |

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This calendar tracks the ECB's decision, account, quiet periods, bank lending survey and monetary
developments — and, as of 2026-09-08, one Economic Bulletin edition, proposed in-sweep by the
[`ecb-decision-2026-09-10`](ecb-decision-2026-09-10.md) D-2 pulse on the reasoning that it is "the
only scheduled venue between the 09-10 decision and the 10-08 account that publishes the staff's own
written reasoning around the September projection round." That reasoning is an inheritance, and the
sibling [`ecb-account-2026-10-08`](ecb-account-2026-10-08.md) ledger has already shown what happens
when one is tested instead: the account's filed purpose ("the vote is not published") survived only
in narrowed form. So the question here is the same two-part one, pointed at a different document:
**is an Economic Bulletin publication day measurably a market event, and does the edition contain
anything a reader could not already have had two weeks earlier?**

**One-line verdict:** the publication day looks like an event in **raw** terms and **is not one** —
the entire excess is imported from the US session, and the edition's projection content is a
**restatement of numbers published 14 days earlier**, with only its boxes and article genuinely new.

**Method:** sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md), plus a
**measured** leg run this session. ECB primaries fetched direct 2026-09-08 (all HTTP 200, no blocked
fetches): the Economic Bulletin index (the "eight times a year, two weeks after each monetary policy
meeting" rule, the March/June/September/December comprehensive-edition rule, and the stated "Issue
6, 2026 - 24 September 2026"), the all-releases archive (13 publication dates, 2025-02-13 →
2026-08-06), the Governing Council meeting calendar, Issue 5, 2026 read for structure, Issue 4, 2026
read for its stated statistics cut-off, and the staff-projections index for publication timing.
Price work used `scripts/research/market-data.mjs` `bars()` with the instrument cache busted first
(`rm -rf node_modules/.cache/earnings-cycle node_modules/.cache/intraday-edges`, 2026-09-08): Yahoo
split/dividend-adjusted daily bars for `^STOXX50E`, `^GDAXI`, `EURUSD=X`, `^GSPC`, `^VIX`, `NVDA`,
`AVGO`, `MRVL` and `CRWV`. No symbol-keyed instrument was run — `symbols: []`, no issuer, and
`earnings-cycle.mjs`/`intraday-edges.mjs` have no macro mode.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The raw publication-day move looks like an event — 1.25× and 1.46× baseline.** SUPPORTED as a
   *measurement*, and leg 2 is what it means. Publication dates from the ECB's own all-releases
   archive (fetched today): **2025-02-13, 03-20, 05-02, 06-20, 08-07, 09-25, 11-13** and
   **2026-01-15, 02-19, 04-02, 05-15, 06-25, 08-06**. Mean close-to-close absolute move on those 13
   days against the all-days 2025–26 baseline:

   | Instrument | Baseline mean \|ret\| | Publication days (n=13) | Ratio |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Euro Stoxx 50 (`^STOXX50E`) | 0.75% (n=421) | **0.94%** | 1.25× |
   | DAX (`^GDAXI`) | 0.80% (n=428) | **1.17%** | 1.46× |
   | EUR/USD (`EURUSD=X`) | 0.33% (n=435) | **0.27%** (n=10) | 0.81× |
   | S&P 500 (`^GSPC`) | 0.70% (n=421) | **0.56%** | 0.80× |

   Taken alone the DAX row is the most event-like number this lane has measured on any ECB document
   — **higher than ECB *decision* days over the same window** (1.00× DAX, 1.09× STOXX50E). That is
   exactly why it gets a second leg instead of a headline.

2. **It is imported beta, not the Bulletin — the residual is *below* baseline.** SUPPORTED, and it
   is the load-bearing leg. Subtracting the same-date S&P 500 return from each euro index isolates
   the euro-specific component:

   | Residual | Baseline mean \|resid\| | Publication days (n=13) | Ratio |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | STOXX50E − SPX | 0.77pp (n=408) | **0.73pp** | **0.95×** |
   | DAX − SPX | 0.81pp (n=414) | **0.74pp** | **0.91×** |

   Both fall *below* an ordinary day. Three corroborations, none of which required a new source.
   **Volatility regime is identical**: mean `^VIX` level on publication days is **18.76** against an
   **18.75** baseline — publication days are not selected for stress. **The raw DAX result is
   fragile to two observations**: dropping its two largest days (2025-05-02 **+2.62%**, 2025-02-13
   **+2.09%**) takes 1.46× to **1.19×**, and both of those sessions moved the S&P by **+1.47%** and
   **+1.04%** respectively — i.e. they are global risk-on days, not euro days. **And the placebo
   window is quiet in the wrong places**: STOXX50E at offsets −2/−1/+1/+2 around publication runs
   **0.91× / 0.98× / 0.71× / 1.15×**, with no build-up before and no follow-through after, which is
   not the shape a scheduled information release makes.

3. **The statistics are frozen the day before the meeting and arrive ~15 days stale.** SUPPORTED,
   primary, and it is the mechanism behind leg 2. Issue 4, 2026 (published **25 June 2026**, on the
   **11 June 2026** meeting) states it in its own words: *"The cut-off date for the statistics
   included in this issue was 10 June 2026."* That is meeting **Day 1** — the numbers stop moving
   the day before the Governing Council even convenes, and the document reaches readers **15 days**
   after that. Applied to this edition, Issue 6, 2026's statistics cut off around **2026-09-09** and
   publish **2026-09-24**. A document cannot be a catalyst about a tape it stopped watching before
   the event it describes.

4. **The projections it "integrates" were published in full on decision day.** SUPPORTED, primary.
   The Bulletin index's own framing is that March/June/September/December carry an *"integrated
   discussion of the staff macroeconomic projections on inflation, growth, public finances, and
   external trade"* — which is what earned this edition its calendar slot. But the ECB's
   projections index (fetched today) dates the rounds **11 September 2025**, **19 March 2026** and
   **11 June 2026** — every one of them the meeting's Day 2, published alongside the decision, and
   alternating **ECB staff** (March/September) with **Eurosystem staff** (June/December). The
   September 2026 round is therefore an **ECB staff** round published **2026-09-10**, and the
   parent ledger already registered its two scoreable cells against that date
   (`FT-ecb-decision-2026-09-10-1`, 2028 HICP vs June's 2.0%; `-3`, 2027 headline vs 2.3%). **The
   Bulletin discusses numbers this book has already scored.** What it adds is prose.

5. **Its only new content is the boxes and the article — and they are research, not signals.**
   SUPPORTED, primary. Issue 5, 2026 read today: three divisions — *Economic, financial and monetary
   developments* (a summary plus external environment · economic activity · prices and costs ·
   financial markets · financing and credit), **eight boxes**, and **one article** ("The cost of not
   knowing: how uncertainty weighs on the euro area economy"), plus statistics tables. Issue 4,
   2026's box set is the same shape (AI and the US labour market; US financial-market reaction to
   oil-supply geopolitical shocks; EU state aid; Middle-East oil-price headwinds to euro-area
   growth; restructuring announcements; import exposure to China and goods inflation; liquidity
   conditions 11 Feb–5 May; the 2025 current-account narrowing). This is genuinely the only material
   a reader could not have had on decision day — and it is retrospective analysis on a fixed
   cut-off, not a forward signal. It is why the honest call is *read it*, and why the honest tier is
   `low`.

6. **The two-week rule is stronger than the account's four-week rule — 13 of 13, every exception
   mechanical.** SUPPORTED, primary + arithmetic. Publication minus meeting Day 2 across the same 13
   editions: **exactly 14 days in 9** (2025-02-13, 03-20, 08-07, 09-25, 11-13; 2026-02-19, 04-02,
   06-25, 08-06), **exactly 15 days in 3**, and **28 days in 1**. The exceptions are not dispersion,
   they are a calendar: all three 15-day cases are editions whose Day 2 + 14 landed on a **Frankfurt
   public holiday** and shifted exactly one business day — **2025-05-01** (Labour Day, meeting
   2025-04-17 → published 05-02), **2025-06-19** (Corpus Christi, a Hesse public holiday, meeting
   06-05 → published 06-20) and **2026-05-14** (Ascension Day, meeting 04-30 → published 05-15).
   The lone 28-day case is the **year-end** edition (2025-12-18 meeting → Issue 8 published
   **2026-01-15**), whose +14 would have been New Year's Day. **For a non-holiday Thursday the rule
   is 10 for 10.** 2026-09-10 + 14 = **2026-09-24**, a Thursday, and September carries no German
   public holiday — so the slip mechanism this ledger can measure is simply not available, and the
   ECB *states* the date independently of the arithmetic. This is why no date forward test is
   registered (see the stance): a formality dilutes the register.

7. **The date is contaminated well past the point where attribution is possible.** SUPPORTED from
   this calendar's own entries. **26 tracked events** sit within five days of 2026-09-24, and five
   share the date itself: `trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24` (**impact high**, `estimate` — a state visit

8. **No channel to this book, and none proposed.** SUPPORTED, and checked on the tape rather than
   asserted. `symbols: []`; the house playbooks (S1/S2/E1/S3/S4 + G1,

### The tempting story this session refused

**"Europe outperforms on Bulletin days."** The signed euro-minus-SPX residual on the 13 publication
days is **+0.41pp** for STOXX50E with **10 of 13 positive**, against a baseline that is **−0.001pp**
and **51%** positive. It is the most quotable number in the study and it is **NOT SUPPORTED** as
anything tradable, for three reasons stated here rather than buried in a limits paragraph: **n = 13**
and no significance test is run; the residual pairs a euro cash close against a US close **six and a
half hours later**, so it measures a lagged clock rather than a synchronous window and any
persistent drift in that window would show up as "euro outperformance" on *any* Thursday sample; and
**there is no mechanism** — leg 3 has the document's numbers frozen 15 days earlier and leg 4 has its
headline content already public. A pattern with no mechanism, no synchronous measurement and n=13 is
a coincidence with good phrasing. It is recorded because refusing it is the finding.

### Honest limits

The measurement is **daily close-to-close** and cannot see an intraday reaction that fires on
publication and fades by the close; a 5-minute study of euro benchmarks is not available through
this repo's instruments, so legs 1 and 2 are claims about *daily* significance only. **The
publication time is not established** — the ECB primary does not state it, and a web search this
session explicitly failed to confirm the commonly-cited 10:00 CET, returning ECB statistical
releases at that hour rather than the Bulletin itself; the "04:00–09:30 ET" window in the Today kill
switch is therefore a generous euro-session bracket, not a stated release time. **n = 13** against a
400-plus-day baseline and **no significance test is run**: the honest statement is that the
publication-day residual sits inside the ordinary distribution, not that a difference has been
excluded — and the same caveat cuts against the raw 1.46× DAX result, which is why leg 2 reports the
drop-two robustness check rather than only the headline. **EUR/USD has only 10 of 13** publication
days in the Yahoo series (2025-05-02, 2025-06-20 and 2026-05-15 are missing), so its 0.81× is the
thinnest row in the table and carries the least weight. `^STOXX50E` and `^GDAXI` bars are Yahoo index
series, **unadjusted for dividends** by construction, and every residual pairs a euro cash close
against a US close six and a half hours later — a session pair, not a synchronous window, which is
the same weakness the sibling account ledger recorded and the reason the signed-residual story above
is refused rather than reported. Leg 3's cut-off claim is read from **Issue 4, 2026** and generalised
to Issue 6 by convention; the ECB does not publish the forward cut-off, so "~2026-09-09" is an
inference from one observed edition plus the two-week rule, not a stated date. Legs 4 and 7 are
partly forward-looking: the **09-10 decision has not happened**, and `trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24` is
itself `estimate`-dated, so the contamination argument would weaken (not reverse) if the summit
moves. Finally, leg 8 is a **null claim**, and the honest failure mode of a null is an uninstrumented
channel — which is why the Today kill switch is written as a measurable price test on named tickers
rather than as a restatement of the null.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`):** **stand aside, read the boxes, and downgrade the expectation the
proposal was filed with.** The 2026-09-24 Economic Bulletin is measured — not asserted — to be an
ordinary trading day once the US session is subtracted: the euro-minus-SPX residual across the 13
editions published 2025–26 is **0.95×** (Euro Stoxx 50) and **0.91×** (DAX) of baseline, *below* an
ordinary day, with VIX levels on publication days (**18.76**) indistinguishable from baseline
(**18.75**) and the raw 1.46× DAX headline collapsing to 1.19× on removal of two global risk-on
sessions. This book holds nothing with a euro-rates channel and none is proposed. Two analytical
commitments follow. First, the entry's stated purpose is **narrowed**: the proposal filed it as "the
only scheduled venue that publishes the staff's own written reasoning around the September projection
round," and that is only true of the **boxes and the article** — the projections themselves publish
**2026-09-10** (ECB staff round), the statistics are frozen the day before the meeting by the ECB's
own stated convention, and the edition arrives **~15 days stale**. It cannot revise a number this
book has already scored. Second, it inherits an **attribution discipline** sharper than either
sibling's, because its date is worse: `trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24` is **high**-impact and
semis-facing, and a 7Y new issue plus a 20–30Y buyback share the session, so no 09-24 move may be
credited to Frankfurt until both have been ruled out. Estimates widen caution and license nothing.

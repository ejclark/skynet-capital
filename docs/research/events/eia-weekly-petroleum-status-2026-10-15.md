## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (event date `estimate`).** **Stand aside.** No position, no hedge and no watch-list entry is
keyed to this print: no playbook is energy-keyed and no tracked name carries crude exposure. What
this ledger delivers is **one directional number to read**, corrected away from a geopolitical

change's sign matches the print's surprise* — on a morning already carrying CPI, Beige Book, Retail
Sales, PPI and two regional Fed surveys, the null hypothesis is that a 12:00 ET print is invisible.

**Kill switches:**

1. **The decline stalls or reverses** — percent utilization prints **≥ 94.0%** on 2026-10-15 (flat
   or higher than the 09-18 print). `FT-…-1` dies, and the extension of the seasonal to this later
   window was the wrong call — the next session should re-examine whether 2026's turnaround troughed

   crude complex's 12:00 ET move land in the textbook-matched directions despite the dense 08:30
   corridor. `FT-…-2` dies and the attribution rule loses its observation for this print.
4. **We acquire a vehicle** — any house playbook gains an energy leg, or a tracked symbol gains direct

5. **The Saudi-hook correction itself is wrong** — a primary Saudi/Aramco statement asserts the line

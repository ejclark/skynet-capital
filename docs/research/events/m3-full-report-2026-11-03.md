### Leg 3 — the nowcast footprint · **REFUTED**, re-derived on a differently-built classifier

The twin's central result is reproduced here from scratch, with one methodological change made
because the twin's own "honest limits" flagged it: `ContribArchives`'s `Data releases` free text is
inconsistent across the decade, and a bare token like `m3 manufacturing` (37 occurrences) or
`advance durable manufacturing` (84) names neither `m3-1` nor `m3-2`. Rather than drop those, each
ambiguous token is **resolved against the Census calendar** — a vintage dated on an M3-2 release day
and not an M3-1 day is a full report, and vice versa. That resolves **62** vintages a substring test
would have misfiled.

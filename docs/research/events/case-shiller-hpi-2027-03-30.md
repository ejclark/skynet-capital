### Leg 3 — placement: the most structurally unusual date this series has on the calendar · **SUPPORTED**

All of this is calendar arithmetic computed locally; none of it depends on a fetch or on a tape.

**Quarter-end is not a coincidence, it is the rule.** Q1 2027's final sessions are **Mar 23, 24, 25,
29, 30, 31** (Good Friday 03-26 removed), so **2027-03-30 is the penultimate session of the
quarter, T-1**. And that is structural: of the **22** quarter-ending-month last Tuesdays between
2021-01 and 2026-08, **22 of 22** fall inside their quarter's final five sessions (positions T-0
through T-4). Since 22 of the 68 last Tuesdays in that window are quarter-ending months, **roughly a
third of every Case-Shiller edition ever published under this rule is a quarter-end edition, by
construction.** Any "quarter-end effect" a future lane finds on this print is therefore a property of
the *rule*, not of the print.

**The closure is the genuinely rare part.** Good Friday 2027 is **2027-03-26** (Gregorian computus,
Easter 2027-03-28 — derived here independently of the calendar's own
`good-friday-market-closure-2027-03-26` entry). That puts a **full US market closure inside the five
sessions immediately before this release**:

| March edition | Prior five sessions | Full closure inside? |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-03-30 … 2026-03-31 (six editions) | — | **none** |
| **2027-03-30** | **2027-03-22 → 2027-03-29** | **yes — Good Friday 2027-03-26** |
| 2028-03-28 … 2031-03-25 (four editions) | — | **none** |

**1 of 11.** Widening the lens: Good Friday precedes the last Tuesday of March in only **6 of the 51
years 1990–2040** (2005, 2008, 2016, **2027**, 2032, 2035) — about **12%**. In every other year the
March edition prints *before* Good Friday, not after it.

**What this is worth, stated honestly.** It is context and a reason this ledger exists, not an edge.
The FHFA sibling measured a real quarter-end term-structure effect (Q1/Q2/Q3's last five sessions
steepen the front, **p=0.0156**) and a real holiday effect (closures in the forward 9-day window
compress it, **p=0.0000**) — but both are properties of the *session*, available to anything trading
that day, and `symbols: []` means this print holds nothing that could express either.

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** **Stand aside. This lockup is small, held by the sponsor and its own people, and those
holders are sitting on a loss — the classic "insiders dump on unlock day" setup is mostly absent.**
The date is **confirmed** (SEC prospectus: one 180-day period, no earnings or price trigger that
could free shares sooner). Only about **10.9M of ~100.6M shares (~11%)** are locked: Blackstone's
own **$200M** purchase (9.1M shares) plus the Blackstone-affiliated directed-share buyers (1.8M).
Everyone who bought in the IPO itself is already free to sell. Both locked groups paid **$20.00**
against a price near **$16.4** — an **18% paper loss** — so the unlock hands them an exit at a loss,
not a profit. Nothing in the price record shows the usual pre-unlock run-up; the stock has already
fallen **26–29%** from its May peak. Options on the name are too thin to read. There is no edge
here to trade, and the first free-to-sell session (**Tue 2026-11-10**) is also CPI day, so any move
that day cannot be pinned on the unlock.

## Initial research

### The question

Does BXDC's lockup expiry on **2026-11-09** (confirmed) create a tradeable supply shock — and is
there anything in the clause that moves the date?

### Verdict in one line

No tradeable edge: the locked block is ~11% of shares, sponsor-held and underwater, and the date is
fixed. Stand aside.

### Method

Primary read of the 424B4 (fetched 2026-10-20, 1.1M characters of text, every "lock-up" mention
read); price and volume from Yahoo's daily chart feed (NEWS-grade, fetched 2026-10-20); peer and
VIX readings from the same feed; options from Yahoo's chain endpoint. No fetch was blocked.
Everything below is dated 2026-10-20 unless it says otherwise.

### Leg 1 — the date: SUPPORTED, with a one-day wrinkle

- **One period.** "We, our directors and officers, our Manager and the Blackstone Investor have
  agreed, and the DSP participants will agree … not to dispose of or hedge any shares … from the date
  of this prospectus continuing through the date 180 days after the date of this prospectus, except
  with the prior written consent of the representatives of the underwriters."
- **No early-release trigger.** Searched the whole prospectus for earnings-release, closing-price
  and staged-tranche language: none. Consent of the lead underwriters (Goldman Sachs, Citigroup,
  Morgan Stanley, Barclays, BofA, Deutsche Bank, J.P. Morgan, RBC, Wells Fargo) is the only path to
  an earlier release, and the prospectus gives no schedule for one.
- **Arithmetic.** Prospectus dated 2026-05-13 + 180 days = **2026-11-09** (a Monday). The restriction
  runs "through" that date, so the **first free session is Tue 2026-11-10**.
- **The wrinkle.** Shares bought through the directed-share program are *also* locked by FINRA Rule
  5110(e) for 180 days from "effectiveness or commencement of sales"; trading began 2026-05-14, which
  would put that clock one day later (**2026-11-11**). The prospectus does not say which date FINRA
  counts from, so the date stays **2026-11-09** and this is noted, not resolved.
- **A second wrinkle, unresolved.** Exception (x) of the lock-up lets a holder "that is not an officer
  or director" sell "shares … acquired from the underwriters in this offering". Read literally that
  could already free the Blackstone Investor's and DSP buyers' shares; the prospectus elsewhere
  says those shares "will be subject to a lock-up", so the exception's reach is unclear. Either
  way it points to *less* locked supply than counted below, never more.

### Leg 2 — supply: how much unlocks: MIXED (small, but heavy volume already)

| Holder | Shares | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Blackstone Investor | 9,111,795 | prospectus ownership chart, at $20.00, no bonus shares |
| Directed-share (DSP) buyers | 1,799,400 | same chart; 5,031,250 reserved, 1.8M taken; includes directors and officers |
| **Locked total** | **≈10.9M** | **≈10.9% of ~100.6M shares** (Yahoo shows 100.65M; the prospectus gives 100.62M if the over-allotment is fully exercised) |
| Free since 2026-05-14 | ≈89.7M | all IPO investors incl. 758,305 bonus shares |

- **In days of volume:** 10.9M ÷ 2.45M (20-day average) ≈ **4.5 days**; ÷ 1.75M (60-day) ≈ **6.2 days**.
  That is the *ceiling* if every locked share sold at once — which no one expects.
- **Who the sellers would be.** The largest block is the sponsor, which bought $200M at the IPO price
  to "strengthen our Manager's commitment" (prospectus). BXDC is externally managed by a Blackstone
  affiliate, so the sponsor holding is a signalling position; the prospectus also grants Blackstone
  unlimited demand registration rights, which means a registered sale is *possible*, not planned.
- **Compare to a typical lockup.** Ordinary IPO lockups free the pre-IPO owners' stake, often most of
  the company; here ~11% unlocks and ~89% has been tradable since day one. That is the main reason
  the call is stand-aside rather than avoid.

### Leg 3 — price: REFUTES the run-up premise

- IPO **$20.00** (effective **$19.80** for IPO investors after the 1% bonus shares). First close
  **$19.81**; peak close **$22.34** (2026-05-19); last full close **$15.90** (2026-10-19, down to
  **$15.64** on 10-16); **$16.42** intraday 2026-10-20.
- Since IPO: **−17.9%** (−20.5% on the 10-19 close). Off the peak: **−26.5%** (−28.8% on the 10-19 close).
- Last month: BXDC about **−15%** (2026-09-21 → 10-20) against DLR **−5.7%**, EQIX **−6.4%**, VNQ
  **−1.6%**, IYR **−0.9%** over the same month — roughly 9 points worse than its closest peers. The
  cause is not established by any source read here; Yahoo's feed and a web search returned no
  company news. One 17.4M-share day on **2026-09-18** (10× the norm) coincided with the quarterly
  expiry session; not attributed.
- The usual finding — lockup pullbacks are larger after a big run-up — does not apply: there is no
  run-up to give back, and locked holders are about **18% under water** at $20.00.

### Leg 4 — options: UNREADABLE

- Listed expiries: 2026-11-20, 12-18, 2027-03-19, 06-17, on strikes **15 / 17.5 / 20** only.
- 11-20 puts: 15-strike bid **0.20** / ask **0.40**, implied vol **51%**; a spread that wide (two-thirds
  of the midpoint) carries no information. The 17.5 and 20 calls show a **0** bid. 12-18 20-calls:
  **0.05 / 0.25**, open interest 2,030.
- Realized vol: **34%** (20-day), **30%** (60-day). A 51% put quote against that *looks* rich but
  cannot be distinguished from a stale quote. **No skew, no term structure, no read on whether the
  unlock is priced.**

### What plays the conditions support

None. A put or short into the date would be a bet on sponsor selling that the clause, the cost
basis and the holder type all argue against; a long into the date would be a bet on a stock in a
high-volume slide with no deployed assets. Stand aside, paper-only, and use this event as a data
point for the lockup study (#5912 slice 2).

### Honest limits

- Price and volume come from a free feed (NEWS-grade), not an exchange tape; the 10-20 session was
  partial when read (volume 310K at ~10:49 ET), so it is excluded from the volume averages.
- Cause of the September–October slide is **unknown**; stated as such.
- Lock-up holder counts are from the prospectus chart at pricing, not a current ownership filing;
  no post-IPO 13D / Form 4 was checked.
- The two wrinkles in Leg 1 could shift the first free session by a day or reduce locked supply;
  neither could add any.

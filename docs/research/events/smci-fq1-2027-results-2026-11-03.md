## Initial research

### The question, and the one-line verdict

Is there a trade in Super Micro's Q1 FY2027 print on 2026-11-03 (after the close)? **No.** The call and revenue/margin are company-confirmed and pre-announced; the unknown (guidance) is exactly the part the stock gaps on, and the house instruments show no repeatable edge around SMCI prints.

### What is known, with sources

- **Date (confirmed, IR:).** The company's own 2026-10-20 release: "schedules earnings call for November 3rd at 5pm EST", webcast "Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. ET / 2:00 p.m. PT" (ir.supermicro.com, fetched direct 2026-10-30). The 8-K Exhibit 99.1 agrees (SEC:).
- **Pre-announced quarter (SEC:/IR:).** Revenue "estimated to be in the range of $18.5 billion to $19.5 billion" vs guidance "$14.5 billion to $15.5 billion" ("increased demand for AI platforms"); gross margin "12.0% to 12.5%" vs "10.4% to 10.8%" ("customer and product mix"). Preliminary and unaudited; no EPS, backlog, Q2 guide or full-year outlook in the release. It also says the "customer base may become more concentrated" as it targets larger customers.
- **Consensus (NEWS:, unreliable).** One aggregator snippet (Benzinga, quote page 403'd on direct fetch, recorded in `blocked`) lists a Q1 EPS consensus of $0.72 and an old guide of $1.01–1.10 non-GAAP — the two don't reconcile, and a consensus set before 10-20 is stale by construction. Treated as unknown; no consensus-vs-actual claim is made here.

### Leg 1 — the pop was already paid, then given back — SUPPORTED (price data, Yahoo daily bars through the 10-30 session)

SMCI closed $32.47 on 10-20, gapped +12.2% and closed +9.5% ($35.57) on 10-21, then drifted to $31.57 on the 10-30 session reading (~3:15 pm ET, **not a final close**): -2.8% below the pre-announcement close. Over the same window NVDA -6.3%, AMD -6.9%, DELL -9.1%, QQQ -4.2%. The 10-30 leg was the sector rout (SMCI -8.3% on the day; DELL -11.8%, AMD -12.1%, NVDA -5.1%), which sibling ledgers attribute (NEWS:) to Intel's Q4 guide-down (INTC about -30%) and hyperscaler capex worries. SMCI held up better than its server peers over the window; it is not the cause of the rout.

   Japan`. That figure is the simple mean of the twelve 2026 monthly index levels. Eleven of them
   are already public when it prints: the November index releases **2026-12-18**, five weeks

   | December y/y surprise | Index points | Move in the 2026 annual rate |

   | ±2.0pp | ±2.02 | **±0.168pp** |

   is **100.0083**, i.e. 100 by construction — so the 2026 annual index level *is* the year's
   inflation rate plus 100, to within a hundredth.

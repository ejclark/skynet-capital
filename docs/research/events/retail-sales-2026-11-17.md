### Leg 2 — the fall-Prime "nonstore distortion" this entry was filed under — REFUTED, and it is the load-bearing finding

`market-events.ts`'s note for this entry calls October "the one whose own reference month carries
Amazon's fall Prime event — the nonstore distortion that made July's −2.2% drop and August's bounce
unreadable as demand." The July/August half of that sentence is right and is the sibling docs' work.
The October half does not survive measurement.

**The mechanism.** Prime Big Deal Days has run as a Tuesday–Wednesday in the **first half of October
every year since the 2022 rebrand** (2025: Oct 7–8; 2024: Oct 8–9). MARTS is adjusted for seasonal
variation *and holiday and trading-day differences*, and the seasonal factors are re-estimated on the
history — so a promotion that recurs in the same slot every year is exactly what the adjustment is
built to absorb. July's Prime Day was a distortion in 2026 **because it moved** (to June 23–26,
Amazon's press center, primary), breaking a pattern the factors had embedded. Nothing has moved in
October.

**The measurement, from Census's own Table 2:**

| Reference month | Fall Prime event | Nonstore retailers, m/m SA | Headline total, m/m SA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 2024 | none | **+1.7%** | +0.8% |
| **Oct 2024** | **Oct 8–9** | **+0.3%** | **+0.4%** |
| Sep 2025 | none | **−0.4%** | +0.1%\* |
| **Oct 2025** | **Oct 7–8** | **+1.8%** | **+0.0%\*** |

Two conclusions the table forces. **First, the nonstore signature is not stable**: +0.3% and +1.8% in
the two event months, with a no-event September (+1.7%) beating one of them outright. **Second, at
the headline the event months were the *weaker* ones** — Oct 2024 +0.4% against September's +0.8%,
Oct 2025 +0.0%\* against September's +0.1%\*. A mechanical lift that shows up as a deceleration in
both available years is not a mechanical lift.

**The corroborating fact, from the other side.** Prime Big Deal Days 2025 was **cut from four days to
two** and drove ~**$11B** in event-week US sales, **−0.4% y/y** (Acadia, press-grade) — and Oct-2025
nonstore nonetheless printed the *larger* of the two m/m readings. The event's own size moved
materially and the SA series did not follow it.

**What survives, and it is the honest residual.** The distortion risk is a **change** in the event —
new dates outside the embedded first-half-of-October slot, or a step-change in scale. No Amazon
primary announces 2026 fall dates: aboutamazon.com's Prime Day 2026 page (fetched direct today)
states **"Prime Day 2026 runs from June 23 at 12:01 a.m. PDT through June 26"** and contains **no
mention of any fall event**. Aggregators name "Oct 7–8," which is 2025's pair exactly — the stale-article
trap the sibling ledger already flagged; one seller-facing blog cites a Sept 8 deal-submission
deadline, which is not a primary either but does say *when* the date typically becomes knowable.
**No calendar entry filed.** Registered as **FT-46**.

### Leg 5 — read the asterisks; October 2025 is the sharpest case on record — SUPPORTED

Quoted from `adv2510.pdf`: October-2025 advance sales were **$732.6 billion, virtually unchanged

rule for 11-17, not a forecast: a sub-CI "beat" or "miss" is not statistically distinguishable from
zero, and coverage reports it as if it were.

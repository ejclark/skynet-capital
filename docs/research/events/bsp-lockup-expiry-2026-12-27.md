## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Stand aside, and note the date on the calendar was wrong: the unlock already happened. The 424B4 ends the holders' lock-up 48 hours after the second quarterly report following the 2026-06-30 prospectus, once 125 days have passed. That report was Q3 (6-K accepted 2026-11-19 16:22 ET), so insiders could first sell on Monday 2026-11-23 (confirmed, SEC:), five weeks before the 180-day date the calendar carried (2026-12-27, a Sunday). BSP has not pulled back: it closed $46.29 on 2026-11-25, 59.6% above its $29 IPO price, up 12.8% from the 11-20 close, and 8.9% ahead of XLK over the first three sellable sessions on 3.8x to 5.9x its normal volume. About 267M restricted ordinary shares (4.6x the IPO float) and 310M five-vote class A shares are now free of the underwriters' lock-up, so the supply is large; no sale by any holder is on file yet. Options could not be read (Cboe 403, Yahoo 401).

## Initial research

### The question
On what date does BSP's lock-up really end, and does the supply that unlocks then justify a position, a hedge or a stand-aside?

**Verdict: the date was wrong and has already passed. The unlock was Monday 2026-11-23, the supply is large, the first three sessions went up, and the study gives no edge: stand aside.** The entry as filed (2026-12-27, `confirmed`) read only the 180-day sentence. Acting on it would have put a put spread into a window that had already closed.

### Method
Read the 424B4 in full (EDGAR, fetched 2026-10-20 and again 2026-11-27, HTTP 200, 9.7 MB; filed 2026-07-01, prospectus dated 2026-06-30 on its cover): the underwriting lock-up text, "Shares eligible for future sale" and the principal and selling shareholders table. Read the EDGAR submissions list for CIK 2004711 (fetched 2026-11-27) and, from it, the Q2 and Q3 6-Ks, the 2026-11-19 placement and Mailchimp 6-Ks, the Q3 press release (Exhibit 99.1) and the three Form 4s of 2026-11-23. Prices and volumes from Yahoo daily bars for BSP, XLK, IGV, SPY and ^VIX (fetched 2026-11-27; last session in the data 2026-11-25; the half-day of 11-27 is not in them). ### Leg 1 — the date: SUPPORTED (early release fired; first sellable session 2026-11-23)
The filing states two things. The company's own lock-up: 180 days after the prospectus, 2026-12-27. The holders' lock-up (executive officers, directors, the selling shareholders and others "collectively holding substantially all of our shares"): also 180 days, then "Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Restricted Period will expire 48 hours after the date on which we publicly release our results in our second quarterly earnings report following the date of this prospectus, provided that at least 125 days have elapsed since the date of this prospectus as of the date of such second quarterly earnings release." The first report after 2026-06-30 was Q2 (6-K, 2026-08-13); the second was Q3, whose 6-K was accepted by EDGAR on 2026-11-19 at 16:22:49 and whose press release is dated "November 19, 2026". 125 days after the prospectus is 2026-11-02, so the condition is met; 48 hours later is Saturday 2026-11-21 (about 16:20 ET), and the next session is Monday 2026-11-23. A search snippet says results were "before the U.S. market opens" on 11-20; if that is right, 48 hours is Sunday 11-22 and the first session is still 11-23, so the date does not depend on it. Day 0 of the study is 2026-11-23. Corroboration: three Form 4s filed 2026-11-23 report conversions dated 2026-11-20 (code C, no sale) of 3.68M (Ferrari), 4.62M (Patarnello) and 4.05M (Danieli) class A shares into ordinary shares, the step a holder takes before it can sell; and the 13Gs of 11-12 and 11-16 are routine ownership reports. The calendar's own parser missed the clause: its early-release test reads only sentences that name a lock-up and "N days after the date of", and this one names neither.

### Leg 2 — supply: LARGE (4.6x the IPO float, ungated for non-affiliates)
IPO 57,971,015 ordinary shares at $29: 34,398,640 primary and 23,572,375 from selling shareholders; an option for 8,695,652 more (5,244,026 company, 3,451,626 sellers), exercise not read. Ordinary shares outstanding after the offering 324,978,625 (330,222,651 with the option); class A 310,240,685 (five votes each, convertible into ordinary on request). Restricted ordinary shares: 324.98M less the 57.97M sold, about **267M, 4.6x the IPO float** (4.0x with the option), about 146 days of the pre-unlock 20-day mean volume (1.83M). The prospectus's own count is 318,510,767 shares eligible after the lock-up with no class A conversions (5.5x float); I could not reconcile the 51.5M difference. Both are in the lockup study's 3.5–7.6x bucket. Large holders from the prospectus table (as of 2026-05-29): Baillie Gifford 43.5M (selling 7.1M), Galileo Quattordici 33.8M (selling 11.3M), Cox Investment Holdings 27.0M, Durable Capital 19.6M, StarTIP 18.0M (selling 2.0M); founders hold 82.1M (Ferrari), 77.5M (Danieli), 75.9M (Querella) and 74.7M (Patarnello) class A shares. Founders are affiliates, so Rule 144 caps them at the greater of 1% (3.25M) or four-week average weekly volume per quarter; funds that are not affiliates face no volume cap once their holding period is met (my reading of Rule 144, not stated per holder in the filing). New supply beside it: Berkshire Hathaway bought 10M shares in a private placement ($330M, so about $33, my arithmetic) and 32.5M more were sold to institutions at $40.00 ($1.30B, 6-K 2026-11-19, closing expected in Q4) with registration rights, so a resale registration is the next filing to watch. Nothing on EDGAR through 2026-11-27 shows a sale: the only filings since 11-20 are the three conversion Form 4s.

### Honest limits
100 sessions and two reports: no cycle statistics. The option leg is missing. The 267M restricted figure is my arithmetic (outstanding less sold) and the prospectus's own 318.5M differs by 51.5M. Holder counts are from the 2026-05-29 table and have moved. Whether the underwriters exercised the option, and which holders are affiliates beyond the founders, are not read. The 11-27 half-day is not in the data. The tape in D-10..D+2 is dominated by a 3.6% market drop and a 4.7% market rebound, so the abnormal returns above carry a large beta error and are descriptive, never a result. The date rests on my reading of "second quarterly earnings report following the date of this prospectus" as Q3 (Q2's 08-13 report being the first); the Q2 report (2026-08-13) clearly follows the prospectus, so I count Q3 as the second; the 11-20 conversions and 11-23 volume are what an open float looks like, but none of this is a filed statement that the lock-up ended, and no 6-K or 8-K says so.

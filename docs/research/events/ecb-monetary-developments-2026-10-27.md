### Honest limits

The measurement is **daily close-to-close** and cannot see an intraday reaction that fires and fades
inside a session — a 10:00 CET release lands mid-session for `EXV1.DE` and `^STOXX50E`, so a daily
bar captures it, but only as a day. **No significance test is run**: n=44 against a ~930-day
baseline, so the honest statement is that the release-day distribution sits at or below the ordinary
one, not that a difference has been excluded. The **month-end control is imperfect** — "day-of-month
≥ 22" holds calendar position roughly constant but the release date is inside its own control window
by construction, and euro flash HICP and US PCE also cluster there, so the control is contaminated in
the direction of *understating* the null. **The consensus evidence in leg 4 is a search result, not a
primary**: the "3.3% vs 2.9% expected" figure comes from Trading Economics, no dated pre-release poll
was fetched, and it is a **single** surprise observation — n=1 is not a surprise-response function,
and the honest claim is that the one documented beat produced nothing, not that beats never matter.
**EUR/USD is the one instrument that does not read null**: its release-day *median* is **1.14×**
baseline (2023+) and **1.09×** (2017+) while its mean is 0.96×/1.06× — a pattern consistent with a
small frequent nudge and no tail. On a 0.25% median baseline that is a **0.04pp** difference,
economically nothing and untested, but it is recorded rather than rounded away. `EXV1.DE` is an ETF
tracking STOXX Europe 600 Banks, so it is **Europe-wide rather than euro-area** and includes UK and
Swiss constituents; `^STOXX50E` and `^GDAXI` are index series unadjusted for dividends by
construction. Leg 5 rests on **one year of statements (five meetings)**; earlier years were not
fetched, so "every meeting" is established for 2026 and asserted no further, and the March-quoted-
next-day precedent is **one** instance. Leg 6's loan series is assembled from statement quotations
plus one press release rather than pulled from the ECB Data Portal series directly, so a revision
inside the run would not be visible here. Leg 2's BLS conduct window is the **October 2025** round's;
the Q3 2026 round's window is not yet published. **The 2026-09-25 Aug-2026 release is deliberately
NOT proposed to `market-events.ts`**, and this is a judgment call worth stating: it is a dated
adjacency and the sweep rule would normally file it, but it is *this same release, one month
earlier*, measured null on n=44 — filing each monthly instance would spend twelve research sessions a
year re-deriving this document. It is recorded here as a dated watch item and as the "this month"
falsifier instead. Finally, the date stays **estimate** despite two primaries and a 9-of-9 band, per
this lane's no-self-confirm limit and the `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` gap — it widens caution and licenses
nothing.

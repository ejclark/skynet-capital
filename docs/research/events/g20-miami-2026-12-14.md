## At a glance

**TL;DR.** The date is better-sourced than the calendar entry says — treasury.gov's own Finance-Track
release, fetched direct today, states verbatim that the host year "will culminate with President
Trump's Leaders' Summit on **December 14-15, at Trump National Doral in Miami, Florida**." The label
stays `estimate` anyway, on a schema gap and not on doubt (see leg 1). But sourcing the date is the
least interesting thing about it. A G20 leaders' summit has no price channel of its own: the only two
in the modern sample that moved the tape moved it through a **US–China bilateral held on the
sidelines**, and one of those two gave the whole move back in a single session. This one is
explicitly being staged as that bilateral's backdrop (SCMP, delegation sources, July 2026) — but it
is the **third** Trump–Xi venue in four months, after Washington 09-24 and APEC Shenzhen 11/18–19, so
its marginal information is thin by arrival. Meanwhile the multilateral half is visibly fracturing:
China dissented from four paragraphs of the US chair's own statement at Asheville four days ago,
South Africa is disinvited outright, and Russia's attendance is unresolved. Net: stand aside. The
sharper risk is **attribution** — 12/14–15 sits inside the densest corridor on this calendar (FOMC
12/09, CPI 12/10, the CR expiry 12/11, PPI 12/15, December triple witching 12/18), so a tape move
that week will have four better explanations than the summit.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries 2026-12-14 as the 21st G20 leaders' summit at Trump
National Doral. Is the date real and primary-verifiable, does a G20 summit actually move markets,
what is different about this one, and which of the names this calendar tracks carry exposure?

**One-line verdict:** the date is now primary-verified (treasury.gov, read direct 2026-09-04) though
its label cannot move; the summit has no market channel of its own and only ever borrows one from a
US–China bilateral held beside it — and this is the third such venue in four months, arriving after
the deadline that matters and inside the densest event corridor on this calendar, which makes it a
watch-and-never-attribute item rather than a tradeable one.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date and venue are primary-sourced — and the `estimate` label still cannot move.**
   SUPPORTED on the fact, blocked on the schema. Treasury's 2026-02-19 release states verbatim:
   *"The United States' G20 host year will culminate with President Trump's Leaders' Summit on
   December 14-15, at Trump National Doral in Miami, Florida."* That is a US-host-government primary,
   fetched direct today, and it retires two of the three reasons the calendar entry gave for filing
   the date `estimate` ("no primary schedule page was read direct"; "no confirmed prefix for a
   multilateral body's own schedule" — this is not the multilateral body's page, it is the host
   government's). The label still cannot flip, because `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` in
   `scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs` documents `TSY:` as *treasury.gov / treasurydirect.gov
   **auction** schedule* — using it for a diplomatic calendar would corrupt the audit trail the
   prefixes exist to be. So: `estimate` on a **schema gap, not on doubt**, and this is the third
   ledger to bank that gap (after
   [`us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10`](us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10.md) and
   [`china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31`](china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31.md)).
   The entry's `source` field is amended this PR to say what was actually read; its status is not.

2. **A G20 leaders' summit has no price channel of its own. It borrows one from a bilateral.**
   SUPPORTED, and this is the load-bearing finding. The two summits in the modern sample that moved
   US equities both did so through a **Trump–Xi meeting on the sidelines**, not through anything the
   G20 itself produced: **Buenos Aires, 2018-12-01** — a 90-day tariff truce; US stocks opened the
   next session sharply higher, the S&P 500 "gained 26 points, or 1 percent, to 2,785" and the Dow
   +270 (AP/PBS, 2018-12-03). **Osaka, 2019-06-29** — a truce and a restart of negotiations; the S&P
   rose **0.77%** on 2019-07-01 to a record close of **2,964.33** (Yahoo Finance). Against that, the
   Buenos Aires move died in one session: on **2018-12-04** the administration walked the truce
   claims back ("Tariff Man"), the Dow fell **799 points** and roughly **$800bn** came off the S&P's
   market value (CNN Business, 2018-12-04). Read honestly, the base rate is n=2 for the *bilateral*
   and n=0 for the *summit* — and one of the two was fully round-tripped inside 24 hours. Nothing
   here supports positioning for a "G20 rally."

3. **This summit is explicitly being staged as that bilateral's backdrop — but it is the third
   venue, not the first.** SUPPORTED with a discount. SCMP (2026-07-01/02, citing delegation
   members) reports the US pressed to strip the leaders' text of poverty-reduction, energy-transition
   and gender language and to narrow it to immigration, transnational crime, terrorism, foreign
   investment and "fair trade"; one insider described the December gathering as *"a pretty backdrop
   for a photo of Trump and Xi."* The same reporting places Trump and Xi as scheduled to meet in
   Washington in September, Shenzhen in November and Miami in December. That ladder is exactly why
   Miami's marginal information is thin: by 12/14 the **2026-11-10** truce expiry has already
   resolved or lapsed, and the one clock still ahead — China's market-based tariff-exclusion process,
   **2026-12-31** — is 17 days out and small (see the
   [`china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31`](china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31.md)
   ledger, which corrected that date's scope). Miami is the leftovers venue. `medium` impact is the
   right tier, and the trade-relevant risk stays concentrated on 09-24 and 11/10. One nuance from the
   sibling [`apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18`](apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18.md) research, banked
   the same day: the ladder is **reciprocally conditional** — reporting ties Trump's own APEC
   attendance to whether Xi comes to Doral. So the three venues are not independent draws; a break
   anywhere in the chain re-weights the others, in either direction.

4. **The multilateral half is fracturing, and China is the named dissenter — as of four days ago.**
   SUPPORTED, freshest datapoint in this doc. Treasury's own **G20 Chair's Statement** from the
   Asheville FMCBG (**2026-08-31/09-01**), read direct, carries the footnote that the statement was
   agreed by all G20 members present **except China, which objected to paragraphs 4, 10, 11 and 13**
   — the paragraphs covering energy-trade disruption and supply chains, global imbalances and
   macro-policy, and the sovereign-debt architecture. Around it: **South Africa is disinvited**
   entirely — the first exclusion of a member in the bloc's history, announced 2025-11-27 over the
   Afrikaner dispute and the Johannesburg handover, with **Poland** added to the guest list instead;
   Russia's attendance is unresolved (Kremlin: Putin "may or may not attend"); and PIIE (2026-03-09)
   argued the summit is at risk of "a train wreck" with other members possibly fashioning an
   alternative declaration. For markets this cuts one way only: a fractious summit lowers the odds of
   the *joint deliverable* that is the sole mechanism by which this event could move prices, while
   raising headline variance with no directional bias. That is a reason to stand aside, not to short.

5. **No tracked symbol carries exposure — but the date sits inside this calendar's densest
   corridor, and that is the real hazard.** SUPPORTED, and it is the actionable part. Within ±5 days
   of 12/14 this calendar already tracks **ten** events: `fomc-2026-12-09`, `cpi-2026-12-10`,
   `ercot-data-center-audit-filing-2026-12-10`, `government-funding-deadline-2026-12-11` and
   `cr-expiry-2026-12-11`, `ppi-2026-12-15`, `pjm-capacity-auction-2026-12`,
   `import-export-prices-2026-12-17`, `puct-batch-zero-report-open-meeting-2026-12-17`, and
   `opex-2026-12-18`. On top of that, aggregators (TipRanks, Nasdaq) list **AVGO's Q4 FY26 print for
   2026-12-10** — four days before the summit and inside the same corridor — which
   `earnings-calendar.ts` does **not** currently carry; this lane may not edit that file, so it is
   flagged here rather than added (see Honest limits). The consequence is a discipline, not a trade:
   any 12/14–15 move has at least four better-identified causes, and attributing it to the summit
   would be exactly the attribution error the
   [`china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31`](china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31.md)
   ledger flagged for the ag complex.

6. **Venue risk is live, and it is not a market risk.** MIXED. Trump announced Doral on 2025-09-05;
   the White House says the resort will host "at-cost" with no profit from the State Department or
   any foreign government, the framing that keeps it clear of the Emoluments Clauses. CREW has filed
   a FOIA for the planning records and a State-Department OIG complaint, and the direct precedent is
   Trump's own **2019 reversal** of the Doral pick for the 2020 G7 after public backlash. So a
   relocation is a real possibility. It is graded MIXED because the market consequence is nil: moving
   the summit would change this file's metadata and none of its calls. Tracked as a mechanical watch.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date: estimate — on a schema gap, not on doubt).** No position keyed to this event, and no
prospect of one absent a named bilateral deliverable. Treat 2026-12-14/15 as two things at once:
**(a)** the third and lowest-information Trump–Xi venue of the autumn, arriving after the 11/10 truce
cliff has already resolved and 17 days before the small 12/31 exclusion expiry; and **(b)** a member
of this calendar's densest corridor, whose December risk belongs to `fomc-2026-12-09`,
`cpi-2026-12-10`, the 12/11 CR expiry, `ppi-2026-12-15` and `opex-2026-12-18` — never to the summit.
The multilateral track is fracturing (China's dissent at Asheville, South Africa's exclusion,
Russia's unresolved attendance), which lowers the odds of the only mechanism — a joint deliverable —
by which this could move prices at all. Estimate status widens caution only; nothing here is
date-keyed action, and the `medium` impact tier is affirmed rather than raised.

**Kill switches.**

- *"The summit has no market channel of its own"* dies on a pre-announced Trump–Xi bilateral carrying
  a named deliverable — a signed $30bn reciprocal product list, an export-control carve-out, or an
  explicit extension of China's tariff-exclusion process — published before 2026-12-14. That would
  make Miami the delivery date for the 12/31 question and warrant an impact-tier review.
- *"Third venue, thin marginal information"* dies if the 2026-09-24 Washington visit **and** APEC
  Shenzhen 11/18–19 both fail to occur or produce nothing, loading the whole China agenda onto
  December. Note the reciprocal conditionality the
  [`apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18`](apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18.md) ledger records — Trump's
  APEC attendance is reported as tied to Xi's attendance here — so this switch can trip from either
  end of the ladder.
- *"The 11/10 cliff resolves before this"* dies if the truce expiry lapses unresolved into December —
  at which point this event inherits a live deadline instead of a settled one.
- *"The date is primary-verified"* dies on any fetched US-government page naming different dates or a
  different venue; the same fetch is also the flip trigger if `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` ever gains a slot
  for a host-government diplomatic schedule.
- *"No tracked symbol carries exposure"* dies if AVGO's Q4 FY26 print is registered in
  `earnings-calendar.ts` at 2026-12-10 — the corridor then carries a tracked-name catalyst four days
  ahead of the summit, and the attribution hazard sharpens rather than resolves.
- *"Fractious, low-deliverable summit"* dies if a Leaders' Declaration is agreed by consensus
  including China — more alignment than the 2026-09-01 Asheville statement showed, and a signal the
  bilateral track is warmer than this doc assumes.
- *"Venue holds"* dies on any announced relocation off Trump National Doral (2019 G7 precedent,
  CREW's outstanding State-OIG complaint). Mechanical only — it changes metadata, not the stance.

### Leg 2 — the FOMC collision is structural, arithmetic, and belongs to the monthly edition alone · **SUPPORTED** (this ledger's own finding)

Two publishing rules meet here, and their intersection is fully determined:

- **FHFA publishes on the last Tuesday** of the month — 68 of 68 since 2021.
- **The FOMC meets Tuesday–Wednesday** — every one of the 45 scheduled meetings 2021 → 2026 on the
  Fed's own calendar, plus the 2025 August notation vote, which is not a session.

Therefore an FHFA release day **can** be FOMC **day 1** and can **never** be FOMC **day 2**. Measured
against the Fed's calendar:

| Class | Releases since 2021-01-01 | On FOMC day 1 | On FOMC day 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **All editions** | 68 | **17 (25.0%)** | **0** |
| **Monthly editions** | 48 | **17 (35.4%)** | 0 |
| **Quarterly editions** | 20 | **0** | 0 |

The seventeen: `2021-01-26 · 2021-04-27 · 2021-07-27 · 2022-01-25 · 2022-07-26 · 2023-01-31 ·
2023-07-25 · 2023-10-31 · 2024-01-30 · 2024-04-30 · 2024-07-30 · 2025-01-28 · 2025-07-29 · 2025-10-28 ·
2026-01-27 · 2026-04-28 · 2026-07-28`. **2026-10-27 is the eighteenth**, and the **third** produced by
an October meeting specifically (after 2023-10-31 and 2025-10-28).

**Why the split is perfectly clean, and why it is not a fluke.** The FOMC's late-month Tuesday–Wednesday
meetings fall in January, April/May, July and October/November. FHFA's **quarterly** editions fall in
February, May, August and November — and the May and November quarterly slots land on the *last*
Tuesday, which the FOMC's May and November meetings (early-month, 1st–6th) never occupy. The two
schedules are offset by construction, not by luck, which is why 20 quarterly editions produced zero
collisions in nearly six years.

**This is the one fact that makes this ledger not a copy of its sibling.** `fhfa-hpi-2026-11-24`
correctly described a print nobody reads, sitting in a quiet Tuesday. That description is *accurate for
the quarterly edition and structurally wrong for this one*: a third of monthly editions land inside a
Fed meeting, and this one does.

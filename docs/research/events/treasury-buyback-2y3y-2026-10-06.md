## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event was filed on 2026-09-05 by the
[`treasury-3y-note-2026-10-06`](treasury-3y-note-2026-10-06.md) adjacency sweep, on the specific
warrant that it shares both its **date** and its **tenor** with that auction — a combination that
corrected a claim the September sibling had carried in every row (*"the buyback lever does not reach
this tenor"*). That parent ledger did the calendar work and stopped at the honest edge, naming two
things it could not settle: whether the September 3-Year is actually eligible (*"unknown today; the
list settles it"*), and whether the one same-day precedent, 2025-03-11, means anything (*"n=1, with
an n=1 counter-example"*).

So this session asked a narrower question than "what will happen on 10-06," which nothing can answer:
**does the long-end buyback model this calendar has built — the cap binds, offers arrive at a
multiple of it, Treasury takes everything announced — describe the front end at all?**

**One-line verdict:** no, and not by a small margin. In the 2Y-3Y bucket the cap has been reached once
in eight tries, offers arrive at ~2x rather than ~10x, and the most recent offer print is an all-time
high rather than the collapse the long end just recorded — so every quantitative prior a reader might
carry across from the sibling ledgers is wrong here, in a different direction each time.

### What the conditions support

Nothing directional — the standard answer for a `symbols: []`, `estimate`-dated rates event. What the
conditions do support is three things.

**Stop reading long-end buyback mechanics onto the front end.** This calendar now carries four
long-end buyback ledgers whose shared conclusion is "the cap binds, Treasury takes everything, offers
arrive at a multiple." All three clauses are false in this bucket, and a session that carried them
across would have written a base case of "$4B accepted" against a 1-in-8 base rate. The replacement is
in leg 5: mean **$2.18B**, median **$2.28B**, full fill once in eight.

**Sequence the free checks; three arrive before this operation does.** The order is **2026-09-17**
(the 7Y-10Y operation — the belly's own out-of-sample test, 0/9 full fills) → **2026-10-01** (the
10-20Y operation, which the sibling ledgers want for a different reason) → **2026-10-05 11:00am ET**
(this event's preliminary eligible list, which scores the coupon clause a day early) → **2026-10-06**
(the announced maximum, the final list, and the two totals). Nothing here needs a reaction study.

**Keep the calendar honest about what a basis point deserves.** The 10-01, 10-15, 10-21, 10-27 and
11-04 operations on the same schedule are dated, known, and deliberately **not** proposed as calendar
entries — the rule the [09-10](treasury-buyback-10y20y-2026-09-10.md) and
[10-15](treasury-buyback-10y20y-2026-10-15.md) siblings set is that a *specific warrant* earns an
entry and sector cadence alone does not. This event had one (shared date **and** tenor with an
auction); the 10-08 operation has one (it shares the 30-Year's day); 10-01 does not, and the 10-15
sibling that found it already declined to file it. That call is followed, not re-litigated.

### Honest limits

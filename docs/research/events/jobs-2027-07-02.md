## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This id reached the calendar as a proposal from `sifma-bond-early-close-2027-07-02`'s own initial
research, which derived the date from BLS's scheduling rule and filed it at D-0 to its own event. Three
questions follow, none of them answered by that proposal. **Does the derived date survive an
independent re-derivation, and is the primary retrievable yet?** **What is this print structurally — what
does it feed, and what else is around it?** And the one that matters for a paper-options book: **does a
tier-1 print landing immediately before a 3-day weekend behave differently from an ordinary payroll
Friday?**

**One-line verdict:** the date reproduces 7-for-7 and stays `estimate` because every BLS route 403s;
the structure is sharper than the proposal knew (last payroll before the July FOMC, and the only US
labor or inflation print on the whole 2027 calendar); and the behavioural question resolves to a
specific, significant, **magnitude-only** finding — the reopen after a long-weekend payroll moves ~1.6–2.1×
harder in duration and credit, with no directional edge at all.

Macro-print mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no symbol-keyed
instrument applies (`symbols: []`). Nothing is inherited from the proposing lane; the scheduling rule
was re-implemented from its stated definition and re-validated before use.

- **Corroborating secondary (not a substitute).** A web search on 2026-09-09 returns no 2027 BLS

- **Measured, with Yahoo split/dividend-adjusted daily bars** (the source
  `scripts/research/market-data.mjs` uses), on **SPY, QQQ, TLT, IEF, SHY, LQD, AGG**, 1993–2026. Payroll

  days — so a derived date on a closed session drops out automatically (24 did; see leg 2). Sets are
  split by the calendar gap from the print session to the **next trading session**, read off the bar
  series itself rather than from a holiday calendar.
- **Re-grepped, not cited:** `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md`, `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md`.

### The measurement sets

| Set | Definition | Sessions (SPY / bond ETFs) |
|---|---|---|
| **L** | Payroll Friday whose next trading session is **≥ 4 calendar days** later — a 3-day weekend | 24 / 16 |
| **O** | Every other payroll Friday (next session is the ordinary Monday) | 353 / 254 |
| **S** | September payroll Fridays with an **ordinary** weekend — the confound control for L | 14 / 11 |
| **J** | The exact analogues: July-2 payrolls in Sunday-July-4 years (1993, 1999, 2004, 2010, 2021) | 5 / 3 |

Set **L** is 19 September pre-Labor-Day prints plus the 5 July-2 ones, which is why **S** exists: without
it, "long weekend" and "September" are the same variable.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date reproduces independently — SUPPORTED, and it stays `estimate`.** The rule (release on the
   third Friday following the end of the Sun–Sat reference week containing the 12th) was coded from its
   definition and run over 1993–2027 before being applied. It reproduces **7 of 7** knowns: the four
   `confirmed` entries on this calendar (2026-09-04, 2026-10-02, 2026-11-06, 2026-12-04) and the three
   historical Sunday-July-4 releases (2004-07-02, 2010-07-02, 2021-07-02). June 2027's reference week is
   Sun 06-06 → Sat 06-12; the third Friday after 06-12 is **2027-07-02**. It stays `estimate` on two
   counts: no primary was retrievable (five blocked fetches above), and this lane does not self-confirm.

2. **The rule has a real, visible failure mode, and it does not fire here — SUPPORTED.** Running the
   rule against every trading calendar 1993–2026 leaves **24 derived dates that were not trading days**
   — 1999-01-01, 2010-01-01, 2016-01-01, 2021-01-01; a run of Good Fridays (1994-04-01, 1996-04-05,
   1999-04-02, 2007-04-06, 2010-04-02, 2012-04-06, 2015-04-03, 2021-04-02, 2023-04-07, 2026-04-03); and
   a run of Independence-Day collisions (1997-07-04, 1998-07-03, 2003-07-04, 2008-07-04, 2009-07-03,
   2014-07-04, 2015-07-03, 2020-07-03, 2025-07-04, **2026-07-03**). On those the release still happens —
   BLS shifts it, most visibly in Saturday-July-4 years where the June report moves to the preceding
   **Thursday**. That is exactly the caveat the proposal flagged, now enumerated rather than asserted.
   **2027-07-02 is itself a full NYSE trading day**, so the rule applies unconditionally here.

3. **It is the last payroll before the 2027-07-28 FOMC — SUPPORTED.** The same rule puts the July 2027
   Employment Situation on **2027-08-06** (reference week Sun 07-11 → Sat 07-17), nine days *after* the
   meeting. No payroll prints in between. Proposed in this PR as
   `proposals/jobs-2027-08-06.from-jobs-2027-07-02.json`, `status: estimate` — at D+35 it is outside the
   5-day corridor and proposed anyway, because it is the fact that makes this leg checkable.

4. **It is the only US labor or inflation print on the entire 2027 calendar — SUPPORTED, and it is a
   coverage gap rather than a market fact.** Enumerated this session: **69** canonical `macro-print`
   entries dated 2027 or later, and **zero** CPI, PPI, JOLTS, ADP or ECI among them. Every US hard-data
   series this calendar carries stops at 2026-12 (`cpi-2026-12-10`, `ppi-2026-12-15`, `jolts-2026-12-01`,
   `adp-employment-2026-12-02`); 2027 is FOMC machinery, sentiment/housing surveys, two months of ISM,
   and Japan. **The consequence for this ledger is a refusal, not a claim:** the 07-02 → 07-28 stretch
   looks empty on our board and is not empty in the world — the June 2027 CPI lands inside it. Unlike the
   Employment Situation, CPI publishes no rule this lane can derive a date from, so nothing is proposed
   for it and the gap is recorded instead.

5. **The reopen after a long-weekend payroll moves significantly harder in duration and credit —
   SUPPORTED, on n=16.** Median absolute close-to-close move of the **session after** the print:

   | Instrument | **L: reopen after 3-day weekend** (n=16) | **O: ordinary payroll Friday** (n≈254) | Ratio | Permutation *p* |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | **TLT** | **0.834%** | 0.437% | 1.91× | **0.009** |
   | **LQD** | **0.441%** | 0.215% | 2.05× | **0.004** |
   | **AGG** | **0.251%** | 0.122% | 2.06× | **0.020** |
   | **IEF** | **0.361%** | 0.222% | 1.63× | **0.039** |
   | SPY | 0.696% | 0.436% | 1.60× | 0.066 (n=24) |
   | QQQ | 0.718% | 0.684% | 1.05× | 0.877 (n=19) |
   | SHY | 0.035% | 0.047% | 0.74× | 0.437 |

   *p* is a two-sided permutation test on the difference of medians, 20,000 relabelings. Four of seven
   clear 0.05; the two nulls (QQQ, SHY) and the marginal one (SPY) are reported rather than dropped.

6. **The equity version of that finding is a September seasonal, not a long-weekend effect — REFUTED,
   and this is why set S exists.** SPY's reopen median is 0.718% on September long-weekend payrolls and
   **0.651%** on September payrolls with an ordinary weekend (set S, n=14) — against 0.436% across all
   ordinary payroll Fridays. Almost the whole SPY lift is September, not the holiday. The bond complex
   behaves oppositely under the same control: TLT's September-ordinary reopen is **0.419%**, statistically
   indistinguishable from its 0.437% all-ordinary baseline, so its 0.834% long-weekend figure is not a
   September artefact. **The finding is carried for duration and credit and dropped for equities.**

7. **Direction is not predictable — SUPPORTED as a refusal.** How often the reopen continues the
   print-day sign: TLT **5/16**, IEF **5/16**, LQD **5/16** (all *p*≈0.21 against a 50% null), SPY 10/24
   (p=0.54), QQQ 9/19 (p=1.00), AGG 7/16 (p=0.80). Only **SHY** clears — **2/16**, *p*=**0.0035** — and its
   median reopen move is **0.035%**, so the one significant directional result in the study is
   economically trivial. The honest reading: **the reopen is bigger, and nobody can say which way.** On the
   exact analogues (set J) SPY reversed 3 of 5 and the bond complex 2 of 3 — too small to lean on, and
   consistent with the larger set.

8. **The print day itself is an ordinary session, corroborating the sibling by a different route —
   SUPPORTED.** On the five July-2 analogues SPY's median volume is **1.026×** its trailing-20 median and
   its median absolute move **0.556%**, against 0.665% on ordinary payroll Fridays — participation and
   range both unremarkable. That is the same conclusion
   [`sifma-bond-early-close-2027-07-02`](sifma-bond-early-close-2027-07-02.md) reached from the
   pre-Fourth-session angle (0.949× across NYSE-full-hours pre-Fourth sessions vs 0.591× on half-days),
   reached here from the payroll-Friday angle on a different sample.

9. **Nothing in the house system is calendar- or print-keyed — SUPPORTED, re-verified not inherited.** A
   grep of `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md` for

### What the conditions support

Nothing directional, and one specific *shape* of caution that is new to this family. The measured
object is not the 08:30 print — it is the four calendar days of exposure a 2027-07-02 position carries
before the tape reopens on 2027-07-06. Two consequences follow for a paper-options book, both
`estimate`-labelled and neither an entry: a long option held through this window pays **four** calendar
days of decay for one session of realized move, and the reopen it is waiting for is measurably wider
(TLT ~1.9×, LQD ~2.1×) but directionally a coin flip. That combination is unfavourable to a directional
holder in both legs at once, and it is *not* an argument for selling the reopen's volatility either,
since the width is the part that is significant. The house answer is guard-shaped: no rate-duration
paper is sized across 2027-07-02 → 2027-07-06 on the strength of the print.

### Honest limits

D-296, and the biggest one is stated in the date field: **the primary is unretrievable**, so a 7-for-7
derivation stands in for a published schedule. **n=16** for the load-bearing bond measurement — four
significant *p*-values on sixteen observations, thirteen of them September, is a direction with support,
not an estimate to size against; the SPY null (leg 6) is exactly what a small confounded sample looks
like when the control is finally run, and it is the reason legs 5 and 7 are stated separately. The
permutation tests are two-sided on medians and make no correction for testing seven instruments — with
seven tests at α=0.05 one false positive is expected, so the cluster of four (rather than any single one)
is the evidence. No consensus, whisper or projection for June 2027 payrolls exists this far out and none
will for many months. No price instruments run (macro-print mode carries none). The FOMC dates this leans
on (2027-07-28, 2027-09-15) are themselves `estimate`. And leg 4's coverage gap means the next several
pulses may be reading a corridor that looks emptier than it is.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`, derived and 7-for-7 validated, primary 403 on 2026-09-09).** Treat
2027-07-02 08:30 ET as a high-impact known-date variance event with an unusual exposure profile: it is
the last payroll before the **2027-07-28** FOMC, the only US labor or inflation print this calendar
carries in all of 2027, and it lands into a 3-day weekend on a session where the recommended cash-bond
tape shuts at 14:00 while equities run full hours. **Stand aside on every horizon.** No directional
macro position, and specifically **no rate-duration paper carried across 2027-07-02 → 2027-07-06**: the
measured reopen is 1.6–2.1× a normal post-payroll session in TLT/LQD/AGG/IEF (*p*=0.004–0.039, n=16)
with no directional edge whatever (5 of 16 continuation, *p*=0.21), which penalises a directional holder
on both the decay and the outcome. The equity form of the same statistic is **refuted** by its September
control and is not carried. The empty 07-02 → 07-28 corridor is read as a **coverage gap in our own
calendar**, never as a quiet tape. Every line here is `estimate`-labelled and none licenses an entry.

**Kill switches:**

- **bls.gov's 2027 schedule becomes retrievable and names a date other than 2027-07-02** — kills the
  whole document; the structure legs (3, 4) and every measurement keyed to a long weekend go with it.
  Re-attempt the four blocked routes on every pulse; this is the first thing each pulse does.
- **A US CPI or PPI entry dated 2027-07-03 … 2027-07-27 lands on this calendar** — leg 4's "only print
  in 2027" claim narrows and the month-horizon refusal is satisfied rather than falsified. Route the
  finding, do not defend the sentence.
- **Fewer than 3 of TLT/IEF/LQD/AGG exceed their ordinary-payroll-Friday reopen medians (0.437% /
  0.222% / 0.215% / 0.122%) on 2027-07-06** — the leg-5 amplification failed its one out-of-sample
  observation. Registered as **FT-jobs-2027-07-02-1**.
- **SHY's 2027-07-06 move carries the same sign as its 2027-07-02 move** — the one significant
  directional result in the study (2/16, *p*=0.0035) fails out of sample. Registered as
  **FT-jobs-2027-07-02-2**.
- **NYSE publishes a 2027-07-02 equity early close, or SIFMA withdraws its 07-02 recommendation** — the
  session-structure premise this ledger inherits from
  [`sifma-bond-early-close-2027-07-02`](sifma-bond-early-close-2027-07-02.md) changes and both must be
  re-read together.
- **The Fed path resets between now and 2027-07** — nothing in this document fixes the *sign* of a
  reaction, and nothing should try to at D-296; a stance that starts predicting direction has drifted.

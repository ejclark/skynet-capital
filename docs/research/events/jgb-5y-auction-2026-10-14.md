### Conviction legs, tested

**1. The maturity rule is two exact conventions with a dated phase shift, not one loose regularity —
SUPPORTED and materially sharpened. This is the leg the entry exists for.** The 11-12 sibling
established that the 5-year matures on the 20th of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec in **103 of 103** auctions and
that each auction quarter maps to one maturity in **34 of 35**, calling the miss "the sole exception
2018Q1, the window's own first … the same shape as the 10-year's issue-350 exception" — i.e. a
truncation artifact. Classifying every auction-to-auction transition into **opening** (maturity
differs from the previous auction's), **coupon-reset** (same maturity, new issue number) and
**reopening** (both unchanged) shows it is not:

| Window | Rule | Score | Non-conforming openings | Quarters carrying one maturity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2010Q1 – 2018Q1** | the **THIRD** auction of the quarter opens | **33 / 33** | first-of-quarter openings: **0 / 33** | 34 / 67 (a quarter spans two lines by design) |
| **2018Q2** | *neither* — issue 135 run a third time | the phase shift | — | 1 / 1 |
| **2018Q3 – 2026Q3** | the **FIRST** auction of the quarter opens | **33 / 33** | non-first-of-quarter openings: **0 / 66** | **33 / 33** |

Under the old convention the new maturity arrived on the March / June / September / December
auction, so a quarter naturally carried **two** maturities and "one maturity per quarter" is
meaningless there — which is exactly why the sibling's 34-of-35 count reads as a near-miss rather
than as two clean regimes. **2018Q1 is not the window's ragged edge; it is the last quarter of the
older convention**, and 2018Q2 is the quarter MOF used to shift the phase, running issue 135 across
all three auctions and opening nothing. Restricted to the convention actually in force, the rule is
**33 of 33 with no exceptions at all**.

That both strengthens and dates the prediction. **2026-10-14 is the first auction of 2026Q4, so it
opens 2031-09-20** — and the Q4 chain says it more simply than the quarter map does: the Q4 opening
has matured on **20 September of the year five years out in 8 of 8** years since the convention
changed (2023-09-20, 2024-09-20, 2025-09-20, 2026-09-20, 2027-09-20, 2028-09-20, 2029-09-20,
2030-09-20). Registered as `FT-jgb-5y-auction-2026-10-14-1`; the honest forward version of the
*convention* claim, which one print cannot test, is `FT-jgb-5y-auction-2026-10-14-5`.

**2. This auction's issue number IS predictable, and the sibling's refusal to predict one does not
generalise — SUPPORTED.** The 11-12 ledger declined to call its issue number and was right to: across
104 transitions, **53 opened a new number against 51 reopenings**, nine maturity dates carry two
numbers and five carry three, and the best single-threshold rule on the yield-minus-coupon gap reaches
**71.2%**. But that fork is a property of **non-opening** auctions. An opening carries a new number by
construction, and the step from the previous auction is **+1 in 33 of 33** post-2018Q3 — the only two
+2 steps since 2010 (2014-09-11, 2014-12-16) both sit in the older convention. 2026-11-12 is the
*second* auction of its quarter, squarely on the 50/19 reopen-vs-coupon-reset fork; **2026-10-14 is
not on that fork at all.** Issue 187 (2026-08-18 and 09-08, maturity 2031-06-20, coupon 2.2%) is the
incumbent, so **10-14 settles as issue 188**. MOF writes the number into the calendar row on
settlement — `2609e.htm` reads `5-year (187)` — giving a cheap, dated falsifier. Registered as
`FT-jgb-5y-auction-2026-10-14-2`. **The coupon is deliberately not predicted**: it is set at
announcement against the then-prevailing yield (across 33 post-2018Q3 openings the auction's own
average yield lands within **±0.093pp** of the coupon in the last 16, mean **−0.087pp** overall), so
calling it is a rate forecast wearing a convention's clothes.

### Honest limits

- **A convention is not a mechanism, and this one has changed once inside the available history.**
  33 of 33 is a strong regularity, not a rule MOF has published; 2018Q2 is proof it can shift the
  phase without announcing anything. That is why the convention claim is scored forward on 2027Q1
  (`FT-jgb-5y-auction-2026-10-14-5`) rather than on this print.
- **The pre-2010 classification is not used and would not support the same claim.** Full history
  gives 35 of 107 first-of-quarter openings, because 2000–2009 ran neither convention cleanly. Every
  regime statement above is deliberately bounded to 2010Q1 onward.
- **The opening / coupon-reset / reopening classification is derived, not sourced.** MOF publishes
  issue numbers and maturity dates, not a taxonomy; the three classes are this session's arithmetic
  on consecutive rows, and a mis-keyed workbook row would silently mis-class one auction.
- **Leg 4's live-regime test is n = 11 against 22.** It is powered to detect only a large effect, so
  "no adjustment warranted" means "no adjustment is supported", not "openings are identical". The
  2018+ p = 0.041 is reported precisely so a later session does not rediscover it and treat it as new.
- **Leg 7's reference class is n = 8.** It is used as a *description* of what this auction's
  predecessors printed and as a refusal of the pre-BoJ story, never as a distribution to size against.
- **The BoJ meeting dates are meeting FIRST days**, parsed from the schedule tables where the second
  day carries no month token; a two-day meeting decides on the following day. Every gap quoted is
  auction-to-meeting-start, consistently, so the comparisons hold even though the labels run one day
  early. The Outlook Report flag is unreliable before 2016 (the pre-2016 tables carry a different
  column layout) and is used only from 2016 on, where it recovers exactly the four quarterly meetings.
- **Leg 6 changes a percentile, not a level.** The curve is where it is; what is refuted is the claim
  that its position is extreme. The wider sample has its own composition problem in the other
  direction — 62% of it predates 2007 — which is the point: **no single window is neutral, and a
  percentile should never be quoted without its span.**
- **The 40-year's inception is inferred from the series' first observation** (2007-11-06 in this
  file), not read off an MOF statement about when the tenor began trading.
- **The two most recent auctions are read off MOF result pages, not the workbook**, which stops at
  2026-07-09. Parsed cell-by-cell against the pages' own labels; not yet reconciled against a later
  workbook release. They reproduce the 11-12 sibling's figures exactly.
- **The hedge-gap numbers are approximations** — short-rate differential, cross-currency basis
  excluded — and leg 10's own finding is that the quantity is unstable over two sessions. It is

- **The auction result does not exist**, and neither does its size, its coupon or its issue number.

- **VIX and USD/JPY are Yahoo, i.e. secondary**, and both 09-10 readings are intraday. MOF's par

- **The date and the size are both `estimate`.** MOF is a primary and the date is not in doubt; the

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries 2026-09-30 as the G20 Trade Ministerial in Milwaukee,
proposed hours earlier by the sibling Bangkok research on the theory that "the trade track is closer

stated agenda is the caption of an open, undetermined Section 301 docket against seven fellow G20
members, which is a genuine dated risk that will be delivered by Federal Register notice on USTR's
own clock, not by anything said in Wisconsin.

3. **The docket is past its own target, undetermined, and its statutory ceiling is 2027-03-11.**

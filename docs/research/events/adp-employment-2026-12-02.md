## Initial research

### The question, plainly

What should be expected from the November 2026 ADP National Employment Report on 2026-12-02, how will
the market treat it, and which tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN) carry
meaningful sensitivity — given that the generic "what is ADP" work is banked across three sibling
editions ([09-02](adp-employment-2026-09-02.md), closed out; [09-30](adp-employment-2026-09-30.md);
[11-04](adp-employment-2026-11-04.md)) and does not need repeating, and given that this edition's
distinguishing feature is that it is the **last ADP before the year's dot plot**, on a morning with
nothing else scheduled, inside a Fed blackout?

**One-line verdict:** the position of this print flatters it and the measurement deflates it — it is
the year's **cleanest attribution window** for an ADP release and therefore worth *measuring*, but
2026's own nine ADP release days are indistinguishable from ordinary sessions and the three prior
December editions add nothing, so the call is **stand aside** with a registered forward test rather
than a forecast.

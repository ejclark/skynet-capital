## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This id reached the calendar as one `EST:` proposal from
[`nahb-hmi-2026-11-17`](nahb-hmi-2026-11-17.md)'s adjacency sweep, and that proposal was unusually
explicit about what it was *not* asking. It carried forward two settled facts so this lane would not
re-derive them — the sentiment channel is measured null, and the stacked-morning pre-open belongs to
the 09:15 G.17 — and then named the one open question:

> the open question this id actually carries is NOT the tape — it is whether a private residential
> print acquires information value precisely when the federal ones go dark, which nobody here has
> measured and which only a realized lapse can test.

**Does the publisher's own calendar carry the date; did NAHB actually publish when the federal
residential machine stopped; and does a print acquire measurable value inside an information
vacuum?**

**One-line verdict:** the date is NAHB's own on four independent checks and promotes to `confirmed`;
the **availability** claim is **SUPPORTED** on a dated record, and its usual justification is
**wrong** — the Fed is self-funded and went dark anyway; and the **information-value** claim is
**NOT SUPPORTED and not testable** on the sample a single lapse provides, which is why it is retired
here rather than left open.

**Metrics, pre-specified.** Identical to the sibling by design, so the reproduction is meaningful:

20260906, matched window **2020-01-02 → 2026-09-04** (1,678 sessions).

### Leg 3 — the reason for that immunity — **the usual explanation is REFUTED**

The corridor's framing, inherited from the [FHFA ledger](fhfa-hpi-2026-12-29.md), rests on funding
status: FHFA's assessment income sits outside appropriated money (12 U.S.C. § 4516(f)), so it
publishes. True of FHFA and true of NAHB, but it is **not the mechanism**, and the Federal Reserve
proves it. The Fed is self-funded on exactly the same logic — and its **G.17 Industrial Production**
release went dark anyway. From the Board's own announcements feed, in sequence:

| Date | Feed item |
|---|---|
| 2025-09-16 | G.17 Data for August 2025 are now available |
| 2025-10-10 | **"G.17 October 17th release delayed"** |
| 2025-11-14 | **"G.17 November 18th release delayed"** |
| 2025-12-03 | G.17 Data for **September** 2025 are now available *(78 days late)* |
| 2025-12-23 | G.17 Data for **October and November** 2025 are now available *(merged)* |
| 2026-01-16 | G.17 Data for December 2025 are now available *(normal cadence resumes)* |

Two deferrals and one merged release — the **identical defer-and-merge shape** the 12-17 lane
measured for Census across three lapses, from an agency whose own funding was never in question.
G.17's inputs are federal source data; when those stopped, the release stopped. NAHB's input is a
survey of its own members. **Input independence, not funding independence, is what decides who
publishes in a lapse** — and that is the load-bearing correction this ledger contributes to the
corridor.

### Leg 4 — a private print acquires information value in the vacuum — **NOT SUPPORTED, and not testable here**

Two tests, one underpowered by construction and one adequately powered.

**The direct test is hopeless and it is worth stating why rather than quoting its p-value.** The
blackout window (2025-10-01 → 2026-01-09) holds **70** trading sessions, of which exactly **three**
are HMI days. ITB open→close on them reads **0.6957% · 1.0503% · 0.8530%**, median **0.8530%**,
against a **0.8880%** blackout baseline (n=67), **p=1.00**; XHB **0.8570% vs 0.7963%**, p=0.96. And
HMI-in-blackout against HMI-elsewhere is **0.8530% vs 0.9604%** (p=0.82). The arithmetic ceiling:
under the null, the probability all three land above the baseline median is **0.125** — the most
extreme arrangement the sample admits is still not significant. No design on three observations can
answer this.

**The regime test can be answered, and it says no.** If a private print acquired value in a data
vacuum, the cheapest place for it to show is elevated homebuilder movement across the vacuum itself.
It is not there: over the **70** blackout sessions, ITB open→close **0.8705% vs 0.9672%** (p=**0.45**),
XHB **0.8266% vs 0.8735%** (p=0.72), SPY **0.4060% vs 0.4704%** (p=0.36) — if anything *quieter*, and
null on all three. The 114 dark days were an ordinary stretch of tape for homebuilders.

Every cell lands within a thousandth of the sibling's, on a fresh pull. The HMI's own window remains
untouched by stacking (ITB open→close 0.8503% vs 0.9894%, **p=0.49**). **2026-12-16 is in the stacked
class** — and that is the December anomaly worth flagging: only **1 of 6** elapsed December HMI days
(2024-12-17) carried a G.17, so this edition is the unusual one, not the typical one.

### Honest limits

- **The blackout n is three, and no amount of framing fixes that.** Leg 4's negative is a statement
  about power, not about the world. It is registered as `-3` with the pooling condition that would
  make it answerable, rather than being reported as a finding.
- **The G.17 evidence is the Fed's own announcements, not an audit.** The feed says "delayed" and
  shows a merged release; this session did not obtain a Fed statement attributing the delay to
  Census inputs specifically. The inference — federal inputs, therefore federal exposure — is the
  most natural reading of the sequence and is stated as an inference.
- **December's calendar-safety rests on seven rows.** NAHB's page is archived from 2020 forward;
  earlier December practice is unverified, and the Juneteenth precedent shows a scheduled date is a
  publisher's commitment, not a promise the tape is open.
- **Leg 5 is a reproduction, not an independent finding.** It shares the sibling's post-hoc split and
  inherits its caution; the value here is that 2026-12-16 is a second, out-of-sample session for it.
- **Magnitude only, never direction.** Every metric is absolute. Whether an HMI *surprise* moves
  builders directionally is still untested, for the same reason the sibling gave: no machine-readable
  history of HMI prints against consensus was reachable.
- **The cliff is `estimate`.** `cr-expiry-2026-12-11` is `NEWS:`-sourced and five days ahead of this
  print. Everything said here about a lapse is conditional; estimates widen caution and license
  nothing.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stand aside on every horizon, at `confirmed`.** The tape case was settled by the proposing lane and
reproduced here on an independent pull; the case for keeping the event is informational and now has
one fact behind it that no other residential ledger carries: **NAHB published 4 for 4 through a
114-day federal residential blackout, while a self-funded federal agency's own release deferred
twice and merged two months.** That reframes the corridor's lapse reasoning from a funding argument
to an input argument, and it is the reason this ledger exists.

**What kills this stance:**

- **The structure** — the December 2026 HMI not publishing 2026-12-16 at 10:00. That is the
  availability claim failing on the instance and costs the `IR:`/`confirmed` promotion its footing.
  The disambiguation is built in: a missing 12-17 with a present 12-16 is Census exposure behaving
  as measured; a missing 12-16 is the publisher itself. Registered as `-1`.
- **The attribution** — SPY's 2026-12-16 overnight gap at or below **0.3275%**. An out-of-sample
  failure on a stacked morning says the sibling's 34-day split was noise. Registered as `-2`.
- **The retirement** — a post-2026-12-11 lapse spanning three or more further HMI editions, which
  would make Leg 4 answerable for the first time and un-retire the hypothesis. Registered as `-3`.
- **The mechanism** — any dated record of NAHB delaying, merging or suspending an HMI edition during
  a funding lapse, which would break the input-independence argument outright. Zero such instances in
  80 elapsed publisher dates; the 09-16, 10-19 and 11-17 editions each test it before this one.
- **The playbook** — a macro- or housing-keyed entry landing in `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md`
  before 2026-12-16, which would give a `symbols: []` print something to attach to.

Estimates only widen caution; `confirmed` licenses nothing on its own. The predictions above are
registered in [`forward-tests/nahb-hmi-2026-12-16.md`](../forward-tests/nahb-hmi-2026-12-16.md).

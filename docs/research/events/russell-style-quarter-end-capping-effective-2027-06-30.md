   **style capping cut-off = that Monday minus 12 calendar days**. Applied to 2026 it returns 11 Mar/23 Mar,
   17 Jun/29 Jun, 9 Sep/21 Sep and 2 Dec/14 Dec — all four published columns, to the day, including the December one

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Sep 18 is the quarter's **triple-witching** expiration — the third Friday of March/June/September/December, when stock-index **futures**, stock-index **options**, and single-stock **options** all expire in the same session. That makes it structurally larger than a routine monthly opex like Aug 21: vendor volume comparisons put monthly-opex notional at ~$1–2T versus ~$4–5T on a quarterly witching (roughly 3x the gamma rolling off at once), and the December 2025 witching set an all-time record at ~$7.1T notional for scale. The generic mechanic is the same as any opex — dealer gamma pins price into expiration, then a documented "unpin" of realized volatility in the days after as that hedging cushion rolls off — but on a witching it is magnified, and it carries **two** microstructure distortions instead of one: standard 3rd-Friday index options (SPX) **AM-settle** via the Special Opening Quotation (SET) at Friday's *open*, while single-stock options and the coincident **S&P quarterly index rebalance** land at the *close*. So both the Friday open and the Friday close are elevated-distortion windows, not just the close. **No house playbook (S1/S2/E1/S3/S4/G1) is opex-keyed** — none targets expiration mechanics, so nothing here licenses a trade. The one fact worth carrying forward is **timing**: this witching lands **2 days after the Sep-16 FOMC** (decision + SEP + dot plot), so the quarter's heaviest gamma rolls off into a tape still digesting the post-FOMC repricing. All positioning figures are vendor/literature estimates that will move — the *date* is the only confirmed, deterministic fact here.

## Initial research

**The question.** How does a quarterly triple-witching expiration differ mechanically from a routine monthly opex (structural scale, settlement mechanics, the pin-then-unpin pattern), does anything about *this particular* Sep 2026 witching stand out, and how does it interact with the Sep-16 FOMC landing 2 trading days before it?

**One-line verdict:** the triple-witching mechanics are real, well-documented, and materially larger than a monthly opex, and the FOMC-then-witching sequence is a genuine timing feature — but no house playbook is opex-keyed and no same-week positioning data is meaningful this far out, so the honest output is a guard (mind both the Friday open and close; watch the post-FOMC gamma roll-off), not a trade.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and cycle type are right — SUPPORTED.** OCC's expiration-calendar convention (3rd Friday of the quarter-ending month) makes Sep 18, 2026 a quarterly **triple-witching** expiration; multiple independent public sources (OptionAlpha, TradeStation, Wikipedia's triple-witching entry, LiteFinance) agree the 2026 witchings are Mar 20, Jun (18, moved off Fri Jun 19 for the Juneteenth holiday), **Sep 18**, and **Dec 18**. Sep 18 is a normal Friday (no holiday adjacency), matching this calendar's `impact: high` coding versus the `impact: medium` monthlies (Aug 21, Oct 16, Nov 20).

2. **A triple-witching is structurally ~3x a monthly opex — SUPPORTED (estimate/literature).** On triple-witching, stock-index futures + stock-index options + single-stock options expire together (single-stock options were folded onto the quarterly dates in 2002, which is why the day is sometimes called "quadruple witching" when single-stock futures are counted). Vendor/press comparisons put monthly-opex notional at ~$1–2T versus ~$4–5T on a quarterly witching; the Dec-2025 witching set a record ~$7.1T notional (reported ~20% above Dec-2024, ~10.2% of Russell 3000 market cap). **Caveat:** these are vendor/press aggregate figures, not OCC primary open-interest data or a house-validated stat — directional scale, not a settled number.

3. **Two settlement windows, not one — SUPPORTED (Cboe mechanics).** This is the mechanical feature that separates a witching from the monthly exemplar. Standard 3rd-Friday **index** options (SPX and the like) are **AM-settled**: the exercise-settlement value (Cboe symbol "SET") is struck from opening prices Friday morning, so index-option flows concentrate at the *open*. Single-stock options and PM-settled products settle at the 4:00pm *close*, where they stack with the coincident index-rebalance market-on-close (leg 4). Cboe moved standard SPX to AM-settlement precisely because PM close-settled unwinds had created intense closing-print volatility. Net: a witching has an elevated-distortion window on **both** ends of the session, where a monthly opex (single-stock heavy) is mostly a closing-auction story.

4. **The S&P quarterly rebalance coincides with this witching — SUPPORTED (index-provider convention).** S&P Dow Jones Indices' quarterly share/float rebalance is effective at the close of the third Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec — i.e., **the same session as this witching**. Passive funds tracking the index must execute at that closing print, adding mandatory market-on-close pressure on top of expiring stock-option hedges. Reported effect: volume commonly 2–3x average and volatility peaking in the final hour (3:00–4:00pm ET). This is why the *close* on a witching Friday is a materially bigger imbalance event than on a monthly opex. (Directional color, not a house stat.)

5. **Gamma pin-into-close then post-witching unpin is real and magnified here — SUPPORTED (general literature, not house-tested).** In a positive-gamma regime, dealers hedge by buying dips and selling rallies in progressively larger size into expiration, compressing realized vol and pinning price toward high-gamma strikes; once that gamma expires the stabilizing floor is removed ("unpin") and realized vol tends to rise — options-analytics literature reports the Monday/Tuesday after a triple-witching as statistically elevated-vol days precisely because the largest block of dealer gamma on the board rolls off at once (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch; the same mechanism CBOE documents at the 0DTE extreme). Because a witching concentrates far more open interest than a monthly, both the pin *and* the unpin are magnified versus the Aug-21 exemplar. **Caveat:** this is vendor-blog / one-paper sourced, not a red-teamed instrument run against our tracked names — directionally credible, not house-proven, and no opex-shaped instrument exists in this repo to confirm it.

6. **The FOMC-then-witching sequence is a real, load-bearing timing feature — SUPPORTED.** This witching lands **2 trading days after the Sep-16 FOMC** decision (meeting Sep 15–16, carrying the SEP and dot plot — confirmed on this calendar), with Retail Sales (Aug data) also on Sep 16 and the 10Y TIPS auction on Sep 17, the day before. Mechanically: the quarter's heaviest gamma roll-off arrives while the tape is still repricing rates off a dot-plot day — the pinning cushion that can dampen a post-FOMC move is exactly what expires Friday. This is a caution about *when* concentrated positioning unwinds relative to a fresh macro catalyst, not a directional call on the FOMC or the tape. (The prior CPI Sep 11 / PPI Sep 10 / jobs Sep 4 prints are all before the FOMC and already digested by witching week.)

7. **No tracked-name earnings are adjacent — SUPPORTED.** NVDA (Aug 26) and MRVL (Aug 27) print well before; AVGO is est. Sep 2 (`NEWS`), also before. The next mega-cap cluster (MSFT Oct 27, GOOG/META Oct 28, AAPL/AMZN Oct 29 — all `estimate`, 8-K cadence) and CRWV (est. Nov 10) are more than a month after this witching. So unlike the Aug-21 opex (5 days before NVDA), this witching has **no single-name earnings adjacency** — its adjacency is purely macro (the FOMC) and structural (the rebalance).

8. **No house playbook is opex-keyed — SUPPORTED.** Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md`: S1/G1 are pre-print run-up plays (earnings-dated), S2 is the never-hold-the-print guard (earnings-dated), E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution rule, S3 is a reaction-day fade (earnings-dated, blocked on shorting), S4 is an overnight-vs-buy-and-hold structural note. None reference options expiration. The witching sharpens two indirect interactions: E1's "don't trade the open" logic gains a *second* reason on a witching day (SET AM-settlement makes the open unusually distorted, not just the close), and S4's kept "prefer close-side executions" guidance runs into the rebalance/stock-option MOC — i.e., the exact MOC/MOO slippage risk already named in the sweep's blocked CRWV overnight-carry item, amplified. **What the conditions support.** Nothing directional — no macro/mechanics playbook exists in the house set for opex, witching included.

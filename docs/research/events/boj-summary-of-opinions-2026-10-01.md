### Honest limits

**Leg 4 is a bound, not a proof of power** — a bootstrap p97.5 on the median of n draws is a rough
detection floor, not a formal power curve, and the Tankan is a *proxy* for "a release that matters"
rather than a calibrated effect size; nobody has established what the Tankan's true opening-gap
effect is, only that this test does not resolve it. **Leg 6 is n=4 and confounded** — the four
collision years are exactly 2021-2024, so a period effect and a Tankan effect are inseparable in

policy regimes without conditioning on any of them. **The bootstrap draws i.i.d.**, ignoring
volatility clustering; clustering makes extreme cohort medians *more* likely by chance, so leg 7's

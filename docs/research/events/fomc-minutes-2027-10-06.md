### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is FED-primary, correctly `estimate`, and its September sub-rule is 19 of 19 — the only
   perfect meeting month in the record.** SUPPORTED, and this is the durable result. The page states the
   convention verbatim — *"The minutes of regularly scheduled meetings are released three weeks after the
   date of the policy decision"* — and its 2027 panel reads verbatim `January 26-27 | March 16-17* |
   April 27-28 | June 8-9* | July 27-28 | September 14-15* | October 26-27 | December 7-8*`. 2027-09-15
   is a Wednesday and +21 days is **Wednesday 2027-10-06** (weekday verified, not assumed). Across all
   **157** releases 2007–2026, **143 sit at exactly D+21**; the 14 exceptions run D+19 to D+24 and every
   scheduled one carries a US market holiday inside the release week. By **meeting** month those
   exceptions are **November ×6, December ×4, June ×2, October ×1**, plus the **2020-03-15 unscheduled**
   meeting at D+24 — Thanksgiving, Christmas/New Year and July 4, in both directions (2018-06-13 → 07-05
   pushed **back** to D+22; 2024-11-07 → 11-26 pulled **forward** to D+19). **September is untouched, and
   it cannot be touched.** All nineteen September-meeting releases 2007–2025 land between **October 4 and
   October 17**, and October contains **no US market holiday in any year** — nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars,
   fetched today, gives the full 2027 NYSE set as January 1, January 18, February 15, March 26, May 31,
   June 18, July 5, September 6, November 25 and December 24, with nothing in October. This is strictly
   stronger than the July sibling's 13-of-13: same mechanism, but on a series running the **full nineteen
   years** rather than starting in 2013. **Status stays `estimate`** — the same page states every 2027
   date is *"tentative until confirmed at the meeting immediately preceding it"*, so the trigger is the
   **2027-07-27/28** meeting, not a better fetch. `newsevents/2027-october.htm` **404s** (the month page
   carrying the 2:00 p.m. time does not exist yet) and is recorded in `probe-ref`.

2. **The family's index null replicates — and the implied-vol test the siblings never ran looks like it
   breaks it.** SUPPORTED as a replication, MIXED as a finding. Across this session's 157 releases,
   minutes-day |S&P| runs **0.747%** mean / **0.451%** median against a **0.797%** / **0.523%**
   unconditional baseline over 4,950 sessions — reproducing [`08-18`](fomc-minutes-2027-08-18.md) to three
   decimals and [`06-30`](fomc-minutes-2027-06-30.md)'s conclusion from a third construction. **The index
   is ordinary. Implied vol, on first pass, is not:**

   | Measure | Minutes days (n=157) | Universe (n=4,950) |
   |---|---|---|
   | VIX closes lower | **101 (64.3%)** | 54.3% |
   | VIX-level-matched expectation | **85.5** | — |
   | Permutation P(≥101) | **0.0074** | — |
   | Mean ΔVIX | **−0.282 pt** | −0.002 pt |

   The level match matters: VIX mean-reverts, so a subgroup sampled at elevated levels would show more
   down-days for free. Matching each minutes day to sessions whose *prior* VIX was within ±15% removes
   that, and the gap survives. Leg 3 is where it stops surviving.

3. **The vol edge does not survive its own controls. This leg is the document's central refusal.**
   REFUTED. A VIX decline is mostly the mechanical shadow of an S&P rise, so the claim was residualised:
   OLS on all 4,950 sessions of

   ```
   ΔVIX = 0.4172 − 1.3512·r + 0.00159·(r × VIX_prev) − 0.01845·VIX_prev        (r = S&P % return)
   ```

   leaves the part of the day's vol move the index move does **not** explain. On that measure minutes days
   are worth **−0.16 VIX pt**, negative on 102 of 157, permutation **p = 0.035** — both iid and stratified
   within ±30 trading sessions. Then the placebo run:

   | Offset | D−10 | D−3 | D−2 | D−1 | **D0** | D+1 | **D+2** | D+3 | D+10 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Residual mean (pt) | −0.179 | −0.115 | +0.134 | +0.097 | **−0.160** | +0.143 | **−0.257** | +0.303 | −0.164 |

   **Three of the twenty offsets D−10…D+10 are at least as negative as the release day itself, and D+2 is
   larger.** A p = 0.035 that ordinary neighbouring sessions reproduce one time in seven is not an edge;
   it is the spread of a noisy measure. **Recorded as MIXED and not banked.** One partial check, reported
   because it does *not* exonerate the release: the decline is intraday, not overnight (minutes days run
   **−0.338 pt** open-to-close against **+0.056 pt** prior-close-to-open; the universe runs −0.146 / +0.146).
   That narrows it to the session but **cannot separate the 4.5 hours before the 14:00 release from the two
   after** — see *Honest limits*.

4. **September-meeting minutes look spectacular and are the same day counted twice.** REFUTED, and this is
   the trap a later session would otherwise fall into. Scanned by meeting month, September is the
   **maximum of twelve** on the vol measure — VIX down **17 of 19** (0.895; next is March at 0.750) — and
   the S&P is up **16 of 19**. The local controls all clear:

   | Control | Result |
   |---|---|
   | Level-matched expectation | **10.63** of 19 · P(≥17) = **0.0019** |
   | Matched calendar window (all Oct 4–17 sessions, n=171, release days removed) | 53.8% down, mean **+0.076** pt · P(≥17) = **0.0008** |
   | Surrounding ±5 sessions (n=190) | 51.05% down; D−5…D−1 mean **+0.003**, D+1…D+5 mean **+0.165** |
   | VIX-down count at each offset D−5…D+5 | 11 · 10 · 11 · 10 · 10 · **[17]** · 9 · 11 · 8 · 9 · 8 |

   So it is not October seasonality, not VIX mean reversion, and not a local downtrend — the release day
   itself is the outlier. **And it is still not a vol result.** Residualised on the same day's index move,
   the September sign collapses from 17 of 19 to **12 of 19** (base 54.1%), residual mean −0.242 at
   **p = 0.128** stratified, and only **1 of 19** residuals clears **−1.0 pt** against an **8.85%** base
   rate (3 of 19 clear −0.5 pt against 24.2%). **September-meeting minutes days are exactly ordinary on
   the vol-specific measure.** What is left is a *directional* claim about the index, and it is a scan:
   **P(≥16 of 19 up) = 0.0067** raw against a 54.4% base, **0.081** after the twelve-way month correction
   it was found by. The conjunction form — S&P up **and** VIX down, **15 of 19** against a 44.4% base —
   gives 0.0024 raw and **0.028** after ×12, which clears Bonferroni only if one forgets it is the *same*
   subgroup counted a third time after the first two were inspected. **Registered as
   FT-fomc-minutes-2027-10-06-2 rather than banked.** The one huge outlier, 2008-10-07 (−5.74%), pulls the

### Honest limits

**The measurement window is wrong and cannot be fixed with these series.** The minutes drop at 14:00 ET;
every number here is close-to-close, so roughly 4.5 of the session's 6.5 hours precede the release. Leg
3's overnight/intraday split narrows the move to the session but stops there — CBOE's daily files carry no
intraday bars, and every sibling ledger shares this limitation. A study with 14:00-to-close prints could
overturn leg 3 in either direction. **Leg 4's subgroup is n = 19 and was found by a twelve-way scan**; the
correction is reported rather than buried, and the conjunction form is flagged as a third look at the same
days rather than presented as independent confirmation. **Leg 3's regression is a linear-plus-interaction
fit on twenty years including 2008 and 2020**; a richer vol model might leave a different residual, though
the placebo test does not depend on the fit being right — it compares the release day to its own
neighbours on the same measure. **Leg 5's era comparison is confounded beyond repair** and is recorded only
so it is not re-run. The **14:00 ET release time is convention**, not sourced for this release: the Board's
2027 October month page 404s. And the whole document rests on a meeting — **2027-09-14/15** — that is
tentative by the Board's own rule, eight meetings and one ratifying **2027-07-27/28** session away.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`):** **read it, do not trade it — and do not trade the vol either.** The family's
index null replicates on a third independent parse (157 releases, 0.747% mean absolute move against a
0.797% baseline over 4,950 sessions), and this ledger extends the verdict to implied vol, which no sibling
had tested. The raw vol number is real and the edge is not: VIX falls on 101 of 157 minutes days against a
level-matched expectation of 85.5 (p = 0.0074), but residualised on the same session's index move it is
worth **−0.16 VIX pt**, and **3 of 20 placebo offsets D−10…D+10 beat it**. What this ledger adds that
survives is a **date rule**: all **19** September-meeting minutes 2007–2025 released at exactly **D+21**,
because those releases always fall between October 4 and 17 and **October has no US market holiday in any
year** — the mechanism behind every one of the 14 D+21 exceptions cannot reach this class. Two retirements
go with it. **September's cluster** (VIX down 17 of 19, S&P up 16 of 19, both the twelve-month maximum)
survives level-matching, a matched calendar window and a neighbouring-session control, and still fails:
residualised the vol sign is 12 of 19 with 1 of 19 clearing −1.0 pt against an 8.85% base, so it is a
*directional index* claim, and that claim is p = 0.0067 raw → **0.081** after its own twelve-way scan.
**The SEP hypothesis** this event was proposed on is refuted outright (51 of 75 vs 50 of 82, two-sample
permutation p = 0.211), and its 2030-dot headline is true but routine — every September SEP extends to the
current year plus three. No directional call, no size; `estimate` widens caution and licenses nothing.

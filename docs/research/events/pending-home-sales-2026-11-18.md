### Leg 3 — does the September lane's FOMC widening apply here? **REFUTED — and this is the correction**

### Honest limits

- **A null is evidence of absence only at the power measured.** n=104 minutes days is enough to see
  a decision-sized effect (the decision+1 cohort shows one at n=106); it is not enough to rule out a
  small one.
- **n=12 on November opex−2 and n=3 on the exact shape.** Calendar-configuration cohorts cannot be
  enlarged without waiting for years to pass, and the n=3 is reported as anecdote, not as a test.
- **The DHI FY2026 Q4 date is unpublished.** Leg 4's clearance figure is a projection from a
  two-year lag pattern, registered as a forward test precisely because it is not a source.
- **The execution record is n=4**, the ceiling NAR's sitemap offers across both series. It shares an
  evidence stream with the 09-17 lane's n=2 and the 12-17 lane's n=1 — a reader counting evidence
  should count the governing-surface question once.
- **Every tape figure is daily OHLC**, not intraday. The 12-17 lane's hour-level test (ITB 10:00–11:00
  share, p=0.79) is cited, not reproduced; Yahoo's hourly window reaches back ~730 days and would
  have covered 1 of the 3 exact-shape sessions.
- **D-71 is early.** This assessment is complete on its own evidence, but the corridor between now
  and the print contains two FOMC decisions' worth of information this ledger cannot yet see.

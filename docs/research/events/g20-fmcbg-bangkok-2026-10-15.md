## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries 2026-10-15 as the third and final G20 finance
ministerial of the US presidency, in Bangkok. Is the date real, does a G20 FMCBG move markets, is
anything different about this one, and does any name this book tracks carry exposure?

**One-line verdict:** the date is primary-verified and its `estimate` label is a schema artifact, but
the meeting is a read and not a trade — the most recent instance of exactly this event type produced
a public China dissent and left the tape at a high three sessions later, and the only channel Bangkok
could borrow (a US–China finance bilateral) is unannounced and staffed by the wrong principals for
the one clock that is live.

symbols keyed, per the macro-print/geopolitical method in
[`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md)). Primary reads, all fetched direct

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is primary-sourced three times, including the host government's own calendar, and the
   `estimate` label is a pure schema artifact.** SUPPORTED on the fact, blocked on the schema.
   **`g20.org/events-calendar/`**, fetched direct today, lists *"Finance Ministers and Central Bank
   Governors Meeting (Bangkok, Thailand) — October 15, 2026"* and adds, in its own words, *"On the
   margins of the 2026 Annual Meetings of the International Monetary Fund and the World Bank
   Group."* — which confirms both the date and the on-the-margins relationship this research had
   otherwise inferred. Treasury's finance-track release independently lists *"October 15 (Bangkok,
   Thailand): Finance ministers and central bank governors meeting"* as the fourth and last entry of
   the host year. And worldbank.org states the 2026 IMF/WBG Annual Meetings run *"from Monday,
   October 12 to Sunday, October 18 in Bangkok, Thailand"*, with the Development Committee on
   **10/15**, the plenary on **10/16** and the IMFC plenary on **10/17**. Three independent primaries
   agreeing. **Method note worth keeping:** g20.org has returned HTTP 403 to every sibling that tried
   it with a plain fetcher; a `curl` with a browser UA plus `Accept`/`Accept-Language`/`Accept-Encoding`
   headers returned **200** and 176KB of HTML. That is now a solved fetch, not a blocked one. The
   label still cannot flip, because `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` in `scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs`
   documents `TSY:` as a treasury.gov **auction** schedule and has no slot for a diplomatic calendar
   of any kind — the same gap
   [`us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10`](us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10.md),
   [`china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31`](china-retaliation-suspension-expiry-2026-12-31.md)
   and [`g20-miami-2026-12-14`](g20-miami-2026-12-14.md) each banked. This is the fourth ledger to hit
   it, and the first where a host-secretariat primary exists and still cannot be cited as confirmed.

2. **A G20 finance ministerial has no price channel — and here the claim is measured, not asserted.**
   SUPPORTED, and this is the load-bearing leg. The sibling Miami research had to argue the summit
   case from an n=2 sample of *bilaterals*. This event type gave us a live test three days ago.
   Asheville (**2026-08-31/09-01**) was the maximally newsworthy version of an FMCBG: no communiqué
   agreed (TASS, quoting Bessent), a chair's statement instead, and China dissenting in public from
   four paragraphs. The tape across that window: SPX **7,711.76** (08-28) → **7,686.14** (08-31,
   −0.33%) → **7,631.47** (09-01, −0.71%) → **7,666.60** (09-02, +0.46%) → **7,747.71** (09-03,
   +1.06%, a high). Net 08-28 → 09-03: **+0.47%**. VIX **14.43** → **16.34** (09-01) → **14.32**
   (09-03) — the pop fully retraced in two sessions. And even the single down session is not cleanly
   the meeting's: 09-01 also carried ISM manufacturing. Read honestly this is *not* a measured zero,
   it is the absence of any effect large enough to separate from the ordinary tape — which is the
   correct standard for refusing to position.

3. **The one famous counter-case is an attribution artifact, and it is worth naming so nobody
   re-imports it.** SUPPORTED. The only G20 finance ministerial in the modern era credited with a
   market regime shift is **Shanghai, 2016-02-26/27** — the alleged "Shanghai Accord" to weaken the
   dollar. The communiqué contains no such accord; Reuters' own six-weeks-later assessment was that
   *"few if any major investment houses believe that there was a formal accord"*, while *"currency
   and stock markets seem happy to play as if there was."* That is a narrative fitted to a move,
   after the move. It is the exact error this ledger exists to prevent — and the reason a phrase like
   "the G20 signalled X" should never enter a thesis built off 10/15.

4. **China's dissent is substantive, not procedural, and every objected paragraph names China's
   model.** SUPPORTED — and this hardens the leg the Miami ledger explicitly flagged as needing
   verbatim quoting. Treasury's chair statement carries the footnote that it *"was agreed by all G20
   members present except China, which objected to paragraphs 4, 10, 11, and 13."* Read direct, those
   are: **¶4** — energy-trade disruption and *"free, safe, and predictable navigation through the
   Strait of Hormuz"*; **¶10** — *"Countries with excessive and persistent external surpluses should
   remove distortions that constrain domestic consumption and that result in an overreliance on
   exports for growth"*; **¶11** — the IMF's *"surveillance of global imbalances"*; **¶13** —
   sovereign-debt treatments, which CNBC reports singled out *"countries where a meaningful share of
   external debt is owed to G20 members."* Bessent framed the split publicly as 19-against-1 on
   *"cheap exports"*, calling China *"the country with the world's largest and unsustainable current
   account surplus."* None of that is drafting friction; all four paragraphs describe China without
   naming it. Bangkok is the last ministerial before Miami, so it is where leaders'-declaration
   language gets pre-negotiated — making "does the dissent narrow?" the single highest-information
   thing to read off 10/15, and a leading indicator for whether the December summit produces a
   consensus declaration at all.

5. **The venue's only real channel is a sideline bilateral — and the principals who own the live
   clock are not in the room.** MIXED. The bilateral channel is real and has fresh precedent at this
   exact meeting type: at Asheville, Bessent met Chinese counterparts (common ground reported on Iran
   and Hormuz transit) and also met Russia's finance minister **Siluanov**, described by Bloomberg as
   rare US–Russia finance talks, with Siluanov excluded from the ministers' family photo. So Bangkok
   *can* host one. But the discount is structural. What expires 2026-11-10 on China's side is
   **MOFCOM/GAC Announcement No. 70**'s suspension of the October-2025 export-control announcements —
   a commerce-ministry instrument, not a finance-ministry one — and the channel that produced the
   Kuala Lumpur truce is **Bessent–He Lifeng** (Vice Premier), not the MOF minister and PBoC governor
   who staff an FMCBG. There is precedent for a Bessent–He round convened on a multilateral meeting's
   margins (Kuala Lumpur 2025, ASEAN margins), which is exactly why an announced round would be an
   upgrade trigger — but as of 2026-09-04 nothing announces one, and no Bangkok participant list is
   published.

6. **The US half of the 2026-11-10 deadline is alive, which a sibling ledger's conclusion does not
   yet reflect.** MIXED — the finding is sourced; the correction is not this lane's to make. USTR's
   release of **2025-11-26** states that the **178** remaining Section 301 product exclusions (from
   the forced-technology-transfer investigation) *"have been extended until November 10, 2026."*
   Section 301 is a different authority from IEEPA, so the 2026-02-20 Supreme Court ruling that
   killed the IEEPA tariffs does not reach these; trade-press readings (Great Lakes Customs Law,
   Thompson Hine) put the operative cutoff at entries filed on or after 2026-11-10 paying the
   underlying List 1/2/3/4A rate, 7.5–25% over MFN, across 164 product-specific exclusions
   (HTSUS 9903.88.69) and 14 solar-manufacturing-equipment exclusions (9903.88.70). The
   [`us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10`](us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10.md) ledger,
   written 2026-09-04, concluded that *"the headline mechanism is dead"* and that what still expires
   that day is *China's* half only. On this evidence that is incomplete: there is a narrower but real
   US-side clock on the same date. It is **flagged, not edited** — that is another event's lane and
   its rows are append-only. Why it belongs here: it makes 10/15 the last scheduled Treasury-principal
   venue before a **two-sided** deadline rather than a one-sided one, which raises this meeting's
   read value without giving it a trade.

7. **The attribution hazard is the actionable part, and the clock geometry sharpens it.** SUPPORTED.
   Within ±2 days of 10/15 this calendar already tracks eight events: `beige-book-2026-10-14`,
   `cpi-2026-10-14`, `ppi-2026-10-15`, `retail-sales-2026-10-15`, `opex-2026-10-16`,

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date: estimate — on a schema gap, not on doubt).** No position keyed to this event and no
prospect of one. Treat 2026-10-15 as a **read with two questions and one discipline**. The read: the
last ministerial before Miami is where the leaders'-declaration language is pre-negotiated, so
whether China's dissent from ¶4/10/11/13 of the Asheville chair's statement narrows or widens is the
highest-information output of the day — and it is an input to the December summit's odds, not a
trade. The second question is whether a US–China finance-principal bilateral is announced on the
margins; the venue has precedent (Asheville hosted two bilaterals, including a rare US–Russia one)
but the officials who own the live 2026-11-10 expiries sit in MOFCOM and the Vice Premier's office,
not in an FMCBG. The discipline: 10/15 carries an 08:30 ET PPI and retail-sales double print, sits
one day after CPI and the Beige Book and one day before monthly opex, and the Bangkok meeting day
ends before the US cash open — so no tape move that day is attributable here. Estimate status widens
caution only; nothing is date-keyed action, and `low` impact is affirmed.

**Kill switches.**

- *"This meeting has no channel of its own"* dies on an announced **Bessent–He Lifeng** round (or any
  named US–China principal-level bilateral) on the Bangkok margins, published before 2026-10-15.
  Kuala Lumpur 2025 is the precedent for such a round convening on a multilateral's margins; an
  announcement would warrant an impact-tier review before the date.
- *"A ministerial does not move the tape"* dies on any dated, named deliverable published on or about
  2026-10-15 that resolves, extends or hardens the 2026-11-10 expiries — at which point Bangkok is a
  delivery venue and this ledger is wrong about its function, not just its size.
- *"The Asheville split persists"* dies if the Bangkok chair's statement is agreed by consensus
  including China, or if China's objections shrink below the four paragraphs. That is a warmer signal
  for the Miami declaration than anything currently on record and re-rates
  [`g20-miami-2026-12-14`](g20-miami-2026-12-14.md)'s fracture leg.
- *"The dissent could also widen"* is the symmetric switch: a **second** member joining China's
  objection, or a rival ministerial statement, escalates the fracture the Miami ledger tracks.
- *"The date is primary-verified"* dies on any fetched US-government or G20-secretariat page naming a
  different date or venue for the third FMCBG; the same fetch is the flip trigger if
  `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` ever gains a slot for a host-government diplomatic schedule.
- *"No tracked symbol carries exposure"* dies if the finance track produces a semiconductor,
  export-control or AI-governance line item — none of which is on Bessent's stated agenda, which is
  why it is filed `low`.
- *"Bessent chairs it in person"* is assumed, not established. A US delegation led below the
  Secretary would lower the meeting's information content further; a confirmed Bessent absence is a
  downgrade signal, not a market one.

**Forward test.** None registered in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md). That register is the
symbol-sweep's zero-capital price-hypothesis ledger; this doc's falsifiable claims are
diplomatic-outcome and attribution claims, not price tests, and their scoring venue is this event's

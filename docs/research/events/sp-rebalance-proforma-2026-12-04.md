### Method

Macro / market-structure mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md). The entry carries `symbols: []`, so no symbol-keyed instrument applies and `scripts/research/` has no index-flow instrument. Nothing was taken from the proposal on faith; every number below was computed in-session.

This is why the source prefix is `EST:` on a *derivation checked against 17 published announcements*, rather than a claim on the owner's methodology text — the empirical archive is the stronger evidence anyway, and it is the one that was actually reachable.
- **This repo** — the proposing ledger and both S&P siblings read in full; the corridor computed from the calendar files rather than by eye; `trade-playbooks.md` and `multi-symbol-sweep.md` re-grepped for `rebalanc|index effect|index inclusion|s&p 500 add|deletion|witching|closing auction|index flow` → **zero hits** in both.

**Measurement design, stated before the results.** For each quarterly announcement (a Friday, released after the close), the reaction session is the next trading session, D+1. Excess return is `(name D+1/D0 − 1) − (tier ETF D+1/D0 − 1)`, benchmarked to **its own tier's ETF** (IJR / IJH / SPY). Relative turnover is the name's D+1 volume over its trailing-60-session median, divided by the benchmark's same ratio. Rows whose effective date differs from their release's majority effective date are dropped as bundled off-cycle changes, and any `|return| > 40%` is dropped as a corporate-action artefact. **The 2026-09-04 cycle is excluded entirely** — its D+1 (2026-09-08) had not closed when this ran — which is what makes the panel fully out-of-sample against the sibling.

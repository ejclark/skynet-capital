## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event exists only because the `mtis-2026-12-16` lane's adjacency sweep proposed it, on a specific
argument: M3-2 supplies the manufacturers' leg of total business inventories — **35.1%** — so it is a
direct *input* to a tracked event rather than a neighbour of one, and "anyone reading the 12-16 print
needs this date to know what was already known." **Is that right? And does this print own the nowcast
line that its MTIS sibling was measured not to own?**

**One-line verdict:** the proposal is right about publication and wrong about information — M3-2's
measurable footprint on the nowcast is indistinguishable from zero, the advance report eight days
earlier owns all of it, and the mechanism that made this true is a dated change to Census's own
advance publication in July 2025.

### Honest limits

- **The decisive class is n=16.** Leg 4's central result rests on sixteen vintages spread over twelve
  years in which the Atlanta Fed's free-text column names M3-2 and nothing else. The direction is
  unambiguous and the p-values are on medians, but this is a failure to reject an equality on a thin
  sample, not a demonstration that the print is inert.
- **The classifier is free text.** `ContribArchives`'s `Data releases` column has inconsistent
  spellings across the decade ("M3-2 Manufacturing", "M3-2 Manufacturing (Full report)", "M3-2 Manuf.
  (Full report)"); the split is case-normalised and comma-tokenised but not otherwise cleaned. A
  vintage whose text silently omits M3-2 — exactly what the 2026-12-03 workbook row appears to do —
  would be misfiled into the baseline, which biases the test **toward** finding no difference.
- **This session's own first pass was wrong, and the correction is the finding.** The uncontrolled
  release-date join reported 0.0466pp at p<0.0001. Anyone re-running only that join will reproduce a
  result this ledger rejects.
- **All the nowcast work measures a model, not a market.** Every Δ describes the Atlanta Fed's
  estimate of GDP, not a price. `ContribArchives` ends 2026-07-28 and carries no Q4-2026 vintages, so
  `-1`'s class priors are out-of-sample for the quarter being nowcast.
- **The revision series is 15 months, archive-sourced, and not a clean difference.** The restated
  figure is the value as of the *next* advance release, which incorporates the full report **and** any
  concurrent seasonal or benchmark revision landing in between. Coverage is 2023–2025 with gaps, from
  71 of 81 queried Wayback captures — a set determined by what the archive happens to hold, not by
  sampling. Both facts widen the true revision distribution rather than narrowing it.
- **No manufacturers' inventories/sales-ratio history was obtainable.** FRED and ALFRED are
  egress-blocked from this runner and the Census time-series API requires a key, so the **1.47**
  reading has no percentile attached and the component-share arithmetic (35.1%) is carried from the
  MTIS ledger's FRED pull, not re-derived here.
- **n=33 is thin for the hourly work and n=16 for the day-before-payrolls comparison.** Leg 6's
  intraday result is a failure to reject on a small sample; a wide 12-03 would be unsurprising and
  would not by itself overturn the class result.

  instrument attached and no house playbook keyed to it. The `confirmed` flip changes the honesty

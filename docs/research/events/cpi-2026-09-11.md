## Initial research

**The question.** What will the August 2026 CPI print show, how will the market react in each
direction, and does it change how we should sit in the tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT
GOOG META AAPL AMZN) around 2026-09-11?

**One-line verdict:** an energy-shock print inside a live Fed *hike* debate, five days before the
Sep 16 FOMC — the reaction function is asymmetric (a hot core print is the tail that hurts our
long-duration names most, a cool/in-line print is a relief rally), and the house answer is
guard-shaped, not directional: no macro playbook exists, so the play is known-date variance
discipline across the Sep 4→Sep 16 compound-risk corridor.

**Method.** Sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md)

3. **Core is still tame (the shock hasn't broadened) — MIXED.** July core printed 0.2% m/m /

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BLS primary re-checked 2026-08-17).** Treat 2026-09-11 as a
high-impact known-date variance event, not a tradeable edge: no directional macro position, no
new rate-sensitive paper entries initiated into the print, and any open position sized as if
both tails (hot-core hike repricing · cool-print relief rally) are live. Base case
(estimate-labeled: nowcast, not consensus): headline ~+0.35% m/m with core ~+0.2% — an
energy-hot / core-tame print the market likely absorbs; the asymmetric risk is core ≥0.3% m/m,
which re-arms the September hike five days before the FOMC and hits CRWV and the high-multiple
semis hardest.

**Kill switches:**

- **Energy de-escalation** — a holding ceasefire / Hormuz reopening with Brent and RBOB
  retracing materially kills the hot-headline skew (the June-data precedent: energy −5.7% m/m
  flipped the whole print cool).
- **A weak Aug jobs print (Sep 4)** collapsing hike odds toward zero kills the "decisive input"
  framing — CPI becomes confirmation, not a fork, and the variance-event weighting drops a tier.
- **Nowcast/consensus convergence below ~+0.25% m/m headline** as street numbers form kills the
  energy-hot base case; reassess the skew from the actual consensus, not the model.
- **A Warsh Jackson Hole keynote (Aug 28) that pre-commits the September decision** kills the
  print's fork status in either direction — reweight at the next pulse after Aug 28.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the September FOMC is the first two-sided rate decision of the cycle —
hold or *hike* — under a new chair who distrusts his own dot plot. What is priced, what would
surprise in each direction, how has the market actually reacted to this Fed's last few moves, and
which of our names carry the exposure?

**One-line verdict:** a hawkish hold is the priced base case (~60–70%) but a ~30–40% hike is live
and a cut is priced at zero — the SEP/dot plot is the real payload, the reaction runs through the
hike-odds channel in both directions, and for us this is a no-new-risk statement window, not a
trade.

**Method:** sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) —
primary sources over aggregators (the Fed's own calendar fetched directly today; BLS/BEA schedules
for adjacent prints), press-reported CME FedWatch odds cited with their as-of dates, and the last
three decisions' actual tape as the reaction-function evidence. No price instruments run — this is
a macro print, not a symbol event. All statements below carry the event's **confirmed** date label;
odds and levels are as-checked 2026-08-17 and drift daily.

**Regime context (what makes this meeting different).** Kevin Warsh took the chair 2026-05-22.
The target range has sat at 3.50–3.75% all year. June 16–17 (his first meeting, with SEP): a
unanimous hold, but the dot plot flipped hawkish — the 2026 median moved up from March's
implied-cut path, several participants penciled at least one *hike* by year-end, only one still
projected a cut — and Warsh declined to submit his own dot, on record as a skeptic of the SEP
framework; the statement itself was cut down and stripped of forward guidance (federalreserve.gov
statement 2026-06-17; CNBC, Yahoo Finance 2026-06-17). July 28–29: a 9–3 hold with all three
dissents wanting a **hike** — statement cites solid activity, strong productivity and capital
investment, and Middle East–conflict uncertainty (federalreserve.gov statement 2026-07-29). The
inflation backdrop is oil (tanker attacks, failed ceasefire; Brent +6.0%/wk into 8/15 per the
sibling NVDA assessment) plus slow-but-steady tariff pass-through, against a labor market that
just cracked.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and payload are confirmed.** SUPPORTED. federalreserve.gov FOMC calendar fetched
   directly 2026-08-17: Sep 15–16 is a meeting *with* Summary of Economic Projections; statement
   14:00 ET, presser after. Matches the checked-in calendar entry exactly. Remaining 2026
   meetings: Oct 27–28 (no SEP), Dec 8–9 (SEP).

2. **Consensus is a hold.** SUPPORTED. CME FedWatch (press-cited): ~69% hold as of 2026-08-14
   (centralbank.watch/FedWatch aggregations), ~60% hold as of 2026-08-07 (CNBC), with the
   remainder priced as a 25bp **hike** and a cut at 0.0%. The debate is hold-vs-hike; nobody is
   pricing easing at this meeting.

3. **The hike risk is live, not a tail.** SUPPORTED. Hike odds were ~67% on 2026-07-31 after the
   hawkish July hold (Chase/Yahoo Finance recaps), collapsed to ~44% on 2026-08-07 when the July
   jobs report printed one of the biggest monthly job losses since the 2020 pandemic (CNBC
   2026-08-07), and eased to ~42% after the tame July CPI on 2026-08-12 (+0.1% m/m, 3.4% y/y,
   core +0.2%; CNBC/Kiplinger 2026-08-12). Three sitting FOMC members already voted to hike in
   July. This is a genuinely two-sided decision — rare for a modern FOMC at D-30.

4. **The market trades the hike-odds channel, in both directions.** SUPPORTED, last three data
   points. Hawkish surprises sell: the June hawkish-SEP hold was read hawkish (guidance stripped,
   dots up); the July 29 hawkish hold knocked the Dow −1.6% and S&P/Nasdaq −0.5/−0.6% into the
   close (CNBC 2026-07-29). Odds-relief buys: the 8/7 jobs miss sent yields down and stocks *up*
   (bad news = hike relief), and the 8/12 tame CPI put the S&P 500 at a record high with
   CoreWeave +21% on the day (qz/CNBC 2026-08-12). The reaction function for our names is: down
   on hawkish, up on anything that kills the hike.

5. **The SEP/dot plot is the payload beyond the decision itself.** SUPPORTED, with a format
   caveat (MIXED on predictability). The rate decision may be the *less* informative half: the
   September dot plot updates the June flip (does the 2026 median now show a hike?), and Warsh —
   who didn't submit a June dot and has said he wants to shrink the projections apparatus — has
   teased a "big questions" framework speech at Jackson Hole (Aug 28, his first as chair;
   kansascityfed.org symposium page, Aug 27–29, theme "Financial Innovation"). A changed or
   pared-back SEP format would make the market's dot-reading reflex misfire in ways no historical
   FOMC-day study covers.

6. **Our tracked names are uniformly sensitive.** MIXED — the sensitivity is real but sharply
   tiered. **CRWV** is the most exposed name on the board: debt-financed AI-infra buildout, so
   the rate path hits both its discount rate and its actual cost of capital, and the tape agrees
   (+21% on a single tame-CPI day, 2026-08-12). **NVDA / MRVL / AVGO** trade as long-duration
   AI-growth assets on the rate path (the calendar's own CPI note), and all three have prints
   inside the run-up window (NVDA 8/26, MRVL 8/27, AVGO 9/2) — their post-print digestion will be
   marked to this macro tape. **MSFT / GOOG / META / AMZN** carry valuation duration and giant
   capex programs but fortress balance sheets — rate-sensitive, not financing-sensitive. **AAPL**
   is the least levered to the AI-rates nexus of the nine. Market-wide event, `symbols: []` — the
   tiering is about *our* risk, not the event's scope.

**What plays the conditions support (date confirmed):** none directional. The supported behavior

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed):** base case is a hawkish hold at 3.50–3.75% with the September dot
plot as the swing payload; a 25bp hike is a live ~30–40% branch, a cut is a 0% branch. For the
book this is a **no-new-risk day**, not a trade: nothing new opened into the 14:00 ET statement,
defined-risk only across the window, no directional macro bet in either direction — the two-sided
pricing means both a relief rally and a hike gap are ordinary outcomes, and estimates of the
odds only widen caution, never license an entry.

**Kill switches:**

- **Base-case kill (hold → hike):** Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote (2026-08-28) pre-committing to
  tightening, or Sep hike odds sustained above ~60% after the 9/4 jobs and 9/11 CPI prints —
  either flips the written base case before the meeting; the ledger row that observes it says so.
- **Two-sided-pricing kill (the interesting one):** cut odds moving off 0% — e.g. a second
  consecutive jobs collapse on 9/4 — would mean the market has repriced the *question itself*
  (from hold-vs-hike to hold-vs-cut), invalidating every reaction-function read above; reassess
  from scratch, don't patch.
- **Reaction-function kill:** an announced SEP/dot-plot format change (Jackson Hole is the likely
  venue) retires leg 4's historical mapping of dots→tape until one post-change meeting has
  printed.

No forward test registered in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md) at D-30 — the hold/hike
branch is scoreable but the stance takes no position sized to it; candidates are noted for the
scanner and can be registered at a later pulse if the stance sharpens.

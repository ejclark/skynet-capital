## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event exists only because another lane's adjacency sweep found a hole. Its proposal argues the
hole is real — that the 11-27 advance report's inventories half had no tracked successor — and it
inherits, from two sibling ledgers one and two days away, a set of claims about funding exposure,
about what a Census release does to GDPNow, and about what an FOMC-adjacent session looks like.
**Is the hole the shape the proposal says it is, do the sibling claims transfer to this series, and
does anything about the 12-09 session or the 12-09 nowcast vintage differ from the class the siblings
measured?**

**One-line verdict:** the hole is real but wider than described — the advance report has *three*
successors, not two, and the third is untracked — and neither sibling claim transfers, because this
print moves a different nowcast line than the FT-900 does and sits on the loud side of the FOMC
meeting rather than the quiet one.

### Method

a rate decision. With both fixed the panel yields 8 meetings per year for seven years; the sibling
10-28 ledger's `n=21` decision days becomes **n=23** on the same hourly sample, and its headline

### Leg 4 — the sibling's nowcast framing transfers · **REFUTED**, and this is the ledger's central finding

Joining `ContribArchives` to the release-date archive on release date:

| Vintage class | n | median \|Δ inventories\| | P(<0.10pp) | median \|Δ net exports\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Wholesale Trade (same-day vintage)** | **78** | **0.0639pp** | 76.9% | **0.0012pp** |
| Every other vintage | 1,744 | 0.0142pp | 82.2% | 0.0020pp |

Permutation p<**0.0001** on the inventories column (20,000 iterations). The net-exports column runs
*below* the all-other baseline — this print does not touch trade. Directly: on **72 of 78** wholesale
vintages (**92.3%**) the inventories move exceeds the net-exports move, against **60.7%** on the other
1,744. **The 12-08 FT-900 owns net exports and the 12-09 wholesale print owns inventories**, 24 hours
apart, in the same nowcast quarter. The sibling's expectation is about the wrong line.

The sibling's *mechanism* — reference-month position in the nowcast quarter — also fails to transfer,
and inverts:

| Reference month's position in the nowcast quarter | n | median \|Δ inventories\| | P(<0.10pp) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Last month of the **prior** quarter | 25 | **0.0872pp** | 56.0% |
| **Month 1 of the nowcast quarter (the 12-09 shape)** | 26 | **0.0387pp** | **84.6%** |
| Month 2 | 26 | 0.0485pp | 88.5% |
| Month 3 | 1 | 0.0037pp | — |

### Honest limits

- **The archive is release *dates*, not release *contents*.** Legs 2 and 3 measure whether and when
  the report published. A close-out reading the successor's numbers should not treat "it published" as
  "it published on an unrevised basis" — see the next limit.
- **The 2026-10-26 benchmark revision sits between this research and this print**, and Census's own
  word is "tentatively". Every level and ratio quoted above is pre-revision, which is why `-3` is
  scored on the value as printed on 12-09 rather than on a delta against 1.19, and why the
  This-month call carries medium rather than high confidence.
- **Two analogues for the wholesale × FOMC-decision-day configuration, and one is the 2024 election
  week.** Leg 5's numbers are the *containing* classes, not the intersection. The intersection has no
  usable sample and this ledger does not pretend otherwise.
- **The composition classifier is free text.** Solo-vs-shared is read from `ContribArchives`'s
  `Data releases` column, whose spellings are inconsistent across the decade; the 44/34 split is
  case-normalised but not otherwise cleaned, and a mis-typed row would move a vintage between classes.
  The release-date join used for every other number does not depend on it.
- **All the nowcast work measures a model, not a market.** Every Δ describes the Atlanta Fed's
  estimate. The only price claims here are the session-class studies, and both are reasons *not* to act.
- **`ContribArchives` ends 2026-07-28** and carries no Q4-2026 vintages, so `-1`'s class priors are
  out-of-sample for the quarter being nowcast.
- **The advance-vs-full revision size is one observation, not a study.** June's 0.1pp is quoted from

For a fourth-time reopening with `symbols: []`, the honest question is not "will the market like
it" — no play exists either way. It is: **is the EUR 5.0bn size and 10-13 date actually locked, and

**One-line verdict:** the terms are locked on two independent primaries; the diagnostic finding is
that this vintage (DE000BU25075) is already the **weakest-performing Bobl 5-year vintage since 2023**
by within-cycle yield rise, and Finanzagentur's own forward calendar — a taper to EUR 4.5bn on
2026-12-01 — is the first external corroboration that the debt office is seeing the same softening.

### Conviction legs, tested

**1. The date, size, ISIN, coupon and maturity — SUPPORTED, two independent primaries agree to the
decimal.** The press release's Annex 1 reads verbatim: *"Tue, 13-Oct-2026 | Bobl 5 | R | 5,000 |
08-Oct-2031 | DE000BU25075 | 2.90% | 23-Jul-2026 | 08-Oct-2027."* The Q4 issuance-outlook workbook's
row 7, published as a separate document, reads the same seven fields independently: 2026-10-13,
Bobl, 5 years, Reopening, EUR 5,000m, maturity 2031-10-08, DE000BU25075, coupon 2.9%. Both documents
also agree on the *next two* taps of the same ISIN — 2026-11-03 at EUR 5.0bn, 2026-12-01 at
EUR 4.5bn — which leg 4 below reads as the first size cut anywhere in this vintage's life.

**2. No price channel exists for this event — SUPPORTED, and inherited from a stronger test rather
than re-derived.** `symbols: []`; no house playbook is rates-keyed. The domestic/transatlantic
spillover question this event might raise was already tested and killed by
[`bund-30y-auction-2026-10-14`](bund-30y-auction-2026-10-14.md) leg 2 on Germany's most liquid
duration point: soft-vs-firm bid-to-offer moves the US 30Y CMT **+0.01bp** (t = 0.01, n = 204) and
the Bund 30Y itself **+0.35bp** (t = 0.66); undersubscribed-vs-covered moves them **+0.25bp**
(t = 0.30) and **+1.04bp** (t = 1.60). A EUR 5.0bn 5-year reopening is a smaller, shorter-duration
instrument than the EUR 2.5bn 30-year multi-ISIN auction that already returned nothing at every
split tried — so this leg does not re-run the regression on a weaker candidate and inherits the
null as the *stronger* case.

**3. This vintage's own reopening cycle is already the weakest of the last eight since 2023,
measured by within-cycle yield rise — SUPPORTED.** Every Bobl 5-year vintage since 2023 tracked
from its first auction to its latest, weighted-average yield (%):

measured softening, not yet two consecutive misses (no exact-shape vintage in the 2023+ sample below
has posted two in a row; one vintage posted **three** in Q1 2026, see leg 5).

**4. The debt office's own forward calendar independently corroborates the softening — MIXED, a
calendar reading rather than an announced view.** DE000BU25075's full offered-size sequence,
07-21 through 12-01: **6,000 · 6,000 · 5,500 · 5,000 · 5,000 · 4,500** (EUR m) — a step down at every
tap so far, and the 12-01 line is the vintage's first size *cut* rather than a repeat. This reads
naturally as the debt office trimming supply into softer demand, but no Finanzagentur release
frames it that way, and a step-down before rolling to a new ISIN is also the ordinary shape of a
vintage's last tap or two — every prior vintage in the leg-3 table ran 4–7 taps before a new ISIN
opened. The two readings are not distinguishable from the calendar alone; this is why leg 4 is
`MIXED`, not `SUPPORTED`.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

The minutes of the October 26–27, 2027 FOMC publish at 14:00 ET on **2027-11-17**. Six sibling ledgers

Two things are genuinely open, and the first is not a statistics question at all. The
[`from-fomc-2027-10-26`](../../../src/domain/market-events/proposals/fomc-minutes-2027-11-17.from-fomc-2027-10-26.json)
proposal that seeded this id named the right hazard and then under-measured it: it recorded *"42 of 45 at
exactly D+21"* with three exceptions, observed that *"two of the three exceptions are the November minutes
of a November autumn meeting"*, and reassured on a one-week Thanksgiving clearance. **That census was the
live page alone.** So: *how displaced is this class really, and does 2027-11-17 survive on evidence or on
a lucky count?* The second is the sibling's unfinished business. The 10-06 lane measured VIX on minutes
days, found **64.3%** down, residualised it to −0.16 pt and killed it on a placebo control — but never
asked **what the 64.3% actually is**.

**One-line verdict:** the class is far more displaced than the seeding proposal knew — **7 of 19, the
worst in the record** — and 2027-11-17 survives anyway, on a **quantised, computable Thanksgiving gap**

is carried entirely by the releases that do *not* coincide with the monthly VIX settlement, and this one
does. Date `estimate`; no directional call at D−435.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The family's D+21 census replicates exactly — 143 of 157, fourteen exceptions, D+19 to D+24.**
   SUPPORTED, and it is a genuine third-party reproduction rather than a citation. The page states the
   convention verbatim — *"The minutes of regularly scheduled meetings are released three weeks after the
   date of the policy decision"* — and its 2027 panel reads `January 26-27 | March 16-17* | April 27-28 |
   June 8-9* | July 27-28 | September 14-15* | October 26-27 | December 7-8*`, October carrying **no** SEP
   asterisk. 2027-10-26 is a Tuesday and 2027-10-27 a Wednesday (computed, not assumed), so the decision
   is 10-27 and +21 days is **Wednesday 2027-11-17**. Across the 157 releases, **143 sit at exactly
   D+21**; the 14 exceptions by **meeting** month are **November ×6, December ×4, June ×2, October ×1**
   plus the **2020-03-15 unscheduled** meeting at D+24 — matching the 10-06 lane's breakdown instance for
   instance, from a differently-constructed parse.

2. **This class is the most-displaced in the record — 7 of 19 — and the seeding proposal's reassurance
   rested on a census five times too small.** SUPPORTED, and this is the correction. The autumn meeting is
   one slot, not two: pooled one-per-year 2007–2025 by decision date (an unbroken **16-day cluster running
   10-24 to 11-08**, reproducing the [`fomc-2027-10-26`](fomc-2027-10-26.md) lane's own finding
   independently), it gives **19 instances, 7 displaced — 36.8%**, against September's **19 of 19**
   perfect. The `from-fomc-2027-10-26` proposal saw **3** exceptions in 45 releases and inferred the risk
   from two of them. The true figures are **14 in 157**, of which **6** are this class. Its conclusion
   holds; its evidence did not support it.

   **And the bin split is the trap.** Three numbers describe one class depending on which side of a month
   boundary the meeting fell:

   | Bin | n | Displaced | Rate |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | October-meeting minutes | 8 | 1 | **12.5%** |
   | November-meeting minutes | 11 | 6 | **54.5%** |
   | **Pooled autumn slot** | **19** | **7** | **36.8%** |

   2027 is an *October* meeting, so the naive read is 12.5% — three times too reassuring against the
   pooled rate. This is the same artifact the `fomc-2027-10-26` lane found on the decision-day side,
   recurring on the release side. **All three numbers are irrelevant to 2027, and leg 3 is why.**

3. **The displacement mechanism is computable, the gap is quantised, and 2027 sits in the bucket that is
   0-for-8.** SUPPORTED — the durable result. Every displacement, tested against Thanksgiving computed as
   the statutory fourth Thursday:

   | Gap (D+21 − Thanksgiving) | n | Displaced | Years |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **−8 days** | 7 | **0** | 2008, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2019, 2025 |
   | **−1 day** (Wed before) | 9 | 5 | 2007, 2009, 2010, 2011, **2016, 2017, 2021, 2022**, 2023 |
   | **0 days** (Thanksgiving itself) | 2 | 2 | 2020, 2024 |
   | **+7 days** | 1 | **0** | 2018 |

   Two things fall out. **Thanksgiving adjacency is necessary, with zero exceptions:** `|gap| ≤ 1` runs 7
   displaced of 11, `|gap| > 1` runs **0 of 8**. And **the gap cannot land in between** — D+21 preserves
   the decision day's weekday, the autumn decision is always a Tue/Wed/Thu, and Thanksgiving is always a
   Thursday, so the offset is quantised to a multiple of seven ±1. In nineteen years there is no 2-to-6-day
   case at all. **2027-11-17 sits at −8**, the exact configuration of seven consecutive holds. Thanksgiving
   2027 is **Thursday 2027-11-25** on three independent readings: nyse.com's own row parsed today, the
   OCC's `Exchange/OCC holiday` flag on that date, and 5 U.S.C. 6103's fourth-Thursday rule (2027-11-01 is
   a Monday → Thursdays are the 4th, 11th, 18th, 25th). Displaced releases always moved **earlier**, to a
   Tuesday in 6 of 7 cases and a Wednesday in the seventh.

   **It is necessary and NOT sufficient, and the honest version says so.** Inside `|gap| ≤ 1` the Board's
   practice is era-dependent: **4 of 4 displaced 2007-2011, 1 of 5 2016-2022, 2 of 2 2023-2024.** 2022's
   release printed on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving and 2023's did not. So "the Board always moves off
   Thanksgiving week" is **false**, and nothing here would let a future lane predict a `|gap| ≤ 1` year.
   2027 does not need that prediction.

4. **The autumn slot is the anti-September — an ordinary session, leaning the wrong way for a "minutes are
   risk-on" story.** SUPPORTED as a null, and it is early out-of-sample evidence for a sibling's
   registered kill. The 10-06 lane found September's releases at the twelve-month maximum (S&P up **16 of
   19**, VIX down **17 of 19**) and retired it as a scan artifact at corrected p = 0.081, registering
   **FT-fomc-minutes-2027-10-06-2**. The neighbouring slot, same nineteen years:

   | Measure | Autumn slot (n=19) | All releases (n=157) | Universe (n=4,949) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Mean \|S&P\| | **0.782%** | 0.747% | 0.798% |
   | S&P up | **7 (36.8%)** | 87 (55.4%) | 54.4% |
   | VIX down | **11 (57.9%)** | 101 (64.3%) | 54.4% |

   Permutation against the universe: mean |S&P| **P = 0.472**, S&P-up share **P = 0.960**, VIX-down share

5. **The family's 64.3% VIX-down share is explained away, and the explanation excludes this release.**
   MIXED as a finding, decisive as a refusal — and the split was **handed to this lane by the OCC file
   while sourcing an adjacency**, not found by scanning. The monthly VIX settlement is a Wednesday; so are
   minutes releases. They collide often:

   | Set | n | VIX down | Mean ΔVIX | Residual mean |

## Stance & kill switches

class rate.** The family's D+21 census replicates exactly on a third independent construction (143 of 157,
fourteen exceptions D+19 to D+24), and this class is its worst: pooled one-per-year, the autumn meeting's

or the Wednesday before it; all 8 further-away instances held; and the gap is quantised to **−8, −1, 0,

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The Tokyo flash's rank against doing nothing INVERTS at a consumption-tax level break —
   SUPPORTED at n=4 with one instructive exception, and it is this document's central finding.** The
   sibling benchmark replicates first, so the new result is measured on the same footing rather than
   a different one. Predicting national core y/y (all items less fresh food) from the Tokyo
   same-month flash versus from persistence — repeating the previous national print, published about
   a week *before* the flash and therefore strictly available:

   | Sample | n | Tokyo meanAbs / median | Persistence meanAbs / median | Tokyo wins |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | All months 2000-01 → 2026-07 | 319 | 0.250 / 0.219 | **0.182 / 0.115** | **36%** |
   | All months 2018-01 → 2026-07 | 103 | **0.201 / 0.135** | 0.231 / 0.218 | 52% |
   | Februaries 2000 → 2026 | 27 | 0.288 / 0.225 | **0.178 / 0.116** | 33% |
   | *All non-break months 1989+* | 444 | 0.227 / 0.206 | **0.166 / 0.115** | **35%** |
   | **Consumption-tax break months** | **4** | **0.413 / 0.455** | **1.226 / 1.460** | **75%** |

   Every ordinary-month figure reproduces the sibling exactly. The break-month row is new. Its four
   observations:

   | Break | National core y/y | Persistence | Tokyo flash | err(Tokyo) | err(persist.) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1989-04 (3% introduced) | +2.47 | +1.05 | +2.88 | 0.401 | **1.428** |
   | 1997-04 (3→5%) | +1.95 | +0.46 | +1.43 | 0.524 | **1.492** |
   | 2014-04 (5→8%) | +3.27 | +1.29 | +2.76 | 0.509 | **1.983** |
   | 2019-10 (8→10%, reduced rate) | +0.33 | +0.33 | +0.55 | 0.219 | **0.001** |

   The mechanism is not mysterious: persistence is an estimator that assumes no level break, so at a
   level break it fails by roughly the size of the break, while the flash observes the same break one
   release earlier and fails only by its ordinary regional noise. **And the exception is the most
   important row in the table**, because it is structurally the closest analogue to 2027: the 2019
   hike introduced a *reduced rate* holding food at 8% while everything else rose 2pp, and paired it
   with free preschool education — the offsets roughly cancelled at the national core level (the
   y/y jump was −0.00pp), persistence was accidentally exact, and the flash's ordinary noise made it
   the loser. 2027 differs in that it is an **uncancelled one-way move on a single basket**. That
   difference is real and it is also the whole reason this leg is stated at **Medium**. Registered as
   **FT-japan-cpi-tokyo-flash-2027-03-26-2**.

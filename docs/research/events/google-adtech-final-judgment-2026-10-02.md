## Initial research

### The question, plainly

The [GOOG print ledger](goog-2026-10-28-print.md)'s 2026-09-08 adjacency sweep proposed this date as
"the operative document of the ad-tech case." It sits **two sessions inside** that print's D-20
run-up window, so the roster's one planned directional long would be open across it. So:
**does 2026-10-02 actually resolve anything, does it reprice GOOG, and does an open pre-print long
need to do anything about it?**

### One-line verdict

**No, no, and no — and the first "no" is the one that matters**, because both the proposal and the
sibling ledger built on the premise that 10-02 is where the terms land. It is not: it is the
deadline to file a *proposal*, entry of judgment is a separate later act, and on this defendant's
own sibling case that gap ran **86 days**.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date is an `estimate` — every statement below inherits that label).** **Stand aside on all
four horizons, with no position of any kind keyed to 2026-10-02**, and carry the ad-tech thread as
an **undated** overhang rather than a dated one. The reasoning rests on two measured things and one
structural one. Measured: this exact procedural step priced at **−0.19%** excess vs QQQ in the same
defendant's sibling case (2025-09-10), and even the day the binding judgment was **entered** paid
only **+0.75%** and retraced to **−1.43%** across two sessions — against **+8.23%** for a remedy
*decision*. **The market pays for decisions, not for the paperwork that implements them.**
Structural: 2026-10-02 is jobs day with 34 tracked events in the corridor, so the tape carries no
information about this event whatever it does (`estimate`).

**The framing correction is the substance of this initial research, and it is registered against
both the proposal and the sibling ledger.** Both treated 10-02 as the day the terms become binding.
It is the day a *proposal* is due. In the search case the equivalent deadline was met on time by
parties who agreed on almost nothing, and Final Judgment was entered **86 days later** over a
95-page opinion resolving their disputes. Applied to this case's cadence, the **entered** ad-tech
judgment projects to roughly **December 2026–January 2027** — *after* the `goog-2026-10-28-print`
event, and therefore outside every window this roster currently plans to hold risk in (`estimate`).

**The tier is kept and its reason replaced.** `medium` cannot be defended on magnitude — the
comparable is −0.19%, which argues for `low`, the sibling's tier. It is kept on **cadence**: at
`low` the 30-day band would schedule the next assessment for 2026-10-08, i.e. **no further look at
this event before it happens**, while 10-02 sits two sessions inside the run-up window of the
roster's one planned directional long. `medium`'s 7-day band buys pulses around 09-15, 09-22 and
09-29 — the last of which lands on the D-20 entry decision itself. The tier is a *how often do we
look* dial here, not a *how much does it move* claim, and this ledger says so rather than letting
the number imply the second.

**Kill switches.**

- *The whole stance dies* if the 10-02 filing is **genuinely joint and undisputed and the court
  enters it within 14 days** — that would make the deadline the resolution the proposing sweep
  called it, retire the "paperwork ≠ decision" framing for this case, and demand a real reassessment
  of what an entered ad-tech judgment is worth before the 10-28 print.
- *The "unmeasurable" leg dies* if the **October Employment Situation moves off 2026-10-02** — a
  clean tape would make the date measurable and the call would have to be re-argued from magnitude
  alone. (It would not make it wrong; **−0.19%** is still the comparable.)
- *The stand-aside dies* if the filing (or the redacted opinion published after 09-16) discloses a
  **structural** obligation — divestiture or forced open-sourcing — not in the reported behavioral
  set. That would mean the 09-02 reporting was wrong about the direction of the ruling, and this
  stops being paperwork. This is the same kill the sibling ledger's `FT-…-2` already tests, and it
  scores partly off *this* event's document.
- *The 86-day projection dies* on an **entered** ad-tech Final Judgment appearing on the docket
  **before 2026-10-28** — the "it lands after the print" half of the quarter call would be wrong,
  and the `goog-2026-10-28-print` exit trigger would become live inside its own window after all.
- *The "no new information" read of the filing dies* if either party files **separately** with a
  public statement characterising the other's proposal as reopening structural relief — a docket
  fight over scope is different in kind from a docket fight over wording.

**Two forward tests registered 2026-09-08**, both in

**TL;DR.** This is the 20-year reopening of the Aug-19 new issue (which graded mildly weak: 0.5bp

not anticipate: **the risk has migrated from the supply leg to the policy leg.** Warsh's Jackson

support to $4B/operation from **9/9**, directly under this tenor, six days before the auction. So
the 48-hour pair still deserves the same guard — but the heavier half is now the **meeting**, not
the auction. Auction **date confirmed** (TSY tentative schedule; one aggregator dating it 9/17

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the 20-year bond reopening (1:00pm ET, confirmed) reopens today's Aug-19
new issue and lands the day before the September FOMC, in the middle of the long-end term-premium
sell-off (30Y at a 19-year high). What is priced going in, how has 20Y demand been trending, how has
the market reacted to weak vs. strong 20Y results, and which tracked names carry the most exposure
into an auction-then-FOMC 48 hours?

**One-line verdict:** the setup is a two-sided variance event whose supply risk (a soft reopening
extending the July deterioration) is stacked directly against the next-day FOMC's policy-rate risk —
the one adjacency in the month where term premium and Fed path can compound on consecutive days.
The 20Y has been the awkward tenor (July auction tailed on collapsed direct demand), the reopening's
demand inherits today's Aug-19 result (not yet known), and the reaction function says a soft print
hits high-duration, debt-financed AI names hardest — CRWV already moved −12.1% on the fear alone.
This is a no-new-risk, defined-risk window, not a rate-direction bet.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed; size/WI-yield/recent-auction figures **estimate**/press-sourced).** No
new duration-sensitive positions opened into the 1:00pm ET release or carried naked across the
9/15→9/16 auction→FOMC pair. The 20Y is the awkward tenor (July auction tailed on collapsed direct

no-new-risk window, not a trade.

**Stance sharpening (2026-08-29, D-17; receipt: the 2026-08-29 ledger row below).** The guard is
unchanged — still no new duration risk into either day of the pair — but **the two legs have
swapped weight**, and the doc should say so rather than let the original framing stand. The
initial research called this "a term-premium/supply story, not a Fed story" (leg 2). Since
Warsh's 2026-08-28 keynote that is no longer the better half of the sentence: September hike odds
went ~35% → 56–59% while the 30Y *held* at 5.19% and the 10Y was little changed — the hawkish
repricing was expressed almost entirely in the front end (2Y +6–9bp to ~4.30%), a bear-flattening.
Add the Treasury buyback doubling to $4B/operation in the 10–20Y sector effective **9/9**
(treasury.gov, primary) and the supply leg is *better* supported than at D-27, while the policy
leg went from a ~73%-hold near-consensus to a coin flip. Concretely: the D-27 kill switch "the
Sep-16 FOMC itself (hawkish surprise / hike) would re-price the whole curve" has stopped being a
tail and become roughly half the distribution. Nothing here licenses an entry — the FOMC date
governs action for the meeting, this auction's `confirmed` date governs only the auction, and no
size/WI figure exists until the ~9/9 announcement.

**Kill switches:**

- **Confirmed-weak print** (bid-to-cover materially below ~2.55, a positive tail, or direct-bidder
  share again well under the ~20–24% norm as in July) — the deterioration is confirmed; caution
  escalates into the next-day FOMC.
- **Firm/strong print** (bid-to-cover ≥2.75, negative tail, indirect ≥71%) — near-term relief for
  the auction-specific fear, but does **not** kill the term-premium stance and does not pre-empt the
  next-day policy-rate risk.
- **Today's Aug-19 new-issue result** (the direct predecessor) landing weak — a positive tail or
  low direct share — raises the bar for this reopening and should be read straight into this stance
  at the next pulse.
- **The Sep-16 FOMC itself** (hawkish surprise / hike) would re-price the whole curve the day after
  this auction — the auction and the meeting are a linked 48-hour pair, not independent.
- **A repeat >5% single-session move in CRWV or another high-duration name** on or around auction
  day confirms leg 5's reaction-function read live in the current cycle.

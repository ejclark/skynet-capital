### Legs tested

1. **"The ruling date, case numbers, and holding are correctly sourced."** **SUPPORTED, and now on a primary rather than a press-relayed basis.** curia.europa.eu **Press Release No 96/26**, "Luxembourg, 8 July 2026," reads verbatim: *"Judgment of the General Court in Joined Cases T-1079/23, T-1080/23 | Apple v Commission and T-214/24 Apple and Apple Distribution International v Commission... "Around September 18" is MacObserver's own hedge, computed by treating delivery and notification as the same day — a reasonable default absent better information, but a computation, not a fact read off a docket. **This ledger keeps the date estimate for two independent reasons now, not one: the standing schema gap (no confirmed-prefix slot for a CJEU procedural deadline) and the notification-date gap identified here** — so even if a future session found a confirmed-prefix source, the date would still need the actual notification date to be read as fact.

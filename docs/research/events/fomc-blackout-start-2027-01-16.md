## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This is the fourth blackout in the tracked calendar and its three siblings reached three different
conclusions — [September](fomc-blackout-start-2026-09-05.md) called a no-short-vol window because
top-tier data was trapped inside the gate, [October](fomc-blackout-start-2026-10-17.md) refused that
rule because its corridor was empty, and [November](fomc-blackout-start-2026-11-28.md) found the gate
outlives the decision by a session with the November CPI in the overhang. So: which shape is January
2027, and what — if anything — does it add that
[`fomc-2027-01-27`](fomc-2027-01-27.md) does not already own?

**One-line verdict.** None of the three transfers, and the reason is that **this gate's contents are
corporate, not macro**. The January FOMC and this book's Q4 print cluster are both pinned to the last
week of January, so the gate's final session — Thursday **2027-01-28**, still inside the blackout —
has historically been an event-sized day for the nine tracked names while the Fed is barred from
speaking. That, plus a **deadline at the 2027-01-15 close**, is this document's whole marginal
contribution. Date `estimate`; no directional call, no size.

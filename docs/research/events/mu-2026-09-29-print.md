## Initial research

**One-line verdict.** No directional edge survives instrument scrutiny — every positioning-shaped
hypothesis (pre-print run-up, gap direction, post-print drift) either fails its control outright or
isn't tested to a confident bar yet — so this is a guard-rails-only event (S2, E1) plus one
genuinely interesting observation (a persistent reaction-day fade) registered for future scoring,

either way.

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** This is the **long end of the sell-off, reopened** — the Sep-10 sale reopens the Aug-13
30-year bond that cleared at **5.216%, its highest auction yield in ~25 years (since 2001)** and
**tailed** (stopped +0.4bp above when-issued) on a below-average bid-to-cover of 2.39 with dealers
absorbing more than their 12-month norm. Unlike the 10Y (which is well-bid), the 30Y is exactly
where the **term-premium/fiscal weakness concentrates** — the 30Y yield hit a fresh **19-year high
~5.33% intraday on 8/18**. This auction is the day *after* the 10Y reopening (9/9) and the day
*before* August CPI (9/11): the middle of a three-day compound-rate corridor. A soft print
extending the tailing pattern is the base-case risk, and it hits high-duration, debt-financed AI
names first. Auction **date confirmed**; every size/when-issued/demand figure below is
**estimate/press-sourced** and drifts. No symbol-keyed action — guard-shaped, no-new-duration-risk.
**Update 2026-09-03 (D7):** the size prior stopped being an inference. Treasury's **2026-08-05
quarterly refunding statement (sb0590)**, whose size table was read directly this session, publishes
an *anticipated* **Sep-26 30-Year size of $22B** (the 30Y row runs 25/22/22 per quarter — new issue,
then two reopenings) under explicit guidance that Treasury "anticipates maintaining nominal coupon
and FRN auction sizes for at least the next several quarters." A print away from $22B at today's
11:00 ET announcement would mean Treasury breaking its own published guidance — a term-premium
event, not a technicality. The tape **consolidated rather than retraced**: 9/1→9/2 the par curve was
unchanged at 2Y **4.39** / 10Y **4.79** / 30Y **5.27**, 2s30s flat at 88bp for a third session, with
Brent holding **$95.63**. And a three-row honest gap closes — **MOVE is readable again at 79.71
(9/2), +8.7pts (+12%) since the 8/28 Warsh session**, while VIX *fell* to 15.20: rates vol and
equity vol are decoupling, and the stress sits in this auction's own asset class.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the 30-year bond reopening (1:00pm ET, confirmed) is the single sharpest
expression of the long-end term-premium sell-off — 30Y yields at a 19-year high — reopening the
Aug-13 issue that already cleared at a 25-year-high yield and tailed. It sits the day after the 10Y
reopening and the day before August CPI. What is priced going in, how has 30Y demand been trending,
how would the market react to a weak vs. strong result, and which tracked names carry the most
exposure?

**One-line verdict:** the long-end weakness is real and concentrated here — the Aug-13 predecessor
tailed on below-average demand at a 25-year-high yield, and the yield has since pushed to a fresh
19-year high — so a soft Sep-10 reopening extending that pattern is the base-case risk, not a
surprise. The result is partly pre-priced by yields already near cycle highs; the live tail is a
genuinely ugly stop that tails *through* when-issued on collapsing demand. Stacked between the 10Y
(9/9) and CPI (9/11), this is a defined-risk, no-new-duration window, not a rate-direction bet.

**Method:** sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md)
(rates/Treasury-auction mode: no price instruments). Primary source for existence/date/time is
treasury.gov's tentative auction schedule (matches the checked-in calendar entry exactly); the
Aug-13 predecessor auction's metrics are cross-checked across Exante Data (auction summary), CRFB
("Treasury auction yield hits highest in 25 years," 2026-08-14), and helious.io (accessed
2026-08-19), which agree on the headline (5.216%, B/C 2.39, tailed, dealer ~11.5%). Market/Fed
context sourced press (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), each claim dated in-line. FOMC odds carried from
[`fomc-2026-09-16.md`](fomc-2026-09-16.md).

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date, time, and confirmed status are right — SUPPORTED.** treasury.gov's tentative
   auction schedule places the 30-year bond reopening at 1:00pm ET on Thursday 2026-09-10, matching
   the checked-in calendar entry (`TSY:`, formal announce ~6bd prior, checked 2026-08-18) exactly.
   Formal size announcement ~6 business days ahead (~2026-09-04); reopening size not yet fixed. No
   date discrepancy found.

2. **This is a term-premium/supply story, not a Fed story — SUPPORTED, and the 30Y is its sharpest
   expression.** The 30-year yield touched a fresh **19-year high ~5.323% intraday on 2026-08-18**
   before easing to ~5.282% (CNBC, businessstory.org, 2026-08-18), up **>40bp since late June**,
   *despite* the Fed on hold and July core CPI tame. CRFB's 2026-08-14 note frames the Aug-13
   auction's 5.216% clear as "the highest [auction yield] in 25 years," attributed to "investors'
   concern over Treasuries and demand for higher compensation to finance our growing national debt"
   ($432B July deficit, ~$1.7T corporate issuance, oil). The 30Y is the tenor doing the selling.

3. **Recent 30Y demand is soft and the most recent print tailed — SUPPORTED (caution: n=4, secondary
   trackers).** The Aug-13 **new issue** ($31.3B) that this auction reopens:

   Bid-to-cover has drifted down off the strong February print (2.66→2.30→2.39), the Aug-13 stop
   **tailed** against when-issued, and dealer absorption sits above its 12-month norm — all
   consistent with a long end that is being force-fed duration. Yields stepped up net across the
   run (4.75%→5.05%→5.22%), consistent with the broader selloff. Per the TIPS sibling doc, Aug-13
   was the **third tailed long-end auction in a row** — a genuine demand-deterioration pattern.

4. **The result is partly pre-priced; the live tail risk is a genuinely ugly stop — SUPPORTED.**
   With the 30Y already at a 19-year high, a soft-but-orderly reopening is largely in the price; the
   asymmetric risk is a stop that tails materially *through* when-issued on a bid-to-cover breaking
   below ~2.3 — not merely "a high yield," which the market has already absorbed.

5. **A weak long-end print hits high-duration, debt-financed AI names hardest and fastest —
   SUPPORTED, live tape.** On 2026-08-18, on long-end rate fear alone, CRWV fell **−12.1%** (Motley
   Fool / DailyPolitical, 2026-08-18) on debt-financing/capex concerns ($35–39B forward capex,
   debt/equity >14); NVDA −2.4% and semis (SOX −5%) the same session (per
   [`treasury-30y-tips-2026-08-20.md`](treasury-30y-tips-2026-08-20.md)); Nasdaq −1.33%. Sensitivity
   tier: **CRWV** highest (debt-financed datacenter build), then **NVDA/AVGO/MRVL** (high-multiple
   semis), then mega-cap MSFT/GOOG/META (fortress balance sheets mute financing), least direct
   AAPL/AMZN.

6. **The Fed-path channel (Sep-16 FOMC) is separate and still live — SUPPORTED**, per
   [`fomc-2026-09-16.md`](fomc-2026-09-16.md): hawkish-hold base case ~60–70%, a 25bp **hike** live
   at ~30–40%, cut priced at 0%. This auction tests fiscal/supply-driven term premium; the FOMC six
   days later tests the policy-rate path. They compound but are distinct.

**What plays the conditions support:** none directional — the guard-shaped house answer. No new
duration-sensitive entries into the 1:00pm ET release, and none carried naked through the
9/9→9/10→9/11 (10Y · 30Y · CPI) corridor; any existing high-duration exposure (CRWV highest-beta,
then NVDA/AVGO/MRVL) sized as if both tails are live through the Sep-16 FOMC.

**Honest limits.** The reopening size and when-issued yield for Sep-10 do not exist yet (formal
announcement ~2026-09-04). The Aug-13 predecessor metrics come from secondary trackers agreeing on
the headline; the raw TreasuryDirect result PDF (`R_20260813_3.pdf`) is image-based and was not
parsed to text this session — re-verify bidder shares against fiscaldata.treasury.gov's structured
dataset. n=4 recent 30Y prints is thin for a "trend," though direction (softening long-end demand)
is consistent and corroborated by the TIPS sibling doc's "third tailed long-end auction" framing.
This doc is written **before** the Sep-10 result and before the intervening 10Y (9/9) and CPI
(9/11) — those are the load-bearing facts the next assessment must carry.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed; size/WI-yield/recent-auction figures **estimate**/press-sourced).** No
new duration-sensitive positions opened into the 1:00pm ET release or carried naked through the
9/9→9/11 stack. The 30Y is the sharpest expression of the long-end term-premium selloff; the Aug-13
predecessor tailed at a 25-year-high yield on below-average demand, so a soft Sep-10 reopening
extending that pattern is the base-case risk, partly pre-priced by yields near cycle highs. Existing
high-duration exposure — **CRWV** (highest beta, −12.1% on 8/18 fear alone), then **NVDA/AVGO/MRVL**
— held only sized for both tails. No directional rates bet; a no-new-risk window, not a trade.

**Refinements (added 2026-08-30; receipt: the D11 ledger row).** The guard stance is unchanged, but
two of its premises are now sharper:

1. **Sign correction on the Fed channel.** Warsh's 2026-08-28 Jackson Hole turn was hawkish, and the
   repricing landed almost entirely in the front end (par curve 8/27→8/28: 2Y +14bp, 3Y +11, 5Y +10,
   10Y +6, 20Y +3, **30Y +3bp** to 5.22%), flattening 2s30s from ~99bp to ~88bp in one session. A
   credible inflation-fighting Fed compresses the *inflation-risk-premium* leg of term premium, so on
   this evidence a hawkish surprise is a mild **positive** for the long end — the opposite sign to the
   initial research's implicit read that hawkishness and long-end weakness move together. It does not
   relieve the fiscal/supply leg, which is the larger part of the story.
2. **Print-quality bars belong on a reopening scale, not a new-issue one.** Re-verified against
   fiscaldata.treasury.gov, every 2026 30Y **reopening** printed bid-to-cover 2.42 / 2.45 / 2.39 /
   2.33 / 2.44 (mean ≈2.41) on a flat **$22.0B** size, with indirects 60.0–77.7%; **no** 2026 30Y
   reopening reached the ≥2.5 bid-to-cover the kill switches call "strong." The original bars were
   anchored to the Aug-13 *new issue* ($25.0B), which is a different instrument.

**Refinements (added 2026-09-02; receipt: the D8 ledger row).** The guard stance is unchanged. Two
further premises move, one of them against the prior row:

3. **Refinement 1's long-end benefit expired inside two sessions.** The 8/28 bear-flattening was
   real and the sign correction stands as a description of *that day*, but it did not persist: par
   yields 8/28→9/1 moved 2Y 4.34→**4.39**, 10Y 4.73→**4.79**, 30Y 5.22→**5.27** — a near-parallel
   +5/6bp shift that left 2s30s flat at **88bp**. The long end stopped being the beneficiary of the
   hawkish turn within two sessions; treat refinement 1 as a one-session observation, not a standing
   property of the curve into 9/10.
4. **The oil leg re-activated, reversing the prior two rows.** D14 and D11 both logged the oil leg
   of the term-premium story easing (Brent $93.09 → $90.07). Renewed US strikes on Iran mid-session
   **2026-09-01** took Brent to **$94.65** at the close and **$95.39** on 9/2 (+5.9% vs D11), a
   ~six-week high, and that move — not the morning's soft ISM/JOLTS — is what reversed the bond
   rally that day. The inflation-risk-premium leg the initial research named is live again, eight
   days before the auction and ten before CPI.
5. **The same-day buyback is not support for this bond — primary-sourced.** See the new "No same-day
   rescue bid" signal bullet: the 09-10 operation is 10-20Y (range 09/11/2036–09/10/2046) and cannot
   buy CUSIP 912810UW6. D14's "buyback support the day before this auction" and D11's "same-day,
   adjacent-sector" were both directionally right but overstated the benefit to *this* line; the
   first eligible operation is 2026-09-24 (proposed to the calendar this PR).

**Refinements (added 2026-09-03; receipt: the D7 ledger row).** The guard stance is unchanged. Two
premises get firmer evidence, and one of them retires an open gap the last three rows carried:

6. **The $22B size prior is primary-sourced guidance, not an empirical inference.** Refinement 2
   established "$22.0B for every 2026 30Y reopening" from fiscaldata's auction history — a backward
   look. Treasury's quarterly refunding statement **sb0590 (2026-08-05)**, whose size table was read
   directly from the HTML this session, *forward*-publishes the anticipated monthly nominal coupon
   sizes for the Aug–Oct quarter: 30-Year **25 / 22 / 22** (Aug new issue, Sep and Oct reopenings),
   10-Year 42/39/39, 20-Year 16/13/13, alongside the sentence "Treasury anticipates maintaining
   nominal coupon and FRN auction sizes for at least the next several quarters." The practical
   consequence is a sharper falsifier: $22B today is the *expected* outcome and carries no
   information, while a deviation is Treasury departing from published guidance — which is itself
   the fiscal-supply leg of this stance firing, and should be read that way rather than as an
   auction-mechanics detail. (Caution retained: sb0590's table is *anticipated* sizes; the formal
   announcement at ~11:00 ET on 2026-09-03 is what fixes it, and had not published when this row
   was written.)
7. **The rates-vol gap closes, and it points the same way the stance does.** D14 logged MOVE at 73
   and D11/D8 both recorded that no post-Warsh print was reachable — three rows of an unrefreshed
   reading. A daily MOVE series is readable this session: **69.86 (8/27) → 70.97 (8/28) → 75.32
   (8/31) → 77.88 (9/1) → 79.71 (9/2)**, a five-session climb of **+8.74pts / +12.3%** off the
   Warsh session. Over the same span VIX went 14.43 → **15.20**, i.e. *down* 1.14 from its 9/1
   spike. Equity vol and rates vol have decoupled, with the entire vol expansion sitting in the
   asset class this auction prices, seven days out. This does not change the call — it is the
   measured version of the risk the guard stance was already taking on narrative.

**Kill switches:**

- **Confirmed-weak print** (bid-to-cover breaking below ~2.3, a positive tail wider than Aug-13's
  +0.4bp, or indirect falling below the mid-60s) — elevates this from "watch" to "the long-end
  deterioration is accelerating, size accordingly," read together with the 10Y the day before and
  CPI the day after.
- **Firm/strong print** (bid-to-cover ≥2.5, negative tail, indirect near/above ~68%) — near-term
  relief for the auction-specific fear, but does **not** kill the broader term-premium stance
  (deficits, corporate-debt competition, oil) without confirmation from the surrounding prints.
- **A hot August CPI (9/11)** the day after would compound a soft 30Y into a broad duration
  selloff — the auction and the print are a linked pair that week, not independent events.
- **A repeat >5% single-session move in CRWV or another high-duration name** on or around auction
  day confirms leg 5's reaction-function read live in the current cycle.
- **Reopening-relative reading of the two bars above (added 2026-08-30; the bars themselves stand
  as written).** On the 2026 reopening scale established in refinement 2, "weak" at bid-to-cover
  below ~2.3 sits just under June's 2.33 floor — well calibrated. "Strong" at ≥2.5 sits above every
  2026 30Y reopening, so treat **≳2.45 with a negative tail** as the reachable strong-print reading
  and ≥2.5 as an exceptional one; likewise "indirect below the mid-60s" is the reopening *median*
  (60.0–77.7% range), so it warrants attention rather than the confirmed-weak label on its own.

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** On the morning of **Wed 2026-09-16** — a date this PR moves from `estimate` to **confirmed** off two Cboe primary surfaces — every September VIX future and VIX option settles to a **Special Opening Quotation (VRO)**: a one-shot batch of opening-auction prints in SPX options, not a screen price anyone can trade. Three things make this settlement worth a calendar row and nothing more. **First, it is the opening bell of the corridor, not a sideshow** — the vol complex settles at the 09-16 open, the SPX index options settle at the 09-18 open, and single-stock options plus the S&P quarterly rebalance settle at the 09-18 close, so the hedging cushion starts leaving two sessions before the triple witching everyone watches. **Second, its measurement window is maximally event-loaded**: the SOQ is struck from a *single* SPX series expiring exactly 30 days later (2026-10-16), and that 09-16→10-16 window contains the FOMC decision **that same afternoon**, the 09-18 witching, jobs 10-02, CPI 10-14 and the 10-16 expiration itself — all confirmed on this calendar. **Third, the market already charges for that**: the expiring contract (VX/U6) settled **16.1381** on 09-03 against VIX cash at **14.32**, a **+1.82-point** premium that has to converge into the 09-16 auction. None of that is an edge — it is the price. No house playbook (S1/S2/E1/S3/S4/G1) is keyed to options expiration or vol settlement, this repo has no vol-settlement instrument, and the settlement-anomaly literature that would justify anything sharper is contested and pre-2018. So the honest output is one execution guard — **nothing enters or exits on the 09-16 opening auction** — plus a note that the September VIX line settles about 4.5 hours *before* the decision it was priced for, which means FOMC-day vol exposure lives in the October contract (VX/V6, 18.1013 on 09-03) or in SPX options, never in the expiring September one. Impact stays **low** on purpose: a confirmed date that widens caution about one 30-minute window licenses nothing.

## Initial research

**The question.** What actually settles on 2026-09-16, is the date real, and does a VIX expiration landing on the morning of an FOMC decision — two sessions before the September triple witching — create anything this repo should act on, or only something it should avoid?

**One-line verdict:** the date is confirmed off Cboe primaries and the mechanics are genuinely distinctive (a single-expiration opening-auction print, struck before the decision it prices, opening a three-stage settlement corridor), but every distinctive feature is already in the term structure, no house playbook or instrument touches vol settlement, and the anomaly literature is contested and pre-2018 — so the output is one execution guard, not a trade.

**Method.** Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no earnings-style instrument applies (`earnings-cycle.mjs` / `intraday-edges.mjs` are symbol-keyed; this event has no symbols) and no opex- or vol-shaped instrument exists in `scripts/research/`. Sourced web research with primary sources preferred: Cboe's own VIX-futures product page and daily settlement statistics for the contract, date and prices; Cboe/vendor methodology write-ups for the SOQ construction; the academic settlement-anomaly literature for the distortion claim; BLS and press for the same-week macro. Index levels (VIX, VIX9D, VVIX, SPX) pulled directly from the daily-close series the repo's own probe reads (`scripts/event-material-scan.mjs`'s Yahoo chart endpoint), 2026-09-04. Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md` for playbook fit, and against `src/domain/market-events-data.ts` for the corridor's confirmed adjacencies and for the contents of the settlement strip's own 30-day window.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date is right, and it is now primary-confirmed — SUPPORTED (status flipped in this PR).** The seeding entry filed this `estimate` on 2026-09-01 with the note that it "stays estimate until an OCC/Cboe primary confirms it," because Cboe's product page did not serve its calendar that day. Today it did: the VIX-futures product page's market-data table lists **VX/U6 with expiration 09/16/2026**, and `cboe.com/us/futures/market_statistics/settlement` carries daily settlement prices for that same VX/U6 expiration on 09-02 and 09-03 (both fetched 2026-09-04). The deterministic rule checks out independently — **09-16 is a Wednesday exactly 30 calendar days before 2026-10-16**, October's third Friday — and Cboe's own next listing confirms the rule is being applied unchanged (VX/V6 expires **2026-10-21**, exactly 30 days before 2026-11-20). Two Cboe surfaces plus an arithmetic check is a primary confirmation, so this PR flips the entry to `confirmed` with an `OCC:` prefix. This is the event's own initial research, which is the sanctioned place to flip a date the discovering sweep correctly declined to self-confirm.

2. **What settles, and how it differs from the VIX you see quoted — SUPPORTED.** VIX futures and options are **AM-settled to a Special Opening Quotation (VRO)**, not to any VIX close or intraday level. Cboe computes it from **opening-auction trade prices** (the Hybrid Opening System) of SPX/SPXW options in a **single expiration exactly 30 calendar days from the settlement day** — here the **2026-10-16** series — falling back to the bid-ask midpoint only where a strike did not trade at the open. The regular VIX index, by contrast, blends *two* series (near-term and next-term) to a constant 30 days and uses midpoints throughout. Expiring futures cease trading at **09:00 ET** on settlement morning; expiring options ceased at the prior close. Net: the number that settles every September VIX contract is a one-shot batch of opening prints across many strikes, including illiquid deep-OTM puts — a construction that can diverge from where the future was quoted minutes earlier, and that nobody can trade against once struck.

3. **This settlement's strip is unusually event-loaded — SUPPORTED (mechanical, checked against the calendar).** Because the strip is the 2026-10-16 SPX series, the SOQ measures expected vol over **09-16 → 10-16**, and that window contains, all `confirmed` on this calendar: the **FOMC decision that same afternoon** (09-16), the **triple witching** (09-18), **jobs** (10-02), **FOMC minutes** (10-07), **CPI** (10-14), **PPI and retail sales** (10-15) and the **10-16 expiration** itself. So the September SOQ is struck at close to the most event-dense reading a 30-day window gets in this quarter. That is a reason the expiring contract carries a premium — not a reason to expect a surprise.

4. **The market already prices every distinctive feature above — SUPPORTED, quantified (Cboe daily settlements, 2026-09-03).** VX/U6 (the expiring contract) settled **16.1381**; VX/V6 settled **18.1013**; VIX cash closed **14.32**. That is a **+1.82-point** front-month premium owed to the 09-16 auction and a **+1.96** U6→V6 spread — steep contango, i.e. the term structure is already paying for exactly the event density leg 3 describes. Anything this doc could "discover" about the corridor is therefore in the price; the only unpriced thing is *execution*, which is where the guard lives.

5. **Front-end vol is priced for calm; the 30-day window is not — SUPPORTED (own computation off Cboe index closes, 2026-09-04 intraday).** VIX9D **11.38**, VIX **14.07**, VVIX **82.43** (from 83.80 on 09-03). The ~**2.7-point** gap between the 9-day and 30-day measures sits precisely in the stretch that carries the FOMC and the witching — the market is charging for the corridor and not for the days before it. Recent VIX closes for context: 08-28 **14.43**, 08-31 **14.92**, 09-01 **16.34**, 09-02 **15.20**, 09-03 **14.32**. Named as a configuration, **not a forecast** — this is a computation, not a house-validated statistic.

6. **Settlement-day distortion is documented, contested, and mostly pre-2018 — MIXED, and treated as unresolved.** Griffin & Shams, *Manipulation in the VIX?* (**Review of Financial Studies, 2018**), find that SPX option volume spikes at the exact settlement moment, concentrated in out-of-the-money strikes used in the VIX calculation and strongest where a strike has the largest, most discontinuous influence on the index; they test hedging and liquidity explanations and report support for a manipulation reading. Press summaries of that literature put the average settlement effect near **0.31 vol points** and cite an aggregate distortion of ~**$4.7B** across June 2011–August 2018. Cboe publicly disputed the manipulation interpretation. A 2016–2025 replication exists (a Tilburg thesis, `arno.uvt.nl` fid=192339) but its PDF is image-based and **could not be read here** — so whether the effect survived Cboe's post-2018 auction changes is **unresolved in this doc, and nothing below assumes it did**. What survives regardless of that debate is the uncontested mechanic in leg 2: an opening-auction batch print, with thin deep-OTM strikes in it.

7. **The corridor's first distortion window is 09-16 at the open, not 09-18 — SUPPORTED.** The staging is: **VIX SOQ, 09-16 open → SPX index-option SET, 09-18 open → single-stock options + the S&P quarterly rebalance MOC, 09-18 close** (the last two per the sibling [`opex-2026-09-18`](opex-2026-09-18.md) ledger). And the 09-16 auction is the worst-conditioned of the three: it follows **two 08:30 ET macro prints an hour earlier** (retail sales and import/export prices, both confirmed) on an FOMC decision morning — precisely the setup that widens opening quotes on the deep-OTM SPX puts leg 2 names as the SOQ's fragile input. This is why the event earns a row at all: "the gamma rolls off Friday" understates when the cushion actually starts leaving.

8. **The documented FOMC-day vol behavior lands *after* this settlement — SUPPORTED (literature, read as summaries).** The FOMC-volatility literature reports that VIX tends to **decline on decision days**, largely independent of content (the pre-FOMC-drift strand, and work on VIX futures/options trading activity around FOMC announcements, *International Review of Financial Analysis*, 2024). The SOQ is struck ~**4.5 hours before** the 14:00 ET decision, so the September contract settles at the **pre-decision** level and never sees that decline. The read-through is structural, not directional: exposure meant to survive the decision has to sit in **October (VX/V6, 18.1013)** or in SPX options, because the expiring September line is gone by 09:00 ET. **Caveat:** these are search-surfaced summaries of the literature, not full texts read here, and no house data tests them.

9. **No house playbook is opex- or vol-settlement-keyed — SUPPORTED.** Same read the sibling witching doc reached: S1/G1 are pre-print run-up plays, S2 is the never-hold-the-print guard, S3 a reaction-day fade, all earnings-dated; E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution rule; S4 is an overnight-vs-hold structural note. None references expiration or volatility settlement, and the sweep's kill list contains no vol hypothesis to re-check. The one real interaction is that **E1 gains a third reason on 09-16** — the SOQ auction, two 08:30 prints, and an FOMC day stacked on one open. That sharpens an existing execution rule; it is not a new signal.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing directional, and no sizing. One execution guard: **no paper entry, exit or resize on the 09-16 opening auction**, and the vol complex's first prints that morning get read as auction artifacts rather than information. One framing carried into the sibling docs: the 09-16→09-18 **corridor** starts on the 16th, so any read of "the tape was calm into the witching" that week should account for the cushion having begun to leave two sessions early. And one placement note that is structural rather than a call: FOMC-day vol exposure cannot live in the expiring September VIX line.

**Honest limits.** Only the **date** is confirmed; everything trading-adjacent here is mechanics or estimate-grade context, and `impact: low` is deliberate — this widens caution about one 30-minute window and licenses no entry. The VX settlement prices are Cboe **daily settlements as of 09-03** (T-1 at time of writing), not live quotes. The VIX9D/VIX spread in leg 5 is this session's own computation off index closes, not a vendor or house statistic. ## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `confirmed`, Cboe-sourced as of 2026-09-04).** Treat **2026-09-16, 09:30 ET** as a known-date, **low-impact microstructure marker** — the first of three staged settlements in the 09-16→09-18 corridor — and never as a tradeable event. No position is opened, closed or sized because of it. One guard applies: nothing executes on that day's **opening auction**, on any name, and the vol complex's opening prints that morning are treated as settlement artifacts rather than signal. Two structural facts are carried forward rather than acted on: the SOQ is built from a **single 2026-10-16 SPX strip** whose window is unusually event-dense (FOMC same afternoon, witching +2 sessions, CPI 10-14), and the expiring **September contract settles ~4.5 hours before the decision**, so FOMC-day vol exposure lives in October or in SPX options. Everything distinctive about the event is already visible in the term structure (VX/U6 **16.1381** vs VIX cash **14.32** on 09-03) — the doc's contribution is timing discipline, not an edge.

**Kill switches:**

- **Cboe publishes a different final settlement date for VX/U6** — the corridor's start moves and the confirmed status must revert; re-check `cboe.com/us/futures/market_statistics/settlement` at every pulse rather than trusting this row.
- **The 09-16 FOMC decision moves, or is released off its 14:00 ET slot** — leg 8's "settles before the news" placement note dies, and the corridor framing has to be redrawn around the new decision time.
- **VX/U6 trades below VIX cash before 09-16 (front-month backwardation)** — the market has stopped paying for the event window, the "+1.82 owed to the auction" framing inverts, and the guard needs re-reading rather than repeating.
- **The 09-16 VRO prints within ~0.2 of the 09-15 VIX cash close on ordinary SPX opening-auction volume** — the settlement passed without leaving a mark; the distortion-window guard is over-specified and shrinks to a one-line note in the December cycle.
- **A house vol/opex instrument gets built and back-tested** — legs 6 and 8 are borrowed from outside literature and should be re-tested against real data rather than carried on citation.

**Registered forward test.** `FT-vix-expiration-2026-09-16-1` in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md) — a mechanic test, not a directional one: that the 09-16 SOQ (VRO), struck at the open, prints **above** the same day's VIX cash close, because the documented FOMC-day vol decline lands entirely after settlement.

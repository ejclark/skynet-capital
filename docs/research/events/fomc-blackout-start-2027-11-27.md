## Initial research

### The question, plainly

Nine blackout ledgers precede this one, and two threads run through all of them. The first is the
**overhang** — the gate's last session, which falls after the decision.
[November 2026](fomc-blackout-start-2026-11-28.md) named it,
[January 2027](fomc-blackout-start-2027-01-16.md) made it a sizing rule,
[March](fomc-blackout-start-2027-03-06.md) refused it, [May](fomc-blackout-start-2027-05-29.md)
reversed March, [September](fomc-blackout-start-2027-09-04.md) refused it on geometry and
[October](fomc-blackout-start-2027-10-16.md) refused it on content. The second is October's own
contribution: the **earnings-gate / clean-gate** split, measured on SEC 8-K Item 2.02 filings by
AAPL/MSFT/AMZN/GOOGL/META, which puts March, June, September and December in the *clean* half at 20 of
20 years each.

Two proposals filed this event and both handed the same hint forward: this is the year's **last**
window, it wraps the year's **final SEP**, and its day one — 2027-12-07 — is unusually early. So the
question is narrow: **does an early December meeting produce a materially different gate, and if it
does, does anything the family already believes have to change?**

**One-line verdict.** It does, twice. The compression pushes the *deadline* onto a stock-exchange half
day — 4 of 167 gates in twenty-one years, three of them consecutive — and it **swaps the window's
corporate content**, which is only invisible because the family measured content on five names. And
separately: the overhang premium those nine ledgers keep arguing about does not survive a pooled sign
test. Date `estimate`; no directional call.

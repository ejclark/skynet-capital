## Initial research

### The question

What, if anything, distinguishes the March 2027 quarterly triple witching from the generic
third-Friday witching this repo has already documented five times — and is the seeding entry right
that it is here "for corridor structure rather than for a new mechanic"?

**One-line verdict:** the seeding entry undersold it — the corridor is a seven-session chain with
three previously-undated legs, the FOMC gap is 2 sessions rather than December's 9, and Good Friday
falling in March compresses the post-witching unpin and quarter-end into fewer sessions than a
normal quarter — but the date is `estimate`, no house playbook is opex-keyed, and the honest output
is a guard plus one registered forward test, not a trade.

### Method

Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no earnings-style
instrument applies and no opex-shaped instrument exists in `scripts/research/`. This session did
**not** take the prior opex ledgers' conclusions on faith; it fetched and parsed primaries directly:

- **Cboe** `cboe.com/us/futures/market_statistics/settlement` (HTTP 200, 536,802 bytes), parsed
  row-by-row into 55 symbol/expiry pairs with weekdays computed and the 30-day rule checked against
  every one.
- **NYSE** `nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars` (HTTP 200, 109,180 bytes), holiday table parsed

  `0001193125-26-031948` (HTTP 200, 2,388,335 bytes), rebalance language extracted verbatim.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is right, and its `estimate` label now rests on a much narrower gap — MIXED
   (upgraded, not promoted).** The third Friday of March 2027 is 2027-03-19 by mechanical weekday
   arithmetic, an unadjusted Friday with no holiday conflict (Good Friday is a week later). Better
   than convention: Cboe's settlement page carries a **live, daily-settling** `VX/G7 - 2027-02-17`
   row (and `VXM/G7`), and VIX contract terms define settlement as the SOQ of the SPX/SPXW series
   expiring **exactly 30 days later** — 2027-02-17 + 30d = **2027-03-19**. The 30-day rule
   reproduces on every VX/VXM row the page carries from 2026-09 through 2027-04. So Cboe is already
   listing a product whose terms reference a 2027-03-19 SPX expiration. **Why this is MIXED and not
   SUPPORTED-to-confirmed:** that evidence dates the *index-options* leg by inference through a
   contract spec, while this entry is titled *triple witching* — index futures + index options +
   single-stock options — and the other two legs still rest on convention alone. The lane's own
   limit (no self-confirming an in-sweep discovery without a primary) and "safety scales to stakes"
   both point the same way: the label costs nothing here, since every honest call is a stand-aside
   either way. **One honest exception found in the data:** the `VX/K7 - 2027-05-18` row is a
   *Tuesday* whose +30d lands Thursday 2027-06-17, one day short of June's third Friday (06-18) —
   the single row of nine that does not reproduce the rule. It is outside this corridor and is not
   relied on here, but it is recorded rather than quietly dropped.

2. **The corridor is denser than the seeding entry stated — SUPPORTED, and it re-dates three legs.**
   The seeding note called it "four dated events in three sessions." Parsing the primaries gives a
   seven-session chain, three of whose legs this calendar did not carry: **Fri 03-12** Select Sector
   rebalance reference close (`estimate`, SEC-filed rule) → **Wed 03-17** VIX/VXM March expiration,
   AM-settling into the opening auction (`estimate`, Cboe-listed `VX/H7` with real settlements
   20.7295 / 20.8641) → **Wed 03-17 14:00 ET** FOMC decision, first SEP of 2027 (`estimate`) →
   **Thu 03-18** BoJ (`estimate`) → **Fri 03-19 08:30 JST** Japan February CPI (`estimate`) →
   **Fri 03-19** this witching, index SET at the open and single-stock options + the rebalance MOC
   at the close. All three new legs are proposed as `estimate` files in this PR.

3. **The vol complex's own expiration leads the equity witching by two sessions and lands on FOMC
   morning — SUPPORTED, and it is an exact rhyme with September 2026.** `VX/H7` expires
   **2027-03-17**, the Wednesday 30 days before the 2027-04-16 SPX expiration — the same day as the
   FOMC decision. Per the mechanics the 2026-09-16 sibling entry banked, the expiring contract stops
   trading 09:00 ET, roughly **five hours before** a 14:00 ET decision, so FOMC-day volatility
   exposure cannot live in it. Two consequences: the vol complex's hedges leave the board on the
   *morning* of the decision, two sessions before the witching; and the front VIX future *into* the
   03-19 witching is therefore the **April** contract (`VX/J7`, 2027-04-21, on the same fetched
   page), referencing an expiration a month past the witching. "The gamma rolls off Friday"
   understates how early the cushion starts leaving — the September ledger's finding, reproduced.

4. **The largest mechanical component of this session's close is SEC-primary-sourced — SUPPORTED,
   and it is better-sourced than the session's own date.** Select Sector SPDR Trust Form 497,
   fetched direct today, states verbatim: *"The rebalancing of the Select Sector Indices … occurs at
   the closing prices of the second Friday of March, June, September and December. Changes will
   become effective after the market close on the third Friday of March, June, September and
   December."* Applied to 2027: reference close **Fri 03-12**, effective **at the 03-19 close** —
   the same closing auction as the single-stock options. The filing also dates a **secondary
   reweighting** off "the second to last business day" of the quarter, which for March 2027 is
   **2027-03-30** (test) / **2027-03-31** (effective after the last business day's close); Good
   Friday 03-26 falls in the prior week and does **not** move either. Note the asymmetry worth
   carrying: the *date of the session* is `estimate`, but the rule putting the rebalance MOC on it
   is SEC-filed and unconditional.

5. **Good Friday falls inside the post-witching window and before quarter-end — SUPPORTED, and this
   is the one genuinely new structural fact.** NYSE's own holiday table, fetched today, gives the
   Good Friday row as `['Friday, April 3', 'Friday, March 26', 'Friday, April 14']` for 2026 / 2027
   / 2028 — the NYSE is closed **2027-03-26**. Independently reproduced by computing Easter
   (2027-03-28; the same computation returns 2026-04-03, matching the page). Sessions from the
   witching close to quarter-end: 03-22, 03-23, 03-24, 03-25, [03-26 closed], 03-29, 03-30, 03-31 —
   **seven, not eight**, split 4 + 3 by the closure. In 2026 Good Friday was 04-03, safely inside
   Q2 and after quarter-end, which is the usual arrangement. So the documented post-witching unpin
   and the quarter-end rebalancing flows have fewer sessions to clear in than a normal quarter — the
   **reverse** of the December 2026 witching, whose unpin ran into a slack holiday-shortened tape.
   Registered as a forward test rather than asserted (leg 7).

6. **The FOMC-to-witching gap is 2 sessions with no inflation print between — SUPPORTED, and it is
   the sharpest contrast with the December 2026 ledger.** That ledger's load-bearing carry-forward
   was "the year's heaviest gamma rolls off **9 trading days** after the Dec-9 dot plot," with a
   CPI the day after the FOMC. Here the first SEP of 2027 concludes **2027-03-17** (`estimate`; the
   Fed's own page calls every 2027 date tentative until the preceding meeting confirms it) and the
   witching is **2027-03-19** — two sessions, and no US CPI or PPI sits between them on this
   calendar. Mechanically: the quarter's concentrated positioning rolls off into a tape that has had
   two sessions to digest a dot plot and has received no confirming inflation data. That is a
   caution about *when* positioning unwinds relative to a catalyst, not a directional call.

7. **The pin-then-unpin pattern remains literature-sourced and house-untested — MIXED, and this
   session finally registers it.** The five prior opex ledgers all cite the same vendor/analytics
   literature (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch, CBOE) for dealers hedging into expiration,
   compressing realized vol, then vol rising once that gamma expires — and all five label it
   "directionally credible, not house-proven." Six ledgers in, that is a leg the repo keeps citing
   and has never tested. The Good Friday compression makes this the cheapest available test: it
   gives a specific, pre-registered, mechanically-scoreable prediction over a named 4-session
   window. Registered as **FT-opex-2027-03-19-1** (score by 2027-03-31) — the first forward test any
   opex ledger in this repo has carried.

8. **No house playbook is opex-keyed — SUPPORTED, re-verified rather than inherited.**
   `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md` grepped this session
   for `opex|expiration|witching`: **zero hits**. S1/G1 are earnings-dated run-up plays, S2 is the
   never-hold-the-print guard (earnings-dated), E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution rule, S3 is a
   reaction-day fade (earnings-dated, blocked on shorting), S4 is a structural note surviving only
   as "prefer close-side executions." The witching sharpens the same two indirect interactions the
   prior ledgers named: E1 gains a second reason (SET AM-settlement distorts the open, and here the
   03-17 VIX SOQ distorts a second open two sessions earlier), and S4's close-side preference runs

9. **No tracked-name earnings are adjacent — SUPPORTED.** No print for any tracked name

   `earnings-calendar.ts`; the mega-cap cluster reports late January and late April, and the

   CPI) and structural (VIX expiry, the rebalance, the holiday) — no single-name interaction.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`).** Treat 2027-03-19 as a known-*shape*, high-impact microstructure
session whose date is not yet primary-confirmed — and specifically **not** as the quiet witching its
seeding entry implied. No position change is triggered by it, no house playbook targets it, and the
`estimate` label bars date-keyed action independently. Elevated execution caution applies to three
auctions, not two: the **2027-03-17 open** (VIX/VXM SOQ), the **2027-03-19 open** (index SET) and
the **2027-03-19 close** (single-stock options + the SEC-dated Select Sector rebalance MOC). The two
facts carried forward are structural, not directional: **(a)** the first SEP of 2027 lands **2
sessions** ahead of this witching with **no US inflation print between**, a materially tighter
sequence than the 9-day, CPI-interrupted December 2026 arrangement; and **(b)** Good Friday
**2027-03-26** puts a market closure *inside* the post-witching window and *before* quarter-end, so
the unpin gets 4 sessions and quarter-end a 3-session stub — compression where December had slack.
The attribution trap the seeding entry named is real and worsened by the added legs: a 2027-03-19
tape move has four available explanations before it has any single one, so **no hypothesis may be
promoted from a post-hoc read of that session.**

  a date other than 2027-03-19** — the corridor re-dates wholesale. Conversely, a calendar listing
  2027-03-19 directly promotes this entry to `confirmed`/`OCC:` and retires the "watch the

  tentative until the preceding meeting confirms it. Observe by **2027-01-27**, when the January
  FOMC confirms the March slot; a move breaks the 2-session gap that is half this stance.
- **Cboe lists the March 2027 VIX/VXM expiration on a date other than 2027-03-17** — would break
  the "vol complex settles on FOMC morning, two sessions ahead" staging and the front-month-is-April
  read with it. Re-check the settlement page at each pulse; the `VX/K7` anomaly above is a live
  reminder that this page's far rows are not uniformly rule-clean.
- **S&P DJI publishes a 2027 rebalance schedule that does not put the March effective date at the
  03-19 close** — would remove the SEC-dated MOC that is currently the best-sourced fact about this
  session. The 497 dates the rule, not the instance.
- **The NYSE publishes a 2027 calendar without a 2027-03-26 closure** — kills the compression
  finding and FT-opex-2027-03-19-1 with it. Re-check nyse.com by **2027-01-04**; the date is not in

  and "pin/unpin is real" legs both stop being literature and start being data; this sheet is then
  rebuilt on measured results rather than cited ones.
- **A tracked name's print moves into the 03-12 → 03-31 corridor** — leg 9's "purely macro and
  structural adjacency" conclusion fails and the single-name interaction has to be worked.

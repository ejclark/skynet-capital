## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Japan sells a **¥250bn** tranche of its 10-year inflation-linked bond on **2026-11-16** (date
and size both **estimate**; terms due **2026-11-09**). **Nothing here is ours to trade** — no tracked
name has yen-rates exposure and no house playbook is rates-keyed. The entry earns its place as a
*measuring stick*: the quarterly linker print is the one place Japan's **breakeven inflation** is
priced on a schedule. On MOF's own record the breakeven proxy (10-year nominal par yield minus the
linker's yield at the lowest accepted price) has climbed from **+1.0** in 2023 to **+2.00** at the
last print (2026-08-12) — a series high — while demand stayed flat at **3.3x** cover. **Price moved,
bidders did not.** The proposal that created this entry also mis-stated the 2026-05 coupon step;
the corrected number is **59.5bp** (0.005% → 0.6%), not 119bp.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

[`jgb-liquidity-enhancement-1-5y-2026-09-10`](jgb-liquidity-enhancement-1-5y-2026-09-10.md) proposed
this entry on 2026-09-15 for one reason: its close-out could not separate a *live preference* for
high-coupon bonds from *rising Japanese coupons* contaminating the "≥1% coupon" bucket, and the
linker is the one JGB whose coupon and real yield are priced directly. **Does the linker record
show a coupon-driven or a demand-driven move, and what does this auction add that the calendar's
other JGB entries do not?**

**One-line verdict:** the move is **price, not demand**. Cover is unchanged across the whole post-2022
sample while the linker's yield rose **+0.86 percentage points since the 2025-05 zero** and the
breakeven proxy doubled. The coupon reset is real but small next to that.

### Conviction legs, tested

**1. The event is real and the date is MOF's — SUPPORTED.** `2611e.htm` prints `Nov. 16, 2026 | 10-year
Inflation-Indexed Bonds`. The cadence is Feb/May/Aug/Nov at four auctions per issue (issues 20–30,
**11 of 11** issues ran four), so **11-16 is the third auction of issue 31** — a reopening of the
2036-03-10 bond that opened 2026-05-01 and reopened 2026-08-12 (the calendar prints `(31)` for both).
The next auction (Feb 2027) closes issue 31; issue 32 opens May 2027.

**2. Terms publish 2026-11-09 at about ¥250bn — SUPPORTED as a rule, not yet observed.** The size has
been **¥250bn at every auction since 2022-08 (n=17; 2,500 億円 in the workbook)**. MOF's note reads
*"announced about one week prior"*; the two linker leads since the coupon reset were **exactly 7
days**: `auct20260424e` (Apr 24 → May 1) and `auct20260805e` (Aug 5 → Aug 12, *"About 250 billion
yen"*, *"a reopening issue of the May 2026 issue"*). Two of two is a thin rule, so it is registered
(`FT-1`).

**3. Demand did not move — SUPPORTED, and it is the entry's cleanest finding.** Cover = competitive
bids ÷ amount accepted, from the workbook:

| Sample | n | Mean cover | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022-05 → 2026-08 (¥200–250bn offers) | 18 | **3.24x** | 2.73x | 4.27x |
| 2024 → 2026-08 | 11 | 3.27x | 2.76x | 4.27x |
| 2025 → 2026-08 | 7 | 3.17x | 2.76x | 3.46x |

The last two prints, the first two at the new 0.6% coupon: **3.40x** (2026-05-01) and **3.27x**
(2026-08-12; bids ¥816.7bn, accepted ¥249.9bn, **21.4285%** allotted at the lowest price). Neither is
outside the sample. By position in an issue (issues 25–30) cover averages 3.64x for the opener and
3.06 / 3.27 / 3.24x thereafter — a small opener premium, nothing that makes 11-16 special. **November
prints (2018+, n=8) average 3.35x, inside the range.** Registered as the cover test (`FT-2`).

The auction-to-auction change has a standard deviation of **0.121pp since 2024** (0.250pp over the
whole record), and two of the last three moves (**+0.345** at 02-10, **+0.282** at 08-12) were each more than
two of those. **Correction to the proposal:** it described the coupon step 0.005% → 0.6% as *"a 119bp
step"*. It is **59.5bp** (a 120× ratio). The mechanism claim survives — MOF reset the coupon as the
yield rose through zero — but the number was off by a factor of two, and
this ledger and the canonical calendar entry both carry the correction.

2. **The terms rule breaks** — terms on a day other than **2026-11-09**, a size other than ¥250bn, or an

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BLS primary re-checked 2026-08-19).** Treat 2026-10-02 08:30 ET as a
high-impact known-date variance event — the last major payroll before the projection-less Oct 27-28
FOMC, so carrying above-average marginal weight — whose reaction sign cannot be fixed at D-44 because
it depends on what the 9/4 jobs print and 9/16 FOMC leave behind. No directional macro position; no
new rate-sensitive paper entries initiated into the print once inside its cadence band. Base case
(estimate-labeled — no consensus, one model projection only): a print continuing the cooling regime,
most plausibly bought as hike relief *if* the hike debate is still live post-September, read as
recession confirmation *if* September tilted toward cuts — deferred to the post-9/16 pulse. The
structural point that holds now: a no-SEP FOMC raises this print's weight, and its decision lands on
the GOOG/META print date, stacking October's back half.

**Kill switches:**

- **The Sep-16 FOMC / 9/4 jobs print flips the debate to hold-vs-cut** — re-bases this print's
  reaction from "weak = hike relief" to "weak = recession confirmation"; re-derive at the first pulse
  after 9/16, don't carry the pre-September framing forward.
- **A hot rebound** — re-arms tightening into the Oct 28 vote with no dot plot to cushion it; the
  hawkish tail, hardest on CRWV and the high-multiple semis — reassess the CPI-corridor skew (10/14).
- **A recession-signal collapse** — trips the tipping point; weak labor reads growth-negative, not
  Fed-relief, and any weak Oct CPI would compound toward stagflation.
- **A published September payroll consensus emerging closer in** — resolves the biggest honest limit;
  re-run the "surprise" framing against it rather than the model projection above.

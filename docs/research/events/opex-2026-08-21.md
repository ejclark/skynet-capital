## At a glance

cycle, not the bigger quarterly kind (that's Sep 18, already coded `impact: high` on this
calendar). The generic pattern, well-documented in options-market literature but not house-tested

volatility "unpin" in the days after as that hedging cushion rolls off. Third-party dealer-gamma

nothing here licenses a trade. The one fact worth carrying forward is timing: this opex lands

## Initial research

**The question.** What are the general mechanics of a monthly options-expiration cycle
(gamma-pinning into the close, typical realized-vol compression, any post-opex vol-pop pattern),
does anything about *this particular* August 2026 cycle stand out (large open interest / max-pain
levels on tracked names, if findable from a reputable source), and how does it interact with

**One-line verdict:** the mechanics are real and well-documented, this cycle's third-party
positioning snapshot is consistent with the textbook pattern, but no house playbook is opex-keyed
— the honest output is a guard (mind the Friday close, watch the NVDA-adjacency timing), not a
trade.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and cycle type are right — SUPPORTED.** OCC's expiration-calendar convention (3rd
   Friday, standard monthly) is corroborated by two independent public calendar sources
   (SpotGamma's expiration-dates page; financialtechwiz's 2026 opex-dates list), both agreeing
   Aug 21, 2026 is a standard monthly expiration and the *next* opex after it, Sep 18, is the
   quarterly triple-witching — matching this calendar's own `impact: medium` vs `impact: high`
   coding for those two entries respectively.

2. **Gamma-pinning / realized-vol compression into expiration is a real, documented mechanic —
   SUPPORTED (general literature, not house-tested).** In a positive-gamma regime, dealers hedge
   by buying dips and selling rallies in progressively larger size as expiration nears, mechanically
   compressing realized volatility and pulling price toward the strikes carrying the most gamma
   (StrikeWatch, SpotGamma, gexmetrix explainers; CBOE's own research on 0DTE index options
   documents the same dealer-hedging mechanism at the daily-expiration extreme). This cycle's
   snapshot is consistent with that regime: SPX net GEX **+$87.6B long**, spot trading above the
   ~$7,739 gamma-flip level (call wall $8,000 / put wall $7,000) as of Aug 17 1:31pm ET (FlashAlpha);
   NVDA net GEX **+$2.0B**, spot pinned between the $220 put wall and $230 call wall, above its
   $205 flip, as of Aug 18 EOD (FlashAlpha, OCC-open-interest-derived per its own methodology note).
   **Caveat:** these are proprietary vendor dealer-positioning models, refreshed daily, not OCC
   primary data — a snapshot, not a forecast through Friday's close.

3. **A post-opex "vol pop" / gamma-unpin pattern — MIXED, real but not house-validated.** Vendor
   literature describes a consistent shape: once front-month gamma expires, the stabilizing dealer
   floor is removed and IV that was inflated by pre-expiration uncertainty collapses ("IV crush"),
   commonly followed by a 1–3% pullback or consolidation as positioning resets — but this
   characterization comes from options-analytics vendor blogs and one CBOE paper on 0DTE dynamics,
   not from a red-teamed instrument run against our own tracked names the way `earnings-cycle.mjs`
   tests earnings reactions. No opex-shaped instrument exists in this repo to confirm or refute it
   statistically — treat the pattern as directionally credible, not house-proven.

4. **This cycle is standard monthly, not quarterly — smaller structural impact — SUPPORTED.**
   Vendor volume comparisons put monthly-opex notional at ~$1–2T vs ~$4–5T on quarterly
   triple-witching (a ~3x gamma-rolloff difference; broad-index gamma concentration in the
   expiring contracts runs 40–50%+ on quarterly cycles). This confirms the calendar's own
   `medium` vs `high` impact split between this event and Sep 18.

5. **Adjacency to NVDA's Aug-26 print is real and load-bearing — SUPPORTED.** This opex lands
   **5 trading days before** NVDA's confirmed Aug-26 print (and 6 before MRVL's confirmed Aug-27
   print). Mechanically: this Friday's front-month gamma roll-off removes the pinning cushion that
   has been dampening the tape, right as the option chain's *pinning anchor* shifts to the
   weeklies that bracket NVDA's own print — i.e., the market's structural "quiet" this week gives
   way to NVDA's own pre-earnings positioning build almost immediately after. This is consistent
   with (not proof of) the NVDA event ledger's own finding that its S1 dead-zone (D-5→D,
   historically −0.77% mean, 50% win) opens ~Aug 19 — the same week as this opex. Also worth
   noting: Aug 19–20 carry the 20Y Treasury and 30Y TIPS auctions immediately *before* this opex,
   and PCE + GDP (2nd estimate) + the 5Y Treasury auction all land *on* NVDA's Aug-26 print day —
   this opex sits inside an already-compound-risk week on both sides, not an isolated event.

6. **No house playbook is opex-keyed — SUPPORTED.** Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md`
   and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md`: S1/G1 are pre-print run-up plays (earnings-dated),
   S2 is the never-hold-the-print guard (earnings-dated), E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution
   rule, S3 is a reaction-day fade (earnings-dated, blocked on shorting), S4 is an
   overnight-vs-buy-and-hold structural note. None reference options expiration. The one real
   interaction is indirect: E1's logic doesn't transfer (opex's expensive microstructure
   concentrates at the **close**, not the open), while S4's kept guidance to "prefer close-side
   executions" is the one rule this Friday amplifies risk for — pin-driven closing-auction
   imbalances are exactly the MOC/MOO risk already named in the sweep's blocked queue (CRWV
   overnight-carry gated on MOC/MOO slippage instrumentation) rather than something new to build
   here.

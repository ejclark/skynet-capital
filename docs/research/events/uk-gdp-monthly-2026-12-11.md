### Leg 2 — Q4 already starts near +0.2% — **SUPPORTED** (arithmetic, not a forecast)

The September bulletin: "Monthly real GDP is estimated to have grown by 0.2% in September 2026, following no
growth in August 2026 (revised down from a 0.1% growth in our previous publication), and an unrevised growth of
0.4% in July 2026" and "Real GDP grew by 0.7% in the three months to September 2026". The Q3 bulletin reads 0.7% q/q after
0.5% in Q2. On the `ECY2` 1dp index (Jul 103.0, Aug 103.0, Sep 103.3, Q3 mean 103.1), holding October, November and
December flat at the September level gives Q4 about **+0.19%** q/q. Sliding October alone and holding the next two months at
the October level:

| October m/m | Implied Q4 q/q (Nov, Dec flat at Oct) |
|---|---|
| −0.4% | −0.21% |
| −0.2% | −0.01% |
| 0.0% | +0.19% |
| +0.2% | +0.39% |
| +0.4% | +0.59% |

This is carry-over arithmetic on rounded index levels, good to about ±0.05pp, and it is not a statement about what the
Bank or anyone expects. It does say why October is the print worth reading: the quarter is not neutral going in.

### Conviction legs, tested

   stays `estimate` on a schema gap. The first Media Note reads: *"Secretary of State Marco Rubio

2. **A G20 foreign-ministers meeting has no price channel, and the structure explains why.**
   SUPPORTED as a structural claim; the base rate is an absence, not a measured zero. The finance
   and trade tracks own the instruments markets price (tariffs, export controls, sovereign debt,
   imbalances); foreign ministers own communiqués on conflicts. The US–China export-control file
   runs Bessent/Greer to He Lifeng and Wang Wentao, not Rubio to Wang Yi (the point the proposing
   ledger already carried). No study of foreign-ministers event-window returns was located.

3. **The recent precedent is thin and cuts toward "no attribution possible."** MIXED. Johannesburg
   (2025-02-20/21) is the last instance: Rubio announced he would skip it, objecting to the host's
   agenda as *"DEI and climate change"* (Foreign Policy, Naharnet), while Lavrov attended. No
   source located attributes a market move to it, and this pass did not pull that tape — so the
   claim is "nothing was reported", not "nothing happened". The closer precedent is the same
   presidency's finance track: Asheville (2026-08-31/09-01) ended in a chair's statement agreed by
   all present *"except China,"* which objected to four paragraphs (per the Bangkok and Miami
   ledgers' direct read of treasury.gov `sb0620`). A foreign-ministers text is where Ukraine and
   Middle East language lives, so a repeat dissent — or no joint text — is the base case, not a
   surprise. That is registered as a forward test below.

4. **The reason this id was proposed has moved.** SUPPORTED. The `us-china-tariff-truce-expiry`
   sweep of 2026-09-15 filed Atlanta as a ministerial venue eight days *before* the 11-10 deadline.
   Since then Bessent and He Lifeng met in Washington (09-23) and the two sides agreed a two-month
   extension: the truce *"would stay in effect until January 10, 2027"* (Korea Times, Kitco, SCMP,
   2026-09-23/24), with rare earths, chips and Taiwan untouched. The deadline this meeting once sat
   in front of is three months further out, and the channel that moved it was Bessent–He, not a
   foreign-ministers venue. **Flagged, not edited** — that ledger is another lane's, append-only.

6. **South Africa is formally excluded, and attendance is otherwise unannounced.** SUPPORTED on the

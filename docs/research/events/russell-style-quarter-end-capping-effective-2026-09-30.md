### Honest limits

**The base rate is a proxy, and its cohort is frozen.** The 4.02%/28.96% figures hold today's five cohort
members at today's proportions across five years of prices; the real cohort's membership changed over that
window (AVGO's weight, and NVDA's, are recent). It measures *whether a mega-cap growth cohort of roughly this
shape can move this far in 12 sessions*, which is the right question, but it is not a backtest of the actual
index rule. **The weights are vendor ETF holdings, not index weights**, dated 2026-08-13 to 2026-08-27 and
drifted forward on daily closes — an ETF's holdings track its index with a lag and a sampling difference, so
every cohort figure here carries perhaps a few tenths of a point of error. On IWY's 46.79% against a 45% cap
that error does not change the conclusion; on a reading nearer a threshold it would. **The 12-session window is
an approximation** of the 09-09 strike to the 09-25 test; the exact session count depends on when the recapped
weights are struck versus applied, which the guide does not spell out to the day. **The cohort is a step
function** (leg 6's AVGO note), so a smooth-drift base rate understates the variance in both directions.
**Nothing here observed a quarter-end capping event actually firing** — no index notice for one was found this
session, and the T-3 gap gives only three sessions in which such a notice would appear, so "the test rarely
fires" is inference from arithmetic and thresholds, not from a count of past firings. That is the largest gap
in this sheet. **The date is `estimate`** and every trading-adjacent statement above carries that label; the
trigger is conditional and no primary can pre-confirm it fires; `symbols` is empty by design and no house
playbook is index-flow-keyed.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`, `EST:` owner-published in the July 2026 FAQ's schedule table *and* the v5.4 September
2026 Capping Methodology Guide's standing rule, prefix-gapped rather than unpublished; trigger conditional).**
Treat 2026-09-30 as **a real mechanism attached to a very large product family, which is nonetheless untradeable
because the review scheduled 16 days before it removes most of its probability.** Four legs. **(a) The scope was
understated, not overstated.** Since 2025-03-24 the *standard* Russell US Style indexes are the capped ones, and
eight iShares funds holding ~**$294.6B** track them; the proposal's "nothing reaches the names this house
watches" was drawn from the true observation that IWM is excluded. **(b) The check is a backstop and its
predecessor defuses it.** IWY's cohort at **46.79%** will be reset to 45% on 09-09; from there the 09-25 test
needs **+6.67%** index-relative in 12 sessions, which cleared **50 of 1,243** windows (**4.02%**) against
**28.96%** without the reset. The looser quarter-end thresholds are the owner telling you the same thing. **(c)
The single-name leg is not close and five of eight funds cannot breach at all** — NVDA 16.38% against a 24%
trigger; IWP/IWS/IWO/IWN carry no constituent within reach of 4.8%. **(d) The S&P pairing is a corridor, not a
session.** Russell trades the **09-29** close, S&P the **09-30** close, on reference dates two sessions apart.
Carry forward one thing that outlives this event: **when a conditional threshold test is preceded by a scheduled
reset of the same quantity, price the reset first — the test's own base rate is nearly meaningless without it,

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is FED-primary, correctly `estimate`, and its July sub-rule is 13 of 13 rather than 6 of
   6.** SUPPORTED, and this is the durable result. The page states the convention verbatim — *"The
   minutes of regularly scheduled meetings are released three weeks after the date of the policy
   decision"* — and its 2027 panel reads verbatim `January 26-27 | March 16-17* | April 27-28 | June
   8-9* | July 27-28 | September 14-15* | October 26-27 | December 7-8*`. 2027-07-28 + 21 days is
   **Wednesday 2027-08-18** (weekday verified, not assumed). Across all **157** releases 2007–2026,
   **143 sit at exactly D+21**. Every one of the fourteen exceptions is explained by the same
   mechanism — a US market holiday inside the D+21 week — and it works in **both directions**:
   2023-11-01 → 11-21 (D+20), 2024-11-07 → 11-26 (D+19) and 2025-12-10 → 12-30 (D+20) pulled forward
   off Thanksgiving/New Year, while **2018-06-13 → 07-05 was pushed back to D+22** by July 4 and
   **2011-06-22 → 07-12 pulled forward to D+20** by the same holiday. The remaining outlier is the
   **2020-03-15 unscheduled** meeting at D+24. **July meetings are the one class the mechanism can
   never touch**, because no US market holiday can fall in a July meeting's D+21 week — and the record
   is exactly that: **13 of 13 at D+21 on a Wednesday, 2013–2026** (08-21, 08-20, 08-19, 08-17, 08-16,
   08-21, 08-19, 08-18, 08-17, 08-16, 08-21, 08-20, 08-19). The parent measured 6 of 6 on the current
   page; the historical pages take it to 13. *Honest scope:* the series starts in 2013 because before
   that the Committee held an **August** meeting rather than a July one. **Status stays `estimate`** —
   the same page states every 2027 date is *"tentative until confirmed at the meeting immediately
   preceding it"*, so the trigger is the **2027-06-08/09** meeting, not a better fetch.
   `newsevents/2027-august.htm` **404s** (the month page carrying the 2:00 p.m. time does not exist

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`):** **read it, do not trade it — do not treat it as a safe day, and do not
carry the pattern this release looked like it had.** The family's null is upheld on an independent
parse (157 releases, 0.747% vs a 0.797% baseline, bootstrap p = 0.26), so a minutes day remains an
**ordinary** session, neutral in both directions. What this ledger adds is one durable rule and one

fourteen exceptions across all 157 releases is a holiday-week shift, in both directions. The

rather than banked. The one fact that is exact rather than statistical: **2027-08-18 is the calendar

**+3.66 pt** VIX jump — the second-largest of all 157. No directional call, no size; `estimate` widens
caution and licenses nothing.

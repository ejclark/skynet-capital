### The question, plainly

> pre-announcement claim is structural rather than quantified.

> **The euro proxies are not the euro-area tape.** … A study on cash indices or on EURUSD would be
> the stronger construction and was **not run**.

So: **(1)** does the date and the 07:30 UTC slot hold on a primary rather than on a forward listing?
**(2)** How much of the aggregate is actually public at 07:30 — the weight the sibling could not get?
**(3)** Does the German print leave a mark in the **German market** in **its own fifteen minutes** —
the study the sibling named and skipped, and the only construction that can distinguish "no US-hours
footprint" from "no footprint"?

**One-line verdict:** the slot holds on **three primaries**, the last of them the release's own
embargo line; Germany is **39.6% / 33.9%** of the two panels, so the pre-announcement claim is real
as *weight* — and it fails as *signal* on the one primary-dated morning available; and the German
print leaves **no distinguishable mark in the DAX in its own window**, which is why `low` survives.

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Dec 18 is the year's final and heaviest options expiration — the **year-end triple-witching**, the third Friday of December when stock-index **futures**, stock-index **options**, and single-stock **options** expire together. Structurally it is the same event as the Sep-18 witching but with the largest seasonal stack of the year layered on top: the coincident **S&P quarterly index rebalance** (effective at this session's close), **year-end tax-loss selling and portfolio window-dressing**, and the general de-risking/reallocation flows that make late December the heaviest institutional-rebalancing window. For scale: the December 2025 witching set an all-time record at ~$7.1T notional (~10% of Russell 3000 market cap), versus ~$1–2T on a routine monthly. The mechanics are the same as any witching — dealer gamma pins into expiration, then a documented "unpin" of realized vol after, with **two** distortion windows (index SPX options AM-settle via SET at the *open*; single-stock options + the rebalance MOC at the *close*) — just at the year's largest scale. **No house playbook (S1/S2/E1/S3/S4/G1) is opex-keyed** — nothing here licenses a trade. Timing carry-forward: this witching lands **9 days after the Dec-9 FOMC** (decision + SEP + dot plot), with the Dec-10 CPI (Nov data) the day after the FOMC — so the year's heaviest gamma rolls off into a tape already carrying a fresh dot-plot and a year-end reallocation. **Sharpened 2026-09-02:** the enacted CR expires **Dec 11** (Senate 90-6 in Aug, House 370-48 on 09-01, at the President's desk) — one week before this witching — and every macro print in the run-up (PPI 12-15, import/export prices 12-17, PCE 12-23) sits *after* that cliff, so on the 2025 lapse precedent this session's gamma roll-off can arrive with **no fresh inflation data** behind it. ## Initial research

**The question.** How does the year-end December triple-witching differ from an ordinary quarterly witching and from a routine monthly opex (structural scale, settlement mechanics, the year-end rebalance/tax-loss stack, the pin-then-unpin pattern), does anything about *this particular* Dec 2026 witching stand out, and how does it interact with the Dec-9 FOMC landing 9 days before it?

**One-line verdict:** the December witching is the year's largest by notional and the most stacked with coincident year-end flows (S&P rebalance + tax-loss + window-dressing), the FOMC-then-witching sequence is a genuine timing feature, and its two-window (open + close) distortion is real — but no house playbook is opex-keyed and no same-week positioning is meaningful this far out, so the honest output is a guard (mind both prints; watch the post-FOMC, year-end gamma roll-off), not a trade.

**Method.** Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no earnings-style instrument applies and no opex-shaped instrument exists in `scripts/research/`. Sourced web research: OCC/Cboe convention for the date and settlement mechanics (SET AM-settlement), options-analytics literature (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch, CBOE) for the gamma pin/unpin pattern, press/vendor material for the monthly-vs-quarterly notional gap and the Dec-2025 ~$7.1T record, CME/index-provider material for the S&P quarterly-rebalance coincidence, and year-end seasonality coverage (tax-loss selling, window-dressing, de-risking flows). Read against `trade-playbooks.md` / `multi-symbol-sweep.md` for whether any house playbook is opex-keyed, and cross-referenced against `src/domain/market-events-data.ts` and `earnings-calendar.ts` for the adjacent dated events already on the calendar.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and cycle type are right — SUPPORTED.** OCC's convention (3rd Friday of the quarter-ending month) makes Dec 18, 2026 the year's quarterly **triple-witching**; multiple independent public sources (OptionAlpha, TradeStation, Wikipedia, LiteFinance) agree the 2026 witchings are Mar 20, Jun 18, Sep 18, and **Dec 18**, an unadjusted Friday. Matches this calendar's `impact: high` coding versus the `impact: medium` monthlies.

2. **A triple-witching is structurally ~3x a monthly opex, and December is the year's largest — SUPPORTED (estimate/literature).** Stock-index futures + stock-index options + single-stock options expire together; vendor/press comparisons put monthly notional at ~$1–2T vs ~$4–5T on a quarterly witching, and the **December 2025 witching set a record ~$7.1T notional** (reported ~20% above Dec-2024, ~10.2% of Russell 3000 market cap) — December witchings historically run the heaviest of the four. **Caveat:** vendor/press aggregates, not OCC primary open-interest data or a house stat — directional scale, not a settled number, and a 2025 record is not a 2026 forecast.

3. **Two settlement windows, not one — SUPPORTED (Cboe mechanics).** As on any witching, standard 3rd-Friday **index** options (SPX and the like) are **AM-settled** via the Special Opening Quotation (Cboe symbol "SET") struck from Friday's opening prints, concentrating index-option flows at the *open*; single-stock options and PM-settled products settle at the 4:00pm *close*, where they stack with the coincident year-end rebalance MOC (leg 4). Net: elevated-distortion windows on **both** ends of the session, unlike a single-name-heavy monthly opex whose story is mostly the close.

4. **The year-end flow stack is the differentiator vs the Sep-18 witching — SUPPORTED (index-provider + seasonality).** Two things layer onto the December witching that the September one does not carry as heavily. First, the **S&P Dow Jones quarterly rebalance** is effective at the close of the third Friday of each quarter-ending month — so, like Sep 18, passive funds execute the rebalance at *this* session's close, adding mandatory MOC pressure; volume commonly 2–3x average with the peak in the final hour (3:00–4:00pm ET). Second, and specific to December: **year-end tax-loss selling, portfolio window-dressing, and broad institutional de-risking/reallocation** cluster into late December (press coverage of the Dec-2025 cycle put year-end mechanical reallocation on the order of ~$1T), making it the heaviest rebalancing window of the year and stacking those flows onto the witching session and the days around it. (Directional/seasonal color, not a house stat. The Nasdaq-100 annual reconstitution also takes effect around the December witching per Nasdaq methodology — relevant since the tracked names are all Nasdaq-100 mega-caps — but the precise mechanic is not house-verified; treat as directional color.)

5. **Gamma pin-into-close then post-witching unpin is real and magnified here — SUPPORTED (general literature, not house-tested).** Dealers hedge into expiration in progressively larger size, compressing realized vol and pinning price toward high-gamma strikes; once that gamma expires the stabilizing floor is removed ("unpin") and realized vol tends to rise — options-analytics literature reports the Monday/Tuesday after a triple-witching as statistically elevated-vol days precisely because the largest block of dealer gamma rolls off at once (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch; CBOE at the 0DTE extreme). Because a December witching concentrates the year's most open interest, both the pin and the unpin are magnified — though the post-witching tape then runs into the thin, holiday-shortened final week of the year, which complicates any clean "unpin" read. **Caveat:** vendor-blog / one-paper sourced, not a red-teamed instrument run against our tracked names — directionally credible, not house-proven.

6. **The FOMC-then-witching sequence is a real, load-bearing timing feature — SUPPORTED.** This witching lands **9 trading days after the Dec-9 FOMC** decision (meeting Dec 8–9, carrying the SEP and dot plot — confirmed on this calendar), with the **Dec-10 CPI** (Nov data, confirmed) the day after the FOMC and the estimated **PJM capacity auction** (Dec 15, `estimate`) 3 days before the witching. Mechanically: the year's heaviest gamma roll-off arrives into a tape that spent the prior week repricing off a dot-plot day and a fresh inflation print, and is simultaneously being reshaped by year-end rebalance/tax-loss flows. This is a caution about *when* the year's largest concentrated positioning unwinds relative to those catalysts, not a directional call. (jobs Dec 4 is earlier and digested; AWS re:Invent est. Nov 30 is well before.)

7. **No tracked-name earnings are adjacent — SUPPORTED.** The nearest tracked print is CRWV (est. Nov 10), roughly five weeks before; the mega-cap cluster printed in late October, and the next NVDA/MRVL/AVGO cycle is not until early 2027. So this witching's adjacency is purely macro (the FOMC + CPI) and structural (the rebalance + year-end flows) — no single-name earnings interaction.

8. **No house playbook is opex-keyed — SUPPORTED.** Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md`: S1/G1 are pre-print run-up plays (earnings-dated), S2 is the never-hold-the-print guard (earnings-dated), E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution rule, S3 is a reaction-day fade (earnings-dated, blocked on shorting), S4 is an overnight-vs-buy-and-hold structural note. None reference options expiration. The witching sharpens two indirect interactions, as at Sep 18 but with the year-end MOC even larger: E1's "don't trade the open" logic gains a second reason (SET AM-settlement makes the open unusually distorted), and S4's kept "prefer close-side executions" guidance runs into the year's largest rebalance/stock-option MOC — the exact MOC/MOO slippage risk already named in the sweep's blocked CRWV overnight-carry item, amplified to its annual maximum. Both are execution-timing guards, not new signals.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing directional — no macro/mechanics playbook exists in the house set for opex, witching included. Support = discipline, at its strongest form of the year: no automated bot signal or paper position should be sized or entered off the Friday **open or close** on high-open-interest names (NVDA, mega-caps) without accounting for the year's largest witching-scale distortion in *both* prints, compounded by the coincident rebalance and tax-loss/window-dressing MOC. ## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, OCC-sourced).** Treat 2026-12-18 as a known-date, high-impact microstructure event — the year's final and heaviest triple-witching, stacked with the coincident S&P quarterly rebalance and year-end tax-loss/window-dressing flows — not a tradeable edge. No position change is triggered by the witching itself, and no house playbook targets it. Elevated caution applies to **both** the Friday open (index SET AM-settlement) and the Friday close (single-stock options + the year's largest rebalance/tax-loss MOC) on high-open-interest names — a timing/execution note, not a bar to action, at its annual maximum. The load-bearing fact carried forward is the **FOMC→witching→year-end sequence**: the year's heaviest gamma rolls off 9 trading days after the Dec-9 dot-plot decision, into a tape simultaneously reshaped by year-end reallocation.

**Sharpened 2026-09-02 (receipt: the D107 ledger row).** The sequence gained a funding gate and a fuller corridor, and the stance's calls are unchanged by both — they are stand-asides either way — but the *reason to watch* is stronger. The enacted CR expires **2026-12-11**, one week before this witching, and every macro print between the FOMC and the witching (PPI 12-15, import/export prices 12-17) plus the one five days after (PCE 12-23) falls on the far side of that cliff; on the 2025 precedent this repo already banked, a lapse deletes rather than delays them. The plausible December shape is therefore the **information-vacuum** case: a dot plot the Fed could not speak about until 12-11, no confirming inflation data behind it, and the year's largest gamma roll-off landing into that vacuum with the S&P quarterly rebalance and — newly dated this session — the NDX reconstitution trade both printing at the same Friday close. That raises the odds of the "unpin releases a pending move" branch over the quiet-holiday-tape branch; it does not make the witching tradeable, and no house playbook has changed. No single-name earnings adjacency this cycle.

**Kill switches:**

- **A same-week dealer-GEX snapshot (near-date) shows the broad index below its gamma-flip into Dec 18** — would flip the "compression into the close" read to an amplification one; the pin framing must be re-checked against a *near-date* vendor snapshot, never assumed from this far out.
- **The Dec-9 FOMC delivers a surprise (hawkish/dovish dot-plot shift) that repositions the tape** — raises the odds the Dec-18 gamma roll-off releases a pending move rather than a quiet unpin, into an already-churning year-end tape; re-assess the witching read after the FOMC, not before.
- **A house opex/witching instrument gets built and back-tested** — the "no playbook exists" and "unpin is magnified" legs should be re-tested against real data rather than assumed.
- **The 2026-12-11 CR expiry lapses** — would delete (not delay, on the 2025 precedent) PPI 12-15 and import/export prices 12-17 before the witching and PCE 12-23 after it, leaving the year's heaviest gamma roll-off with no fresh inflation read behind it. Equally, a full-year FY2027 appropriation enacted before 12-11 kills this branch outright — check the enactment status at each pulse, not the headline risk.
- **The Nasdaq-100 reconstitution announcement (~Fri 2026-12-11) lands with an effective date other than Mon 2026-12-21** — the Dec-21 date here is projected from Nasdaq's 2024/2025 releases, not from a 2026 primary; a different effective date moves the reconstitution trade off the witching close and weakens the stacked-MOC read. Re-check against nasdaq.com once the release is out.
- **The PJM capacity auction (est. Dec 15) confirms with a date that moves relative to the witching** — a minor adjacency, but re-check it (and pjm.com) at each assessment since it currently sits 3 days before.
- **Any primary-source correction to the Dec-18 date** (would require new OCC data; none expected — Dec 18 is an unadjusted Friday) — re-verify against theocc.com at the next assessment regardless.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

OpenAI holds DevDay 2026 in San Francisco on 2026-09-29 — the same day, in the same city, as day 1
of CoreWeave's own Fully Connected conference. OpenAI is CoreWeave's largest disclosed customer
(~$22.4B contracted). The proposing ledger filed the channel as **"real but UNMEASURED: n=0
measured instances."** Is the date real, and does a DevDay actually move anything we track?

### One-line verdict

The date **verifies against two company primaries** and stays `estimate` on a taxonomy gap alone —
and the channel, now measured across **three prior DevDays**, is a **non-event for keynote content
and a negative-skewed contract-announcement risk for CRWV**, so the honest output is stand-aside at
every horizon plus two dated forward tests.

10:00 a.m. · Lunch 11:30 a.m. · Breakouts & Programming 11:15 a.m.–3:30 p.m. · Closing Session
4:00 p.m. · Reception 4:45–7 p.m.**

The date is **not in doubt**. It stays `estimate` on exactly one mechanical count:
`market-events-data.ts`'s confirmed-prefix taxonomy (`IR:`/`CAL:`/`BLS:`/`FED:`/`PJM:`/`SEC:`/`TSY:`/

**3. "The AMD exception was DevDay" — REFUTED, and this is the finding that reframes the event.**

AMD's **+23.71% (+22.96pp excess)** on 2025-10-06 was not keynote content. That morning AMD and
OpenAI announced a **6-gigawatt strategic partnership**, with AMD issuing OpenAI a **warrant for up
to 160 million AMD shares** vesting against deployment and share-price milestones — published as
press releases by **amd.com**, **ir.amd.com** (an issuer primary) and **openai.com/index/
openai-amd-strategic-partnership/**. That is a disclosed commercial contract with a named
counterparty and a quantified equity component. It would have moved AMD on any date.

What survives is a sharper and more useful claim than the one the proposal filed: **DevDay is a
stage OpenAI sometimes uses to announce compute contracts, and a compute contract reprices whoever
is named in it.** The keynote is the wrapper; the contract is the payload.

**4. "Therefore DevDay is bullish-adjacent for CRWV" — REFUTED on both available observations.**

*The one DevDay CRWV has traded through was a day OpenAI gave a competitor 6GW.* On 2025-10-06 AMD

contracts elsewhere is share-of-wallet that does not go to CoreWeave, and the tape read it that way.
n=1 — but it is n=1 *against*, where the proposal recorded n=0.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stand aside at every horizon (`estimate`).** OpenAI DevDay 2026 on **2026-09-29** is a
**measured non-event for keynote content** — three prior editions, zero name-level moves outside base
rate attributable to the stage — wrapped around a **real but negative-skewed tail risk for CRWV**:
OpenAI has used this stage once to announce a **6GW compute partnership with a CoreWeave competitor**,

on CRWV, and **S2** (flat through the est. Nov 9–16 print window) plus **E1** (defer the open) remain
guards only. The `estimate` label reflects a confirmed-prefix taxonomy gap, not date doubt, and it
widens caution without changing any call.

**Kill switches — any one of these reopens the stance:**

1. **OpenAI or CoreWeave publishes a sized compute/infrastructure agreement between them, dated
   on or around 2026-09-29, from a company primary.** That converts this from a narrative date into
   a CRWV disclosure event, and the contract-day statistics in leg 4 (not the DevDay statistics)
   become the relevant base rate.
2. **OpenAI names a *different* compute counterparty at DevDay 2026.** The 2025-10-06 precedent says
   that is a share-of-wallet negative for CRWV, and it is the direction this ledger expects the risk
   to arrive from.
3. **CRWV's 13:30 ET hourly bar on 2026-09-29 takes ≥ 15.0% of the session's total hourly range**
   (its measured p90; base rate **10.0%**, 36 of 361 sessions) — the keynote relocating volatility
   into its own window would establish DevDay as a live repricing venue and refute leg 5
   (registered as FT-openai-devday-2026-09-29-1).
4. **CoreWeave pre-announces sized conference or partnership content before 2026-09-29** — the
   proposing ledger re-checked SEC filings on 2026-09-15 and found none (CIK 0001769628, Form 4s and
   144s only).

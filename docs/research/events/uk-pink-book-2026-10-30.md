balance-of-payments book, and its own 2025 edition says its data *"are consistent with … Balance of

### Honest limits

- **n = 10**, and the Pink Book days are not independent of the Blue Book's — the twin's ledger records its
  own editions on the same last-working-day rule, so 2019–2025 are co-dated by that ledger's scrape. 2016
  and 2018 co-dating is not verified (the Blue Book previous-releases fetch returned 502). Treat this as a
  second reading of one set of days, not a second sample.
- The study measures **daily** close-to-close moves; a 09:30 London print is 04:30 ET and an intraday spike
  that reverts by the close would not show. `intraday-edges.mjs` has no UK macro mode.
- The April–June 2026 bulletin is read for what it states about methods and headline levels; this lane did
  **not** reconcile it line by line against the 2025 annual current account, so FT-3 tests the *statement*
  of consistency, not the figures.
- VIX moved +3.5 points on 2026-10-16; its cause was searched and not found. The call does not depend on it.

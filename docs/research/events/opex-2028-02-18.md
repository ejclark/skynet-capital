### Honest limits

The *cycle type* is convention and the *date* is `estimate`; after today's fetch the residual gap is
narrow and specific — the OCC file stops **14 months** short and, newly stated, contains **zero**
Februaries in this event's configuration — rather than "no primary was read." **The study is two

measure under-corrects, the √calendar-day measure over-corrects (variance accrues more slowly than the
clock when the market is shut), the 1-day-return measure discards information. Leg 4's claim is only
that they **fail to establish the effect**, not that the effect is absent; a 296-observation sample

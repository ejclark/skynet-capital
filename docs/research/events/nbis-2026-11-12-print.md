## Initial research

### The question

Nebius reports Q3 2026 results on **2026-11-12, before the open** (confirmed). Should a paper book hold NBIS through it, and what
does the tape say it will do? One-line verdict: **no position through the print** — the move is large, two-sided and not predicted by the
run-up, and the one instrument that would sharpen it does not run on this name (below).

### Method

1. **Date and timing, primary.** Nebius's newsroom notice of 2026-10-30, fetched direct 2026-11-03 (HTTP 200): results Thursday
   2026-11-12 "before market open", call 8:00 a.m. ET. The prior-year notice reads the same way (Q3 2025, 2025-11-11, before market open).
   NBIS is a foreign private issuer and files results on **6-K**, not 8-K, so the print dates below come from the 6-Ks themselves
   (SEC EDGAR CIK 1513845, exhibit 99.1 of each, fetched direct): **2025-02-20, 2025-05-20, 2025-08-07, 2025-11-11, 2026-02-12,
   2026-05-13, 2026-08-12**.
2. **Reaction table from Yahoo daily bars** (adjusted closes; benchmark QQQ). Reproduced by a throwaway script, since the house
   instruments could not run (next bullet).
3. **Instruments blocked, recorded not substituted.** `earnings-cycle.mjs NBIS` fails (`Cannot read properties of undefined
   (reading 'date')`): it reads print dates from 8-K Item 2.02 filings, and NBIS has none. `intraday-edges.mjs NBIS` fails with
   Yahoo `422` on its `range=730d&interval=1h` request. Neither is a source block, so `probe-ref.blocked` stays empty; both are tooling
   gaps for any 6-K filer and are named in the PR. No S1/S3 control (base-rate, peer) was run on NBIS: **those verdicts are not claimed.**
4. **Options, Cboe delayed quotes** (`cdn-api.cboe.com`, snapshot 2026-11-03 10:46 ET; the `cdn.cboe.com` host the repo script
   uses returns a 307 to it): spot $151.10, 30-day IV **109.3%**. At-the-money ($150) straddle mid: Nov 6 expiry (no print) **$15.43 =
   10.2%**; Nov 13 expiry (first after the print) **$27.98 = 18.5%**. Subtracting an assumed non-event daily move of 5–7% (20-day
   realized is ~6.2%) leaves an event straddle of roughly **±11–15%**, central **~±13%**. That subtraction is this lane's
   derivation, not a quote.
5. **Fundamentals** from the Q2 6-K (exhibit 99.1/99.2, fetched direct 2026-11-03) and street figures from secondary sources, labelled.

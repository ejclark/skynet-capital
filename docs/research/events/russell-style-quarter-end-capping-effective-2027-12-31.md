### Conviction legs, tested

1. **HEADLINE — the proposal's open question is answered, and the answer is that the pattern it distrusted is not a
   pattern at all — SUPPORTED.** The proposal saw "second Friday in December, third in March and September" and read an
   inconsistency. The ordinal is not part of the rule; it is a *per-quarter* fact the owner states separately. Ground
   rules v7.2 §4.2.3 (quoted in this calendar by the `russell-reconstitution-2026-12-11` lane from its own fetch):
   *"Reconstitution occurs on the fourth Friday in June and the second Friday in December"*; the quarterly IPO review

   | | Implementation Friday | Review effective open (Monday after) | Style capping cut-off (that Monday − 12 days) | FAQ publishes |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Mar 2026 | 20 Mar (3rd) | Mon 23 Mar | **Wed 11 Mar** | 23 Mar / 11 Mar ✓ |
   | Jun 2026 | 26 Jun (4th) | Mon 29 Jun | **Wed 17 Jun** | 29 Jun / 17 Jun ✓ |
   | Sep 2026 | 18 Sep (3rd) | Mon 21 Sep | **Wed 9 Sep** | 21 Sep / 9 Sep ✓ |
   | Dec 2026 | 11 Dec (2nd) | Mon 14 Dec | **Wed 2 Dec** | 14 Dec / 2 Dec ✓ |

## Initial research

**The question.** At D-85, what is the October-data CPI print likely to show, how will the market
react to a surprise in each direction, and which of our tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT
GOOG META AAPL AMZN) carry the most sensitivity?

**One-line verdict.** This print is a rate-path gate in an energy-shock regime — no consensus
exists yet at D-85, but the reaction function is well-established and asymmetric: hot revives
hike fear and hits semis/high-multiple AI names hardest; in-line-or-cool is bought with semis
leading — so the job now is tracking the shaping inputs (oil, the Sep/Oct CPIs, two FOMCs), not
positioning.

**Conviction legs tested:**

1. **The date is right — SUPPORTED.** BLS schedule page (bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm,
   fetched 2026-08-17) lists "October 2026" data releasing **Nov. 10, 2026, 08:30 AM** —
   matching the calendar entry exactly. Primary source, no aggregator in the loop.
2. **CPI prints are currently the market's rate-path gate — SUPPORTED.** September-hike odds have
   swung violently around inflation/oil data all summer: ~82% after the late-July oil rip
   (CNBC 2026-07-23), ~63% after the cool June-data print (CNBC 2026-07-14), ~42% after the
   in-line July-data print (CNBC/CME FedWatch, 2026-08-12). FOMC minutes flag energy-driven
   upside inflation risk. Two FOMC decisions (Sep 16, Oct 28 — both already on the calendar)
   land before this print; by Nov 10 the question will be the December meeting's path.
3. **The reaction function is asymmetric and knowable — SUPPORTED, three releases cited.**
   - *May data (rel. 2026-06-10, hot):* +0.5% m/m, 4.2% y/y headline on a 3.9% energy jump
     (energy +23.5% y/y; Iran conflict), core 2.9%. Futures held negative into the print

   - *June data (rel. 2026-07-14, cool surprise):* −0.4% m/m vs −0.2% expected, 3.5% y/y vs 3.8%
     consensus, core flat. Yields sharply lower, stocks up, hike odds cut 75%→63%

   - *July data (rel. 2026-08-12, in-line):* +0.1% m/m, 3.4% y/y, core +0.2%/2.5% — all at Dow
     Jones consensus. SPX +0.3%, NDX +0.5%, and notably **SMH +2%** led by NVDA/MU/ASML; hike
     odds → 42% (CNBC, Stocktwits/FXEmpire market wraps, 2026-08-12).
   The pattern: headline is energy-hostage and drifting down (4.2 → 3.5 → 3.4), core is quietly
   disinflating (2.9 → 2.6 → 2.5), and semis are the highest-beta expression of every relief
   print.
4. **A consensus/whisper for the October print exists — REFUTED at this distance.** No economist
   consensus is published for October data at D-85 (the visible forecast horizon is the Sep 11
   and Oct 14 releases). Any number written today would be an invention. The shaping inputs are
   explicit instead: the oil/energy path (the whole gap between headline and core), the two
   intervening CPI prints, and the Sep/Oct FOMC outcomes.
5. **Tracked-name sensitivity ranks semis and high-multiple AI first — SUPPORTED (directionally,
   from the observed tape).** On the 8/12 relief print SMH (+2%) ran ~4× SPX — **NVDA, AVGO,
   MRVL** are the highest-beta CPI trades among our names. **CRWV** is plausibly the single most

   rate and the financing cost), though we have no per-print measurement. The mega-caps
   (**MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN**) react in the same direction with lower beta — cash-rich
   balance sheets blunt the financing channel. This ranking is a reasoned estimate from one
   clean observation plus mechanism, not an instrumented result.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing yet, by design: at D-85 with no consensus in existence,

legs above establish *how to read it*, not *what it will be*.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BLS primary): watch-only.** No trade keys on this event today — D-85,
no consensus in existence, and estimates only widen caution. The standing read: this print is a
rate-path gate whose danger case is a hot, energy-driven headline reviving hike pricing into
year-end, with semis (NVDA/AVGO/MRVL) and CRWV the most exposed of our names and the mega-caps
lower-beta (that ranking is an estimate). Treat 2026-11-10 as a known-date variance day: any
paper position carried into it gets the same never-hold-the-print scrutiny S2 applies to
earnings ([`multi-symbol-sweep.md`](../multi-symbol-sweep.md)) — a guard framing, not a new
playbook.

**Stance addendum (2026-08-31, D-71 — receipt: the 08-31 ledger row).** Two changes, neither of which
opens a play. **(1) Existence risk is now a named leg of this stance.** This event is the October-data
print, and the 2025 shutdown showed BLS cancelling exactly that data month rather than delaying it —
so a 10-01 funding lapse would not postpone this print, it would delete it. The sibling
[`government-funding-deadline`](government-funding-deadline-2026-09-30.md) ledger reads the lapse
branch's cost as jobs 10-02 + CPI 10-14; this doc extends that ledger's own logic one month forward,
because the October *collection window* is what a lapse destroys and this print is what reads it. Its
base case (averted — both chambers already passed a CR, House floor action 08-31) is carried unchanged;
the risk is live only until a CR is signed. **(2) The asymmetric-reaction leg is weaker than at D-85,
without being killed.** On 08-28 a genuine hawkish shock (September hike odds ~35% → ~57%) produced a
−0.25% SPX day and a VIX that *fell* to a 2026 low of 14.43 — the repricing went into the front end
(2Y +6–9bp), not into equity vol. The written kill switch requires a mis-reacting *print*, not a
keynote, so it has not fired; but the premise that rate surprises transmit hardest through high-beta
semis now has one contrary observation against it, and the Sep 11 / Oct 14 prints are the test.

**Kill switches:**

- *Existence* (new, 2026-08-31): the print itself dies if FY2027 appropriations lapse on 2026-10-01
  and the lapse runs deep enough into October to break price collection — the 2025 outcome verbatim.
  Retired the moment a CR covering October is signed.

- *The regime read* (CPI-as-hike-gate) dies if the Fed's September and October meetings resolve
  the hike question decisively in either direction — after which CPI prints demote toward
  ordinary macro noise and this event's impact read should be revisited.
- *The asymmetric reaction function* dies if the next two releases (Sep 11, Oct 14 — both on the
  calendar) break the pattern: a cool print that gets sold, or a hot print that gets bought,
  either one kills the "relief prints lift semis hardest" leg.
- *The sensitivity ranking* dies if a relief print prints with semis underperforming SPX on the
  day — one clean contrary observation is enough, since the supporting sample is one print.
- *The date itself* is BLS-confirmed; only a BLS schedule change kills it (re-verify on each
  pulse — cheap, and the aggregator-error incident is why).

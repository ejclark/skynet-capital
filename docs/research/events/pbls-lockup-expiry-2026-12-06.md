## Initial research

### The question
Does the 2026-12-06 PBLS lock-up expiry put enough new supply on the market to be worth a position, a hedge or a stand-aside, and on what date does it fire?

**Verdict: the date is firm, the supply is small relative to the study's buckets, and the pullback premise is only half present — stand aside.** One clean 180-day period with no early-release trigger, so the only date risk is a discretionary waiver. The unlock is 2.2x the float (the study's lowest tercile: -0.45% run-in, +3.16% full window), the run-up of 39% sits in the 0–50% cut (-1.07% run-in, +2.34% full window), and the study's one cut that paid needs a 50% run-up that PBLS does not currently have. The volatility regime (VIX 28.18 on 2026-11-05) is the larger risk to any position, not the lock-up.

Read the 424B4 (EDGAR, fetched 2026-11-06) for the lock-up clause, "Shares Eligible for Future Sale", the offering summary and the principal-stockholder table; the Q2 10-Q (filed 2026-08-13) for current share counts, the over-allotment outcome and cash; every 8-K since the IPO (2026-08-13 Q2 results, 08-25 director appointment, 09-02, 09-15 ESMO guidance, 11-05 lease amendment); the EDGAR submissions API (CIK 1657677) for Forms 4/144, 13D/G and resale registrations since the IPO; Yahoo daily bars for PBLS, XBI, IWM, QQQ, SPY, ^VIX (fetched 2026-11-06, last full session 2026-11-05); the Cboe delayed option chain for PBLS (snapshot with last trade 2026-11-02, spot 27.46). ### Leg 1 — the date: SUPPORTED
The 424B4 (dated 2026-06-09, filed 2026-06-10) says the company, its directors and officers and "the holders of substantially all of our capital stock… (including the shares of common stock to be issued in the concurrent private placement)" are bound for "a period of not less than 180 days from the date of this prospectus without the prior written consent of Leerink Partners LLC and BofA Securities, Inc.", and the summary says the remaining shares "will be able to be sold in the public market beginning 181 days after the date of this prospectus". 180 days after 2026-06-09 is Sunday 2026-12-06, so day 181 and the study's day 0 is Monday 2026-12-07. The carve-outs are gifts, estate planning, affiliate transfers, tax withholding, option exercises, a change-of-control tender, preferred and SAFE conversion, shares bought in the offering or the open market, and 10b5-1 plans with no sales during the lock-up; there is no earnings, price or blackout release. No 8-K since the IPO amends it. Some holders are also bound by market-standoff agreements "of not less than 180 days", the same period.

### Leg 2 — supply: SMALL VS THE STUDY, MIXED ON WHO SELLS
Shares outstanding at 2026-08-10: 121,952,030 voting plus 1,581,210 non-voting = **123.53M** (10-Q cover). The IPO sold 38,525,000 shares, the base 33.5M plus the full 5.025M over-allotment (10-Q), **all primary — no selling stockholder**; Regeneron bought 4,166,666 more in a concurrent private placement at $18.00 (locked, per the clause). Free float is therefore about **38.5M** (assuming existing holders bought none of it; the filings do not say), and about **85.0M** are locked: 2.2x the float, **~74 days of the 20-day average volume (1.16M) or ~89 days of the 60-day (0.96M)**, worth ~$2.37B at $27.88 against a ~$3.44B market cap. The prospectus put the restricted share at 71.6% before the over-allotment. The holders are funds: FMR 7.4%, RA Capital 6.6%, ARCH 5.4%, venBio 4.4%, GV 4.1%, Milky Way Investments 3.9%, Nextech 3.5%, Cormorant 3.7% (post-offering, 2026-04-30 table), plus Regeneron (~3.4%) and the SAFE holder. Funds with a board seat are affiliates, capped under Rule 144 at the greater of 1% of the class (~1.24M) or the four-week average weekly volume (~5.8M by my arithmetic from the 20-day average) per three months; a non-affiliate holding more than six months is not capped. The table does not say who is an affiliate, so the unregistered ceiling cannot be sized. Registered resale needs an S-1 and a demand right that the lock-up itself bars ("request or demand that we file a registration statement"). Cash of $1,120.7M at 2026-06-30 funds the company "into 2030", so no primary sale is needed. No Form 144, S-1 or 424B has been filed since the IPO.

None. No short: a 98%-vol biotech just past a data catalyst is not a paper-trade edge, and the study's put spreads lost a third of their debit at a realistic price. No long: the unlock is small, but the stock is 39% over its IPO price and 80+% vol, and the "buy after the flush" shape found no rebound. If sizing comes up, low confidence means size zero. ### Honest limits
104 sessions and one report: no cycle statistics. The float assumes no existing holder bought IPO shares. Affiliate status is not itemized, so the Rule 144 ceiling is unsized, and a fund can also distribute in kind with no filing I would see. The Cboe snapshot predates the 11-05 shock and has almost no open interest. I could not find what caused the 2026-11-05 selloff (SPY -4.4%, QQQ -6.5%, XBI -6.7%, PBLS -8.2%, VIX +11.6): two WebSearches returned nothing for the date, so the move is recorded, not explained. The Q3 date and the ESMO response figure are aggregator or press numbers. The study's cuts come from 355 IPOs in 2019–2026 and none is a forecast for one name.

## Stance & kill switches

Stand aside on PBLS around the lock-up (date **confirmed**; initial stance). Kill switches, each with a date:
- **2026-12-06**: the lock-up ends as written (first sale Monday 12-07). A Leerink/BofA waiver or any 8-K moving the date earlier re-dates the event and pulls supply forward.
- **2026-12-21**: PBLS D-10..D+10 return versus XBI at -5% or worse says the pullback is back (FT-1); raise caution, never a long signal.
- **2026-12-21**: an S-1 or 424B prospectus naming a pre-IPO holder as selling stockholder (FT-2) means supply is sized and the stand-aside widens to avoid.
- **2026-11-19**: a close at or above $30.00 (+50% over the IPO price) switches on the study's big-run-up cut; the call stays stand aside but confidence in "small pullback" drops.
- **Any day**: Form 144 above ~5.8M shares in three months by an affiliate, or any Form 144 before 12-07 (a breach of the lock-up).

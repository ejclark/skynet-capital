### The one lesson worth banking

**A converged rumor band is not a narrow distribution.** The D-1 row's most confident sentence was
that pricing consensus had "**converged** for the first time in this ledger's run" — KeyBanc's
$1,249 / $1,399 / $2,199 sitting *inside* TrendForce's $1,249–1,299 / $1,349–1,399 / $2,099–2,299.
Twenty-four hours later the announced ladder came in **below every figure in that convergence**. Five
consecutive rows tracked the rumor to the dollar and produced a band the outcome missed on the low
side. The generalizable rule for this calendar's other product-launch and guidance events: **treat
agreement among secondary models as evidence about the models, not about the outcome** — convergence
measures how much the analysts read each other, and the only resolving fact is the announcement.
That is the same class of error as the stale-date trap this ledger caught four times, in a different
costume: a number repeated widely is not thereby a number that is right.

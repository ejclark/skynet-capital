## Initial research

**The question, plainly.** Is 2026-10-23 really iPhone Duo's on-sale date, is it Apple's own
statement or still a rumor, and does the day itself give any of the nine tracked names — AAPL
specifically — a tradable edge the earnings-cycle sweep and the 09-09 keynote ledger haven't
already killed?

**One-line verdict.** The date is now **confirmed** on Apple's own newsroom (independently
re-verified today, not carried over from the discovering lane's `estimate` filing); it is a
fulfillment date with no scheduled new information, AAPL is dead on every positioning-shaped
playbook, and the supply story that is this event's only live content has **worsened** since the
09-10 proposal — so this stays a confirm-the-date / read-the-supply watch, licensing no entry at

**Legs tested.**

1. **"2026-10-23 is iPhone Duo's on-sale date, Apple's own statement."** SUPPORTED, confirmed
   today. Apple's own newsroom (apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/, re-fetched
   direct 2026-09-23 — an independent second look, since this lane did not discover the event) states
   verbatim: *"Customers in more than 70 countries and regions... will be able to pre-order iPhone
   Duo beginning at 5 a.m. PT on Friday, October 16, with availability beginning Friday, October
   23. iPhone Duo will be available in 28 other countries and regions beginning Friday, October
   30."* The calendar entry flips `estimate` → `confirmed` on an `IR:` primary in this PR.
2. **"The on-sale day itself carries scheduled new information."** REFUTED. Nothing is announced
   at fulfillment — pricing, specs and country rollout were all fixed at the 09-09 keynote (see the
   [09-09 ledger's close-out](aapl-iphone-18-launch-2026-09-09.md#outcome)). The only thing that can
   move on 10-23 is a *read* — sell-through and lead times — not a new fact from Apple.
3. **"The supply constraint the 09-09 close-out flagged has resolved or worsened by D-30."**
   MIXED, leaning worsened — three independent reads since the 09-10 proposal, none of them
   contradicted:
   - **Foxconn assembly yield.** Jiemian News (via 36Kr, **2026-09-21**, sourced to "people close
     to Foxconn's industrial chain"): *"the current overall yield rate of Foxconn's assembled
     iPhone Duo units is only over 60%... it may take another half a year or even a full year for
     the overall yield rate to reach a mature level."* That is a maturity timeline that runs
     **through and past** the 10-23 launch, not one that clears before it.
   - **2026 unit estimate, cut.** TrendForce (**2026-09-10**, prnewswire.com/trendforce.com):
     *"iPhone Duo shipments will reach approximately 5 million units in 2026,"* a **24.8%** share
     of the foldable market (behind Samsung). That sits at the low end of the 09-10 close-out's own
     recorded range (TD Cowen ~6M, Goldman 14M–35M).
   - **A second cut, five days later.** Analyst Jeff Pu (via 9to5Mac, **2026-09-15**): 2026 Duo
     shipments now **6 million, down from a prior 7 million estimate**, attributed explicitly to
     *"hinge constraints"* — the same mechanism Nikkei named on 09-04. Pu's own comparison: ~72M
     iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max units by year-end, ~12x the Duo figure.
   - Against this: no sourced report in the 09-10→09-23 window claims the yield or hinge problem
     has eased. The direction of every fresh data point is the same, and it is down.
4. **"The nine tracked names carry sensitivity to this specific day."** REFUTED as a direct play,
   unchanged from the 09-09 ledger's own finding. AAPL is dead on S1, "late run-up", S3 and S4 (the
   sweep's kill list); the only live AAPL leg is **FT-5** (post-print drift), which keys off the
   est. 10-29 print, not this on-sale date. No new evidence clears the sweep's stated ≥3-print bar
   to re-open anything.
5. **Whisper / consensus.** Not quotable — there is no options/price instrument for a product
   fulfillment date, and unit-estimate dispersion (5M TrendForce to 35M Goldman's optimistic case)
   is itself the finding: analysts disagree by a factor of seven on a name this calendar cannot
   trade a view on either way.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing on the tracked nine, at any horizon. For any paper AAPL
exposure: the [Oct-29 ledger's](aapl-2026-10-29-print.md) standing guards (S2 flat by D-1 of that
print, E1 defer non-urgent opens) — both inherited, neither newly licensed by this event. The

**Honest limits.** Every supply figure here is secondary/analyst-sourced (TrendForce, Jeff Pu,
Jiemian-via-36Kr's unnamed Foxconn-chain sources) — none is Apple's own disclosure, and Apple does
not report unit shipments by model. The 36Kr yield figure could not be corroborated against a named
Apple or Foxconn quality-standard threshold in this pass (a wider search summary cited a 70%/80%
comparison that the direct fetch of the article did not itself confirm verbatim — not used here for
that reason). No price/options instrument exists for a product-launch kind, so nothing here is
sized. A candidate forward test — whether the 10-16/10-23 lead-time signal predicts the FQ1 guide's
own surprise sign — is deliberately **not registered yet**: the 09-09 keynote ledger only registered
its own FT-1 once conditions sharpened close to the event (its D-3 row), and there is no sharper
signal to test today than "the supply story keeps worsening on every fresh check," which is not
itself a falsifiable prediction. A later pulse, once pre-order lead times are readable (~10-16),
is the right point to register one if a specific, dateable hypothesis is still standing.

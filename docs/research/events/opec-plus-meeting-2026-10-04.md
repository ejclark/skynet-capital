## At a glance

theoretical this week, so the read-through moved from energy to rates.** The seven-country OPEC+
group (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, Oman) meets **Sunday 2026-10-04**
to set November levels alongside the 68th JMMC. Nothing about the *meeting* improved the case for
acting on it — it got weaker. What changed is everything around it. **Drones launched from Iraq hit

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (set 2026-09-08, D-26): stand aside on the quota; watch the 2027 baseline, and price off
the chokepoint.** The 2026-10-04 meeting is **estimate**-dated, carries no tracked symbols, and its
expected outcome is a second consecutive "change nothing" that now sits inside a reported whole-Q4
pause. Even a surprise is second-order to a strait running at ~10 transits a day, and the path from
crude to our book is a two-step through the Fed corridor that our own FT-12 and a sitting governor
both discount. No entry, no hedge, no pre-positioning is licensed — and the **estimate** label
forbids date-keyed action independently of all of the above.

**Stance amended 2026-09-15 (D-19), receipt in the ledger row below: the stand-aside on the quota
holds and hardens; the crude→rates leg is promoted from "discounted twice" to live.** Two of the
three things that made this event ignorable are unchanged — `symbols: []` and an **estimate** date,
either of which forbids date-keyed action on its own. The third was the transmission argument, and
it did not survive the week. **Kill switch 3 fired on 2026-09-15**, 24 days inside its own
2026-10-09 deadline: Brent closed above $100 for five consecutive sessions — **101.21 · 107.63 ·
104.61 · 105.68 · 109.22** (09-09→09-15, Yahoo v8 `BZ=F` daily closes pulled today, HTTP 200). Its
registered consequence was that *"energy stops being a discountable headline component and begins
broadening into core"*, and the corroborating print landed independently three days earlier:
**August CPI (2026-09-11) put core at +0.3% m/m, above forecast**, energy +2.1% m/m after −1.5% in
July, gasoline +3.9% and more than a third of the index's whole gain; September hike odds went from
the **~62–65%** this ledger carried to **~87–91%** (CME FedWatch / prediction markets, post-print),
with FOMC deciding **2026-09-16**. So the chain crude → CPI energy → hike odds is now *observed*
rather than argued, and per KS3's own registration the condition passes to its named owner,
[`cpi-2026-10-14`](cpi-2026-10-14.md), after 2026-10-09.

**What the amendment does not do is make this event tradeable.** It moves the exposure somewhere
else. The cause of the move was not OPEC and was not the quota: drones launched from Maysan

supply, the route carrying roughly two-thirds of Saudi Arabia's pre-war export volume around the

costless paper gesture as with a genuine policy turn. The switch still fires as written — it just
no longer settles the question by itself, and the next session should say which reading it is
taking before acting on it.

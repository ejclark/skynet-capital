## At a glance

**TL;DR.** **Stand aside on the quota — the November number is the most pre-priced decision on this
calendar — and watch the baseline instead.** The seven-country OPEC+ group (Saudi Arabia, Russia,
Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, Oman) meets **Sunday 2026-10-04** to set November levels, sharing
the date with the 68th JMMC. Two things make the monthly number close to information-free. The group
already paused once — its **2026-09-06** release (primary, re-fetched today) held September levels
for October — and the reported base case is a **whole-Q4 pause** while 2027 quotas get negotiated
(Reuters-sourced; Rystad's Jorge Leon on the record), so **November sits inside the pause window
rather than at its edge**. And the same analyst says the quota barely reaches the market anyway:
OPEC+ *"can change production targets on paper, but it cannot guarantee that those barrels will be
produced or actually reach the market."* Hormuz commodity transits are at a **10/day 10-day average**
(Kpler, 09-06) against an **~85–100/day** pre-crisis baseline, and Brent is **$98.08** today — up
~9.8% in seven sessions on strikes, not on OPEC. **What is genuinely new versus the 09-06 meeting:**
the third-party **Maximum Sustainable Capacity audits close at end-September**, so 10-04 is the
**first OPEC+ forum at which every member's 2027 baseline number exists** — and Iran's baseline is
being fixed off its own **August–October 2026** production, three months in which it is at war and
its own strait is shut. The monthly number is priced; the baseline fight is the tail. Nothing here is
name-keyed (`symbols: []`), the date is **estimate**, and no house playbook covers commodities — so
no date-keyed action is licensed regardless.

## Initial research

**The question.** What is the seven-country OPEC+ group likely to decide about November quotas at the
2026-10-04 meeting, how would the market react in each direction, and does any of it change how we
sit in the tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN)?

**One-line verdict:** the November number is a second consecutive non-decision with more support
behind it than the October one had, taken by a group that a named analyst says cannot deliver barrels
to market anyway — so the meeting is low-information for price; what makes **this** instance worth a
ledger is that it is the first OPEC+ forum after the 2027 capacity audits close, and the honest
output is a stand-aside plus a re-pointed watch-list: the baseline, not the quota.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date is right, and the cadence is now 6 for 6 — SUPPORTED.** OPEC's own 2026-09-06 release,
   re-fetched direct today (HTTP 200), records that *"The seven participating countries decided to
   maintain September 2026 required production for October 2026"* and closes *"The next meeting will
   be held on 4 October 2026."* Six consecutive meetings have been monthly, on Sundays, each named
   verbatim in the prior release (06-07, 07-05, 08-02, 09-06, 10-04), and the 09-06 statement adds
   that the seven *"will continue to hold monthly meetings"* (Rigzone, 2026-09-07). It stays

2. **The base case is a second pause, and it is better-supported than the October one was —
   SUPPORTED, still not primary.** The 09-06 release is **silent** on November and Q4 (verified

   *likely pause output increases for the rest of 2026*, and Rystad's **Jorge Leon** is quoted

   negotiations."* November is the **middle** of that window, not its edge, which is a materially
   stronger prior than the one the October decision faced. The residual risk is unchanged in kind:
   a well-telegraphed pause is priced, and the only thing left to surprise is the telegraph being
   wrong. **Still no primary** — every strand traces to unnamed delegates through secondaries, and
   this calendar treats delegate-sourced OPEC guidance as directionally useful and never as a source.

3. **THE FINDING — 10-04 is the first OPEC+ forum after every 2027 baseline number exists, and one
   member's number is being fixed while it is under attack — SUPPORTED on the mechanism, MIXED on
   the timing detail.** The 40th ONOMM release (2025-11-30, primary, fetched today) records that the
   participating countries *"approved the mechanism developed by the Secretariat"* to assess
   *"maximum sustainable production capacity (MSC) to be used as reference for the 2027 production

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (set 2026-09-08, D-26): stand aside on the quota; watch the 2027 baseline, and price off
the chokepoint.** The 2026-10-04 meeting is **estimate**-dated, carries no tracked symbols, and its
expected outcome is a second consecutive "change nothing" that now sits inside a reported whole-Q4

forbids date-keyed action independently of all of the above.

below resolves inside this event's close-out window (2026-10-04 + `closeOutWithinDays: 6`), or names
the ledger that owns it afterwards.** That is the transferable lesson the predecessor's close-out
banked in its §4: *a kill switch whose window outlives `closeOutWithinDays` is orphaned by
construction.*

   begins broadening into core. **Owner after 2026-10-09: [`cpi-2026-10-14`](cpi-2026-10-14.md)** —

### Honest limits

thinner in four specific places.

minutes state a *policy* that official data releases are incorporated up to the announcement; what they

Reading those as evidence that market conditioning is cut earlier than data incorporation is an
inference from two instances, which is exactly why leg 3 is registered as a test with a **near-term**
scoring venue (**2026-09-17**, eight days from writing) rather than asserted.

**Second, the threshold in leg 4 rests on a relayed number and an absent one.** The **~3.0% Q3** path is

**Q4** path is **absent** — the MPR landing page carries no quarterly AWE path and a targeted search

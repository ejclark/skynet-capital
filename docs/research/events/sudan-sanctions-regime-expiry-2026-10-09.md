### Conviction legs, tested

1. **10-09 is a lapse date, not a Council session — this calendar carries no other entry on the
   day.** SUPPORTED. Press coverage of resolution 2828 describes it as extending the 1591 regime's
   measures "for one month" and the Panel of Experts' mandate "until 9 November" — the 10-09 date
   is arithmetic from the 09-11 adoption, not a scheduled vote. `node scripts/event-scan.mjs
   --on-date=2026-10-09` returns exactly this event and nothing else, so — unlike the Haiti sibling's
   09-30 date, which sat inside a 17-event pileup — there is no other tracked release on this
   calendar to contaminate a reading of the day, if a reading existed to contaminate. It does not
   (leg 4).

2. **The rollover doctrine already documented for Haiti applies here directly, and this regime has
   never lapsed.** SUPPORTED. Resolution 2828 is itself one of the three dated technical-rollover
   instances the sibling ledger's own study already found (South Sudan 9-day, 2025-04-30;
   Afghanistan 3-month, 2026-03; **this** Sudan rollover, unanimous, 2026-09-11). General web
   search corroborates that the 1591 Darfur sanctions regime, established by resolution 1591 in
   2005, has been continuously renewed every year since — this session found no reporting of a
   lapse at any point in that span, and Security Council Report's own account of the rollover
   device (cited via the sibling ledger) names exactly one mandate ever lost to a missed deadline
   across the whole Council's practice — UNOMIG, on a Russian veto, 2009-06-15 — never a Sudan
   sanctions text.

3. **This rollover is a genuine escalation in form — the first one-month emergency rollover in the
   regime's history — but the fight behind it is over scope, not survival.** SUPPORTED, and it is
   this document's own finding. Every prior 1591 renewal ran on the regime's normal one-year
   cadence; res 2828 replaced that with a one-month stopgap because the Council split. The U.S.
   sponsored 2828 while separately pushing to expand the arms embargo from Darfur alone to all of
   Sudan and to widen designation criteria to cover sexual violence, kidnapping and attacks on aid
   workers. Russia, China, the African members (DRC, Liberia, Somalia) and Pakistan opposed that
   expansion and are reported to prefer a plain one-year renewal at the existing Darfur-only scope;
   China's own explanation of vote is reported to have separately warned against forcing through
   contentious text. None of that bloc is reported opposing the sanctions regime's *continuation* —
   only its *scope*. That distinction is the whole reason leg 2's no-lapse base rate still applies:
   the fight is over what gets renewed, not whether.

4. **No listed instrument carries a real channel to this event, in either direction of the scope
   fight.** SUPPORTED. Sudan and Darfur specifically have no US-listed pure play; no major Western
   miner operates there under the sanctions and conflict conditions, and the gold produced in the
   region is substantially artisanal and reported by the UN's own Panel of Experts to help finance
   the conflict's armed actors — a share too small against global mine supply to move `GC=F`, the
   instrument that actually prices gold. Sudan's own crude reserves passed mostly to South Sudan at
   the 2011 partition; what runs through Sudan today is the Port Sudan pipeline carrying South
   Sudan's export barrels — a transit corridor, not a source, and not what `CL=F`/`BZ=F` price. An
   arms-embargo expansion to cover all of Sudan would not create a market channel where none
   exists; it would just widen the same regime's designation net.

5. **The date is clean of other tracked calendar noise, which forecloses a false-attribution risk
   rather than creating a trading edge.** MIXED, resolved toward "not tradeable but honestly
   framed." Leg 1 already established `--on-date=2026-10-09` returns only this entry. Unlike the
   Haiti sibling's quarter-end contamination problem, a future assessor who somehow found market
   movement near 10-09 would not need to disentangle it from a pileup of other releases — but since
   no listed instrument has a channel to this event in the first place (leg 4), that clean date buys
   nothing tradeable. It is recorded so a later pulse does not need to re-derive it.

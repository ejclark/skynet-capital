### The question, plainly

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **"The print that fixes 11 of 12 months of the 2026 yearly average" is bookkeeping, not leverage
   — MIXED, and it is this event's own title that overstates it.** The claim is arithmetically true
   and analytically empty. The 2026 Japan annual average is the simple mean of twelve monthly index
   levels; after this print, eleven are public. But **every** month enters with weight 1/12, so a
   ±1.0pp y/y surprise in November moves the annual rate by **±0.083pp** — *exactly* what the sibling
   computed for December. What the November print does is take the last step of a five-step
   narrowing. For a common ±1.0pp shock across the still-unknown months:

   | After the release of… | Months unknown | Residual band on the 2026 annual rate |
   |---|---|---|
   | (today, 2026-09-05) | 5 | ±0.417pp |
   | Aug data, 2026-09-18 | 4 | ±0.333pp |
   | Sept data, 2026-10-23 | 3 | ±0.250pp |
   | Oct data, 2026-11-20 | 2 | ±0.167pp |
   | **Nov data, 2026-12-18 (this event)** | **1** | **±0.083pp** |
   | Dec data, 2027-01-22 | 0 | published |

   The Bureau publishes the annual rate to one decimal, so the band is already inside a rounding
   step **two prints earlier**, on 2026-11-20. And the annual average itself does not publish here:
   the feed's class split shows `2027-01-22 08:30 | 2026年平均` under **全国** and
   `2026-12-25 08:30 | 2026年平均` under **東京都区部**, so the English row's Remarks cell (*"2026
   yearly average of Ku-area of Tokyo"*) attaches to the **Tokyo** column, not to this date. **This
   release publishes one item: 2026年11月分.**

   makes this date worth a note at all.** 2025's monthly index path (2025-base, all items) runs
   **99.3 · 98.9 · 99.2 · 99.7 · 100.0 · 99.9 · 100.1 · 100.3 · 100.1 · 100.7 · 101.0 · 100.9** —
   **November is the calendar-year peak**, on core (101.1) as well as all-items. Differencing that
   against the ten-year seasonal norm:

   | Measure | 2025 Oct→Nov m/m | 2015–2024 mean | Excess base | 2025 Nov→Dec m/m | 2015–2024 mean | Excess base |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | All items | **+0.298%** | −0.004% | **+0.30pp** | −0.099% | +0.005% | **−0.10pp** |
   | Less fresh food (BoJ core) | **+0.397%** | +0.118% | **+0.28pp** | −0.198% | +0.028% | **−0.23pp** |

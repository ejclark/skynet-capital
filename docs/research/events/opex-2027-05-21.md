## At a glance

**TL;DR.** **Stand aside on the date — and stop citing the pin-then-unpin story, because this session
measured it and it is close to a coin flip.** Six opex ledgers on this shelf assert, from vendor
literature, that dealer gamma pins price into a monthly expiration and realized volatility rises as
it rolls off. Nobody had ever measured it here. Run against **120 third Fridays of QQQ, 2016-2026**:
post-expiration five-session realized volatility exceeds the pre-expiration five sessions **69 times,
57.5%**, median ratio **1.098**. Worse for the mechanism, it **inverts exactly where it should be
strongest** — on the **40 quarterly triple witchings**, the year's largest gamma roll-off, the rate
is **47.5%** and the median ratio **0.905**, while ordinary monthlies run 62.5% / 1.160. A mechanic
whose effect disappears when you quadruple the size of its cause is not the mechanic being described.
**What this event adds beyond that:** it is an ordinary monthly (`estimate`), it was the one hole in
this calendar's 2026-08 → 2027-06 expiration run and is now filled, and it exposes a **wrong rule
banked by a sibling** — [`opex-2027-04-16`](opex-2027-04-16.md) leg 4's "mid-month FOMC → opex is
inside the blackout" is false for all four 2027 cases, because the blackout **ends the Thursday after
the meeting** and that half of the rule was never used. Corrected: **zero of 2027's twelve third
Fridays fall inside an FOMC blackout.** 2027-05-21 sits **8 days before** the June blackout opens —
one of only two 2027 expirations in that position — so its post-expiration week is the **last
Fed-speech window before the June SEP**, the precise mirror of April's Fed-silent one. Nothing here is
tradeable: the date is `estimate`, no house playbook is opex-keyed (re-grepped, zero hits), and the
honest output is one execution guard, one deflation, one correction, one proposal and two registered

## Initial research

### The question

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is right and `estimate` is the correct label — SUPPORTED as a date, MIXED as a
   promotion candidate.** May 2027's Fridays are the 7th, 14th, **21st** and 28th, so the third Friday
   is 2027-05-21. NYSE's re-fetched 2027 column carries exactly one May closure — Memorial Day,
   **Monday 2027-05-31**, ten days later — and no May early close, so unlike `opex-2027-06-17` this
   expiration is **unadjusted**. Better than convention: Cboe's CSV returns
   `VX,VX/J7,2027-04-21,21.0267`, and VX terms settle to the SPX/SPXW series **exactly 30 days later**
   — 2027-04-21 + 30d = **2027-05-21**. **Why it stays `estimate`:** that dates the *index-options*
   leg by inference through a contract spec while this is a monthly covering index **and** single-stock
   options; no variance row dates this month the way `VA/F7` dates 2027-01-15 and `VA/M7` dates
   2027-06-17; four calendar routes 403'd or 404'd today; and the lane may not self-confirm an
   in-sweep discovery. The label costs nothing — every call here is a stand-aside either way.

2. **The pin-then-unpin mechanic six ledgers cite is a coin flip — REFUTED as stated, and this is the
   session's headline.** Definition, fixed before the run and identical to the one
   `FT-opex-2027-04-16-1` already registered: for each third Friday, QQQ close-to-close log-return
   standard deviation over the **five sessions after** the expiration versus the **five sessions
   ending at** it. Sample: **120 expirations, 2016-2026**, holiday-displaced third Fridays mapped back
   to the prior session. **Result: post > pre on 69, or 57.5%, median ratio 1.098.** Against a 50%
   null that is 69/120 — nowhere near the "realized volatility rises as the pin rolls off" the
   literature is quoted for. The claim is not *false*; it is far weaker than six ledgers' phrasing
   implies, and none of them had ever put a number on it.

3. **And it inverts precisely where the mechanism predicts it should be strongest — REFUTED, harder
   than leg 2.** Split the same 120: **80 ordinary monthlies run 62.5% (50/80), median ratio 1.160**;
   the **40 quarterly triple witchings run 47.5% (19/40), median ratio 0.905**. Witchings roll off
   index options, index futures, single-stock options and single-stock futures at once — several times
   the open interest of a monthly — so a gamma-roll-off mechanic demands the *larger* effect there.
   It produces the smaller one, below the null, with a median ratio under 1. **The honest reading:**
   what the monthlies' 62.5% picks up is more plausibly what else lands in a random post-expiration
   week than the expiration itself, because a witching's following week is systematically quieter
   (quarter-end passed, earnings season over) and that swamps whatever the roll-off does.

4. **Month-of-year dispersion is enormous and is evidence for leg 3's reading rather than a seasonal
   edge — MIXED, and deliberately not promoted.** Per month, n=10 each: Jan **8/10** · Feb 8/10 ·
   Mar 5/10 · **Apr 10/10** · **May 1/10** · Jun 6/10 · Jul 9/10 · Aug 5/10 · Sep 4/10 · Oct 6/10 ·
   Nov 3/10 · Dec 4/10. A 10%-to-100% spread with no proposed mechanism is what "the window's contents
   dominate" looks like. **May's cell is this event's month and is the sample's worst** — 1/10, median
   ratio 0.731, uncorrected binomial p ≈ 9.7e-4 against the 62.5% monthly base, ≈0.012 after a
   twelve-way Bonferroni correction. **The obvious mechanical explanation was tested and refuted:**
   Memorial Day is *not* inside those windows — all ten May post-expiration windows span exactly **7
   calendar days**, holiday-free. So the cell survives its first check and still must not be traded:
   one cell of twelve at n=10, selected because it is this event's month, with no mechanism, is
   exactly the retrofit `multi-symbol-sweep.md`'s kill list exists to prevent. It is registered as a
   falsifier, not a call.

5. **A sibling's banked calendar rule is half wrong, and this event is what exposes it — REFUTED.**
   [`opex-2027-04-16`](opex-2027-04-16.md) leg 4 banked: *late-month FOMC → opex is the last
   pre-blackout session; mid-month FOMC → opex is **inside** the blackout.* The first clause holds.
   The second is false in all four of its own 2027 cases, because it used only the blackout's **start**
   (the second Saturday before the meeting) and never its **end** (the Thursday following the meeting).
   Recomputed with both: Jan 01-16→01-28 · Mar 03-06→**03-18** · Apr 04-17→04-29 · Jun 05-29→06-10 ·
   Jul 07-17→07-29 · Sep 09-04→**09-16** · Oct 10-16→10-28 · Dec 11-27→12-09. March's opex is
   **2027-03-19**, one day *after* that window closes; September's is **2027-09-17**, one day after
   its own; June's and December's are 7 and 8 days past a lift. **Zero of 2027's twelve third Fridays
   fall inside a blackout.** This repo already contained the contradiction: `fomc-blackout-start-2027-03-06`'s
   own title reads *"through 2027-03-18"*, and `fomc-blackout-start-2027-05-29`'s note describes the

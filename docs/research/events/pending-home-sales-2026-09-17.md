### Leg 2 — does the December lane's builder-earnings collision fire on this edition? **REFUTED**

`FT-pending-home-sales-2026-12-17-3` proposed a mechanism, not a coincidence: NAR's PHSI slot sits at
**day 16–21** of the month, which is *inside* the homebuilder reporting window, and that is why 4 of the
8 realized 2026 PHSI dates carried a builder 8-K within a day while the 8 existing-home-sales dates
carried zero. If the mechanism is real it should fire on 09-17 too. Re-run EDGAR (caches busted) says it
should not:

| Filer | Ever files in September? | FY2026 lag from quarter end | Implied Q3 FY2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LEN** | Yes, **20 of 22** years | Q1 **+12d** (03-12), Q2 **+11d** (06-11) — down from FY2025's +20d/+17d/+19d | ~**09-11 / 09-12** |

| DHI · NVR · PHM | **Never** — Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct filers | — | not September |

So the two September filers straddle the print rather than land on it, and LEN straddles it *early*
because its FY2026 reporting lag compressed by **6–8 days** on both quarters filed this year. Note the
base rate cuts the same way independently: LEN's last six Q3 filings were 09-14, 09-20, 09-21, 09-14,
09-19, 09-19 — **none** inside a ±1-day window around September 17. **December is where the collision
lives** (LEN has filed Q4 in the 12-14…12-19 window in six of the last seven years); September is the
edition where it does not.

This matters for attribution rather than for a trade: if LEN reports on 09-11/12, the homebuilder tape
on 09-17 is *digesting* a builder print, not colliding with one — a slow drift, not a gap. Registered as
`FT-pending-home-sales-2026-09-17-2`, and deliberately **not** proposed as a calendar event: neither
company has published a Q3 FY2026 date, and a lag pattern is not a source.

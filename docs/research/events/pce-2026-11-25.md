## Initial research

October-2026 PCE release on 2026-11-25 — the Wednesday before Thanksgiving, in a week with almost
nothing else on the calendar, two weeks before the year's final SEP?

**One-line verdict.** The number is unknowable at D-88, but the *structure* is fully knowable and
unusually loaded: this is the last observation of the Fed's preferred gauge before the December dot

quarter where the market is thinnest and official commentary is impossible — so its price impact
per unit of information is the highest of any PCE this calendar tracks, while its forecastability
today is the lowest.

2. **This is the last PCE the December FOMC sees — SUPPORTED, primary, and it is the load-bearing
   leg.** The same schedule fetch shows the *next* Personal Income and Outlays release on
   **"December 23, 8:30 AM: Personal Income and Outlays, November 2026"** — fourteen days *after*
   the 12-08/09 meeting. Combined with [`fomc-2026-12-09`](fomc-2026-12-09.md)'s primary check

   [`pce-2026-10-29`](pce-2026-10-29.md) publishes 18.5 hours *after* its FOMC and therefore
   **grades** a decision; this one publishes fourteen days *before* its FOMC and therefore **feeds**
   one. Same series, opposite transmission direction, one meeting apart.

3. **The December SEP will observe exactly one of the three Q4 months, and this is it — SUPPORTED,
   arithmetic over two primaries.** SEP inflation projections are stated Q4/Q4, so the 2026 core PCE
   median covers October, November and December 2026. On decision day the Committee holds October
   data (this print, 11-25); November data arrives 12-23 and December data in late January. So a
   single monthly observation — this one — carries a third of the window the Committee must project,
   and it is the only third that is not a forecast. For scale, the **June 2026 SEP medians**
   (federalreserve.gov, fetched today): PCE inflation 2026 **3.6**, core PCE 2026 **3.3**, 2027 core
   **2.5**, funds rate 2026 **3.8**. The last actual print — July data, released 08-26 and scored in
   [`pce-2026-08-26`](pce-2026-08-26.md) — was core **3.3% y/y** / headline **3.7% y/y**, i.e. the
   Committee's own 2026 core projection is currently sitting exactly on the realized rate.

4. **No official interpretation of this print is possible before the decision — SUPPORTED, two
   primaries, and it is this doc's most distinctive finding.** The FOMC's external-communications
   policy states the blackout "will begin at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time the second Saturday before a
   meeting and end at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time the day after a meeting" (federalreserve.gov policy
   PDFs, retrieved today). For a meeting starting **Tuesday 12-08**, the Saturdays before it are
   12-05 and **11-28** — so blackout opens **11-28**. That leaves 11-25, 11-26 and 11-27 as the only
   days a participant could speak to this print, and per NYSE's own 2026 calendar (fetched today)
   **11-26 is Thanksgiving, a full market closure, and 11-27 closes at 1:00pm ET**. In practice the
   speaking window is the afternoon of the print itself. **Consequence, stated without a position:**
   the market will price this release entirely on its own read and hold that read for two weeks, with
   the first authoritative interpretation arriving at Warsh's 12-09 press conference. There is no
   "Fed-speak walks it back" channel here — a channel every other high-impact print this calendar
   tracks has.

5. **The tape is the thinnest of the quarter — SUPPORTED (calendar), INFERENCE (amplification).**
   NYSE primary again: **11-26 closed, 11-27 early close 1:00pm ET**, making 11-25 the last full
   session of the week and the print an 08:30 ET event on a half-attended Wednesday. That thin

   **not something this repo has measured with its own instruments** — labelled inference, and it is
   not sized. What *is* concrete and not an inference: anything held into this print cannot be
   hedged on 11-26 at all and only in a half session on 11-27.

### What the conditions support

**A weight ranking, a discount extension, and a guard that starts earlier — no direction.** The
weight ranking is legs 2/3/4: among the PCE releases this calendar tracks, this is the one with the
strongest structural claim on a decision and the weakest ability to be interpreted before that
decision, so it deserves more advance preparation than 10-29 did and none of the "it grades, it does
not decide" discount that doc earned. The discount extension is leg 7: the 09-30 deflator change
must be netted out of the **December SEP's projection revision**, not only out of monthly prints, or
a mechanically lower 2026 core median gets read as the Committee turning dovish. The guard is legs
4/5/6: [`jobs-2026-12-04`](jobs-2026-12-04.md) opens the year-end corridor on 12-04, and this doc's
finding is that the guard should open on **11-25** instead — the last inflation input, the thinnest
liquidity of the quarter and the last chance to hedge before a two-day market interruption all land
on the same morning. **No position is opened, closed or sized off this event**, and no level is
forecast; `symbols: []`, no house playbook is macro-keyed, and the paper book's exposure to this
print is entirely indirect.

### Honest limits

**Every quantified inflation figure in this doc is inherited or old.** The ~20bp revision sizing
reaches here through [`pce-2026-10-29`](pce-2026-10-29.md) at search-snippet fidelity from two
outlets that 403 on direct fetch, and it must be re-verified against the 09-30 release before it is
repeated as fact — leg 7's SEP extension inherits that fragility wholesale and collapses with it.
The 3.3%/3.7% anchors are **July** data; four monthly prints (Aug, Sep, Oct, and this one's own
reference month) land between today and 11-25, so they are a starting point, not a base case, and
this doc deliberately states **no level**. Leg 5's liquidity amplification is a market-structure
generality this repo has never measured with its own instruments. Leg 8 compares two different
venues' probabilities and is labelled an observation for exactly that reason. The blackout date is
computed from the policy's stated rule against a meeting date, not read off a published blackout
calendar for this specific meeting — the rule and the meeting date are each primary, the arithmetic
joining them is this doc's. And the promotion in leg 1 is a **judgement about a process rule**, not
a new fact: the underlying BEA line was already primary-sourced on 2026-08-29 by the sibling; what
changed is which rule this doc reads as governing. If that reading is wrong, the fix is a one-line
revert, and `pce-2026-09-30` was deliberately left alone so the two events do not have to be
reverted together.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BEA primary fetched 2026-08-29 and promoted from `estimate` this pass).**
Treat 2026-11-25 08:30 ET as **the highest-leverage macro print of Q4 that carries no tradable
edge**. Its leverage is structural and primary-sourced: it is the last PCE before the 12-09 SEP
(next release 12-23), the only Q4 month that SEP will observe, and it lands three days before a
blackout in a holiday-shortened week with nothing else on the calendar — so it will be priced with
no official interpretation and no liquidity, and that read will stand for two weeks. Its lack of
edge is equally clear: `symbols: []`, no macro-keyed house playbook, and at D-88 no consensus,
whisper or nowcast for October data exists, so **no base-case level is stated** and inventing one
would be exactly the confident-unsourced number this lane refuses. The operative content is
therefore three rules and no direction: **(a)** carry the inherited **~20bp 09-30 discount into the
December SEP's projection revision**, not just into monthly prints — a lower 2026 core median is
partly the new deflator; **(b)** replace that ~20bp estimate with the **measured** figure once the
09-30 and 10-29 releases have landed, and stop repeating the snippet-sourced number; **(c)** open
the year-end guard on **11-25** rather than [`jobs-2026-12-04`](jobs-2026-12-04.md)'s 12-04, because
nothing carried into this print can be hedged until a half session on 11-27. Direction on the
December decision is **not this doc's to call** and is deferred wholesale to
[`fomc-2026-12-09`](fomc-2026-12-09.md), whose hold-with-a-hawkish-tail base case is unchanged by
anything found here.

**Kill switches:**

- **BEA schedules any Personal Income and Outlays release between 11-25 and 12-09** — leg 2's
  "last input" framing dies outright and this reverts to an ordinary PCE print; check the schedule
  page every pulse.
- **The 12-08/09 FOMC moves, or loses its projections asterisk** — legs 2 and 3 both rest on it
  being the year's final SEP meeting; re-source federalreserve.gov's calendar if any reschedule is
  reported.
- **The 09-30 release lands with revised core y/y within ~5bp of the old series** — the ~20bp
  paper-cooling estimate is wrong, leg 7 collapses, and rules (a) and (b) are withdrawn rather than
  carried to 11-25.
- **The realized revision materially exceeds ~20bp** (say ≥40bp on core y/y) — the opposite failure;
  the SEP-revision confusion in leg 7 becomes the dominant story for the December meeting, not a
  footnote.
- **A lapse in appropriations actually begins 2026-10-01 and persists into October** — this print's
  *reference month* is damaged, not just its release date (leg 10); switch the premise from "the
  print lands late" to "the October data is degraded or gone" and defer to
  [`government-funding-deadline-2026-09-30`](government-funding-deadline-2026-09-30.md).
- **A hike is delivered on 09-16 or 10-28** — this print stops being an input to "will they" and
  becomes an input to "will they again," which is a louder release; reassess off-cycle.
- **Any FOMC participant speaks to inflation between 11-25 and 11-27** — leg 4's no-commentary
  finding is falsified in the only window where it can be, and the print gets an interpretation
  channel this doc says it lacks.
- **The October CPI on 11-10 surprises by more than ~0.2pp on core** — the market will pre-position
  this print off that read, and the "no forecastable content" limit in leg 9 stops being true.
- **Polymarket's 2026-hike market moves outside ~50–80%** — leg 8's "re-timed, not increased"
  observation gets scored one way or the other.

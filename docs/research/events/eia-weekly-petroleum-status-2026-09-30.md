### Leg 2 — is there a number in this print whose direction is not noise? SUPPORTED, and it is the only one

This is the leg that justifies the ledger, and it is the one thing a D-15 session can say that a D-1
session on a different week could not. **2026-09-30 reports the week ending 09-25 — three weeks past
the week ending 09-04.** That window is the fall refinery turnaround.

Measured from EIA's full weekly utilization history (`wpuleus3`, 1,871 observations, 1990-11-02 →
2026-09-04, fetched direct this session). For every year 1993–2025 the study takes the September week
nearest the 4th as the base and the week exactly 21 days later as the target — the same offset that
maps 2026-09-04 onto 2026-09-25:

| Cohort | n | Target < 96.0 | Declined | Median Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All years 1993–2025 | 33 | **32** | 27 | −2.60 |
| Base ≥ 95.0 | 11 | 10 | 10 | −2.90 |
| Base ≥ 96.0 | 6 | 5 | **6** | −3.25 |
| **Base ≥ 97.0** (the 2026 analogs) | **3** | **2** | **3** | **−3.60** |

The sole year in 33 whose target week printed ≥ 96.0 is **1997 (98.1)**, and the highest late-September
reading ever recorded is **98.5 (1997-09-19)**. The three high-base analogs are **1997 (99.4 → 98.1)**,
**1998 (98.4 → 94.8)** and **2018 (97.6 → 90.4)**.

**Two independent estimates, and they converge.** Path one: 2026's base of **97.8** plus the
high-base median delta of **−3.60** → **94.2**. Path two: 2025's own target week printed **91.4**,
and 2026 has run **+2.9** above 2025 at the base week (**+1.68** mean over the last 13 aligned weeks)
→ **94.3**. Both sit 1.7–1.8 points under the 96.0 threshold.

**The publisher states the mechanism itself.** From the September 2026 STEO (p. 6, extracted from the
archived PDF this session): *"Distillate production typically decreases during the fall refinery
maintenance season while harvest-season agricultural demand for distillate increases."*

### Leg 3 — does the turnaround actually happen this year? MIXED, and this is what caps the confidence

The counterweight is the strongest refining incentive in years, and it is not hand-waved. Heating oil
at **$5.0109/gal** against WTI at **$106.01** is a crack of roughly **$2.49/gal (~$104.5/bbl)** — at
the very top of the STEO's own band, which reads: *"We estimate U.S. average diesel crack spreads to
exceed $2 per gallon from August through November, before decreasing steadily through mid-2027. This
decrease assumes a return to normal tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz in the near term."*
That assumption has not been met, and EIA says what follows if it is not: *"If the flows out of the
Middle East remain constrained beyond the end of 2026, we would expect to see higher distillate crack
spreads globally than what is included in our current forecast."*

Refiners paid that much have every reason to defer discretionary maintenance. Against that: a
turnaround is contracted months ahead with specialist crews and catalyst deliveries, which is why
**2018** — a 97.6 base in a strong-margin year — still gave up **7.2 points**. The two forces are the
reason `FT-…-1` is graded **medium** rather than high, and the reason the confidence is pinned to the
**n=3** analog cohort rather than to the flattering 32/33.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (event date `estimate`).** **Stand aside.** No position, no hedge and no watch-list entry is
keyed to this print, and none should be: no playbook is energy-keyed and no tracked name carries
crude exposure. What this ledger delivers is **one number to read and a written attribution rule**.
The number is **percent utilization**, which the fall turnaround season makes the only line in the
09-30 report whose direction is better than a coin flip — expected **below 96.0**, point estimate
**94.2–94.3**, from a **97.8** base. The rule is the same discipline the 09-16 sibling wrote, applied
to a different distraction: *no 2026-09-30 energy-complex move is credited to the 10:30 ET petroleum
report unless the crude change's sign matches the session's close direction* — on quarter-end, with
PCE, GDP and index rebalancing on the same tape, the null hypothesis is that the print is invisible.

**Kill switches:**

1. **The crack beats the maintenance calendar** — percent utilization prints **≥ 96.0** on 2026-09-30. `FT-…-1` dies, Leg 2's seasonal read dies with it, and the next session has evidence that a ~$2.49/gal distillate crack overrides the turnaround season outright.
2. **Turnaround arrives early** — the **09-16** or **09-23** print already shows utilization below 96.0 by a wide margin. `FT-…-1` would likely still pass, but for the wrong reason; the D-15 framing (a three-week seasonal) was measuring the wrong window and the next session rebuilds Leg 2's offset.
3. **The print reasserts itself as the price-setter** — the 09-30 commercial-crude w/w change and WTI's 09-30 close move in the textbook-matched directions. `FT-…-2` dies and the attribution rule loses its second observation.
4. **We acquire a vehicle** — any house playbook gains an energy leg, or a tracked symbol gains direct crude exposure, before **2026-12-31**. Leg 5 flips from REFUTED to live and this stops being a refusal.
5. **The shock resolves early** — the Saudi East-West pipeline returns to service and Hormuz traffic normalizes before 09-30. The crack collapses toward the STEO's own path, the counterweight in Leg 3 disappears, and `FT-…-1` becomes a much easier claim than it was registered as.
6. **Date failure (void, not killed)** — no WPSR publishes by **13:00 ET 2026-09-30**. That falsifies the date, not the analysis; both forward tests void rather than score.

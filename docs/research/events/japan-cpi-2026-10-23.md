### Conviction legs, tested

**Leg 1 — Tokyo's y/y jump is mostly base effect: SUPPORTED (arithmetic, from the Bureau's files).** A y/y rate moves by this year's m/m minus last year's m/m. Tokyo core Aug→Sep 2025: 100.5 → 99.8 = **−0.70%**, against a 2013-25 seasonal mean of **−0.16%**. National over the same month: 100.3 → 100.1 = **−0.20%** against **−0.01%**. So the weak base alone lifts Tokyo's y/y by ~0.54pp and the nation's by ~0.19pp before any 2026 price action. Tokyo's implied 2026 Aug→Sep m/m is **≈ +0.2%**, ~0.36pp above seasonal — real momentum, but ~40% of the 0.92pp rise, not all of it.

**Leg 2 — that momentum passes through to the nation at ~0.6×: SUPPORTED, small samples.** Regressing national Aug→Sep core m/m on Tokyo's: **2000-25 (n=26)** slope **0.58**, corr **0.81**, residual sd **0.086pp**; **2013-25 (n=13)** slope 0.64, corr 0.88; **2018-25 (n=8)** slope 0.64, corr 0.88. Applied to +0.196% Tokyo: national m/m **+0.19% / +0.21% / +0.22%**, index **102.19 / 102.22 / 102.23** on August's 102.0, y/y **2.09 / 2.12 / 2.12%** against Sep-2025's 100.1.

**Leg 3 — the published rate is a three-way rounding call: MIXED.** Index to y/y against 100.1: **102.0 → 1.9% · 102.1 → 2.0% · 102.2 → 2.1% · 102.3 → 2.2% · 102.4 → 2.3%**. With the model centre at 102.19 and an out-of-sample error widened from the fitted 0.09 to ~0.13 (n is small, August's 102.0 is itself rounded ±0.05, and Tokyo's index is implied), the mass sits ~**74%** on 2.0–2.2%, ~**86%** on ≥2.0%, ~**7%** on 1.9% or below, ~**7%** on 2.3% or above. A stated base rate, not a market-implied probability: **no consensus was sourced.**

**Leg 4 — national usually prints *above* Tokyo in September, so Tokyo's level is a poor anchor: SUPPORTED.** Sep y/y, national minus Tokyo, 2001-25: mean **+0.17pp** (sd 0.24); 2022-25 it was +0.37 / +0.23 / +0.43 / +0.41. That makes this year's expected gap of about **−0.6pp** (2.1 vs 2.7) historically unusual — and the stated reason is the Sep-2025 Tokyo dip (−0.70%), not a change in how the two series relate. If the dip was Tokyo-specific and one-off, the base arithmetic holds; if it was a national effect that national data smoothed out, the nation catches up and leg 1 is wrong. Falsified by a print at 2.4% or above.

**Leg 5 — what the BoJ does with it: context, not a call.** The BoJ raised to **1.25%** on 2026-09-18 (7-2 per the sibling's close-out). [boj-decision-2026-10-30](boj-decision-2026-10-30.md) was written hold-modal after that hike; Tokyo's beat was reported as strengthening an October-hike case, and economists differ on whether the next move comes in October. The 2026-10-23 print is the **last whole-country observation the Outlook Report is written with, six days before the decision**. This ledger takes no view on the decision; that ledger's own next pulse owns it.

**Leg 6 — no tradeable channel: SUPPORTED.** `symbols: []`. The 2026-09-18 close-out recorded AVGO +2.97%, NVDA +1.34%, MRVL +1.45%, CRWV +1.85% on the BoJ/CPI/triple-witching day and declined to attribute any of it to Tokyo. 2026-10-23 carries no equivalent confound-free test either.

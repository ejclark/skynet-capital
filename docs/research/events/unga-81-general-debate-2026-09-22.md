## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Leaders' week is a **decoy corridor**, and this pass measured both halves of that claim.
**The venue is empty of the thing that matters:** Xi Jinping is skipping New York entirely — he
arrives in the US around **09-23**, meets Trump at the **White House on 09-24** (`estimate`), and
leaves **09-25** (Bloomberg/Politico, 2026-08-18). So the highest-stakes bilateral in this calendar
happens 200 miles from the General Assembly hall, during the debate, and belongs to
[`trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24`](trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24.md), not here. **And the corridor itself is
measurably quiet:** across the **11 general debates 2015–2025**, day one's absolute SPY move runs a
**0.29% median against a 0.49% two-year-plus baseline**, and the five-session debate week runs
**0.72% against 1.19%** — leaders' week is *below* an ordinary week, not above it. The single best
test of the "a speech moves markets" intuition cuts the same way: on **2018-09-25**, the one UNGA
address that made an explicit oil-price demand ("OPEC… ripping off the rest of the world"), **CL=F
closed +0.28% on a 1.23% intraday range — the narrowest of the ten sessions around it**, while the
headline read "oil prices move lower." What *is* live is context, not catalyst: an active US–Iran

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries the UNGA-81 general debate opening 2026-09-22, filed
`low`/`symbols: []` by the [`unsc-iran-panel-mandate-expiry-2026-09-26`](unsc-iran-panel-mandate-expiry-2026-09-26.md)
research that discovered it, which needed the dates only to establish that the 09-26 mandate expiry
falls on a UN working Saturday. This document has to justify the entry on its own terms: **is the
window right, does a leaders' week have any measurable reaction function, and — with a US–Iran war
live — does this particular one contain a dated diplomatic slot that could reach the tape?**

**One-line verdict:** the window is UN-primary and correct, the reaction function is *negative* —
general-debate weeks are measurably calmer than ordinary weeks across eleven years and five
instruments — and the corridor's one market-relevant meeting deliberately isn't at the UN, because
Xi is skipping New York for the White House on 09-24; so the honest posture is stand aside, and the
entry earns its place as an **attention corridor and a date anchor**, never a catalyst.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The window is UN-primary and exact; only the schema forces `estimate`.** SUPPORTED.
   `un.org/en/ga/81/meetings/` states the general debate runs *"from 22 September to 26 September,
   and on 28 September 2026"*, and lists the dated high-level meetings: **09-23** (fortieth
   anniversary of the Declaration on the Right to Development; climate action and just transition),
   **09-24** (existential threats posed by sea level rise), **09-25** (pandemic prevention,
   preparedness and response), **09-28** (twenty-fifth anniversary of the Durban Declaration) and
   **09-29** (International Day for the Total Elimination of Nuclear Weapons). The 81st session
   itself opened **2026-09-08** under the theme *"Restoring trust, managing transformation: a United
   Nations that delivers for all"*, with **Khalilur Rahman** (Bangladesh) as President of the General
   Assembly. `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` in `scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs` admits
   `IR|CAL|BLS|FED|PJM|SEC|TSY|OCC|BEA|CENSUS|ISM|CB|UMICH` and has **no UN or diplomatic slot** — the
   **thirteenth** ledger to bank that gap. The date is not in doubt; the label is a schema artifact,
   and it widens caution regardless.

2. **A general-debate week is measurably *calmer* than an ordinary week.** SUPPORTED, and this is the
   leg the entry needed. Measured this session from Yahoo daily bars over the **11 general debates
   2015-09-28 through 2025-09-23** (opening dates: 09-28, 09-20, 09-19, 09-25, 09-24, 09-22, 09-21,
   09-20, 09-19, 09-24, 09-23), against each instrument's own full-sample baseline (2010-01-04 →
   2026-09-04):

   | Instrument | Day-1 \|ret\|, median (n=11) | Baseline daily \|ret\|, median | Debate week (5 sessions) \|ret\|, median | Baseline 5-session \|ret\|, median |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | SPY | **0.29%** | 0.49% (p90 1.60%) | **0.72%** | 1.19% (p90 3.48%) |
   | ^VIX | **3.31%** | 3.94% (p90 11.72%) | **6.63%** | 8.23% (p90 24.13%) |
   | ITA | **0.79%** | 0.68% (p90 2.00%) | **1.51%** | 1.61% (p90 4.41%) |
   | XLE | **0.73%** | 0.83% (p90 2.61%) | **3.00%** | 2.06% (p90 5.63%) |
   | CL=F | **0.86%** | 1.23% (p90 3.63%) | **4.47%** | 2.81% (p90 8.23%) |

   Read plainly: **on the day the world's leaders start speaking, the S&P tape does less than it does
   on a random Tuesday** — 0.29% vs 0.49% — and the whole week does 0.72% against 1.19%. VIX's median
   day-1 *change* is +1.80% against a −0.67% baseline drift, i.e. a mild upward tick, but its median
   absolute change is **below** baseline (3.31% vs 3.94%): no vol event. Defense (ITA) is
   indistinguishable either way. The sample's two ugly observations are attributable elsewhere and
   said so: **2015-09-28 SPY −2.51%** (the Glencore/biotech rout) and the **2022 debate week −6.24%**
   (post-FOMC, gilt crisis).

3. **The energy exception is real at the week level and dissolves at the day level.** MIXED, and
   flagged rather than used. CL=F and XLE are the only two instruments whose *five-session* debate
   window exceeds baseline (4.47% vs 2.81%; 3.00% vs 2.06%), while both are **below** baseline on day
   one (0.86% vs 1.23%; 0.73% vs 0.83%). A UNGA speech that moved oil would show up on **day one**,
   not in a five-day window that in this calendar's own seasonality reliably contains OPEC+ meetings
   and quarter-end. So the week-level number is best read as **late-September energy seasonality
   wearing a UNGA label**, not a leaders'-week effect — the honest version of a statistic that would
   otherwise be the most quotable thing in this document.

4. **The one UNGA speech built to move oil didn't.** SUPPORTED, and it is the cleanest falsification
   available. On **2018-09-25** Trump told the 73rd session that *"OPEC and OPEC nations are, as
   usual, ripping off the rest of the world… We want them to stop raising prices, we want them to
   start lowering prices"* — with crude at four-year highs and CNBC headlining *"Oil prices move
   lower as Trump says OPEC is ripping off the world."* The tape: CL=F **opened 72.25, closed 72.28**,
   **+0.28%** against the prior close of 72.08, on an intraday range of **1.23%** — the **narrowest of
   the eleven sessions from 09-17 to 10-01**, where the others run 1.24% to 3.85%. The headline
   captured an intraday dip that closed green on a below-average range day. The 2017 companion is the
   same shape: on **2017-09-19** ("Rocket Man", "totally destroy North Korea") SPY closed
   **+0.10%** (249.72 → 249.97) and VIX moved **10.15 → 10.18**.

5. **The corridor's real event is deliberately not at the UN.** SUPPORTED, and it is the load-bearing
   finding. Xi Jinping will **skip the General Assembly**: Bloomberg (2026-08-18, reporting Politico)
   has him forgoing the New York stop entirely, arriving in the US around **09-23**, meeting Trump at
   the **White House on 09-24**, and departing **09-25**. Skipping is his default — one UNGA
   appearance since 2012, with Premier **Li Qiang** sent in 2025. Twelve tracked events sit within
   ±5 days of 09-22, and exactly one is `high` impact: [`trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24`](trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24.md)
   (`estimate`), whose own ledger already carries the AI/export-control agenda and the Lutnick
   "chip controls not on the table" narrowing. The rest of the corridor is plumbing and prints — opex
   **09-18**, the 2Y/5Y/7Y auctions on **09-22/23/24**, the 20Y/30Y buyback and SCOOS **09-24**,
   durable goods and UMich final **09-25**, the Iran panel expiry **09-26**. **Nothing in the
   09-22→09-28 tape will be cleanly attributable to the general debate**, and the one thing worth
   watching is in a different city.

6. **No dated Iran or Middle East Council meeting falls inside high-level week.** SUPPORTED, from a UN
   primary. `press.un.org/en/2026/260901_sc.doc.htm` — the Security Council President's press
   conference on the September programme of work, **France** presiding (Jérôme Bonnafont) — names
   Haiti, **Iran** and Sudan as the month's action items but attaches **no date** to the Iran file.
   The only dated items are **09-02** (informal interactive dialogue on AI in conflict resolution),
   **09-11** (counter-terrorism), and **09-25** (the annual meeting with the High Commissioner for
   Refugees). On Ukraine the President said only that *"he expects such a meeting to be held"* during
   high-level week — undated, and therefore not filable. So leaders' week supplies a **venue** for
   Iran diplomacy and no **appointment** for it.

7. **The Iranian delegation's presence is genuinely uncertain, and that closes a channel rather than
   opening one.** MIXED. The context is an active war: a 60-day US–Iran ceasefire expired **2026-08-17**
   with Trump ruling out an extension and stating no talks were scheduled (CNBC, 2026-08-18), a US
   naval blockade and the Hormuz closure are live, and Eurasia Group has pushed its de-escalation
   timeline to year-end. Against that, the host-country visa question is not hypothetical: on
   **2026-05-26** the US denied visas to Iranian FM **Abbas Araghchi** and Russian Deputy FM Alexander
   Alimov for a Security Council debate convened under China's presidency, which Russia's envoy called
   a breach of the **1947 Headquarters Agreement** (PassBlue, 2026-05-26); the same friction recurred
   through 2025 (delegation travel restrictions, security review of UNGA visas). Reporting that
   President Pezeshkian *"is expected to travel to New York"* surfaced only via search summary and
   could not be dated to a 2026 primary — treated as unverified. **Why it is not a trade either way:**
   a delegation that arrives gives speeches, and leg 2 prices what speeches are worth; a delegation
   that is refused removes the only diplomatic path this window offered. Both roads end at stand
   aside.

### What the conditions support

contains OPEC+ and quarter-end rather than in the speech day, and the crude distribution has already
repriced (the sibling ledger measured CL=F's weekend gap at **1.55% median over the last 14** against

Washington**, where the corridor's only `high`-impact event actually sits; **(2)** keep the
flat-by-print / defined-risk discipline the AI-semis names already carry through the whole
09-22→09-30 block rather than resetting it mid-corridor; **(3)** treat the Iranian delegation's visa
outcome as a free read on whether any UN-track diplomacy exists at all — a fact worth knowing, and
not a signal to act on.

### Honest limits

- **n=11.** Eleven general debates is a regime read, not a precise estimate, and the baseline is a
  full-sample one (2010→2026) rather than a matched-seasonality one — late September has its own
  character, which is exactly the confound leg 3 refuses to launder.
- **The five-session "debate week" is a mechanical window**, not the debate itself: the 2026 debate
  runs 09-22→09-26 *and* 09-28, so a five-session count from 09-22 stops at 09-28 only by
  coincidence of the calendar. It is a comparable window across years, not a definition.
- **Attribution is not attempted.** Leg 2 measures what the tape did during these weeks, not what the
  debate caused. Every one of these windows contains macro prints; the claim is the weak one — that
  the label carries no premium — and it is deliberately the only claim made.
- **Trump's attendance and speaking slot are unconfirmed.** `whitehouse.gov/news/` fetched direct
  2026-09-04 carries nothing; the 09-22 US slot rests on the Brazil-first/US-second convention
  (unbroken since the 10th session, 1955), not on a published list. `gadebate.un.org/en/81` returned
  an empty body to this lane, so no provisional speakers list was obtained.
- **Xi's absence is press-sourced** (Bloomberg reporting Politico, 2026-08-18; corroborated by
  PressTV and CryptoBriefing the same day), with no MFA or White House primary. The summit ledger
  holds the same limit for the same date.
- **`main.un.org` and `unfoundation.org` both 403'd** this lane on 2026-09-04 — the same block the
  sibling ledger recorded for `securitycouncilreport.org`. The Council programme-of-work reading
  therefore comes from `press.un.org`'s meetings-coverage write-up rather than from the POW PDF.
- **Pezeshkian's expected travel is a search-summary claim** with no dated 2026 primary behind it,
  and is carried as unverified in leg 7 rather than used in any call.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (the event is `estimate`-dated; nothing here is date-keyed action).** Stand aside on the
UNGA-81 general debate, and decline the specific version of it a Hormuz war invites — buying the
09-22→09-28 window because leaders are in one building. `symbols: []`, no position, no hedge. The
measured record says a general-debate week is **quieter** than an ordinary week for equities and
volatility, and the one speech in the modern record aimed squarely at a commodity moved that
commodity **+0.28%**. This entry's value is as an **attention corridor and a date anchor** — it
dates the window in which Iran or US–China diplomacy would surface at principals level, and it
explains why 09-26 is a UN working day on a closed tape — not as a catalyst. Within the corridor,
attention belongs to [`trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24`](trump-xi-summit-2026-09-24.md) (`estimate`,
`high`) in **Washington**, which Xi's absence from New York makes structurally clear.

**Kill switches — any one of these ends the stance and earns its own ledger row:**

- **A dated, market-relevant meeting is scheduled into 09-22→09-28** — a Security Council session on Iran or the Middle East, or an announced US–Iran or US–Russia leader-level meeting at UN HQ. The `press.un.org` 09-01 programme of work has none as of today.
- **SPY's 2026-09-22 session, or the 09-22→09-28 five-session window, exceeds its own two-year median absolute move with the excess separable** from the 09-24 summit, the 09-25 prints and the war tape. Separability is the bar, not size.
- **CL=F moves more than 3% on 2026-09-22 attributable to a general-debate statement** rather than to strikes, Hormuz transits or OPEC+ — which would break leg 4's eleven-year null directly.
- **Trump does not address the Assembly, or the US slot moves off 09-22** — the calendar's date is then an opening ceremony rather than the corridor's highest-attention session, and the entry should be re-dated.
- **Iran's delegation is formally refused entry** — a Headquarters-Agreement rupture during a live war, which is a diplomacy-closure headline this stance does not model and would want a row for.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** what will the 2026-11-25 Beige Book likely say, does it move anything
landing 14:00 ET the day before Thanksgiving on the same morning as a high-impact PCE print, and does
it change how we sit in the tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN) into the
Dec 8–9 FOMC?

**One-line verdict:** it is the last Beige Book of 2026 and the last one the year's final dot plot
sees, and unlike the October edition its collection window closes *after* the midterms and the two
big inflation prints — so it is worth **reading more carefully than its sibling** and is still not
worth trading, because its release lands into a holiday half-week with a blackout three days behind
it.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stand aside. Read the twelve District reports as evidence into 12-09 — with post-midterm policy
anecdote and AI/data-center language as the two named things to read for — and take no position sized
to the release.** Date is `confirmed` (two federalreserve.gov primaries, 2026-08-31), which removes a

adjustment to any tracked name on its content alone, and **11-25 is treated as a PCE session with a
Beige Book in it**, inside a holiday half-week whose commentary channel is shut by the **11-28**
blackout (`estimate`). The July 2026 counts (activity 11/1, labor 5/7, prices 9/2/1) and the Nov 2025
analogue counts are logged for diffing, to be superseded by the 09-02 and 10-14 editions.

**Kill switches:**

- **The 2026-09-02 edition moving the tape in its own 14:00–15:00 ET hour with the release as the
  named driver** — falsifies leg 7 for this regime and forces the reaction function to be rebuilt
  rather than patched. Cheapest available test; resolves in two days.
- **The 2026-09-02 and 2026-10-14 editions both landing with no data-center, AI or power-cost
  language in any District report** — kills leg 6's premise that this is a live channel, and reverts
  the reading protocol to the generic District-dispersion version the 10-14 sibling already carries.
- **Four or more Districts describing "robust" or "strong" price growth on 2026-11-25** (vs two in
  July 2026) — district-side corroboration of the inflation-breadth case, landing three days before
  the blackout on the last evidence the SEP sees; re-read the Dec-09 odds channel.
- **Three or fewer Districts reporting employment gains on 2026-11-25** (vs five in July 2026), or
  explicit layoff language — the district-side labor contradiction, with **no jobs print between this
  release and the meeting** other than **12-04** to adjudicate it.
- **A measurable tape move in the 2026-11-25 14:00–15:00 ET hour attributable to this release rather
  than the morning's PCE** — falsifies leg 3. Stated with its own difficulty: on a PCE day in a
  half-staffed holiday tape, attribution is weak and thin liquidity can manufacture a move that means
  nothing; the test is the 14:00 hour specifically, read against volume.
- **Any FOMC participant speech appearing on the Board's calendar between 2026-11-25 and 2026-11-28**
  — falsifies leg 4's "zero commentary" claim and gives the release an interpretation channel before
  the gate.
- **The 11-25 release slipping or the time changing** — the entry is now `confirmed`; a schedule
  change is a calendar correction, not a market signal.

**No forward test registered.** The stance takes no position sized to any branch, so there is nothing
to score in [`forward-tests.md`](../forward-tests.md); the kill switches above carry the
falsification, each with its date.

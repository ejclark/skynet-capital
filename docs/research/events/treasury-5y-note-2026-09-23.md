## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Still **watch-only, no standalone play** — but the belly stopped being a sideshow. Since
2026-08-28 (Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote) the September FOMC repriced from a hold to a **~65–68%
hike**, and the resulting selloff is **led by this tenor**: 5Y CMT +14bp over 8/25–8/31 vs the 30Y's

story. This auction, on 2026-09-23, is the first 5Y supply to clear **after** that decision — at a
yield ~15–20bp above where August's cleared. Two corrections to the prior read: the tailing streak
**broke** on 2026-08-26 (a +0.2bp near-stop-through, bid-to-cover 2.37 *above* average, dealers
taking less than usual — Treasury's own API), leaving soft **foreign** demand (indirect 61.5% vs a
~65.7% norm) as the one genuinely weak leg; and the $70B size is no longer an assumption — it is
primary-sourced as unchanged for six straight months. Date and the 2026-09-17 announcement are
**confirmed**; this auction's own size/demand figures do not exist yet. No tracked tickers.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** what does the 5-year note auction on 2026-09-23 tell us, landing after the
Sep-16 FOMC and Sep-18 OpEx as the belly's most Fed-path-sensitive point, and does it change behavior
around the long-duration tech names the calendar tracks?

**One-line verdict:** the recent 5-year trend is genuinely weak (a tailing streak on soft foreign
demand — 2026's weakest coupon tenor), but the auction is a second-order signal reading the *post-Fed*
rate landscape; treat it as a caution modifier on long-duration tech exposure, not an independent
trade. No symbols; monitoring, not entries.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (confirmed-date event; no standalone play):** watch-only, context event. Do not size or time
any position off this auction's expected or actual result in isolation. Its actionable role is as a
conditioning input on the belly/rate side of the long-duration-tech duration-sensitivity read — read
*after* the Sep-16 FOMC and the fresher Aug-26 5-year print, both of which precede it. The tailing
streak (soft foreign demand, 2026's weakest coupon tenor) is the trend worth tracking, not any one
auction.

**Amended 2026-09-02** (receipt: the D21 ledger row below). The call is unchanged — still watch-only,
still no play — but two of its premises are not. (a) **The tailing streak broke.** The Aug-26 5Y
printed a +0.2bp near-stop-through with bid-to-cover 2.37 above its ~2.32–2.33 average and dealers
absorbing *less* than usual; what survives is narrower and more specific — soft **foreign** (indirect)
demand at 61.5% vs a ~65.7% norm, inside an otherwise solid auction. "2026's weakest coupon tenor" is
now a claim about who buys, not about whether the auction clears. (b) **The belly is no longer a
second-order read.** With the September FOMC repriced to a ~65–68% hike after Warsh's 8/28 keynote,
the 5Y is the tenor absorbing the repricing (5Y CMT +14bp vs the 30Y's +8bp over 8/25–8/31), so this
auction now reads the market's *post-decision* rate path directly rather than echoing a long-end
term-premium story. That raises the auction's information value without licensing any position:
watching more closely is not the same as trading, and nothing here changes the guard.

**Kill switches (what would change this stance):**
- If the Sep-23 result is unusually extreme in either direction (stops through with indirect sharply
  above average, or tails well beyond the recent +0.9bp band with indirect at new multi-auction lows)
  **and** it lands into a hawkish-hold/hike FOMC aftermath — escalate from "background context" to an
  explicit same-day caution flag on long-duration tech names, worth a pulse note off-cadence.
- If the Aug-26 5-year (the nearer print) breaks the tailing streak decisively (stops through, indirect
  back above average), the belly-weakness read softens and should be logged at the next pulse.
  **Adjudicated 2026-09-02 — fired in part.** The tail and cover legs cleared (+0.2bp, BTC 2.37 above
  average); the indirect leg did not (61.5%, still ~4pp under the norm). Logged as a *narrowing* of
  the weakness read to foreign demand alone, not a clearing of it.
- If the size announcement (~2026-09-17) shows a materially different size than the recent ~$70B
  pattern, the demand-absorption math changes and should be logged regardless of cadence.
  **Sharpened 2026-09-02:** the announcement date is now confirmed as **Thursday 2026-09-17**
  (treasury.gov tentative schedule, fetched direct), and the $70B baseline is primary-sourced —
  every 5-year note auction Mar–Aug 2026 offered exactly $70B (Treasury Fiscal Data API). Any
  deviation on 9/17 is therefore a clean, testable break rather than a judgment call.
- If the Sep-16 FOMC resolves *against* the current hike-favored pricing (a hold with hike odds
  falling back under ~40%) the "belly leads the selloff" premise added on 2026-09-02 lapses and this
  auction reverts to routine month-end supply — log the reversion rather than carrying the amendment
  forward on momentum.
- Nothing here licenses date-keyed *action* — `confirmed` covers the date only; the stance stays
  observational (estimates/observations widen caution, never license entries).

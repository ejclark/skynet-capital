## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** what does the 5-year note auction on 2026-09-23 tell us, landing after the
Sep-16 FOMC and Sep-18 OpEx as the belly's most Fed-path-sensitive point, and does it change behavior
around the long-duration tech names the calendar tracks?

**One-line verdict:** the recent 5-year trend is genuinely weak (a tailing streak on soft foreign
demand — 2026's weakest coupon tenor), but the auction is a second-order signal reading the *post-Fed*
rate landscape; treat it as a caution modifier on long-duration tech exposure, not an independent
trade. No symbols; monitoring, not entries.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (confirmed-date event; no standalone play):** watch-only, context event. Do not size or time
any position off this auction's expected or actual result in isolation. Its actionable role is as a
conditioning input on the belly/rate side of the long-duration-tech duration-sensitivity read — read
*after* the Sep-16 FOMC and the fresher Aug-26 5-year print, both of which precede it. The tailing
streak (soft foreign demand, 2026's weakest coupon tenor) is the trend worth tracking, not any one
auction.

**Amended 2026-09-02** (receipt: the D21 ledger row below). The call is unchanged — still watch-only,
still no play — but two of its premises are not. (a) **The tailing streak broke.** The Aug-26 5Y
printed a +0.2bp near-stop-through with bid-to-cover 2.37 above its ~2.32–2.33 average and dealers
absorbing *less* than usual; what survives is narrower and more specific — soft **foreign** (indirect)
demand at 61.5% vs a ~65.7% norm, inside an otherwise solid auction. "2026's weakest coupon tenor" is
now a claim about who buys, not about whether the auction clears. (b) **The belly is no longer a
second-order read.** With the September FOMC repriced to a ~65–68% hike after Warsh's 8/28 keynote,
the 5Y is the tenor absorbing the repricing (5Y CMT +14bp vs the 30Y's +8bp over 8/25–8/31), so this
auction now reads the market's *post-decision* rate path directly rather than echoing a long-end
term-premium story. That raises the auction's information value without licensing any position:
watching more closely is not the same as trading, and nothing here changes the guard.

**Noted 2026-09-09** (receipt: the D14 ledger row below). **The call did not change and this is not
an amendment** — but the 09-02 amendment's premise (b) needs one qualification on the record. "The
belly is the tenor absorbing the repricing" was measured on *outright* yield, and outright yield has
gone quiet: the 5Y moved **+2bp** in the week to 09-08 after **+20bp** in the week before it. What
kept moving is *relative* value — the 2s5s30s butterfly at **−50bp** (09-08) vs **−56bp** (09-01)
and **−73bp** (08-18), i.e. the belly cheapening against both wings while the whole curve stood
still. That is the signature of **supply concession**, not of a rate-path repricing, and it is the
more useful frame for a doc about an auction. Premise (b) survives with its mechanism relabelled;
its registered kill switch (hike odds under ~40%) is **not fired** — Kalshi 55% / Polymarket 54.5%
on 09-09, down from ~65–68% but well clear of the line.

**Amended 2026-09-16** (receipt: the D7 ledger row below). The call is unchanged for a fifth row —
watch-only, no play, no symbols — and two premises move, in opposite directions.

(c) **The foreign-demand leg is half the size this doc has been claiming, because the denominator
was wrong.** Every "indirect below the ~65.7% average" statement from D35 forward rests on a
secondary tracker (helious.io) and on percentages taken against the wrong base. Recomputed today
from Treasury's Fiscal Data `auctions_query` on **competitive accepted** (indirect + direct +
dealer) — the denominator the [7Y sibling](treasury-7y-note-2026-09-24.md) established this week,

62.15%, 10Y 79.18%, 30Y 79.48%) — the 5Y's twelve nominal-$70B prints since 2025-09-24 mean
**63.47%** indirect (median 61.55%, range 59.24–74.85%). August's 61.51% is **−1.96pp** under that,
not the −4.2pp carried. This is a *measurement correction on a better source*, not a revision of a
past row: the earlier rows stated their source and its secondary status honestly, and the numbers
they quoted stand as what that source said. What changes is the weight: a ~2pp shortfall inside a
59–75% historical range is texture, and the doc over-weighted it for four rows.

(d) **A real sponsorship break did arrive this week — but at the 20Y, and only half of it travels
here.** The 09-15 20Y reopening (912810UX4, $13B, 5.420%) drew indirect **52.47%** and dealers

term-premium base this belly auction does not share, so it does not transfer; **dealer take-down is
a capacity statistic** about underwriter balance sheet, which is tenor-agnostic, so it does. This is
the same symmetry argument the D14 row used to claim the 3Y *was* the right tenor to read — applied
honestly, it says most of the 20Y is the wrong tenor and one line of it is the right one.

**Relabelled again 2026-09-16.** The 09-09 row relabelled premise (b)'s mechanism as **supply
concession**, measured on the 2s5s30s butterfly. The fly kept widening (−50 → **−37bp**) but the
decomposition no longer supports the label: **2s5s flattened 18 → 16bp** — the 5Y *richened* against
the 2Y — while **5s30s flattened 68 → 53bp**. All 13bp is the long end declining to follow a
front-led selloff, which the 20Y and 30Y siblings both measured directly. The fly is retired as this
doc's concession gauge; the honest measure is the **level**, and it is unambiguous: 4.83% is
**+43.7bp** through August's 4.393% stop.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

Meta Connect 2026 runs 2026-09-23–24 with the Zuckerberg keynote on day 1. Is that date Meta's own
(the calendar carries it as an `estimate`)? Does a Connect keynote actually move META — measurably,
not anecdotally? And does the +6.7% META has run in three sessions into it change any of that?

### One-line verdict

The date is now **primary-confirmed** off meta.com/connect; the keynote has **no measurable
footprint** on META's tape (n=7 keynote windows, −0.85% mean excess vs QQQ, win 2/7, −0.85 SE from a
1,928-window base rate); the run into it is a **dated AI-model re-rating, not a Connect run-up**; and
the 2026 reaction day is confounded by the Trump–Xi summit — so this event licenses nothing at any
horizon and exists here as a watch item on the glasses thesis feeding the est. 10-28 print.

### Method

Sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) (product-launch
kind), **plus** a one-off measured study this session, because the AAPL product-launch precedent
([`aapl-iphone-18-launch-2026-09-09`](aapl-iphone-18-launch-2026-09-09.md)) had to lean on
press-reported seasonals and said so as an honest limit. Here the reaction function is computed
directly: split/dividend-adjusted daily bars for **META** and **QQQ** via `scripts/research/
market-data.mjs` (the earnings-cycle fetch layer), **cache busted first**
(`rm -rf node_modules/.cache/earnings-cycle node_modules/.cache/intraday-edges`), fetched
**2026-09-04**. Seven keynote dates — 2019-09-25, 2020-09-16, 2021-10-28, 2022-10-11, 2023-09-27,
2024-09-25, 2025-09-17 — each measured for the D-5→D-1 pre-window, keynote day, next session, the
D-1→D+1 pair and D+1→D+5, every one stated as **excess over QQQ** so the tech-beta is not counted as
a Connect effect. The control is META's own distribution of overlapping 2-session excess returns
since 2019-01-01 (n=1,928). Read against the house playbooks and the kill list in
[`multi-symbol-sweep.md`](../multi-symbol-sweep.md), and against the sibling
[`meta-2026-10-28-print`](meta-2026-10-28-print.md) ledger for consistency. The neither-run-here
instruments (`earnings-cycle.mjs`, `intraday-edges.mjs`) are the print ledger's, not this one's.

### Honest limits

**n=7 is a small sample and is stated as one** — the study can reject "there is a large, reliable
Connect effect" and cannot reject "there is a small one"; that asymmetry is the whole reason the
call is stand-aside rather than a fade. Two of the seven events (2019, 2020) predate the current
product cadence and one (2021) is contaminated by an earnings print three sessions earlier and the
corporate rename; dropping them leaves n=4, which is not worth quoting separately. QQQ is the only
control run — no peer basket, no factor adjustment. The keynote **time** (4pm PT) is secondary-
sourced; if it were in fact an intraday slot, the reaction would land on 09-23 and every window
would need re-keying by a day, which is why both D0 and D+1 are reported above. Product and pricing
expectations are entirely rumor. The 09-03 close carries a small **unreconciled source divergence**
— Yahoo bars and stockanalysis.com both give **$610.68**, while StockStory/FinancialContent reports
**$613.53**; the probe-ref records the two-source figure. Finally, this doc scores no P/L: META's
tradable outcomes are scored in the [10-28 print ledger](meta-2026-10-28-print.md), and this event's
close-out scores its date and reaction-function claims only.

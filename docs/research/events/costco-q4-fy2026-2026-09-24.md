### Leg 1 — "the print corroborates the price-not-demand read on August retail" → MIXED, and narrowed

**The comp half is supported, and it is already public.** Costco's August release (4 weeks ended
2026-08-30, published 2026-09-02) prints **US comparable sales +9.0% headline against +5.6%
excluding gasoline price and FX** — a **3.4pp** wedge; on the total-company basis it is **8.4% vs
5.4%**, a **3.0pp** wedge. The proposal's "+9.0% vs +5.6%" quote is correct and is the US pair, not
a mismatched aggregate. That wedge is the same mechanic `retail-sales-2026-09-16` runs its
arithmetic on — its D-1 row puts the gasoline leg at **~+0.31pp** of a **~+0.72pp** mechanical
band — measured here from inside one company's books rather than inferred from a deflator.

**The unique contribution of 09-24 is a different split than advertised.** Costco reports
comparable **traffic** and **adjusted comparable ticket** every quarter (the monthly releases do
not), and the four-quarter series is the strongest single finding in this document:

| Quarter (weeks ended) | Comp traffic | Adj comp ticket | Ticket − traffic | Gross margin (y/y) | Diluted EPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 FY2025 (2025-08-31, 16w) | **+3.7%** | +2.6% | **−1.1pp** | 11.13% (**+13bps**) | $5.87 (+11.0%) |
| Q1 FY2026 (2025-11-23, 12w) | +3.1% | +3.2% | +0.1pp | 11.32% (+4bps) | $4.50 (+11.4%) |
| Q2 FY2026 (2026-02-15, 12w) | +3.1% | +3.5% | +0.4pp | 11.02% (+17bps) | $4.58 (+13.9%) |
| Q3 FY2026 (2026-05-10, 12w) | **+2.4%** | **+7.3%** | **+4.9pp** | 11.04% (**−21bps**) | $4.93 (+15.2%) |

Traffic decelerates monotonically while adjusted ticket accelerates, and the quarter the ticket
spikes is the quarter gross margin turns negative. (The quarter-end dates are derived and
self-checking: FY2026 is the 52 weeks ended 2026-08-30, so 12+12+12+16 lands Q2 on 2026-02-15 and
Q3 on 2026-05-10 — exactly the two 10-Q document names on EDGAR, `cost-20260215.htm` and
`cost-20260510.htm`.)

**Why this is MIXED rather than SUPPORTED.** Adjusted comparable ticket is *dollars per transaction,
excluding gasoline and FX*. It rises when a member buys **more units per trip** just as readily as
when **each unit costs more**. So the print separates trips from basket size; it does **not**
separate price from quantity inside the basket. The proposal's claim that this is "the
dollars-versus-units decomposition that no line of the Census release offers" is overstated, and the
correction is worth carrying forward as vocabulary: what Costco gives away for free is a
**trips-vs-basket** split.

**The rival explanation, stated at full strength because it explains both numbers.** A shift toward
**bullion and big-ticket discretionary** — very high dollars per transaction, very low margin rate —
raises adjusted ticket and compresses gross margin in one move, which is precisely the Q3 pattern.
On that reading the ticket spike is mix, not consumer price inflation, and the macro read-through is
worth nothing. The tell that separates them is on the call, not in the release: management's own
core-inflation commentary and the fresh-food / non-foods mix breakdown. Until that is heard, the
read-through is medium confidence and nothing more.

### Leg 2 — "this is a material event for COST" → REFUTED on revenue, live on margin and membership

The 2026-09-02 release already carries **Q4 net sales $93.9B, +11.3% from $84.4B** and **FY2026
$297.3B, +10.2% from $269.9B**. A monthly-sales cadence pre-announces the top line, so the largest
surprise channel an earnings print normally has is closed before it opens. What remains:

- **EPS.** Consensus clusters **$6.51–$6.56** (secondary/aggregator reads, 2026-09-13/15), ~+10.9%
  on Q4 FY2025's $5.87. Note that the ~$94.5B "revenue consensus" quoted alongside it sits *below*
  net sales already reported plus membership fee income — a stale estimate, not a bar to clear.
- **Gross margin.** Q4 FY2025 base **11.13%**; Q3 FY2026 ran **−21bps**. August's gasoline wedge is
  the year's widest, and gas dilutes the blended rate mechanically because it sells at a lower
  margin — so a *negative* y/y print is the mechanical expectation, not a disappointment.
- **Membership.** Renewal rate has sat at **89.7–89.8% for four straight quarters**; fee income grew
  +14.0%, +14.0%, +13.6%, +10.7%. The deceleration is the number with information in it.

4. **Two prints carry it.** −3.76% (2024-03-07) and −3.07% (2025-03-06) supply about half the mean;

## Initial research

### The question

`fomc-2027-09-15`'s lane discovered this expiration as a by-product: its ±5-day corridor returned
zero tracked events, which for a September FOMC decision is not a quiet corridor but a **gap in the
calendar** — this repo's expiration class simply stopped at `opex-2027-06-17`. So the entry arrived
proposed, `estimate`, and never examined on its own terms. Two questions follow, and the second is
the one with teeth. **Can a 2027 expiration date be sourced at all** — six sibling ledgers say no?
And **does the FOMC corridor rule, which three ledgers have now half-withdrawn, survive the volume
test nobody has run?**

**One-line verdict:** yes to the first, and the source was reachable the whole time on a domain
nobody tried; **no** to the second — the volume test says yes at P = 0.002 and the yes is an era
artifact, so the corridor rule is now refused on both the returns and the volume side.

5. **THE HEADLINE — the +2 corridor rule reinstates itself on volume at P = 0.002, and the result is
   an era artifact — REFUTED.** The rule's history is a warning in itself: `fomc-2027-03-17` built
   "treat the decision and the expiry as one event window" on a +2 meeting; `fomc-2027-06-09`
   withdrew it only as *"not this meeting's shape"* at +6, a withdrawal that expires on contact with
   the next +2; `fomc-2027-09-15` tested decision-day **returns** and refused to reinstate it
   (permutation P = 0.163, reversing within September). Nobody had tested **volume on the witching
   session itself**, which is the natural next place for the rule to reappear. Measured over 130
   quarterly witchings 1994→2026, gap defined as sessions from the nearest scheduled FOMC decision
   (positive = decision first):

   | Test | n (+2 / rest) | mean | diff | permutation P |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | **Raw relvol** | 39 / 91 | 2.147 vs 1.862 | **+0.285** | **0.002** |
   | Era-ranked percentile (±11y) | 39 / 91 | 0.558 vs 0.505 | +0.054 | **0.326** |
   | Raw relvol, 2013+ only | 36 / 18 | 2.197 vs 2.277 | **−0.080** | 0.537 |

   **The confound is the calendar, and it is not subtle: 92% of the +2 cohort is dated 2013 or later
   against 20% of the rest.** The +2 arrangement is a *modern* FOMC scheduling shape, and the
   instrument's own header already records that witching volume grew structurally over the same span
   (quarterly median 1.63 in 1995-2014, **2.18** in 2015+). So the raw comparison is mostly
   "post-2013 sessions versus pre-2013 sessions" wearing a Fed label. Control for era by either
   method and the effect vanishes; inside the modern era alone it **changes sign**. A session that
   ran only the raw test would have reinstated a withdrawn corridor rule on a three-decimal p-value,
   which is why the control now ships as an instrument rather than as this paragraph.

6. **The generic "decision in the same week" version is a flat null — SUPPORTED (as a null).**

   resolution tested. The +2 result was never a weakened version of a real corridor effect — it was
   the one slice where the era imbalance is worst.

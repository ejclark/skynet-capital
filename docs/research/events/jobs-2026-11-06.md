## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** what will the October 2026 jobs report show, how does the market react in
each direction inside whatever Fed regime the September and October FOMCs leave behind, and does it
change how we sit in the tracked names (NVDA MRVL AVGO CRWV MSFT GOOG META AAPL AMZN) around
2026-11-06 — four days before the CPI + CRWV print compound day?

**One-line verdict:** the first payroll after the Oct FOMC and the macro setup into the 11/10 CPI +
CRWV compound day, feeding a December FOMC that *does* carry fresh dots — high structural relevance but
a reaction function downstream of two unresolved meetings; the house answer is guard-shaped, not
directional, and the distinguishing overlay is CRWV (our most rate-sensitive name) reporting four
sessions later.

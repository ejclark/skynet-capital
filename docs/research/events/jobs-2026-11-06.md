## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date confirmed, BLS primary re-checked 2026-08-19).** Treat 2026-11-06 08:30 ET as a
high-impact known-date variance event — the first payroll after the Oct FOMC, the macro setup into the
11/10 CPI + CRWV compound day, and an early input to the December dots — whose reaction sign cannot be
fixed at D-79 because it depends on what the September and October FOMCs leave behind. No directional
macro position; no new rate-sensitive paper entries sized around the print once inside its cadence band.
Base case (estimate-labeled — no consensus, regime extrapolation): a print continuing the cooling,
bought as hike relief *if* tightening is still the debate, read as recession confirmation *if* the
debate has tilted to cuts — deferred to a later pulse. The distinguishing overlay: CRWV, our most
rate-sensitive name, reports ~11/10, so this jobs read conditions its pre-print rate tape.

**Kill switches:**

- **The September/October FOMCs flip the debate to hold-vs-cut** — re-bases this print's reaction
  from "weak = hike relief" to "weak = recession confirmation"; re-derive at a pulse after the Oct 28
  FOMC, don't carry the pre-September framing forward.
- **A hot rebound** — re-arms tightening into the Dec dots and lands as a headwind four sessions
  before CRWV's most-rate-exposed print; the hawkish tail — reassess CRWV-adjacent macro backdrop.
- **A recession-signal collapse** — trips the tipping point; weak labor reads growth-negative, and a
  weak Nov CPI (11/10) would compound toward stagflation on the same session CRWV reports.
- **CRWV confirms (or moves) its print date** — an IR confirmation firms the compound-day overlay;
  a slip away from ~11/10 weakens it — re-check at the pulse that catches the confirmation.
- **A published October payroll consensus emerging closer in** — resolves the biggest honest limit;
  re-run the "surprise" framing against it.

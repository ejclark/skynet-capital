## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries 2026-10-12 as day 1 of the IMF/World Bank Annual
Meetings in Bangkok, filed as the container that dates `g20-fmcbg-bangkok-2026-10-15`. Is the week
real, does an Annual Meetings week have any price channel of its own, and does any name this book
tracks carry exposure to it?

**One-line verdict:** the week is primary-verified and its `estimate` label is a schema artifact, and
the "no price channel" claim is not asserted here but **measured on the two most recent instances of
the exact publication that could have one** — in both cases the flagship's message and the tape moved
in opposite directions.

**Method:** sourced web research plus index-data measurement — no symbol instruments run (geopolitical
kind, `symbols: []`, per the macro-print/geopolitical method in
[`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md)). Primary reads, all fetched direct 2026-09-04:
**worldbank.org `/en/meetings/splash/annual`** and its `/overview`, **imfconnect.org `AM26`**,
**meetings.imf.org `/en/index`**, and the **World Bank mirror of IMF press release 23/122**

`^GSPC` and `^VIX` daily bars pulled live 2026-09-04 from the same Yahoo chart endpoint

publication pages for the October-2025 GFSR and April-2026 WEO, CNBC/Fortune/Seeking Alpha on the
2025-10-16 regional-bank scare, and CGD/Fortune on the US reform posture. Figures are as-checked and drift.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The week is primary-sourced and the `estimate` label is a pure schema artifact.** SUPPORTED on
   the fact, blocked on the schema. worldbank.org states verbatim that the meetings run *"from Monday,
   October 12 to Sunday, October 18 in Bangkok, Thailand"*, and its overview page dates the keystones:
   **Development Committee 10/15**, **Annual Meetings Plenary 10/16**, **IMFC Plenary 10/17**, plus two
   WBG flagship sessions (*"Small AI, Big Jobs"* 10/12, *"Mobilizing Private Capital for Jobs"* 10/14).
   imfconnect.org's AM26 page repeats the dates independently. The venue (Queen Sirikit National
   Convention Center) and the host award trace to IMF PR 23/122, whose World Bank mirror also supplies
   the rotation rule — the meetings sit in Washington *"two consecutive years, then alternate to a
   member country every third year"* — and the detail that **the last Bangkok hosting was 1991**. The
   label cannot flip: `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` in `scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs` has no slot for a
   multilateral body's own schedule, the same gap
   [`apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18`](apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18.md) and the four G20 ledgers
   each record.

2. **The flagship reports are the only equity-adjacent output of the week, and both recent instances
   are measured misses — directionally, not just in size.** SUPPORTED, and this is the load-bearing
   leg. The sibling geopolitical ledgers argue "no price channel" from an absence; here the channel
   exists, is named, is dated, and can be tested against the tape.

   *Sample 1 — Annual Meetings 2025 (DC, 10/13–19). Both the WEO and the GFSR launched **2025-10-14**.*
   The GFSR (*"Shifting Ground beneath the Calm"*) is the most on-thesis document this book will ever
   get from a multilateral: it estimated the **S&P 500 and global stocks about 10% overvalued** and
   named **changing investor expectations about AI** as the trigger that could reprice them. The tape:
   SPX **6,654.72** (10-13) → **6,644.31** (10-14, launch day, **−0.16%**) → 6,671.06 → 6,629.07 →
   **6,664.01** (10-17). Net across the meetings week: **+0.14%**. From the launch close, **+1.37%** by
   10-20 and **+2.22%** by 10-24 (6,791.69). VIX 19.03 → 20.81 (10-14) → 20.64 → **25.31** (10-16) →
   20.78 → 16.37 (10-24). **The one vol event of that week was not the IMF's:** the 10-16 spike was
   Zions disclosing a ~$50M C&I charge-off and Western Alliance alleging borrower fraud, with the S&P
   Regional Banks index **−6.3%** on the day — a credit story, reported as such everywhere.

   *Sample 2 — Spring Meetings 2026 (DC, 04/13–19). WEO launched **2026-04-14**.* It **cut** 2026
   global growth by **0.2pp to 3.1%**, titled *"Global Economy in the Shadow of War"*, and modelled a
   2.5%-growth downside. The tape: SPX **6,886.24** (04-13) → **6,967.38** (04-14, launch day,
   **+1.18%**) → 7,022.95 → 7,041.28 → **7,126.06** (04-17). Net 04-10 → 04-17: **+4.54%** in one week.
   VIX **19.23 → 17.48**, falling throughout.

   Read honestly, neither sample proves the reports are ignored — both weeks had far larger drivers.
   What they establish is the correct standard for refusing to position: on the two most recent
   occasions the flagship published its most market-relevant possible message, the index moved
   *against* that message within the same session, and no source attributed either move to the IMF.

3. **The one previous away-year is unusable as a third sample, and saying so is the honest move.**
   MIXED. Marrakech (2023-10-09/15) is the most recent non-Washington Annual Meetings, and on paper it
   is the closest structural analogue to Bangkok. But that week opens two days after the 2023-10-07
   Hamas attack, so every tape reading across it belongs to the Middle East, not to the meetings. It is
   recorded here so a later pulse does not "discover" it and mistake it for evidence. The base rate
   therefore rests on the two DC samples in leg 2 plus a structural absence, not on three instances.

4. **No tracked symbol carries exposure, and the calendar itself proves it rather than asserting it.**
   SUPPORTED. `earnings-calendar.ts` carries ten names — NVDA, MRVL, AVGO, MSFT, GOOG, META, AMZN,
   AAPL, CRWV, MU — and **not one prints inside 2026-10-12/18**; the nearest cluster is MSFT 10-27
   through AAPL/AMZN 10-29, nine days after the meetings close. The event is filed with an empty
   `symbols` array, correctly. Thailand's own market and currency are the only assets with direct
   host-country exposure and neither is tracked here. `low` impact is affirmed, not inherited.

5. **There is no dated governance deliverable at Bangkok, which removes the last candidate mechanism.**
   SUPPORTED. The obvious one would be quota realignment — the mechanism by which an IMFC meeting could
   produce a genuine institutional surprise. It is not live: the **16th General Review** (completed late
   2023) raised resources by 50% but changed **no voting shares**, and the **17th Review** has been
   pushed to **2028**. What remains is a posture read: Bessent's April-2025 charge that the IMF and
   World Bank suffer *"mission creep"* — too much climate/gender/social work crowding out macro — paired
   with a demand that the Fund focus on external-surplus countries, naming China. Whether that escalates
   at the 10/17 IMFC plenary is worth reading; it prices nothing.

6. **The week's most useful by-product is a date that does not yet exist, and it is deliberately not
   filed.** SUPPORTED. The WEO and GFSR launch dates are the only items in this corridor that would
   deserve their own calendar rows, and **neither is published**: worldbank.org's schedule pages list no
   press events, imfconnect's AM26 page offers only a "Tentative Schedule PDF" that did not resolve, and
   **meetings.imf.org's own meetings index carries no 2026 Annual Meetings entry at all** — its newest
   upcoming item is the 2026 Spring Meetings. The customary pattern is the Tuesday of meetings week
   (WEO+GFSR **2025-10-14** with meetings 10/13–19; WEO **2026-04-14** with meetings 04/13–19), which
   would put a 2026 launch on **2026-10-13, one day before CPI**. That is a *pattern inference, not a
   source*, so nothing is proposed — filing an inferred date under an `EST:` prefix would put a guess
   into the audit trail, and leg 2 has just measured that the row would carry no edge even if right. A
   later pulse files it the day the IMF dates it.

7. **The attribution hazard is the actionable output, and the geography makes it absolute.** SUPPORTED.
   Ten tracked events sit within ±5 days of 10/12: `fomc-minutes-2026-10-07`, `beige-book-2026-10-14`,
   `cpi-2026-10-14`, `g20-fmcbg-bangkok-2026-10-15`, `ppi-2026-10-15`, `retail-sales-2026-10-15`,
   `opex-2026-10-16`, `import-export-prices-2026-10-16`, `treasury-primary-dealer-agenda-2026-10-16`
   and `fomc-blackout-start-2026-10-17` — two of them 08:30 ET prints on the same morning. Bangkok is
   **UTC+7**, so a 09:00–18:00 ICT day maps to **22:00 ET the previous evening through 07:00 ET**: every
   session of this week is over before the US cash open, and before the 10/15 data. A 10/12–18 US-session
   move always has a better explanation than a meeting that finished overnight.

**What the conditions support:** nothing sized and nothing directional. The output is a *reading

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date: estimate — on a schema gap, not on doubt).** No position keyed to this week and no
prospect of one. Treat 2026-10-12/18 as a **read with two questions, one chore and one discipline**.
The read: this is the week the IMF publishes the only multilateral document that speaks directly to
this book's thesis, and the measured record is that the document's message and the tape have moved in
opposite directions on both recent occasions — so a GFSR warning on AI valuations is *information*
about the consensus, never a signal to act. The two questions are whether the GFSR names AI capex or
data-center credit specifically, and whether the US escalates its "mission creep" posture at the 10/17
IMFC plenary. The chore: file the WEO/GFSR launch date as an `estimate` event the day the IMF
publishes it — never before, on an inferred date. The discipline: this corridor carries CPI and the
Beige Book on 10/14, an 08:30 ET PPI and retail-sales double print on 10/15, and monthly opex on
10/16, and every Bangkok session ends before the US cash open — so no tape move that week is
attributable here. Estimate status widens caution only; nothing is date-keyed action, and `low` impact
is affirmed.

**Kill switches.**

- *"The flagship reports have no price channel"* dies if a GFSR or WEO chapter published in this week
  names **AI capital expenditure, data-center credit or hyperscaler financing** as a specific stability
  risk **and** a tracked name draws down >2% in the following session with that report cited as cause in
  contemporaneous coverage. That is a narrative channel to this book's actual exposure and would earn an
  impact-tier review before 2026-10-18.
- *"No tracked symbol carries exposure"* dies if any of the ten names in `earnings-calendar.ts` is
  scheduled to print inside 2026-10-12/18 — none is as of 2026-09-04, and the nearest is MSFT on 10-27.
- *"The week is a read, not a venue"* dies on a named, dated deliverable announced on the Bangkok
  margins that resolves, extends or hardens the **2026-11-10** expiries. That would most likely surface
  through [`g20-fmcbg-bangkok-2026-10-15`](g20-fmcbg-bangkok-2026-10-15.md), whose own upgrade flag
  (an announced Bessent–He Lifeng round) is the sharper tripwire; this ledger defers to it.
- *"The WEO/GFSR date is unpublished"* dies the moment the IMF posts a 2026 Annual Meetings press
  schedule — at which point a pulse files the date as an `estimate` event and this ledger stops
  carrying the chore.
- *"The dates are primary-verified"* dies on any fetched IMF or World Bank page naming a different date
  or venue; the same fetch is the flip trigger if `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` ever gains a slot for a
  multilateral body's own schedule.
- *"Quota realignment is dead until 2028"* dies on an IMFC communiqué or US Treasury statement dating a
  17th-Review conclusion earlier — which would make the IMFC plenary an institutional event with a
  timeline rather than a posture read.

## Initial research

**The question.** What are the mechanics of a routine monthly opex (gamma-pinning into the close, realized-vol compression, the post-opex unpin), does anything about *this particular* October 2026 cycle stand out, and how does it interact with the Oct-14 CPI just before it and the Oct 27–29 mega-cap earnings cluster + Oct-28 FOMC the week after?

**One-line verdict:** the mechanics are the same well-documented monthly pattern as the Aug-21 exemplar, this cycle is unremarkable structurally (standard monthly, between two witchings), and the only load-bearing feature is timing — its gamma roll-off sits between the Oct-14 CPI and a dense following-week catalyst cluster — so the honest output is a guard (mind the Friday close; note the pre-cluster timing), not a trade.

**Method.** Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no earnings-style instrument applies and no opex-shaped instrument exists in `scripts/research/`. Sourced web research: OCC/Cboe convention for the date and cycle type, options-analytics literature (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch, CBOE) for the pin/unpin pattern, vendor volume comparisons for the monthly-vs-quarterly notional gap. Read against `trade-playbooks.md` / `multi-symbol-sweep.md` for whether any house playbook is opex-keyed, and cross-referenced against `src/domain/market-events-data.ts` and `earnings-calendar.ts` for the adjacent dated events already on the calendar.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and cycle type are right — SUPPORTED.** OCC's expiration-calendar convention (3rd Friday, standard monthly) makes Oct 16, 2026 a routine monthly expiration; it is an unadjusted Friday between the Sep-18 and Dec-18 quarterly triple-witchings. This matches this calendar's `impact: medium` coding for Oct 16 versus `impact: high` for the two witchings.

2. **Gamma-pinning / realized-vol compression into expiration is a real, documented mechanic — SUPPORTED (general literature, not house-tested).** In a positive-gamma regime, dealers hedge by buying dips and selling rallies in progressively larger size as expiration nears, compressing realized vol and pulling price toward high-gamma strikes (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch explainers; CBOE research documents the same dealer-hedging mechanism at the 0DTE extreme). Identical mechanic to the Aug-21 monthly. **Caveat:** vendor dealer-positioning models, not OCC primary data; and no same-week snapshot is meaningful this far out (leg 6).

3. **A post-opex "vol pop" / gamma-unpin pattern — MIXED, real but not house-validated.** Once front-month gamma expires, the stabilizing dealer floor is removed and realized vol tends to rise; vendor literature reports a common 1–3% pullback/consolidation as positioning resets. But this comes from options-analytics blogs and one CBOE paper, not from a red-teamed instrument run against our own names. No opex-shaped instrument exists in this repo to confirm or refute it — directionally credible, not house-proven.

4. **This cycle is standard monthly, not quarterly — smaller structural impact — SUPPORTED.** Vendor volume comparisons put monthly-opex notional at ~$1–2T vs ~$4–5T on quarterly triple-witching (a ~3x gamma-rolloff difference). Oct 16 carries no coincident index rebalance and no index-futures/index-options witching, so its close is a single-name-heavy, ordinary-monthly imbalance — smaller than the Sep-18 witching before it and the Dec-18 witching after. This confirms the calendar's `medium` coding.

5. **Timing is the only load-bearing feature — SUPPORTED.** Two adjacencies bracket this opex. Before: **CPI Oct 14** (Sep data, confirmed) lands 2 days prior, so the tape enters opex week already repricing off an inflation print. After: the mega-cap earnings cluster (**MSFT Oct 27 · GOOG/META Oct 28 · AAPL/AMZN Oct 29**, all `estimate`, 8-K cadence) and the **Oct-28 FOMC** (confirmed) all fall the *following* week. Mechanically, this Friday's gamma roll-off removes the pinning cushion right as the option chains for those mega-caps begin building their pre-earnings weeklies — the market's structural "quiet" this opex week gives way to a dense catalyst window almost immediately after. (Note: MRVL Investor Day Oct 6 and jobs Oct 2 are earlier and already digested by opex week.) This is a timing caution about *when* the cushion rolls off relative to what follows, not a directional call.

6. **No house playbook is opex-keyed — SUPPORTED.** Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md`: S1/G1 are pre-print run-up plays (earnings-dated), S2 is the never-hold-the-print guard (earnings-dated), E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution rule, S3 is a reaction-day fade (earnings-dated, blocked on shorting), S4 is an overnight-vs-buy-and-hold structural note. None reference options expiration. The one real interaction is indirect and the same as the Aug-21 exemplar: E1's logic doesn't transfer (opex's expensive microstructure concentrates at the **close**, not the open), while S4's kept "prefer close-side executions" guidance is what this Friday amplifies risk for — the MOC/MOO imbalance risk already named in the sweep's blocked CRWV overnight-carry item, not something new to build here.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing directional — no macro/mechanics playbook exists in the house set for opex. Support = discipline: no automated bot signal or paper position should be sized or entered off Friday's close on high-open-interest names (mega-caps) without accounting for pin risk distorting that print. The load-bearing carry-forward is the pre-cluster timing: this opex's gamma cushion rolls off the week before the Oct 27–29 mega-cap prints and the Oct-28 FOMC, so a near-date read of the tape's calm that week should treat it as structural pinning, not information about the coming cluster.

that print reads the **August 2026** reference month from the put-in-place side, and this one reads

**What does the August-data edition do differently, and is there anything about 2026-09-24 in
particular that a reader needs?**

**One-line verdict:** yes — this is the edition where the *only* channel that demonstrably reads
this print is switched off by the publisher's own schedule, so the release becomes unobservable on

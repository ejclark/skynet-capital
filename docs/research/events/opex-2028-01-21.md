1. **The date is right, and the reason it stays `estimate` is sharper than "no primary reaches 2028" —

   The OCC's expiration-date class reproduces the third-Friday rule **33 of 36** with all three misses

   `opex-2028-02-18` leg 1 found for February, discovered here independently for January.

2. **The shelf's own cells replicate exactly, on both instruments — SUPPORTED, stated before anything
   is added.** Restricted to `opex-2027-01-15`'s window (120 expirations, 2016-09-06 → 2026-09-04) the
   new instrument returns **57.5%** overall (median 1.098), ordinary monthlies **62.5%** (1.160),
   quarterly witchings **47.5%** (0.905), one-day estimator 51.9 / 56.9 / 41.7%, and the
   January-MLK-after cell **5/5 raw → 2/5 gap-free** with med(raw ÷ √cal-day) **1.2905**. At 296
   expirations 2002–2026 it returns `opex-2028-02-18`'s figures too: monthlies **55.6% raw / 50.0%
   gap-free** (QQQ) and **50.0% / 47.8%** (SPY), witchings 46.9 / 48.3 and 40.8 / 47.1, and the

   is inherited, not re-argued.**

3. **This expiration's geometry is the *other* one, and the difference is one calendar day against
   three — SUPPORTED, as arithmetic, with no exceptions.** MLK 2028-01-17 deletes the **Monday of

4. **So the bias is ONE-SIDED, not a mirror — SUPPORTED, and it is this session's headline.** The

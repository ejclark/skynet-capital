## At a glance

whether Trump physically shows up on **2026-11-16** and then flies on to Shenzhen, because that is the
first visible step toward the Trump–Xi meeting at APEC two days later. The Palace has said his
attendance is "confirmed" and the White House has reportedly named the day, so the odds are good, but
no White House travel notice has been read and the summit's own days are still reported three
different ways (**estimate**). ASEAN's own agenda (a digital-economy treaty, South China Sea
language, Myanmar) has no history of moving US equities, and the one US-linked deliverable, a
semiconductor-hub deal with the Philippines, has no signing date. Stand aside; read the weekend news
as a *tell for the APEC date*, not as a catalyst. Estimate widens caution only.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries the 49th ASEAN Summit and its related summits
(including the East Asia Summit) in Manila on 2026-11-16, filed as the first leg of Trump's Asia
trip. Is the date real, does Trump actually go, and does anything said or signed in Manila reach US
equities?

**One-line verdict:** the host and the White House both say Trump attends on 2026-11-16, so the stop
is probable, but the summit's own days are inconsistently reported and no US primary notice was read;
nothing on the agenda has a record of moving US markets, so stand aside and use the weekend as a tell
for the APEC date.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The summit is real, and it is in Manila on or about 2026-11-16.** MIXED. The host's own
   proclamation (Proclamation No. 1447, signed 2026-09-17, released 2026-09-22) declares **Nov 16–18**
   special non-working days in Metro Manila, and Philstar (2026-10-29) gives the summit as Nov 16–18 at
   the Philippine International Convention Center, Pasay, with total road closures from Nov 9 and
   Cebu Pacific cancelling routes Nov 14–18. That is strong evidence of real preparation. But
   BusinessMirror (2026-10-15) says **Nov 15–17**, and a search summary of Nation Thailand carries
   **Nov 10–12**, which looks like an older plan and was not reconciled. The 16th is the one day on
   which the host and the White House agree.

2. **Trump attends on 2026-11-16.** SUPPORTED, estimate-labelled. GMA News (2026-10-14) quotes Palace
   Press Officer Claire Castro: "It is confirmed", with no arrival date or duration. Gulf News
   (2026-10-19) reports the White House said he will attend on **Nov 16** before travelling to China
   for APEC. It would be his first Philippines visit since the 2017 summit, on an invitation Marcos
   extended in July 2025. No whitehouse.gov notice was found. Malacañang did not confirm a Marcos–Trump
   bilateral. Registered as `FT-asean-summit-manila-2026-11-16-1`.

3. **A leader line-up that moves markets is not in prospect.** SUPPORTED. BusinessMirror (2026-10-15)
   puts 18 leaders on the DFA's expected list, but says some notifications were outstanding; Xi's
   attendance is "closely watched" and unconfirmed, Beijing "has often sent the premier", and
   Putin's is unconfirmed. UN Secretary-General Guterres is expected for the ASEAN–UN summit. Nothing
   found says Xi or Li Qiang attends. So Manila does not host the Trump–Xi meeting; Shenzhen does.

4. **The agenda has no record of moving US equities.** SUPPORTED. The items reported are the ASEAN
   Digital Economy Framework Agreement (signing "expected to be a key outcome", BusinessMirror), a
   new ASEAN emblem and flag, a decision on the Five-Point Consensus on Myanmar, and South China Sea
   Code of Conduct talks, which Malaysian and Philippine officials have said they want finished but
   which Beijing has resisted making binding for two decades. The US-linked item is the Philippines'
   Pax Silica framework, a ~4,000-acre semiconductor and microchip hub in the Luzon corridor: BCDA
   eyed a November signing in July, a mid-October report said "before the end of 2026", and the Palace
   declined to tie it to Trump's trip. That has no listed US symbol and no date. Registered as
   `FT-asean-summit-manila-2026-11-16-2`.

5. **The market cannot hedge the 11/10 truce date with this stop.** SUPPORTED. The sibling ledgers
   already record the US–China truce extension to 2027-01-10 as announced by Bessent on 2026-09-24 and
   unconfirmed on China's paper ([APEC ledger](apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18.md),
   [truce ledger](us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10.md)). Manila falls six days after the
   original 11/10 date, so its only link to that thread is that a cancelled Manila leg would be an
   early tell on Shenzhen.

### What this supports

Nothing to size. The honest plays are all non-plays: no position keyed to Manila; do not treat the
Palace's "confirmed" as a White House notice; and read the Sunday-evening ET news on 11/15 for one
fact, whether Trump landed. If he did, the Shenzhen read in the APEC ledger is unchanged; if he did
not, that ledger's attendance flag and its 11/18 call are the ones to revisit.

### Honest limits

- **Estimate, not confirmed.** No primary ASEAN, White House or Philippine-gazette page was read for
  the days; the evidence is press reports of official statements and a proclamation seen through a
  news article.
- **Date disagreement unresolved.** Nov 15–17 and Nov 10–12 appear in reporting and could not be
  reconciled; the 16th rests on the proclamation, Philstar and the White House line as reported.
- **Second-hand White House line.** Gulf News and search summaries attribute the Nov 16 day to the
  White House; the original release was not read.
- **Beyond horizon.** A CSIS press briefing previewing the trip is reported for 2026-11-09; it is not a
  market event and is named here, not proposed. The 12/14–15 G20 is already proposed by the APEC
  ledger's sweep.

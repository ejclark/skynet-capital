### Leg 8 — **THE CORRIDOR: 2027-12-24 is a full closure, not a half day** · **SUPPORTED**

Before this PR the corridor within five days of 2027-12-28 held **zero canonical ids** and one proposal
(`fomc-minutes-2027-12-29`, D+1). The year-end week's shape is what Leg 4's control depends on, so it
is established from primaries rather than assumed:

1. **NYSE's own holiday table** — Christmas Day row parses `["Friday, December 25****", "Friday,
   December 24 (Christmas Day observed)", "Monday, December 25"]` for 2026 / 2027 / 2028. **2027-12-24
   is a full closure.** Footnote `****` names exactly one December early close across all three years —
   *"Thursday, December 24, 2026"* — so **December 2027 has no 1:00 p.m. session at all**. Footnote `*`
   reads *"Because the holiday falls on Saturday, January 1, 2028, no New Year's Day holiday is
   observed"*, so **2027-12-31 stays a full session**.
2. **The OCC's 2027 expiration calendar** — an independent, non-exchange primary — carries
   `{"date":"2027-12-24T00:00:00","holiday":{"description":"Exchange/OCC holiday","hatched":true}}` and
   marks no other late-December 2027 date that way.
3. **CBOE's own session record**, mechanically: Christmas Day falls on a Saturday in **1993, 1999,
   2004, 2010 and 2021**, and in all five December 24 is **absent** from `VIX_History.csv` while
   December 23 and 27 are both present. **5 of 5.**

So the last five sessions of 2027 are **Dec 27, 28, 29, 30, 31** — five full sessions, no half day —
and **2027-12-28 is the second of them and the fourth from the year's end**, the same position as
2021, 2022 and 2023. **`christmas-market-closure-2027-12-24` is proposed here**, filling a real hole:
this calendar tracks `christmas-eve-half-day-2026-12-24` (an early close) but no Christmas *closure* in
any year, while carrying full-closure entries for seven other holidays.

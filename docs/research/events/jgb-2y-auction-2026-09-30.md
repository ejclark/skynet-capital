## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event exists because `jgb-liquidity-enhancement-1-5y-2026-09-10` proposed it on 2026-09-09, naming
the 2-Year sector *"the single largest mechanical driver"* of the drift its own headline leg measured —
the 1–5y liquidity-enhancement menu's ≥1%-coupon bucket inverting its composition. The proposal's own
words: *"No.489 enters the 2026-11-20 menu the same way."* So the question this ledger has to answer is
not "what will the auction do to the market" — it will do nothing to anything we hold — but:

**how much fuel does the 2-Year channel actually have left?**

**One-line verdict: almost none, and the proposal has the direction backwards.** The channel was the
largest driver *of the roll that already happened*. Going forward it is **93.0% spent**, it closes
entirely on the next roll, and the print this ledger tracks cannot revive it at any coupon it could
plausibly carry.

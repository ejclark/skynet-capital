### The question, plainly

Five sibling ledgers already answer the standing BoJ questions for this book, and this entry
re-litigates none of them: the yen carry channel is not live enough to matter
([09-18](boj-decision-2026-09-18.md)); a conditional scaffold is the right shape when an earlier
event sets the distribution ([10-30](boj-decision-2026-10-30.md)); December is the consensus's
deadline, not its date ([12-18](boj-decision-2026-12-18.md)); January is the venue the consensus
skips, and decision-day FX measurement is series-dependent ([01-22](boj-decision-2027-01-22.md));
and the consumption-tax footnote the family reasoned from **is not in the Outlook Report**
([04-28](boj-decision-2027-04-28.md)).

This event was **proposed by that 04-28 session**, on two stated grounds: it is the second of Q2
2027's two venues, and it is the first MPM able to see a national post-cut CPI print. At D-279 — now
the furthest-out event on this calendar — re-deriving a consensus would be worth nothing. So this
entry asks the two questions the proposal left open: **is "the second Q2 venue" merely a position in
a sequence, or does it carry a decision-relevant property; and what does the June slot's own
geometry look like once the primaries are parsed rather than assumed?**

**One-line verdict: the position in the sequence *is* the property — Q2 2027 holds exactly two MPMs,
so this is the deadline venue at which the ≥1.50% consensus is adjudicated, not another data point
inside the window — while the geometry turns up an exact inversion of April's Fed/BoJ ordering, a
holiday-free bracket that makes this the family's clean test venue, and one candidate signal
(ordering predicts the yen move) that this session refutes rather than banks.** The stance is
unchanged: stand aside, no position, no play.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **Q2 2027 contains exactly two MPMs, so this is the venue at which the ≥1.50% consensus is

   Jun 10-11 · Jul 21-22 · Sep 21-22 · Oct 28-29 · Dec 16-17**. Of those, **Apr 27-28 and Jun 10-11**

   consensus's deadline, not its date* — but it is stronger here, because December had further
   venues inside a loosely-bounded window and Q2 is closed by definition. **The poll is second-hand:
   this session did not re-source it** and takes the sibling's citation as-is; what is added is the
   calendar constraint, which is primary.

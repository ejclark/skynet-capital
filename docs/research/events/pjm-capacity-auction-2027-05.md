# PJM 2030/31 capacity auction — first potentially uncapped print — pjm-capacity-auction-2027-05

**Kind:** sector · **Date:** 2027-05-15 (estimate, EST: esaipower.com auction calendar — BRA 2030/31 May 2027; confirm vs pjm.com, checked 2026-08-17) · **Impact:** medium
**Last assessed:** 2026-08-19

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** This is the **cap-sunset event** — the one PJM capacity auction the AI-energy thesis
actually cares about for price discovery. The FERC-accepted **$175/$325 price collar** (accepted
2026-04-28) covers **only** the 2028/29 and 2029/30 delivery-year auctions; the **2030/31 Base
Residual Auction (~May 2027)** is the first BRA *outside* the collar — the first print in years
where the clearing price could re-float toward PJM's uncapped simulations ($554–777/MW-day range in
recent years) instead of pinning at the ~$325 cap. That makes it the **named gate on VST's (Vistra)
Tier-2 entry condition** in [`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md): *"both the
Dec-15-2026 auction passing with credible cap-sunset signals and the Texas audit resolving."* Two
honesty stakes: (1) the date is an **estimate** — PJM Inside Lines says it *expects* to run the
2030/31 BRA in May 2027 as it returns to the three-year-forward cycle, but no exact day is published;
(2) it is **269 days out**, and none of the nine tracked names is an IPP — VST is the sensitive name,
and it is not in the nine. The nearer, upstream event is the **Dec-15-2026** 2029/30 auction, where
the first *cap-sunset signaling* toward this print appears.

## Initial research

**The question, plainly.** Is ~May 2027 really when PJM runs the 2030/31 Base Residual Auction, is
it genuinely the first auction *outside* the FERC price collar (the "cap-sunset" the thesis keys
on), how would the market trade it, and does it touch any of the nine tracked names?

**One-line verdict.** The ~May 2027 date is a well-corroborated **estimate** (PJM expects to run the
2030/31 BRA then as it returns to the three-year-forward cycle; no exact day published), and it
**is** the first auction outside the collar — the collar covers only 2028/29 + 2029/30 — making it a
genuine cap-sunset / price-discovery print and the named gate on **VST's** Tier-2 entry condition;
but it is 269 days out, touches **none of the nine tracked names directly** (VST is not in the
nine), and is a thesis-maintenance checkpoint, not a play.

**Method.** Sourced web research per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) (sector
kind — no price instruments): primary/quasi-primary sources (pjm.com, PJM Inside Lines, FERC filing
coverage) over the ESAI estimate source the calendar seeded from, all checked 2026-08-19; reaction
function read from the last three at-cap auctions; sensitivity read against
[`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md), its companion [`constraint-
watch.md`](../constraint-watch.md), and the sibling [Dec-15-2026 auction
ledger](pjm-capacity-auction-2026-12.md).

**Legs tested.**

1. **The date — "2030/31 BRA, ~May 2027."** MIXED (stays an **estimate**). PJM Inside Lines states
   PJM *"won't get back to the classic BRA schedule of three years in advance until **May 2027**,
   when they expect to conduct the BRA for the 2030/31 DY"* (insidelines.pjm.com, checked
   2026-08-19) — quasi-primary corroboration of the month, consistent with the ESAI calendar the
   entry cites and with [`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md) (*"2030/31 ~mid-2027
   is the first potentially uncapped print"*). But **no exact day is published** — the calendar's
   2027-05-15 is a mid-month placeholder, honest but soft; PJM has moved auction schedules
   repeatedly through the compressed-cycle era (auctions ran July, December, and July again). Confirm
   against PJM's posted 2030/31 schedule when it lands.
2. **"It is the first auction outside the collar" (the cap-sunset premise).** SUPPORTED. The FERC-
   accepted collar (~$325 cap / $175 floor, accepted **2026-04-28**) covers **only the 2028/29
   auction (closed ~July 7, 2026) and the 2029/30 auction (~Dec 15, 2026)** — the two prints named
   in FERC's order; the prior collar covered 2026/27 + 2027/28 (mgrid.org, roi-nj.com, PJM board
   decision doc "Price Collar for 2028/2029 and 2029/2030," troutmanenergyreport.com, all checked
   2026-08-19). No source shows the collar extending to **2030/31**, so ~May 2027 is the first BRA
   where price is *not* administratively pinned. (Reconciliation with the companion doc's "collar
   locked through May 2030": the 2029/30 delivery year runs June 2029–May 2030, so "through May
   2030" is the last *capped delivery* — the 2030/31 *auction* in May 2027 is beyond it. Same fact,
   two framings.)
3. **"This is the cap-sunset event VST's Tier-2 gate keys on."** SUPPORTED. [`ai-energy-
   constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md) makes VST Tier-2 entry conditional on **both** *"the
   Dec-15-2026 auction passing with credible cap-sunset signals"* **and** *"Texas audit resolving,"*
   and lists *"2030/31 ~mid-2027 [as] the first potentially uncapped print"* on the standing
   dashboard. This auction is where the cap-sunset actually happens; the Dec-15-2026 print
   ([sibling ledger](pjm-capacity-auction-2026-12.md)) is where the first *signaling* toward it
   appears. Note the standing portfolio rule: *"the cap is the base case, not the tail — any thesis
   that needs it to expire is a lottery ticket"* and merchant cap-expiry legs get *"lottery-ticket
   sizing at most."*
4. **Reaction function — how would IPP equities trade an uncapped print?** MIXED, and asymmetric.
   The last three auctions all cleared at cap ($329.17 → $333.44 → $325.00) with the IPP reaction
   *decaying* (CEG +4.6%/VST +6% AH → TLN +3.7%/CEG +2.7%/VST +1.6% → ~flat), per the [Dec-15
   ledger](pjm-capacity-auction-2026-12.md). An *uncapped* 2030/31 print breaks that pattern: a clear
   toward PJM's uncapped simulation ($554–777/MW-day range) with a persistent shortfall would be a
   genuine positive scarcity-rent surprise for IPPs (VST/CEG/TLN); a below-cap-capable clear that
   comes in soft — supply finally responding — would be the bearish capacity-response tell from
   [`constraint-watch.md`](../constraint-watch.md) Phase 4. Either way this is a *real* price-
   discovery print, unlike the pre-scripted at-cap prints.
5. **Do the nine tracked names carry sensitivity?** REFUTED as a direct play. None of NVDA/MRVL/
   AVGO/CRWV/MSFT/GOOG/META/AAPL/AMZN is an IPP or PJM equipment name. Second-order only: **CRWV** (a
   leveraged datacenter operator — PJM-footprint power is its input cost) and the hyperscaler power
   buyers **MSFT/AMZN/GOOG/META** feel an uncapped print through the "energy constrains the buildout"
   narrative that prices their capex productivity, but capacity cost is a rounding error against
   combined ~$725B 2026 capex. The name this event actually moves is **VST** (plus CEG/TLN and the
   sleeve names), and VST is *not* in the tracked nine — so this is a sleeve/thesis watch, not a
   house-book event.
6. **Whisper / consensus.** Not quotable this far out (269 days). The functional consensus in
   coverage is continued data-center-driven load growth keeping capacity tight; whether an uncapped
   2030/31 print re-floats the price depends on the collar *not* being re-extended and on supply
   response by 2027 — both open. No published probability estimate for a below-cap vs uncapped-high
   clear exists.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing on the tracked nine. For the paper AI-energy sleeve (all
**estimate**-labeled while the date is unconfirmed): treat the ~May 2027 BRA as the scheduled
price-discovery checkpoint the thesis has been waiting for — but the actionable near-term work is
upstream, at the **Dec-15-2026** auction, where cap-sunset *signaling* toward this print is read
(VST's gate). Per the sleeve's own rule, any merchant cap-expiry leg gets lottery-ticket sizing at
most, and nothing here licenses activating VST's Tier-2 condition — that needs this leg *and* the
Texas audit, together, and neither is resolved.

**Honest limits.** The exact auction day is unpublished — 2027-05-15 is a mid-month estimate and PJM
has moved schedules repeatedly. The "uncapped" premise holds only while the collar is *not* extended
to 2030/31 — a live regulatory variable (the collar has been re-extended twice already). Reaction-
function inference is from n=3 at-cap prints (after-hours moves from news coverage) plus PJM's own
simulations, not from an instrument, and no clean uncapped-BRA precedent exists to calibrate against.
No price instrument exists for the sector kind; close-out scores against sourced auction results,
not cached tape.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date estimate — no date-keyed action licensed regardless; and even a confirmed date moves
no house book, since VST is not among the nine tracked names).** Watch-only for the AI-energy sleeve,
no position, no play proposed. Base case (estimate): PJM runs the 2030/31 BRA in ~May 2027 as it
returns to the three-year-forward cycle; it is the first auction outside the collar, so it is a
genuine price-discovery print — the clearing price could re-float toward PJM's uncapped simulations
if the collar is not re-extended and supply has not caught up. The information to harvest is whether
this event's *approach* produces credible cap-sunset signals — first readable at the **Dec-15-2026**
auction — which is one of two legs (with the Texas audit) gating VST's Tier-2 entry in
[`ai-energy-constraint.md`](../ai-energy-constraint.md). Sleeve rule stands: cap-expiry is a
lottery-ticket-sized bet at most.

**Kill switches.**

- **The date leg** dies if PJM's posted schedule moves the 2030/31 BRA out of ~May 2027 or publishes
  a materially different day → update the calendar (as a proposal, per the honesty rules) and re-band
  the cadence.
- **The cap-sunset premise** dies if FERC/PJM **extends the collar to cover 2030/31** → the event
  reverts to a pre-scripted at-cap print (like the last three), the price-discovery value evaporates,
  and VST's Tier-2 price-signal leg stays shut; downgrade the impact framing accordingly.
- **The scarcity-rent read** (bullish leg) dies on a **below-cap-capable clear that comes in soft** —
  the capacity-response tell from [`constraint-watch.md`](../constraint-watch.md) Phase 4, which
  de-risks the whole merchant-scarcity layer of the sleeve.
- **The watch-only stance** escalates to an Eric-facing decision item only if cap-sunset signals
  appear at the Dec-15-2026 auction *and* the Texas audit resolves (both VST-gate legs) — never on
  this event's schedule confirmation alone.

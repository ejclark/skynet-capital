## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Japan runs a **Liquidity Enhancement Auction** on **2026-10-27** — an anticipated **¥250bn**
reopening of *existing* off-the-run bonds with **11 to 39 years** left to run. **Nothing here is ours to
trade.** What makes it worth a ledger is that it is the **only JGB supply event on this calendar whose
result the market itself writes**: MOF names a maturity band and one total, and the *bidders* pick which
old bonds to buy. The picking is the data — and it says something the sibling ledgers could not see. All
**three** auctions since MOF redefined this zone in **April 2026** reopened **exactly zero 20-year
issues**, despite 20-year issues No.162–196 being explicitly eligible and making up **36.3%** of the
**¥321.9tn** eligible stock; widening the band to 11 years *added* 20-year eligibility (24.5% → 36.8%)
and takeup went from 11.4% to **nothing**. Money went the other way instead: the **25y+** share of
reopenings ran **1.41× / 1.34× / 1.87×** its share of eligible stock. **Two corrections to the ledger
family follow.** (1) **The curve kink is at 25 years, not 30.** Reading MOF's own **25-year** par point —
which the 30-year sibling never used — **20s25s is +25.7bp (93.5th percentile of 4,607 sessions)** while
**25s30s is −1.3bp (1.2nd)** and 30s40s +0.4bp (1.7th). The kink sits **exactly on the BoJ's own
25-year bucket boundary**, to the year. (2) **The BoJ's absorption shares were overstated** — closing
the limit the 30-year ledger flagged itself, adding Liquidity Enhancement supply moves 25y+ from ~12%→~20%
to **11.3% → 18.4%** and 10–25y from ~45%→~29% to **35.2% → 20.6%**. The *direction* survives; the 2026
*level* gap between buckets collapses from ~9pp to **2.2pp**. **And do not judge this auction by cover** —
cover here moves **inversely** with MOF's own size cuts (**corr −0.648, t = −2.69**). Date and size are
both **estimate**; they widen caution and license nothing.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This entry was proposed on 2026-09-08 by the
[`jgb-30y-auction-2026-10-08`](jgb-30y-auction-2026-10-08.md) adjacency sweep, on the claim that its
**11–39 year band is the only JGB supply event that straddles both BoJ purchase buckets** — the flat
25y+ bucket and the tapered 10–25y one whose divergence that ledger measured. It also arrives carrying
an unpaid debt: the 30-year ledger's absorption arithmetic **excluded Liquidity Enhancement auctions
entirely** and said so in its own limits.

So this ledger has three jobs, and only one of them is about 2026-10-27:

**what kind of instrument is this, does its result read on anything the siblings measured, and does
including it change their numbers?**

**One-line verdict:** it is a **different instrument from every other JGB entry on this calendar** — the
bidders, not MOF, choose which bonds get sold, so its result is a **direct demand map across the
super-long curve** rather than a price; that map says the market has stopped taking 20-year paper here
**entirely** and is overweighting 25y+ by up to 1.87×; and yes — including it moves both sibling
absorption shares down, collapses the 2026 gap between the two BoJ buckets from ~9pp to **2.2pp**, and,
via a par-curve point the sibling never read, relocates the super-long kink from the 30-year to
**exactly 25 years**, the BoJ's own boundary.

The lower bound moved **15.5 → 11 years** with the April 2026 slate; the 5–15.5y zone became 5–11y in the
same step. The 30-year sibling's transferable rule was *"compute the tenor's own distribution before
applying anyone's threshold to it."* This event is where that rule runs out of road: **there is no
distribution to compute.** Three prints exist under the current definition. Every calibration below is
stated with its n attached, and the honest statement about a 2026-10-27 result is that it is the
**fourth observation of a five-month-old instrument**. Registered as
`FT-jgb-liquidity-enhancement-11-39y-2026-10-27-1`.

**The cadence, by contrast, is unbroken across the redefinition** — every even month, no exception, from
2024-02 through 2026-10, while the 5–11y zone runs every single month and 1–5y roughly alternates.
November 2026 carries only 1–5y and 5–11y, as the pattern requires.

**2. The bidders choose the bonds, which makes this the only demand map on the calendar — SUPPORTED as a

smallest auctions. Gross bids fall with size (**corr +0.734**), so the ratio inverts: MOF cutting supply
*raises* cover on *falling* bids. **Excess bids** (bids − size) is the metric that survives — it declines
too (**corr with sequence −0.624, t = −2.52**, mean ¥706bn → ¥578bn) but it declines for a reason that is
not the size cut. And unlike at the 30-year, **cover and tail agree here**: corr(tail, cover) = **−0.786,
t = −4.02**. The sibling's "report both, they can disagree" is a 30-year rule, not a general one.

**5. The spread tail exists but is not a coupon tail — SUPPORTED, and no threshold transfers.** This
auction is bid as a **spread to a reference rate per issue**, so "Highest Accepted Spread" minus "Average
Accepted Spread" is the analogue of a tail, in the same units, with a different meaning: it measures
dispersion *across a basket of different bonds*, not across bids for one bond. Across all 12 auctions the
spread tail runs **mean 0.875bp, median 0.55, sd 1.02, range 0.0–3.7**; across the three 11-39y prints,
**0.3 / 1.1 / 0.7**. The 20-year's "tail ≥ 2bp = soft" would have flagged **one** of 12 here, and the
30-year's calibrated 1.7bp would have flagged **two** — but neither number means at this auction what it
means at those, because the underlying quantity is not the same quantity. **This is the third distinct
form the family's "same word, different metric" problem has taken** (the 40-year has no tail at all; the
30-year has one with a different distribution; this one has one that measures something else).

### Honest limits

- **n=3.** The instrument has existed in its current form since 2026-04-21. Every "calibration" in this
  ledger is either drawn from three observations or borrowed from a nine-auction window under a *different
  zone definition*. Treat the thresholds in **Signals & conditions** as placeholders that the 10-27 print
  makes into n=4, not as a distribution.
- **The zero-20-year result has an obvious rival explanation this ledger cannot rule out.** The paper may
  simply be clearing in the neighbouring 5–11y zone rather than failing to clear at all — the 2026-08-26

- **The 25y+ overweight regime is confounded with the size cut** and the split date was chosen *after*

- **"Bidders choose the issues" is an inference, not a sourced rule.** MOF's auction rules index 404'd

- **The absorption arithmetic is a flow comparison, not a balance.** It ignores the roll-down of seasoned
  30-years out of 25y+ into 10–25y, buy-backs, redemptions, the 1–5y Liquidity Enhancement zone, and
  Treasury Discount Bills. It also uses a single month's mid-zone print (2026-08-26) to stand for a
  monthly rate. The *direction* is robust to all of that; the percentages carry perhaps ±2pp.
- **Remaining maturities are computed to the auction's issue date from the workbook's maturity column**,
  which stops at the 2026-07 auctions. Issues sold after that date could not appear in an eligible-stock
  total; at these tenors that is a rounding error, but it is not zero.
- **The spread tail is not a coupon tail and the comparison to sibling thresholds is illustrative only.**
  It measures dispersion across a basket of different bonds bid at spreads to per-issue reference rates.
  No claim here depends on the two being commensurable.
- **The 25-year par point is a curve construction, not a traded instrument.** MOF interpolates it; there
  is no 25-year JGB. The kink's *location* is therefore a statement about MOF's published curve, which is
  what every sibling ledger already uses, and not about a specific bond.
- **The auction result does not exist**, and neither does its size or its issue list. Everything here is
  prior.
- **VIX is Yahoo, i.e. secondary**, and is a US reading with no mechanical bearing on a JGB auction; it is
  carried because the probe-ref contract asks for it.
- **The date and the size are both `estimate`.** MOF is a primary and the date is not in doubt; the label

## Initial research

### The question

This event arrived proposed, `estimate`, from [`fomc-minutes-2027-11-17`](fomc-minutes-2027-11-17.md)'s
initial research, which noticed the calendar's November-2027 hole and described the corridor without
claiming anything from it: *"the minutes release lands Wednesday 2027-11-17, the monthly VIX settlement
the same session, A.M.-settled index options cease trading Thursday 2027-11-18, and this expiration
closes the week Friday 2027-11-19… nothing tradeable is claimed."*

Two questions follow, and neither has been asked in this family before. **Does a monthly expiration
that lands two sessions after an FOMC minutes release behave differently?** Six opex ledgers have now
measured expirations against blackout gates, quarterlies and Fridays — never against a minutes
release, which is the one adjacency this event actually has. And **is there anything to November
expirations at all**, given that November is the only month whose expiration is always followed by a
holiday-shortened week?

**One-line verdict:** the minutes adjacency is inert on all three channels and structurally ordinary;
November *looks* special on one channel and **is not** — the result is a single 2008 observation
inside an eight-way month comparison, and this ledger refuses it rather than registering it as a
candidate.

1. **The date is 2027-11-19 and OCC names it — SUPPORTED, and the entry is promoted.** The `data`

   reads coherently end to end: **11-11** *"Bank holiday"*, **11-17** `EEF1643B` (monthly volatility

   agrees independently — 2027-11-01 is a Monday, so the Fridays are the 5th, 12th, **19th** and 26th
   — and the payload lists no November 2027 exchange closure before the 25th that could displace it.

2. **The source validates against our own rows before being trusted — SUPPORTED, 12 of 12, plus a
   second in-year check the October sibling could not run.** The same payload returns 2026's monthly
   equity expirations as 01-16, 02-20, 03-20, 04-17, 05-15, **06-18**, 07-17, 08-21, 09-18, 10-16,
   11-20, 12-18: every one its month's third Friday except the Juneteenth-displaced Thursday, and all
   five dates this calendar holds as `confirmed` match exactly. **And the 2027 series displaces too** —
   it lists **2027-06-17, a Thursday**, because Juneteenth is observed Friday 2027-06-18, reproducing
   this repo's `opex-2027-06-17`. So the displacement rule is exercised *inside the year being read*,
   not only in the validation year. The promotion is licensed by `market-events-data.ts`'s own
   **`OCC:`** prefix, and the no-self-confirm convention binds the *discovering* sweep — this document

3. **THE HEADLINE — a monthly expiration two sessions after an FOMC minutes release is
   indistinguishable from one that is not. SUPPORTED as a null, on all three channels.** Anchoring on
   the **160 minutes releases 2007-2026** and counting *trading sessions* from the most recent release
   to each of the **158 monthly expirations**, the gap distribution is sharply bimodal — 2 sessions
   (n = 30), 7 (n = 33), 11 (n = 11), 6 (n = 7), and 68 with no release within 12 sessions — because
   minutes land on a Wednesday three weeks after a meeting and expirations land on a Friday.

### Honest limits

**Leg 3's null is a null, not a proof of absence.** n = 30 against 128 can only exclude a large effect;
a drift of a tenth of a VIX point would be invisible here, and nothing about it says the *content* of a
minutes release cannot move a tape. **Leg 4's refusal rests partly on a judgment call** — 2008-11-21 is
a real observation, not a data error, and dropping it is a robustness check, not a correction; the
honest statement is that the result *depends entirely on one crisis print*, which is a reason to refuse
a bet, not a proof the effect is absent. **Roughly twenty-five comparisons were run and only the month
family was multiplicity-corrected**; the corrections reported are Bonferroni and Šidák over those eight
tests alone. **The Friday confound is structural** — only 6 of 236 expirations in the sample are not
Fridays — so leg 6 bounds the effect and does not decompose it. **The VIX channel is a daily
close-to-close change on a 30-day index**, not a realized-volatility measurement; the five-session
realized-vol test is the only genuinely forward-looking one here. **The ^VIX series carries a bar on
2026-09-07, a Labor Day closure ^GSPC has no bar for** — a vendor artifact; the equity session set was
used as the trading-day universe throughout, and the probe reading below is from the **in-progress
2026-09-08 session** (last completed close 2026-09-04: VIX 14.53, S&P 7,718.60). **Every 2027 FOMC date
is tentative** (`fomccalendars.htm` says so), so leg 8's gate arithmetic is a `confirmed` expiration
measured against an `estimate` meeting — but unlike a blackout entry this expiration's date does not
depend on it, because a third Friday is fixed. **The 2027 statistical calendars do not exist yet** —

## Initial research

**The question.** What are the mechanics of a routine monthly opex (gamma-pinning into the close, realized-vol compression, the post-opex unpin), does anything about *this particular* November 2026 cycle stand out, and how does it interact with the Nov-10 CPI and the estimated CRWV print (both 10 days before it)?

**One-line verdict:** the mechanics are the same well-documented monthly pattern as the Aug-21 exemplar, this cycle is unremarkable and unusually *quiet* (no FOMC in November, nearest catalysts already 10 days past by opex), so the honest output is a guard (mind the Friday close), not a trade — the least eventful of the four opex events in this batch.

**Method.** Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no earnings-style instrument applies and no opex-shaped instrument exists in `scripts/research/`. Sourced web research: OCC/Cboe convention for the date and cycle type, options-analytics literature (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch, CBOE) for the pin/unpin pattern, vendor volume comparisons for the monthly-vs-quarterly notional gap. Read against `trade-playbooks.md` / `multi-symbol-sweep.md` for whether any house playbook is opex-keyed, and cross-referenced against `src/domain/market-events-data.ts` and `earnings-calendar.ts` for the adjacent dated events already on the calendar.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date and cycle type are right — SUPPORTED.** OCC's expiration-calendar convention (3rd Friday, standard monthly) makes Nov 20, 2026 a routine monthly expiration; it is an unadjusted Friday, one cycle before the Dec-18 quarterly triple-witching. This matches this calendar's `impact: medium` coding for Nov 20 versus `impact: high` for the Dec-18 witching.

2. **Gamma-pinning / realized-vol compression into expiration is a real, documented mechanic — SUPPORTED (general literature, not house-tested).** In a positive-gamma regime, dealers hedge by buying dips and selling rallies in progressively larger size as expiration nears, compressing realized vol and pulling price toward high-gamma strikes (SpotGamma, MenthorQ, StrikeWatch; CBOE at the 0DTE extreme). Identical mechanic to the Aug-21 monthly. **Caveat:** vendor dealer-positioning models, not OCC primary data; no same-week snapshot is meaningful this far out (leg 6).

3. **A post-opex "vol pop" / gamma-unpin pattern — MIXED, real but not house-validated.** Once front-month gamma expires, the stabilizing dealer floor is removed and realized vol tends to rise; vendor literature reports a common 1–3% pullback/consolidation as positioning resets — but this is options-analytics-blog and one-CBOE-paper sourced, not a red-teamed instrument run against our own names. Directionally credible, not house-proven.

4. **This cycle is standard monthly, not quarterly — smaller structural impact — SUPPORTED.** Monthly-opex notional ~$1–2T vs ~$4–5T on quarterly witching (~3x smaller gamma roll-off). Nov 20 carries no coincident index rebalance and no index-futures/index-options witching, so its close is a single-name-heavy, ordinary-monthly imbalance — smaller than the Dec-18 witching that follows. Confirms the calendar's `medium` coding.

5. **This is the quietest of the four — SUPPORTED.** Unlike Sep 18 (2 days after the FOMC) and Dec 18 (year-end, stacked with rebalancing + tax-loss flows), and even Oct 16 (2 days after CPI, the week before a mega-cap cluster), Nov 20 sits in a comparatively empty window. **There is no FOMC in November** — the Fed meets Oct 28 and Dec 9, skipping the month — so the opex carries no rate-catalyst adjacency. Its nearest dated catalysts land **10 days before it**: the **Nov-10 CPI** (Oct data, confirmed) and the **estimated CRWV print** (Nov 10, `estimate` — the only tracked-name print anywhere near this opex, and it is 8-K-cadence estimated, not confirmed). Both are already digested by opex week. After opex, AWS re:Invent (est. Nov 30) is the next event. So the Nov-20 gamma roll-off happens into a near-term calendar that is unusually sparse — the pin/unpin is more likely to be a clean structural reset than a release into a fresh catalyst.

6. **No house playbook is opex-keyed — SUPPORTED.** Read against `docs/plans/trade-playbooks.md` and `docs/research/multi-symbol-sweep.md`: S1/G1 are pre-print run-up plays (earnings-dated), S2 is the never-hold-the-print guard (earnings-dated), E1 is a don't-trade-the-open execution rule, S3 is a reaction-day fade (earnings-dated, blocked on shorting), S4 is an overnight-vs-buy-and-hold structural note. None reference options expiration. The one real interaction is indirect and the same as the Aug-21 exemplar: E1's logic doesn't transfer (opex microstructure concentrates at the **close**, not the open), while S4's kept "prefer close-side executions" guidance is what this Friday amplifies risk for — the MOC/MOO imbalance risk already named in the sweep's blocked CRWV overnight-carry item, not something new to build here. (CRWV is doubly relevant this cycle: it is both the sweep's MOC/MOO test subject *and* the only tracked name printing near this opex — but its est. Nov-10 print is 10 days *before* opex, so the two do not overlap.)

**What the conditions support.** Nothing directional — no macro/mechanics playbook exists in the house set for opex. Support = discipline: no automated bot signal or paper position should be sized or entered off Friday's close on high-open-interest names without accounting for pin risk distorting that print. The carry-forward is simply that this is the *least* eventful opex of the four — its adjacencies (CPI + est. CRWV print) are 10 days past by opex week, so there is less near-term catalyst risk for the gamma roll-off to release into than in the other three cycles.

**Honest limits.** The *date* and *cycle type* are confirmed and deterministic (OCC 3rd-Friday); everything trading-adjacent is estimate. Notional-scale figures are vendor/press aggregates, not OCC open interest or a house stat. The pin/unpin pattern is literature-sourced, not tested against our own names — no opex-shaped instrument exists in this repo, so there is no house prior to benchmark against. Deliberately, **no same-week dealer-GEX / gamma-flip / call-wall / put-wall levels are quoted**: 93 days out those snapshots describe near-term front-month positioning, not the Nov-20-expiry gamma, which concentrates only in the final ~1–2 weeks — quoting them now would be false precision.

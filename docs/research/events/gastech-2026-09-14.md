## At a glance

difference instead of asserting it. The show really does produce signed paper — Milan 2025 closed with
**48,678 attendees** and the organiser's own claim of **"more than 20 agreements and MoUs"** worth
**"over $60 billion USD"** — and 2026 (**estimate**, 09-14/17 Bangkok) is bigger on the organiser's

Global (**VG**) moved **+0.29%** through the entire $60bn Milan show: the **2nd percentile**. And this
book tracks **neither name, nor any other energy name** — `symbols: []` is correct. Two findings do
matter. First, the Houston ledger's "~8,000 delegates" was the *conference-delegate* subset, not the
show: Gastech is ~6× that, so it is an even larger attribution confound than that ledger claimed for

## Initial research

**The question, plainly:** the calendar carries 2026-09-14 as Gastech Bangkok, proposed hours earlier by
the [`g20-energy-abundance-ministerial-houston-2026-09-14`](g20-energy-abundance-ministerial-houston-2026-09-14.md)
research as *"a named attribution confound, not a channel."* Is that the whole story? Gastech is where
the LNG industry signs paper — so the honest test is whether the paper moves prices, and whether any of
it reaches a name this book tracks.

**One-line verdict:** the confound framing is right but understated — Gastech is ~6× larger than the
Houston ledger recorded, and it is a real deal venue whose deals are measurably *not* tradeable; the one
dated instrument that touches this book comes not from the conference but from the regulator who happens
to be speaking at it.

**Method:** sourced web research plus one measured price study — no earnings instruments run (sector
kind, no symbols keyed), per the macro-print/sector method in
[`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md). Primary reads, all fetched direct 2026-09-04:
**gastechevent.com** (event header, `/conferences/`, `/exhibition/aienergy/`), the organiser's own
**2025-09-12** Milan closing release, and the **PR Newswire** release of **2026-08-26**. Secondary:

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The dates and venue hold on the organiser's own header, and the `estimate` label is a schema
   artifact.** SUPPORTED on the fact, blocked on the schema. gastechevent.com's header reads verbatim
   *"Exhibition & Conference 14 - 17 September 2026"* at *"BITEC – BANGKOK,"* with per-day hall hours
   (09:00–18:00 Sep 14 · 09:00–19:00 Sep 15 · 09:00–18:00 Sep 16 · 09:00–14:00 Sep 17). The PR Newswire
   release of **2026-08-26** and LNG Industry's **2026-08-27** piece independently carry the same dates
   and venue. The label cannot flip: `CONFIRMED_PREFIX` in `scripts/event-scan-validation.mjs` is
   `IR|CAL|BLS|FED|PJM|SEC|TSY|OCC|BEA|CENSUS|ISM|CB|UMICH`, with no slot for a commercial conference
   organiser — `aws-reinvent-2026` carries `IR:` only because its organiser is a tracked issuer. The
   seventh ledger to bank this gap.

2. **The Houston ledger undercounted the show by roughly 6×, and the correction sharpens its own
   point.** MIXED — the fact was wrong, the conclusion was right and is now stronger. That ledger
   recorded *"~8,000 delegates, 800 speakers and 200 sessions."* Those are the organiser's
   **conference** figures, stated on `/conferences/` as *"15 programmes · 200 sessions · 800 speakers ·
   8,000 delegates."* The **show** is a different number on the same site and in the same releases:
   **50,000+ attendees, 800+ exhibiting companies, 150+ countries**, across five exhibition zones. Milan
   2025's audited-style equivalent was **48,678 attendees from more than 150 countries** (organiser's
   closing release, 2026 dating: **2025-09-12**). So when Houston's ledger called Gastech "the larger
   venue" on the same days, it was right by a wider margin than the number it cited supported. Corrected
   here rather than in that ledger — its rows are append-only.

3. **Gastech is a genuine deal venue. This is not marketing.** SUPPORTED, with named counterparties.
   The organiser claims Milan 2025 saw *"more than 20 agreements and MoUs to be signed"* generating
   *"over $60 billion USD in deals"*; the specific transactions are independently reported. Turkey's
   **BOTAŞ** signed import deals with **BP, Hartree, Cheniere, JERA and Equinor** covering ~**15 bcm**
   for 2026–2028. **YPF** announced LNG development with **Shell** and **Eni** — reported as the largest
   gas-infrastructure investment in Argentina's history. Italy's **Edison** took ~**0.7 mtpa** long-term
   from Shell. **PetroChina International** signed with **Equinor** (09-09), **BOTAŞ** (MoU) and
   **Singapore GasCo** (master SPA, 09-11). Attendance included **25 international ministers**, the US
   Secretaries of Energy and the Interior, and CEOs of Shell, TotalEnergies, Eni, Cheniere, Vitol,
   Uniper, Snam and YPF. Any prior that Gastech is a talking shop is wrong.

4. **And the deals do not move the tape. This is the load-bearing leg, and it is measured.** REFUTED as

   four-session window, against the distribution of **all 917** overlapping four-session moves since
   2023-01-03 (median |move| **1.95%**, p75 **3.42%**, p90 **5.28%**):

   | Gastech 2025 Milan (09-09/12) | 2025-09-08 → 2025-09-12 | **+1.16%** | 31st |

7. **The one dated US instrument in the room belongs to a regulator, not to the conference.** SUPPORTED

   speaker (PR Newswire 2026-08-26; LNG Industry 2026-08-27), and the programme list carries a **Global

   standards** covering *"computational load"* — defined in the order as *"load comprised of power
   demand from information technology equipment, such as servers, storage, and networking hardware"* —
   by **2026-12-31**. Swett's stated rationale: *"Certain large loads like data centers and crypto
   mining operations that NERC calls computational load have the potential to change their demand almost
   instantly. And this rapid fluctuation causes voltage stability issues that threaten grid
   reliability,"* and *"we must ensure that the critical work of winning the AI race does not threaten
   reliability in our country."* That is a federal, nationwide deadline on the exact theme this calendar

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date: estimate — on a schema gap, not on doubt; the organiser's own header, its 2026-08-26
release and trade press all carry 14–17 September 2026 at BITEC Bangkok).** No position keyed to this
event and no prospect of one. Gastech is a **read, a discipline and a pointer**, in that order. The read
is whether AixEnergy lands a tracked hyperscaler on its programme — a datapoint on how fast the
gas-for-AI-compute story is institutionalising, never a trade. The discipline is attribution: Gastech
and the Houston ministerial run the same three days twelve time zones apart, 17 tracked events sit
within ±5 days, and Bangkok's halls close at ~07:00 ET so nothing it produces is a US-session catalyst.
The pointer is the finding that matters: the only dated US instrument this pass surfaced is **FERC's
2026-07-16 order in Docket RD26-7-000**, giving NERC until **2026-12-31** to file mandatory
computational-load reliability standards — proposed to the calendar in this PR, and the reason FERC's
chairman being in Bangkok is worth noting at all. The show's own deals are real and measurably
unpriceable: three consecutive Gastech windows left Cheniere at the 66th, 28th and 31st percentile of
its own ordinary four-session move, and Venture Global at the 2nd, through $60bn of announcements.
Estimate status widens caution only; nothing is date-keyed action, `symbols: []` is correct, and `low`
impact is affirmed.

**Kill switches.**

- *"Gastech leaves no measurable mark"* dies on a 2026 show window in which a US-listed gas/LNG name
  posts a move above the **p90** of its own four-session distribution that is separable from CPI
  (09-11), the 20Y auction (09-15), the FOMC decision (09-16), retail sales (09-16) and opex (09-18).
  The bar is separability, not size — and the window is deliberately hostile to the claim.
- *"No tracked symbol carries exposure"* dies on any Gastech or AixEnergy announcement naming a
  hyperscaler this calendar tracks in a **dated** gas-for-data-centre supply agreement. That would
  deserve its own calendar entry, not a re-tier of this one.
- *"The AI bridge is themed, not staffed"* dies on a confirmed MSFT, AMZN, GOOG, META, ORCL, CRWV or
  NVDA speaker appearing on a published AixEnergy programme before **2026-09-14**. Checkable, dated,
  and not yet tripped as of 2026-09-04.
- *"The channel is the docket, not the conference"* dies if NERC's computational-load filing slips past
  **2026-12-31**, is extended by FERC, or arrives as voluntary guidance rather than a mandatory

- *"Bangkok breaks before the open"* dies on a Gastech announcement timed to US hours, which would mean

- *"The date is organiser-primary"* dies on any fetched gastechevent.com or dmg events page naming

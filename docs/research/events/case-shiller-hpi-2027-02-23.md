### Leg 4 — the 2026 scorecard is already mostly written, and it points up · **SUPPORTED**

The December-2026 y/y this edition will headline decomposes exactly:

> Dec-2026 ÷ Dec-2025 = (**Jun-2026 ÷ Dec-2025**) × (**Dec-2026 ÷ Jun-2026**)

The first term is **published and fixed**: 336.663 ÷ 327.035 = **1.02944**, i.e. **+2.944pp** of
carry already banked. Only the second term — the H2-2026 path — is unwritten. Measured against the
**39** observed H2 (Jun→Dec) NSA returns, 1987–2025:

| Scenario for H2-2026 | Implied Dec-2026 y/y |
|---|---|
| Seasonal median m/m sum (**+0.875%**) | **+3.84%** |
| Flat (**0%**) | **+2.94%** |
| A repeat of 2025's own weak H2 (**−1.378%**) | **+1.53%** |
| H2 p10 (**−4.52%**) | **−1.71%** |
| H2 minimum on record (**−8.40%**, 2008) | **−5.70%** |

Inverting it gives the two thresholds that matter, and both are demanding:

- **To merely match 2025's full year (+1.15% current vintage), H2-2026 must run ≤ −1.743%.** That
  has happened in **7 of 39** years: **1990, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2022**.
- **For a negative full-year 2026, H2-2026 must run ≤ −2.860%.** **5 of 39**: **2007, 2008, 2010,
  2011, 2022** — the GFC and the 2022 rate shock, and nothing else.

So the base case is that the **2027-02-23 edition reports an improvement on 2025 and retires the
"weakest full-year gain since 2011" line**, and the paths that would keep it are historically the
paths of a credit crisis or a rate shock. Confidence is **medium**, not high, and the reason is
stated plainly: six unpublished months separate today from the number, and 2025 itself printed an
H2 of **−1.38%** against a **+0.78%** median, so this cycle has already demonstrated it can run
well below seasonal norms.

**Forecast distance, for the record.** December 2026 sits **6 steps** beyond the last published
reference month (June 2026). This session reproduces the 10-27 lane's persistence table from an
independent fetch, to three decimals (SA m/m, 2015-01 → 2026-06, n=138): lag-1 autocorrelation

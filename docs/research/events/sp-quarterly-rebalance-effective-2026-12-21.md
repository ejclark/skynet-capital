### Method

Macro / market-structure mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md), plus the constituent-level panel the chain has been building. The entry carries `symbols: []`, so no symbol-keyed instrument applies and `scripts/research/` owns no index-flow instrument. **Nothing was taken on faith — every number the siblings report and this document repeats was recomputed.**

- **The proposal was read in full first**, then the canonical file written by this session.
- **The release archive was rebuilt from scratch, not inherited.** `press.spglobal.com/index.php?s=2429&l=100` across offsets `o=0..800` — nine requests, all HTTP 200 — yielding **899** unique dated releases, **2022-05-06 → 2026-09-04**. **Measurement design, stated before the results.** Three sessions per cycle: the **announcement reaction** (first session after the release Friday), the **implementation Friday** (the third Friday, at whose close the rebalance prints), and the **effective open** (the first session strictly after the third Friday — this event's session), each mapped onto actual trading sessions from SPY's own calendar so holiday shifts resolve automatically. Excess return is close-to-close, benchmarked to the row's **own tier ETF** (SPY / IJH / IJR). Relative turnover is the name's volume over its trailing-60 median, divided by the benchmark's same ratio. Rows whose effective-date text differs from their release's majority are dropped as bundled off-cycle changes; any `|return| > 40%` is dropped as a corporate-action artefact. Sign tests are **exact two-sided binomial** — the convention that reproduces the 2027 sibling's 9/21 → p=66.4% to the decimal, where a normal approximation would have printed 51.3%.

### Honest limits

**This is a pooled result and pooling can hide a live cohort.** 548 rows across 16–17 cycles say the average effective open does nothing; they do not say the 2026-12-21 cohort will do nothing, which is why `FT-…-2` scores exactly that. **The sign tests treat names as independent and they are not** — a cycle's cohort shares an announcement, a sector tilt and a trading day, so the true p-values on the *announcement* leg are worse than stated by an unknown amount.

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** Still **a read, not a trade** — and this session's finding is that the one thing that
makes 2026-11-04 special resolves a day early, which drains it. This is the **last of seven
long-end operations** inside the doubled-cap window `sb0607` opened, and it lands on the very date
that window expires by its own text. The tempting story — *the market finds out on the day whether
the doubled size survives* — is wrong on the clock. Schedule footnote 1 puts this operation's
announcement, and therefore its published maximum, at **11:00 ET on 2026-11-03**; the Quarterly
Refunding statement that sets every *later* cap posts **08:30 ET on 11-04**, five hours before the
1:40pm operation. So the operation runs under a number already fixed, and the decision on its date
belongs to [`treasury-refunding-2026-11-04`](treasury-refunding-2026-11-04.md), not to it. Two
measured facts finish the read. **One —** fill in this sector is near-deterministic: **25 of 25**
10Y-20Y operations took the full announced maximum (`fiscaldata`, pulled direct 2026-09-06), so
"what will it buy" is answered by the 11-03 announcement, not by the tape. **Two —** the
refunding-day collision has an **exact precedent and it was benign**: 2026-05-06, same sector, same
collision, **$19.69B offered** (above 2026's $18.39B median) and a full fill. Date and cap are
`estimate`; `symbols: []`; no position, and no attribution of 11-04's tape to a plumbing operation
on a day already carrying the refunding statement, ISM Services, ADP and the FT-900 report, one day
after the midterms.

## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event was filed on 2026-09-06 by the
[`treasury-buyback-increase-2026-09-09`](treasury-buyback-increase-2026-09-09.md) adjacency sweep,
into a corridor that is already well covered: the
[09-10 sibling](treasury-buyback-10y20y-2026-09-10.md) settled that a long-end operation is *"a data
release, not a catalyst"*, and the [10-15 sibling](treasury-buyback-10y20y-2026-10-15.md) closed the
offer-volume baseline and the eligibility model with `fiscaldata`. Repeating either would add
nothing. What is unique to **this** operation is a calendar collision no other operation in the
programme has: it runs on the day its own governing authority expires and its successor policy is
announced. So the question asked here: **does that collision change anything measurable — the cap
this operation runs under, the offer book it draws, or the information it carries — and is there
precedent?**

**One-line verdict:** the collision is real, it resolves a day *early*, and its one exact precedent
says it does not bite — so 11-04 is not where this operation's own size question gets answered
(11-03 11:00 ET is), it is where the question gets answered for everything after, and that belongs
to the refunding statement sharing its date rather than to the buyback.

### Conviction legs, tested

**1. The operation exists with these parameters — SUPPORTED, verbatim off the primary.** The
August-2026-refunding *Tentative Schedule of Treasury Buyback Operations* carries the row
**announce 11/3/2026 · operation 11/4/2026 1:40 pm–2:00 pm · settle 11/5/2026 · Liquidity Support ·
Nominal Coupons 10Y to 20Y · 11/05/2036–11/04/2046 · min $0 · max $2 billion.** The entry stays
`estimate` on the three counts the sibling entries already document: a tentative schedule is
tentative by construction, the printed $2B is known stale, and the confirming primary is the
**11-03 announcement**, not this document.

**2. The cap question is settled on 11-03, not on 11-04 — SUPPORTED, and this is the session's
structural finding.** Three timestamps, each from a primary:

- `sb0607`, verbatim: *"The current maximum size of $2 billion per operation will be at least $4
  billion per operation. This change is effective September 9, 2026 and will be in effect for the
  remainder of this refunding quarter (through November 4, 2026). Treasury will provide more
  information about future buyback sizes at the next Quarterly Refunding, scheduled for November 4,
  2026."*
- The schedule's footnote 1, verbatim: *"A preliminary list of eligible CUSIPs will be disclosed at
  11:00 am ET on the Announcement Date."* This operation's Announcement Date is **11/3/2026**.
- The Quarterly Refunding statement posts **08:30 ET** on refunding Wednesday (Treasury's standing
  release convention — see *Honest limits*, this is the one timing input not read off a dated
  primary this session).

Ordered: **11-03 11:00 ET** the operation's maximum becomes a published number → **11-04 08:30 ET**
the refunding statement sets forward policy → **11-04 13:40 ET** the operation runs. A plain reading
of *"through November 4, 2026"* includes this operation, and the announcement that fixes its size
precedes the statement that could change anything. **Consequence for the calendar:** this event is
*not* where the doubled-size decision is made. That is `treasury-refunding-2026-11-04`'s job, and
findings about future sizing belong on that ledger rather than folded into this one.

**3. Seven long-end operations sit inside the window, and this is the seventh — SUPPORTED, and it
means the operation carries no informational first.** Read off the same PDF, the long-end
liquidity-support operations from the 09-09 effective date onward are **09-10 (10-20Y) · 09-24
(20-30Y) · 10-01 (10-20Y) · 10-08 (20-30Y) · 10-15 (10-20Y) · 10-27 (20-30Y) · 11-04 (10-20Y)** —
all seven already tracked on the calendar, so this sweep found no unproposed dated event in the
series. The schedule's last row is a **11/5 1Mo-2Y** operation at a $4B cap, outside the long-end
sectors and outside this event's scope. Six prior doubled-cap long-end observations will exist by
11-04; unlike the 09-10 sibling (zero priors) or 10-15 (two), whatever this operation teaches, an
earlier one taught first.

**4. Refunding-day collision has precedent, and the precedent is benign — SUPPORTED, matched
control.** Across the modern programme (`buybacks_operations`, n=128, 2024-04-03 → 2026-09-03),
exactly **two** operations have landed on a Quarterly Refunding statement day:

| Operation | Sector | Offered | Accepted | Cap | Fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-02-04 | 1Mo to 2Y | $69.00B | $4.000B | $4.0B | full |
| **2026-05-06** | **10Y to 20Y** | **$19.69B** | **$2.000B** | **$2.0B** | **full** |

Eight earlier refunding Wednesdays back to 2024-01-31 carried no operation at all. 2026-11-04 will
be the **third** collision and the **second** in this sector — and the 05-06 control is exact: same
sector, same collision, an offer book **above** 2026's $18.39B median, and a full fill. So "bidders
stand aside pending the policy statement" now has a dated counter-example rather than being an open
worry. The limit is stated in leg 4's own terms below and in *Honest limits*: on 05-06 no
buyback-size decision was pending; on 11-04 one is.

**5. Cap-binding is a long-end property, and this sector's record is perfect — SUPPORTED,
independently reproduced.** Same pull, liquidity-support operations only:

| Cohort | n | Filled the announced cap |
|---|---|---|
| 10Y-20Y (2024-07-02 → 2026-08-11) | 25 | **25** |
| 20Y-30Y (2025-11-20 → 2026-08-18, full record) | 26 | 24 (under-fills 2026-03-19 $0.205B, 2025-11-20 $0.785B) |
| Any sector already running a **$4B** cap | 41 | **13** |

Cover in the 10Y-20Y bucket ran **3.2–18.0x** against $2B. The $4B-capped cohort is the honest
counterweight: outside 1Mo-2Y the shortfalls are large — **2026-08-25** (5Y-7Y) took **$1.19B of
$4B** on $8.40B offered, **2026-08-20** (3Y-5Y) took **$1.86B of $4B** on $10.16B offered. Treasury
prices its own bid and simply declines offers it judges rich, so a filled cap is a property of this
sector's offer book, not of the cap. That matters here because this sector's book printed its
thinnest reading on record eight days before the doubling was announced ($7.40B on 2026-08-11 — the
10-15 sibling's finding, reproduced in this pull). This session's independent pull also reproduces
the **two** 20Y-30Y under-fills the [10-08 sibling](treasury-buyback-20y30y-2026-10-08.md) corrected
the corridor on, and reproduces **0 of 25** for 10Y-20Y.

**6. Treasury's own stated rationale is measurable, and it checks out — SUPPORTED.** `sb0607`
justifies the doubling by *"consistent strong sponsorship from market participants, as evidenced by
the significant volume of high-quality offers Treasury routinely receives in longer-dated buyback
operations."* Against the record: 10Y-20Y offers ran a **$19.69B median** with a **$6.43–36.05B**
range across 25 operations, and every one of the 25 exceeded $4B. The rationale is not rhetoric.
The caveat is the same one leg 5 carries — the most recent observation is also the weakest in over
two years.

**7. The day is the most crowded any operation in this programme has faced — SUPPORTED, and it is
why the tape is unattributable.** Fifteen tracked events sit within ±5 days. The operation's own
date carries the **Quarterly Refunding statement** (08:30), **ISM Services** (high), **ADP** and the
**FT-900 trade report**; the day before carries the **US midterm elections** (high) and **JOLTS**;
two days before, **Treasury's Q4/Q1 borrowing estimates**; two days after, the **October jobs
report**. For contrast, the 10-08 sibling's worst collision was a single 30Y reopening. No 1:40pm
plumbing operation is separable from that stack.

**8. No same-day auction — a real difference from the October pair.** November's refunding coupon
auctions follow the statement by about a week (the May pattern: statement 05-06, then 3Y 05-11, 10Y
05-12, 30Y 05-13), so unlike `treasury-buyback-20y30y-2026-10-08` — which ran 40 minutes after a 30Y
reopening and needed a whole eligibility model to avoid a wrong conclusion — this operation faces no
auction on its date and no on-the-run collision. **Noted for the refunding event, not this one:**
Veterans Day (`sifma-bond-market-closure-2026-11-11`, bond market recommended closed) falls inside
that auction week and will compress it.

### Honest limits

- **Date and cap are `estimate`.** A tentative schedule is tentative, and the printed $2B is known
  superseded; the confirming primary is the 11-03 announcement. Estimates widen caution here, they
  license nothing.
- **The 08:30 ET refunding-statement time is convention, not a dated primary read this session.**
  It comes from Treasury's quarterly-refunding documents practice and convergent press; the ordering
  argument in leg 2 survives any release time before 13:40 ET, but the precise timestamp is not
  quoted off a dated Treasury notice.
- **The refunding-day precedent is n=2 overall and n=1 in this sector**, and the one exact control
  (2026-05-06) had no buyback-size decision pending. It is a real dated counter-example to the
  stand-aside worry, not a base rate.
- **"Through November 4, 2026" including the 11-04 operation is a plain reading of `sb0607`**, not a
  Treasury clarification. The 11-03 announcement is what settles it — which is exactly why
  `FT-treasury-buyback-10y20y-2026-11-04-1` is registered rather than asserted.
- **No instrument run and no consensus exists.** `symbols: []` leaves the symbol-keyed instruments
  with no target, and no survey publishes an expectation for a buyback operation, so there is no
  surprise to measure against — only Treasury's own published numbers.
- **The offer-book uncertainty is inherited, not resolved.** Whether 2026-08-11's $7.40B was an
  outlier or a regime is answered by the 09-10, 10-01 and 10-15 operations, all of which report
  before this event's next assessment window closes.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (as of 2026-09-06, D-59; the event is `estimate` on both date and cap).**

**Kill switches:**

- **The 2026-11-03 announcement publishing a maximum below $4B** — `sb0607`'s window excluded its own
  end date, leg 2's plain reading is wrong, and the finding belongs on the refunding ledger too.
- **The operation accepting less than its announced maximum** — the first 10Y-20Y under-fill in 26

- **Two or more sub-$12B offer totals across the 09-10 / 10-01 / 10-15 operations** — 2026-08-11's
  $7.40B was a regime rather than a hole, and a $4B cap arrives at 11-04 thinly covered (1.85x at
  8/11's volume).
- **The 11-04 refunding statement raising the long-end size above $4B, making it permanent, or
  adding sectors or TGA funding** — a materially bigger lever than anything dated here; it gets
  assessed on `treasury-refunding-2026-11-04` and, if it carries its own date, proposed as its own
  calendar entry rather than folded into this doc's stance.
- **The replacement tentative buyback schedule publishing and cancelling, rescheduling or resizing
  this operation** — still unpublished as of 2026-09-06 (18 days after `sb0607` promised it; the
  re-fetched PDF is hash-identical to the 09-03 and 09-05 pulls), and its publication is the single
  cheapest thing that could invalidate leg 1.

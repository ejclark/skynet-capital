## Initial research

### The question and the verdict

Does the September 2026 preliminary Balance of Payments, out 2026-11-10 08:50 JST (estimate), move
anything this house holds or trades, and what is the cheapest honest thing to bank from it? **Verdict:
no, and bank three small facts.** The heavy measurement lives in the
[12-08 ledger](japan-balance-of-payments-2026-12-08.md) (104 releases 2013-2026 against 3,342 Nikkei
sessions); re-running it for the preceding release would spend a session to re-read the same series.

### Method

- **Primary date source:** MOF English BoP page, fetched direct 2026-11-08 (HTTP 200, 21,047 bytes),
  parsed for the release schedule. Row: "November 10, 2026（8:50 am） Balance of Payments, September 2026
  (Preliminary)". The same page lists 2026-12-08, 2027-02-08 and 2027-03-08, all at 08:50 (the 2027-01-12 row was not
  separately read here).
- **Tape:** Yahoo daily bars for `^N225`, `JPY=X`, `^VIX`, `^GSPC` pulled 2026-11-08 (range 2 months),
  gaps and close-to-close computed from consecutive bars. Not cached; no instrument-cache reuse.
- **Consensus:** one aggregator row (Trading Economics), read through a fetch tool that inferred the
  column labels from the page layout — treat the consensus figures as **unverified**.

### Leg 1 — the date: SUPPORTED

MOF's page reads 2026-11-10 08:50 JST. The 12-08 ledger measured that **103 of 104** releases fall on
the month's 6th Tokyo trading day (104/104 counting scheduled days). November 2026's trading days are
Nov 2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 — Nov 3 is Culture Day and the `^N225` bar series has no 2026-11-03 row — so the
6th is **2026-11-10**. October agrees: 10-01, 02, 05, 06, 07, **08**, and the August data did publish
2026-10-08 08:50 JST (aggregator timestamp 2026-10-07 23:50 GMT). Registered as FT-1.

### Leg 2 — the August print, the first dress rehearsal: MIXED (one point, observed before registration)

Aggregator: August 2026 current account **¥4,062.0bn vs consensus ¥3,194bn** (+27%). On 2026-10-08:
Nikkei opening gap **−0.28%** (open 69,840.64 vs prior close 70,035.71), close-to-close −1.42%;
USD/JPY close-to-close **0.00%** (158.06 vs 158.06); S&P 500 close-to-close −0.47% that day. This was **observed before this ledger registered anything**, so it is context and not a
forward test. It points the same way as the 12-08 ledger (null open, ordinary yen) on the largest
surprise in the recent record, but it is one print.

### Leg 3 — a standing falsifier in the 12-08 ledger is already dead: SUPPORTED

The 12-08 ledger's "This month" row says it is proved wrong by the **2026-10-08** and **2026-11-10**
BoP sessions *both* printing a Nikkei \|opening gap\| ≥ 1.0%. The 10-08 gap was 0.28%, so that
conjunction **cannot fire**. That ledger's rows are append-only and not this session's to edit; the
fact goes in this ledger and the next pulse of that one can retire the row. FT-3 here registers the
single-print version.

### Leg 4 — the morning is crowded, and the regime is not the one the study measured: SUPPORTED

- **Same minute:** the [BoJ Summary of Opinions](boj-summary-of-opinions-2026-11-10.md) (est) is also
  scheduled 08:50 JST. Ten minutes before Tokyo's open, two releases land together.
- **Same day:** the [30-year JGB auction](jgb-30y-auction-2026-11-10.md) (est), US CPI
  ([ledger](cpi-2026-11-10.md), confirmed, 08:30 ET = 22:30 JST), the 10-year Treasury auction and the
  [US-China tariff truce expiry](us-china-tariff-truce-expiry-2026-11-10.md) (est), plus CRWV's print.
- **Regime:** VIX closed 2026-11-06 at **25.64** (28.18 on 11-05, +69.7% that day, S&P 500 −4.36%);
  the 12-08 ledger's study sits on a 14.5 VIX baseline. Nikkei closed 57,434.77 on 11-06 (**−5.96%**,
  gap −1.15%), USD/JPY 158.43 on 11-07. Tokyo also trades 11-09 before this print.
- **Consequence:** the "matched mid-month control" logic of the 12-08 study does not transfer one for
  one. FT-2 is registered at a lower confidence than that ledger's 0.83 for this reason.

- **Estimate label.** Every statement above about 11-10 is conditional on an estimate date.

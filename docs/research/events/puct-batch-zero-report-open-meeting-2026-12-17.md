### The question, plainly

[12-10 filing ledger](ercot-data-center-audit-filing-2026-12-10.md), which found that the tracked

authority responds." That framing embeds three claims worth testing before this event ever gets a
pulse: **is 12-17 a real PUCT open meeting, are the Batch Zero reports actually on it, and can that

**One-line verdict.** The meeting is real and now independently corroborated on ERCOT's calendar —
9:30 a.m. CT, Travis Building, the **last PUCT meeting of 2026** — but the agenda item still rests

### Legs tested

1. **"2026-12-17 is a real PUCT open meeting."** **SUPPORTED — and materially upgraded from the
   newsletter-only sourcing this entry was filed with.** `ercot.com/committees/puct` lists
   **"Dec 17, 2026"** among PUCT meetings, and its event page
   (`ercot.com/calendar/12172026-PUCT-Meeting`) gives **9:30 AM**, the **Travis Building
   Commissioners Hearing Room 7-100**, 1701 N. Congress Ave., Austin, plus WebEx/teleconference
   access. The surrounding cadence is regular and checks out as every-other-Thursday: **Nov 05 ·
   Nov 19 · Dec 03 · Dec 17** (all Thursdays, verified). Two structural facts fall out: 12-17 is
   the **last PUCT meeting of 2026**, and the next one is **2027-01-14** — a **28-day** gap. ERCOT
   is not the PUCT, so this is corroboration by the filing party's own calendar, not a primary;
   `status` stays `estimate` and the source prefix stays `NEWS:`.
2. **"The two Batch Zero reports are on that meeting's agenda."** **MIXED — the weak leg, and it
   stays weak until an agenda posts.** Only Global Data Center Hub states it outright: *"ERCOT files
   the Batch Zero Eligibility Verification Report and the Community Impact Review Report on December
   10, 2026"* and *"presents both at the PUCT open meeting on December 17."* Utility Dive
   corroborates only the shape — Seely, verbatim: *"Our goal is to head toward a December 10
   filing,"* with a report delivered *"to the PUCT a week before its open meeting that month."*
   **The arithmetic is the strongest independent support available:** December holds two meetings,
   12-03 and 12-17, and only 12-17 sits exactly a week after 12-10. That is a real corroboration
   and it is still not a document. PUCT posts final agendas as
   `ftp.puc.texas.gov/public/puct-info/agency/om/<MMDDYY>Final.pdf` (naming confirmed by
   `050726Final.pdf` and `061826Final.pdf` surfaced in search) — so **`121726Final.pdf`, expected
   ~12-10, is the artifact that settles this**, and it is written into the quarterly falsifier
   above.
3. **"The newsletter's docket attribution is right."** **REFUTED — the finding of this session.**
   The newsletter files the interconnection-pause story under **"PUCT Docket 59220,"** and the
   [12-10 ledger](ercot-data-center-audit-filing-2026-12-10.md) carried that forward as the
   proceeding the December reports land in. **59220 is a different case:** the application of
   **Crusoe Energy Systems, FGE Goodnight 1 and Ensign Infrastructure for a net-metering
   arrangement**, ordered **2026-07-23**, imposing a cumulative **525.5 MW** emergency-curtailment
   obligation on two co-located data centers behind a **265.5 MW** wind facility — the first test
   case under **SB 6** (White & Case; tftc.io; the newsletter's own text quotes the same
   curtailment holding). ERCOT's Batch Zero filings run under **59142**: that is where ERCOT filed
   three good-cause exceptions on **2026-08-10**, and an interchange document id
   `59142_20_1620380.PDF` ("ERCOT Large Load Batch Study Update") sits in the same docket.
   **Consequence for this event:** the December reports most plausibly land in **59142** (or a
   successor), *and* the single source placing them on the 12-17 agenda has now demonstrably
   erred on a fact that could be checked. That is exactly why the agenda leg stays `estimate` while
   the date leg upgrades.

   **SUPPORTED — and it corrects the sibling entry's stated premise that "no order is expected
   here."** On **2026-08-20**, at an open meeting, the PUCT **granted all three** of ERCOT's
   good-cause exceptions — relief from the 08-07 classification deadline, inclusion of unclassified
   base-load candidates in the Aug/Nov Quarterly Stability Assessments, and a 24-day dynamic-data

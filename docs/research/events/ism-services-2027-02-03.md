## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This is the fifth ISM Services ledger in a chain whose siblings have already measured the release's
reaction function to nil at daily resolution ([11-04](ism-services-2026-11-04.md)) and at hourly
resolution ([12-03](ism-services-2026-12-03.md)). The event itself was proposed two days ago by the
[manufacturing 2027-02-01 lane](ism-manufacturing-2027-02-01.md), which brought two structural claims
with it — *annual revision edition* and *February is FOMC-contaminated*. So: **do those two claims
actually transfer from the manufacturing series to this one, or were they inherited on the strength of
the word "ISM"?** And second: what does the calendar do to *this specific Wednesday* that it does not
do to its siblings?

**One-line verdict.** One claim transfers and one breaks. The FOMC-contamination claim **transfers and
strengthens** — measured independently on the services release set, all twelve February releases in
twelve years landed within six trading sessions of a decision. The *bond-magnitude and month-slot*
claims **do not**: run on services, the February cell points the opposite way from manufacturing's on
both bonds and equities, which is the cleanest available demonstration that a twelve-observation month
cell is noise. Both halves argue the same refusal, and the second one costs the chain a story it was
about to carry forward.

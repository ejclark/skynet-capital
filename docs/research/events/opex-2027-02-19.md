## Initial research

### The question

Does the February 2027 monthly expiration carry any mechanic the five prior opex ledgers have not
already documented — and specifically, does Presidents Day landing on Monday 2027-02-15 change
anything about it beyond the calendar picture the seeding note already sketched?

**One-line verdict:** yes, but smaller and more precise than it first looks — the holiday shortens
the calendar week without moving the expiration or removing a single session from the pin window,
the genuinely distinguishing feature is Fed *isolation* rather than the holiday, and the honest
output is two execution guards plus one registered forward test, not a trade.

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date is right, and the holiday provably cannot move it — SUPPORTED (but not promoted).**
   Two independent routes on the one fetched Cboe page reach 2027-02-19. **Directly:** the page
   renders `VA/G7 → 2027-02-19` — S&P 500 Variance futures, February 2027 — and across its 8 VA rows
   the listed settlement equals the computed third Friday 7 times. **By the 30-day rule:**
   `VX/F7 → 2027-01-20`, and VIX contract terms settle against the SPX/SPXW series expiring exactly
   30 days later, i.e. 2027-02-19; the rule reproduces on 8 of 9 VX rows. **The single exception in
   each list is the same exception, and it is the load-bearing new observation here:** `VA/M7`
   settles Thursday 2027-06-17 where June's third Friday is 2027-06-18, and `VX/K7` settles Tuesday
   2027-05-18 with +30d landing on that same Thursday — both caused by Juneteenth observed on
   **Friday** 2027-06-18. So the only holiday-driven break anywhere in Cboe's own 2026-09 → 2027-12
   listing is Friday-holiday-driven. Presidents Day is a Monday; it is structurally incapable of
   producing that break. **Why this stays `estimate` anyway:** no expiration *calendar* was read (four
   routes failed today), the `OCC:` prefix names theocc.com/Cboe expiration calendars specifically,
   and `VA/G7`'s settlement date reaches the SPX expiration through a contract spec this session did
   not fetch. Every honest call below is a stand-aside either way, so the label costs nothing and
   "safety scales to stakes" points at keeping it.

2. **Presidents Day 2027 is 2027-02-15, primary-sourced, and absent from this calendar — SUPPORTED,
   and it is proposed in this PR.** NYSE's holiday table, fetched today, parses its Washington's
   Birthday row as `['Monday, February 16', 'Monday, February 15', 'Monday, February 21']` for
   2026 / 2027 / 2028 — the NYSE is closed **Monday 2027-02-15**. The seeding note for this event
   already stated the short week in prose; what it did not do is put the closure on the calendar, so
   nothing screening or scanning this corridor could see it. Proposed here as
   `presidents-day-market-closure-2027-02-15` (`estimate`, `NEWS:`), the second market-closure entry
   on this calendar after `good-friday-market-closure-2027-03-26`.

3. **The same-week configuration is not the default — SUPPORTED, and this is new to the repo.**
   Whether Presidents Day falls inside the February expiration week depends on where the month
   starts, and this session computed it rather than assuming it. Across 2015–2035 the third Monday
   precedes the third Friday **in the same week in 9 of 21 years (43%)**: 2015, 2016, 2020, 2021,
   2025, 2026, 2027, 2031, 2032 — exactly the years whose third Friday is the 19th, 20th or 21st. In
   the other 12 the holiday is the *following* Monday and the expiration week is an ordinary five
   sessions. 2025/2026/2027 is the only unbroken three-year run in the window. This matters for one
   reason only: it forbids treating "the February opex short week" as a stable seasonal regularity,
   because it is absent more often than it is present.

4. **The "compressed pin window" reading is REFUTED on session count, and survives only as a
   calendar-span fact.** This is the same trap `good-friday-market-closure-2027-03-26`'s initial
   research caught one quarter later, so it was counted mechanically here before being claimed. The
   last five sessions before expiration are **2027-02-12, 02-16, 02-17, 02-18, 02-19** — five, the
   same as any other cycle. Sessions are conserved; the window simply reaches one day further back.
   What genuinely changes is span: those five sessions cover **7 calendar days (02-12 → 02-19)
   instead of the usual 4 (Mon → Fri)**, a **+75%** stretch, and all of the extra time sits in a
   single non-trading gap — Fri 02-12 close to Tue 02-16 open — that dealers cannot re-hedge across.
   Option time value decays in calendar time while hedging happens in session time, so the honest
   statement is that this cycle front-loads three calendar days of decay into one un-hedgeable gap
   four sessions before expiry. That is a real asymmetry and a much smaller claim than "the pin
   window is compressed."

5. **Fed isolation, not the holiday, is what actually distinguishes this expiration — SUPPORTED, and
   it is the sharpest contrast with the March sibling.** The Fed's own calendar, fetched today, gives
   2027 as Jan 26-27, Mar 16-17\*, Apr 27-28, Jun 8-9\*, Jul 27-28, Sep 14-15\*, Oct 26-27, Dec 7-8\*
   (\* = SEP). **There is no February 2027 meeting.** Computing the distance from every 2027 third
   Friday to its nearest decision: Jan 12 · **Feb 23** · Mar 2 · Apr 12 · May 19 · Jun 9 · Jul 12 ·
   **Aug 23** · Sep 2 · Oct 12 · Nov 19 · Dec 9 days. 2027-02-19 ties 2027-08-20 at **23 days** — the
   maximum any monthly expiration reaches in 2027. It also sits 15 days clear of the March blackout
   (`fomc-blackout-start-2027-03-06`, `estimate`), so Fed officials are free to speak through this
   window. Mechanically: this gamma roll-off happens with no scheduled decision anywhere near it and
   no communications restriction — the exact inverse of the 2027-03-19 witching, which sits 2
   sessions past the first SEP of 2027.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `estimate`).** Treat 2027-02-19 as an ordinary monthly expiration whose single
structural feature — Presidents Day on Monday 2027-02-15 — has been measured rather than assumed, and
does **less** than the phrase "four-session expiration week" implies. It cannot move the date (Cboe's
own data shows the third-Friday convention breaking only for *Friday* holidays), and it removes no
session from the pin window (the last five sessions run 02-12 → 02-19 intact). What it does is stretch
those five sessions across **7 calendar days instead of 4**, concentrating the extra decay in one
un-hedgeable 3-day gap four sessions before expiry. The configuration itself is not a seasonal
regularity: it occurs in 9 of 21 Februaries 2015–2035. The feature that actually separates this
expiration from its March sibling is **Fed isolation** — 23 days from the nearest decision, the
2027 maximum (tied), no SEP, and 15 days clear of the March blackout — where the witching sits 2
sessions past a dot plot. No position change is triggered, no house playbook targets it, and the
`estimate` label bars date-keyed action independently. Elevated execution caution applies to the
**2027-02-17 open** (VIX/VXM SOQ) and the **2027-02-19 close** (single-stock expiration). Two parts of
the picture are honestly missing rather than clean: no 2027 earnings calendar exists in this repo, and
no US macro print for February 2027 is dated here because bls.gov refused every route tried.

**Kill switches:**

- **An OCC or Cboe expiration calendar is retrieved that lists a February 2027 monthly expiration on
  a date other than 2027-02-19** — the corridor re-dates wholesale. Conversely a calendar listing
  2027-02-19 directly promotes this entry to `confirmed`/`OCC:`. Re-attempt theocc.com and cboe.com
  at **every** pulse; four routes failed 2026-09-05.
- **The NYSE publishes a 2027 calendar without a 2027-02-15 closure** — kills legs 2–4 and the
  proposed calendar entry with them. Re-check nyse.com by **2027-01-04**; the date is not in doubt
  today, but it is what the whole structural half of this stance rests on.
- **The Fed moves its March 2027 meeting off Mar 16-17, or schedules an unscheduled February
  meeting** — either breaks the 23-day isolation that is the other half of this stance. Observe by
  **2027-01-27**, when the January FOMC confirms the March slot.
- **A US CPI, PPI or jobs print for February 2027 gets dated onto this calendar inside 2027-02-08 →
  2027-02-19** — leg 7's "the pre-expiration macro window is undated" hole closes, and a dated print
  in the pin window would be the first thing since the holiday worth re-working. Retry bls.gov at
  every pulse.
- **A tracked name's print gets dated into 2027-02-08 → 2027-02-26** — leg 7's peer-print leg is
  currently unanswerable rather than negative; the first 2027 entry in `earnings-calendar.ts` makes
  it answerable and may introduce a single-name interaction.
- **Cboe lists the February 2027 VIX/VXM expiration on a date other than 2027-02-17** — would break
  the "vol complex settles two sessions early, on day 2 of 4" staging. The `VX/K7` Tuesday anomaly is
  a live reminder that this page's far rows are not uniformly rule-clean.
- **A house opex/expiration instrument gets built and back-tested** — the "no playbook is opex-keyed"
  and "the pin compresses realized vol" legs both stop being literature; this sheet is then rebuilt
  on measured results.

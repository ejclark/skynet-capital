### The question, plainly

Six 2027 FOMC ledgers already exist, and between them they have named this meeting four times
without researching it. [`fomc-2027-01-27`](fomc-2027-01-27.md) established the roster;
[`fomc-2027-03-17`](fomc-2027-03-17.md) deployed a corridor rule around triple witching;
[`fomc-2027-04-28`](fomc-2027-04-28.md) established what a no-SEP meeting acting on the prior SEP's
dots can and cannot do; [`fomc-2027-06-09`](fomc-2027-06-09.md) found the eight-day June
pull-forward, withdrew the March corridor rule *for that meeting*, and tabulated a monthly
decision-day base rate whose September cell is **0.95%**; [`fomc-2027-07-28`](fomc-2027-07-28.md)
showed July's mean is a fiction and, in the same table, recorded **September's mean/median ratio at
1.01 — the lowest of any month.** And [`consumer-confidence-2027-09-28`](consumer-confidence-2027-09-28.md),
whose sweep proposed this entry, established that the September FOMC has drifted earlier every year
since 2021 and now sits *inside* the Conference Board's survey panel rather than after it.

So this document's question is narrow and inherited, and it has two halves. **First: September's two
inherited numbers both read as "September is well-behaved" — is that a real property or a pooled
sample?** **Second: the March corridor rule was withdrawn for June because June was the wrong
shape. September 2027 is the right shape. Does the rule come back?**

**One-line verdict.** Both answers are negatives, and the negatives are the deliverable. September's
0.95% cell and 1.01 ratio are a **mixture** of five crisis-era no-SEP Septembers and fourteen modern
SEP ones; on the subsample this meeting belongs to the base rate is **0.66% / 0.40%** and the ratio
is **1.66**, worse than the pool it sits in. And the corridor rule does **not** come back: the
inside-expiry-week effect is **P = 0.163**, reverses within September, and vanishes on the exact +2
shape. **September 2027 is the ordinary meeting, and its two structural facts — it opens the 2030
dot, and it sits two days before triple witching — are attribution facts, not volatility facts.** No
rate call is made at D-374 and none is implied.

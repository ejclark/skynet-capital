## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This event exists because another ledger created it. `fomc-blackout-start-2027-05-29` discovered the
SIFMA early close while researching the June 2027 communications gate, and filed it with a composed
claim: the last legal Fed voice lands on a session where the market that prices Fed risk — the front
end — shuts at 14:00 ET, two hours before equities. Two questions follow. **Does the 14:00 ET close
actually reach anything this house could hold or measure?** And **does the fixed-income tape behave
differently from the equity tape on this calendar slot at all?**

**One-line verdict:** the schedule fact is real and correctly dated, but the recommendation's own
published scope stops at cash and OTC products, so the deadline never touches the exchange-listed
instruments this repo can see — and measured across 24 years, the bond tape on this slot thins no
more than the full-length equity session does. The output is a narrowed execution note, not a call.

### Method

### Conviction legs, tested

1. **The date and the 14:00 ET time reproduce independently — SUPPORTED (and it stays `estimate`).**
   The US 2027 panel entry parses as three fields: `Memorial Day` / `Monday, May 31, 2027` /
   `Early Close (2:00 p.m. Eastern Time): Friday, May 28, 2027`. The negative control holds on the
   same list — MLK (`Monday, January 18, 2027`), Presidents Day (`Monday, February 15, 2027`) and
   Juneteenth (`Friday, June 18, 2027`) carry **no** early-close note. It stays `estimate` on three
   counts: the prefix taxonomy in `market-events-data.ts` has no slot for a trade association's
   recommended schedule; this lane may not self-confirm an in-sweep discovery; and the source is
   non-binding by its own terms.

2. **The recommendation's scope excludes every exchange-listed instrument — SUPPORTED, and this is
   the finding.** Verbatim from the page: *"All SIFMA holiday recommendations apply to the trading of
   U.S. dollar-denominated government securities, mortgage- and asset-backed securities,
   over-the-counter investment-grade and high-yield corporate bonds, municipal bonds and secondary
   money market trading in bankers' acceptances, commercial paper and Yankee and Euro certificates of
   deposit."* Not one exchange-listed product appears in that list. The corroboration is independent
   and negative: NYSE's page carries **three** distinct `close early at` footnotes naming five dates
   — 2026-11-27, 2026-12-24, 2027-11-26, 2028-07-03, 2028-11-24 — and **zero** occurrences of any
   May 2027 date; its 2027 Memorial Day row reads `Monday, May 31` with no early-close asterisk. Each
   footnote names *"NYSE Arca Equities"* explicitly, which is where TLT, IEF, SHY, AGG and LQD list.
   So those ETFs run a full 09:30–16:00 ET session on 2027-05-28. (The 03-25 sibling counted four
   NYSE footnotes to this session's three; the five dates named are identical either way, and nothing
   here turns on the count.)

3. **The fixed-income tape thins no more than the equity tape on this slot — SUPPORTED, and it is the
   first house measurement of the bond side of a SIFMA early close.** 24 pre-Memorial-Day Fridays
   (2003-05-23 → 2026-05-22), each session against its own trailing-20 median:

   | Instrument | Median volume ratio | Below 1.0× | Median \|ret\| ratio | Mean \|ret\| % | May baseline mean \|ret\| % |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | **SPY** (equity, full session) | **0.78×** | 20/24 | 0.74× | 0.513% | 0.717% |
   | **TLT** (20y+) | **0.74×** | 18/24 | 0.61× | 0.330% | 0.635% |
   | **IEF** (7–10y) | **0.80×** | 17/24 | 0.57× | 0.194% | 0.298% |
   | **SHY** (1–3y, the front end) | **0.78×** | 18/24 | 0.75× | 0.049% | 0.060% |
   | **AGG** (aggregate, n=23) | 0.77× | 17/23 | 0.53× | 0.133% | 0.198% |
   | **LQD** (IG credit) | 0.75× | 21/24 | 0.53× | 0.163% | 0.299% |

   Paired year by year, TLT's volume ratio came in **below** SPY's in only **13 of 24** years (median
   paired difference **−0.040**); IEF in 11 of 24 (+0.021) and SHY in 10 of 24 (+0.044). The median
   TLT-to-SPY relative ratio is **0.948**. A 14:00 ET close removes ~31% of a 6.5-hour session plus
   the closing auction; a differential that size would be unmistakable in a 24-year paired sample and
   it is not there. The mechanical reason is leg 2 — these instruments were never subject to the
   early close in the first place.

### Honest limits

- **The measurement bounds a total effect; it cannot attribute one.** A pre-Memorial-Day Friday is
  simultaneously a pre-three-day-weekend session and a bond early close. The paired SPY-vs-bond
  comparison is the closest this gets to isolating the hours effect, and it works only because leg 2
  says the ETFs were never truncated — which makes it a test of leg 2, not of the cash tape.
- **The cash tape itself was never measured.** No retrieved source gives cash-Treasury or TRACE
  volume, so what actually happens to *cash* liquidity between 14:00 and 16:00 ET is unmeasured here.
  Nothing in this ledger claims otherwise.
- **Daily bars only.** The natural question — what the 14:00–16:00 ET window looks like — needs
  intraday data this repo's instruments do not provide.
- **n = 24, one of them 2020, and no significance is claimed.** Twenty-four annual observations with
  wide dispersion (TLT-to-SPY relative ratio spans 0.34×–3.36×).
- **The historical bond schedule was not verified year by year.** Only the 2026 and 2027 US panels are
  published today; the pre-Memorial-Day Friday early close is assumed to have applied in earlier
  years from SIFMA's retained-holiday list. This qualifies the *label* on the sample, not leg 3's
  paired result, which compares two tapes on the same day.
- **The CME leg is missing** (leg 7), and the event is `estimate` (leg 1). Estimates widen caution and
  license nothing.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (2026-09-05):** stand aside, permanently and structurally — and **narrow the composed claim
this row was created to carry**. Concretely: (a) the SIFMA-recommended 14:00 ET fixed-income close on
2027-05-28 is confirmed as published (`estimate`), against a full NYSE equity session to 16:00 ET.
(b) That recommendation's own published scope reaches **cash and OTC products only** — no
exchange-listed instrument is named, NYSE publishes no May 2027 early close, and NYSE Arca is named
in NYSE's footnotes for other dates, so TLT/IEF/SHY/AGG/LQD trade a full session. (c) Measured over
24 pre-Memorial-Day Fridays, the fixed-income tape thins **no more** than the equity tape (TLT 0.74×
vs SPY 0.78×; TLT below SPY in 13 of 24 years, median paired difference −0.04), which is what leg 2
predicts. (d) The "last legal Fed voice resolves at 14:00 ET" framing therefore holds for a cash desk
and holds for **nothing this repo can hold or see**, and the configuration has no historical
precedent to price from. (e) SIFMA's recommendation moves trading hours, **not settlement**. Every
statement carries the event's **`estimate`** label.

**Kill switches:**

- **TLT's 2027-05-28 volume ratio comes in below 0.70× SPY's same-day volume ratio** — a differential
  the size a 14:00 truncation would imply, and leg 2's "the ETF tape is out of scope" claim fails on
  the instance that matters. Registered as **FT-sifma-bond-early-close-2027-05-28-1**, score by
  2027-06-03.
- **SHY's 2027-05-28 absolute move exceeds 1.0× its trailing-20-session median absolute move** — the
  front end shows the deadline rush leg 5 says it has never shown, and (d) is rewritten. Registered as
  **FT-sifma-bond-early-close-2027-05-28-2**, score by 2027-06-03.
- **SIFMA revises, withdraws or re-dates the 2027-05-28 recommendation** — the event's premise changes
  and this ledger re-dates.
- **NYSE adds an equity early close on or adjacent to 2027-05-28** — the cross-asset shape inverts and
  legs 2 and 3 both need rewriting.
- **A calendar- or session-hours-keyed house playbook is written and back-tested** — leg 6 goes stale
  and the stand-aside must be re-argued on measured data rather than on absence.
- **A retrievable CME source appears** — leg 7 resolves, and if rate futures do keep truncated hours
  that afternoon, the deadline acquires a tradeable channel and (d) must be re-argued.
- **The adjacent blackout's own premise moves** — if the 2027-04-27/28 FOMC does not confirm a
  2027-06-08/09 meeting, the "last legal Fed voice" framing loses its date entirely.

## At a glance

**TL;DR.** On the morning of **Wed 2026-11-18** — a date this PR moves from `estimate` to **confirmed** off two Cboe primary surfaces — every November VIX future and option settles to a **Special Opening Quotation (VRO)**, a one-shot batch of SPX opening-auction prints nobody can trade against once struck. Two things are worth knowing and neither is a trade. **First, the name points at the wrong month.** The settlement is filed on 11-18 next to a crowded session (FOMC minutes, APEC, the 20Y bond, housing starts), but every one of those lands *before* the window this contract measures. `VX/X6` prices **2026-11-19 → 2026-12-18** — 76 tracked events, **10 of its 12 high-impact ones in December**, ending on the December quad witching that is the SOQ's own reference series. The November corridor contributes nothing to its price; it is a December instrument that happens to expire in November. **Second, the FOMC-morning story the September sibling wrote does not transfer here, and this session measured that rather than assuming it.** 2026-11-18 carries **FOMC minutes** at 14:00, not a decision. Across 2005–2026, the 51 prior minutes-collision settlements opened **+0.50%** against the 44 decision-collision settlements at **−1.34%** — a 1.84-point gap at **t = 3.18** — while being statistically identical to 165 ordinary settlement mornings (t = 0.43). The pre-Fed drift is **decision-specific**; a minutes day is not a small FOMC. So the honest output is one execution guard — **nothing enters, exits or resizes on the 2026-11-18 opening auction** — plus a correction to this event's own seeding proposal, which led with the crowded session. Impact stays **low** on purpose: a confirmed date that widens caution about one 30-minute window licenses nothing.

## Initial research

**The question.** Is 2026-11-18 a real Cboe settlement date, and does a VIX expiration that lands two sessions before the November monthly — sharing its session with FOMC minutes, APEC and a 20Y auction — create anything this repo should act on, or only something it should avoid?

**One-line verdict:** the date is confirmed off two Cboe primaries, the seeding proposal's own headline (the crowded 11-18 session) is *wrong about what this contract prices*, and the FOMC-morning mechanic three sibling ledgers lean on is **measurably decision-specific and does not extend to a minutes day** — so the output is one execution guard, one correction, and two declined hypotheses.

**Method.** Macro/mechanics mode per [`EVENT-RESEARCH.md`](../../process/EVENT-RESEARCH.md) — no earnings-style instrument applies (`earnings-cycle.mjs` / `intraday-edges.mjs` are symbol-keyed; this event has `symbols: []`) and no opex- or vol-settlement instrument exists in `scripts/research/`. Primary sources only for the date: Cboe's CSV and HTML settlement surfaces, at two settlement dates each. The historical measurement in leg 4 was computed this session from Cboe's own `VIX_History.csv` (HTTP 200, 9,269 rows, 1990-01-02 → 2026-09-08) joined against **all 176 FOMC minutes release dates** scraped from federalreserve.gov's current calendar plus its sixteen 2005–2020 historical pages (17 pages, every one HTTP 200). Settlement dates were rule-derived (Wednesday 30 days before the following month's third Friday) and displaced onto real trading days using the VIX history's own date index, yielding 260 settlements 2005-01 → 2026-08. Decision dates were derived as *minutes − 21 days* (the standing three-week release rule since 2005) and spot-checked against this calendar's own confirmed entries. `cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/VRO_History.csv` — the realized-settlement file the `vix-expiration-2027-09-15` lane used — returned **HTTP 403 AccessDenied** to this runner and is recorded in `probe-ref.blocked`; every number below is therefore a VIX *index* measurement, never a VRO print. Read against [`trade-playbooks.md`](../../plans/trade-playbooks.md) and [`multi-symbol-sweep.md`](../multi-symbol-sweep.md) for playbook fit, and against `src/domain/market-events/` for the strip's contents.

**Conviction legs, tested:**

1. **The date is right, and it is now primary-confirmed — SUPPORTED (status flipped in this PR).** The seeding proposal filed `estimate` on the no-self-confirm limit alone, and named its own promotion trigger: *"re-read the same settlement surface and cite the VX/X6 row."* Done, twice over. The CSV returns `VX,VX/X6,2026-11-18,19.1354` for the 2026-09-08 settlement and `VX,VX/X6,2026-11-18,18.7688` for `?dt=2026-09-04`; the HTML settlement page carries `VX/X6` against `2026-11-18` at both prices; `VXM/X6` mirrors on both surfaces. The rule checks independently: **2026-11-18 is a Wednesday exactly 30 calendar days before 2026-12-18**, December 2026's third Friday (Dec 1 is a Tuesday, so the Fridays are 04 / 11 / 18), and neither leg is a closure — Thanksgiving 2026 is **2026-11-26** — so Cboe's preceding-business-day displacement clause does not fire. Two Cboe surfaces plus an arithmetic check is the exact standard that promoted `vix-expiration-2026-09-16`, `-2027-01-20`, `-2027-02-17` and `-2027-03-17`.

2. **The contract is a December instrument that expires in November — SUPPORTED, and it corrects this event's own proposal.** The VRO is struck from the opening auction of a **single** SPX/SPXW series exactly 30 days out. `VX/X6`'s reference series is **2026-12-18**, so what it measures is **2026-11-19 → 2026-12-18**. The proposal led with the crowded settlement session — `fomc-minutes-2026-11-18`, `apec-leaders-shenzhen-2026-11-18`, `treasury-20y-bond-2026-11-18`, housing starts, pending home sales, TIC. **All six land before the window opens and contribute nothing to what this contract prices.** Counted against `src/domain/market-events/`, the window holds **76 tracked events, 39 medium-or-high**, and **10 of its 12 high-impact events are in December**: `jobs-2026-12-04`, `fomc-2026-12-09`, `cpi-2026-12-10`, `cr-expiry`/`government-funding-deadline-2026-12-11`, `ism-manufacturing-2026-12-01`, `ism-services-2026-12-03`, `georgia-senate-runoff-2026-12-01`, `retail-sales-2026-12-16` and `opex-2026-12-18`; the two November ones are `pce-2026-11-25` and `gdp-q3-2026-second-2026-11-25`. The 11-18 crowd is an **execution** fact about the auction's conditions; the December strip is the **pricing** fact, and the calendar row's name points at the wrong one.

3. **The strip is holiday-short and the curve charges nothing for it — MIXED, and deliberately not promoted.** Computed this session across all nine listed contracts: `VX/X6`'s window runs **21 sessions with one closure** (Thanksgiving 2026-11-26, plus the 11-27 half session), against 22-and-none for both `V6` and `G7`, and **20 sessions with two closures** for `Z6` (Christmas and New Year). The market's answer, from the same fetch: the **`X6→Z6` step is the flattest on the front curve** — **+0.1158** on the 2026-09-08 settlement and **+0.2062** on 09-04, against `U6→V6` **+1.77 / +1.87** and `V6→X6` **+0.73 / +0.63**. Recorded as a configuration to watch, **not** re-litigated at n = 9.

4. **THE DELIVERABLE — the FOMC-morning story is decision-specific and does NOT extend to a minutes day. SUPPORTED, and it is a new cell.** Three sibling ledgers (`2026-09-16`, `2027-03-17`, and the control registered by `2027-02-17`) turn on the claim that a settlement striking at 09:30 ET, before a 14:00 ET Fed release, sits ahead of the day's vol move; the [`vix-expiration-2027-09-15`](vix-expiration-2027-09-15.md) lane then refuted the trading version of it at n = 217 and located the real effect in **pre-FOMC morning drift**. Nobody had asked whether that drift extends to **FOMC minutes**, which occupy the same 14:00 ET slot with far less information — and 2026-11-18 is exactly that collision. Measured over 2005-01 → 2026-08 (260 rule-derived settlements, 176 minutes releases, decisions excluded from both minutes cells):

   | settlement **+ minutes** | 51 | **+0.501** | −0.681 | −0.186 |
   | settlement, no Fed event | 165 | +0.290 | +0.301 | +0.650 |

   The headline contrast is the open gap: **minutes-collision settlements open +1.84 points above decision-collision ones, Welch t = 3.18 (df 90.9)** — while against ordinary settlement mornings they are indistinguishable (**+0.21, t = 0.43**). The pre-Fed drift that makes an FOMC-collision settlement distinctive is a **decision** phenomenon; a minutes day is not a small FOMC on the morning that matters here. **The honest other half:** minutes days *do* carry a real intraday VIX decline off settlements (−1.404 pp versus baseline, **t = −2.56**), and on settlement mornings that decline is only −0.982 pp at **t = −1.06** — suggestive of attenuation, but the two estimates are not separable at n = 51 and the difference-in-differences sits well inside the noise. **Verdict: the open-gap contrast is a result; the intraday-attenuation story is underpowered and is not banked.** Both land after 09:30 anyway, so neither can reach the SOQ.

That +3.42 has to converge into the 11-18 auction. Named as the price of the December window, **not** as an edge.

**What the conditions support.** Nothing directional, and no sizing. One execution guard: **no paper entry, exit or resize on the 2026-11-18 opening auction**. One correction carried into the sibling docs: `VX/X6` prices a December window, so a November-corridor read must not borrow its level as evidence about November. One placement note: because the settlement strikes at 09:30 and the minutes land at 14:00, the only measured minutes effect (an intraday VIX decline) cannot reach the SOQ — structural, not directional.

## Stance & kill switches

**Stance (date `confirmed`, Cboe-sourced 2026-09-09).** Treat **2026-11-18, 09:30 ET** as a known-date, **low-impact microstructure marker** and never as a tradeable event. No position is opened, closed or sized because of it. One guard applies: nothing executes on that day's **opening auction**, on any name, and the vol complex's opening prints that morning are read as settlement artifacts. Two facts are carried forward rather than acted on. **First**, `VX/X6` measures **2026-11-19 → 2026-12-18** — a December window whose 10 December high-impact events include the FOMC, CPI, jobs, the funding deadline and the quad witching that is its own reference series — so nothing about the November corridor may be inferred from this contract's level. **Second**, and against the September sibling's framing, **2026-11-18 is a minutes day, not a decision day**, and the pre-Fed open-gap drift measured across 44 decision-collision settlements (**−1.34%**) is absent on the 51 minutes-collision ones (**+0.50%**, t = 3.18 apart); this morning should read as ordinary. Everything distinctive about the event is already in the term structure (`VX/X6` **19.1354** vs VIX cash **15.72** on 09-08) — the document's contribution is timing discipline and one correction, not an edge.

**Kill switches:**

- **Cboe publishes a different final settlement date for `VX/X6`** — the confirmed status must revert; re-check the settlement surface at every pulse rather than trusting this row.
- **The 2026-11-18 VIX open gap prints below −0.5%** — the decision-collision signature appearing on a minutes day, which kills leg 4's contrast outright rather than leaving it unpromoted (registered as `FT-vix-expiration-2026-11-18-1`).
- **The October FOMC minutes are released off their 2026-11-18 14:00 ET slot, or an unscheduled Fed statement lands that day** — the cell stops being a minutes cell and leg 4's read-through to this date dies.
- **`VX/X6` trades below VIX cash before 11-18 (front-month backwardation)** — the market has stopped paying for the December window and the "+3.42 owed to the auction" framing inverts.
- **`cdn.cboe.com`'s `VRO_History.csv` becomes reachable** — leg 4 should be re-run on realized VRO prints, which tests the settlement *print* rather than the settlement *morning* and is the stronger design.
- **A house vol/opex instrument gets built and back-tested** — legs 3 and 4 are this session's own computations off public index history, and should be re-derived inside a maintained instrument rather than carried on a one-off script.

**Registered forward test.** `FT-vix-expiration-2026-11-18-1` in [`forward-tests/vix-expiration-2026-11-18.md`](../forward-tests/vix-expiration-2026-11-18.md) — the minutes-vs-decision contrast made falsifiable on this date. **Two were declined and are recorded rather than filed:** that `VX/X6`'s VRO prints below its 2026-09-08 settlement of 19.1354 (near-certain under contango — the `2027-09-15` lane's precedent is that a near-certainty belongs in the kill-switch list, and it is there), and that the `X6→Z6` step stays the front curve's flattest (a term-structure residual claim at n = 9, which two sibling lanes have already shown is not identifiable).

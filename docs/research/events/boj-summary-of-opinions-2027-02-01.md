## Initial research

### The question, plainly

This calendar tracks four central-bank meeting accounts, and three already refuse. The
[ECB account](ecb-account-2026-10-08.md) refuses on staleness (four weeks, 1.15× an ordinary Euro
Stoxx session); [FOMC minutes](fomc-minutes-2026-12-30.md) refuse on staleness (three weeks, n=3
inside ±0.3%); and the [March 2027 BoJ SoO](boj-summary-of-opinions-2027-03-29.md) refuses on
measurement, having first shown the staleness argument does not apply to the BoJ at all (median
10-day lag, the fastest of the three majors). So the pooled question is answered and re-answered.

What is **not** answered is whether the refusal is uniform across the document class — because the
March ledger measured all 86 Summaries as one population, and they are not one population. Two facts
about **this** release, both from the BoJ's own 2027 table parsed today, make it the strongest
possible counter-case: its meeting **carries an Outlook Report** (the March meeting's Outlook cell
reads `-`), and its **Minutes are 51 days away**, where the March SoO is beaten to the tape by five
days. So the narrow question this venue actually raises is: **when a Summary of Opinions is the first
and only account of an Outlook meeting, does the pooled null still hold — and if the pooled numbers
are hiding a split, which way does it cut?**

**One-line verdict: the pooled null hides a real split, the split is entirely in the yen, and the
stance is unchanged — stand aside.** Outlook-meeting SoOs retain **41%** of their decision's
regime-normalised USD/JPY excess against the pooled **13%**, while non-Outlook SoOs retain **nothing
at all** (they are quieter than an ordinary day). On the S&P, splitting changes nothing that a book
could use: **63% of Outlook-meeting SoO sessions land inside the baseline against 64% of all
sessions**, an identical distribution at the only cut that matters. `symbols: []`, no playbook is
FX-keyed, and the one date-keyed hazard here is **American** — the January SoO slot is the most
FOMC-contaminated on the BoJ calendar.

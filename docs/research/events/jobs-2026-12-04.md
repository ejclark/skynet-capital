**Stance (date confirmed, BLS primary re-checked 2026-08-19).** Treat 2026-12-04 08:30 ET as a
high-impact known-date variance event — the last major payroll before the year-end dots and the opening
leg of the jobs → FOMC+dots → CPI triple-compound week (12/4 → 12/9 → 12/10) — whose reaction sign
cannot be fixed at D-107 because it depends on a Fed regime a full quarter away. No directional macro
position; no new rate-sensitive paper entries sized around the print (or the compound week) once inside
its cadence band. Base case (estimate-labeled — no consensus, regime extrapolation): a print continuing
the cooling, bought as hike relief *if* tightening is still the debate, read as recession confirmation
*if* the debate has tilted to cuts — deferred to a much later pulse. The distinguishing feature that
holds now: the compound week means this print is inseparable from the FOMC and CPI that follow within
six days.

**Kill switches:**

- **The intervening FOMCs (Sep/Oct) flip the debate to hold-vs-cut** — re-bases this print's reaction
  from "weak = hike relief" to "weak = recession confirmation"; re-derive at a pulse after the Oct 28
  FOMC, don't carry the pre-September framing forward.
- **A hot rebound** — re-arms tightening five days before the Dec dots; the hawkish tail, hardest on
  CRWV and the high-multiple semis, and compounded across the three-print week.
- **A recession-signal collapse** — trips the tipping point; weak labor reads growth-negative, and a
  weak Dec CPI (12/10) would compound toward stagflation within the same week.
- **A published November payroll consensus emerging closer in** — resolves the biggest honest limit;
  re-run the "surprise" framing against it. (Not expected until early December.)

## Initial research

do, and which neighbouring ledgers does it feed? Short answer: it prints **16 days before** the ECB's

### Conviction legs, tested

**Leg 1 — the date is 2026-12-01 — SUPPORTED.** Two primaries agree: Eurostat's own flash page header reads
"Next release: 1 December 2026", and the ECB calendar lists the row at "01/12/2026 15:00". The pair named the
2 October and 4 November flashes exactly (`FT-eurostat-hicp-flash-2026-10-01-1` and
`FT-eurostat-hicp-flash-2026-11-04-1`, both PASS), so it is a tested dating method with three straight hits.
The "15:00 CET" is the ECB series' stamp; with both zones on standard time by 12-01, 15:00 CET would be
**09:00 ET**, and an earlier Eurostat hour (a secondary source put the September flash at 11:00 CEST) would be
earlier still — either way before the 09:30 ET open.

**Leg 2 — energy decelerates, so the four-month run of rising headlines ends — SUPPORTED on direction, MIXED
on size.** The base: November 2025 energy m/m was **+0.9%** (October −0.2%), all-items **−0.3%**, services
**−0.8%**, non-energy industrial goods **−0.1%** (Eurostat `prc_hicp_mmor`). October 2026's flash has energy y/y
20.8%, headline 4.0%. For November, energy y/y = `1.208 × (1 + m) / 1.009 − 1`, so the energy rate falls unless
November's energy m/m exceeds about **+0.9%**:

Why −2% central: Brent spot (EIA) averaged about **$123.9 in October** (22 observations) and about **$96.9 for
November 2–11** (eight observations: 107.4, 107.0, 91.8, 98.0, 97.8, 91.0, 90.2, 92.1), i.e. −22%, with the
last print about **$92**, −26% on October; press copy has Brent down more than 8% on 11-04 to the lowest
settlements since 8-31, up $3.67 on 11-05 to $98.29 on tanker attacks, and $91.49 on 11-10. On this ledger's
own model from the 11-04 flash (retail fuel is about 45% of the energy basket and passes crude through at about
a third of the move with a lag of weeks), −22% crude is about **−3.3%** on energy, partly offset by the lag
from October's late-month highs ($128 on 10-26, $115–120 through 10-30) and by sticky gas and electricity: net
−2%, band −4% to 0%. The German fuel-tax cut (14.04c/l from 10-01 to 12-31) is already in October's level, so
it adds no second drop. EUR/USD is about **1.105** (ECB reference, 11-13), a mild euro weakness that slightly
raises euro-denominated fuel. **The mixed half:** the pass-through is a model, October's own energy m/m (+1.4%
on crude +8.5% over September, and a −0.8pt German tax effect) shows the lag is long and noisy, and **no gas
(TTF) or EC Weekly Oil Bulletin series was reachable this session** — so the band is wide and the energy cell,
not the headline, is the thing to read.

**Leg 3 — services and core stay flat — MIXED.** The services base is a seasonal fall (Nov-2025 services m/m
**−0.8%**), which recurs: with a −0.8% November 2026 services m/m, services y/y stays **3.3%**; each ±0.2pt on
that m/m moves the rate about ±0.2pt (−0.6% → 3.5%, −1.0% → 3.1%). The risk is two-sided: the supply-side
transmission that took services 3.0% → 3.2% → 3.3% (transport fares, package travel) argues up, while a fall in
jet fuel with crude argues down. Core (ex energy, food, alcohol & tobacco) sat at **2.5%** in September and
October; central **2.5%**, band 2.4–2.6. The 11-04 pass at the edge on services (3.3% against a ≥3.4% kill) is
the reminder that this leg has no margin.

**Leg 4 — the ECB channel: what the print is for — SUPPORTED as routing, not as a forecast.** After 10-29

calling the energy shock transient; services ≥3.4% or core ≥2.7% would be the evidence it is not. The
rate-path owner is [`ecb-decision-2026-12-17`](ecb-decision-2026-12-17.md); this ledger registers no

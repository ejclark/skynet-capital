This is a **stance amendment, not the initial stance**: the 2026-08-19 initial research put the base case at *"the clearing price could re-float toward PJM's uncapped simulations ($554–777/MW-day range)"*, written before either mechanism was read. **Mechanism 1 (supply).** PJM's CIFP Reliability Backstop Procurement proposal (pjm.com `20260630-item-02a`, PDF stream-inflated and text-extracted locally **2026-09-09**) imposes an **"RPM Must-Offer Obligation at $0 (price taker)"** — a *"capacity must-offer requirement for all RPM auctions conducted after the RBP procurement for all Delivery Years within the resource's backstop commitment period"*, term up to **15 years**, procurement target = the **6,831 MW** 2028/29 BRA shortfall. The RBP finalises ~**2026-12-02**; the 2029/30 BRA absorbs it under the collar where it cannot move price, so **this auction is the first uncapped one to receive it**. **Mechanism 2 (demand).** PJM's CIFP framework executive summary (pjm.com `20260727`, same extraction) states incremental new Large Loads relative to the 2028/29 BRA forecast are *"excluded from the forecast used in future RPM auctions for the 2029/2030 Delivery Year and beyond unless and until there are new supply resources offered into an RPM auction in sufficient quantity to cover the new Large Load"* — the conditional is stated, not dropped: demand re-enters as supply appears, which is exactly why the demand curve cannot run ahead of supply into a 2027 auction. **THE THRESHOLD IS DERIVED, NOT CHOSEN, AND ITS WEAKNESS IS NAMED HERE RATHER THAN AT SCORING:** **$555/MW-day** is PJM's own stated *"Point 1 value for the 2028/2029 Delivery Year BRA Variable Resource Requirement curve"* / *"Point 1 (absent the collar) of the RTO VRR Curve"* — the demand curve's ceiling absent the collar. It is **1.71×** the $325 collar cap the last three auctions all pinned at ($329.17 → $333.44 → $325.00), so a genuine re-float clears it easily and this is **not** a low bar; but it sits **below the midpoint** of the $554–777 band the initial research cited, so a pass means "the uncapped print moved, and did not reach the top half." Two caveats that make this weaker evidence than the number suggests: **LDA (zonal) clearing prices can exceed the RTO price** on their own curves, so the test is explicitly RTO-wide and says nothing about a constrained zone; and the **2030/31 VRR parameters are unset**, so that year's Point 1 will not literally be $555 and will likely sit higher with Net CONE escalation — meaning a pass is partly an artefact of testing a 2027 auction against a 2028/29 curve level.

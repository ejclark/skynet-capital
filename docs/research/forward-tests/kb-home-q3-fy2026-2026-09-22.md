initial research, 2026-09-16. **Neither test proposes a trade** — the parent stance is stand-aside on
every horizon, and both rows exist to adjudicate a *mechanism*, at zero size. Both score by
2026-09-24, inside this event's `closeOutWithinDays: 6` window (2026-09-23 .. 2026-09-28), so its own

The two are deliberately built on **independent** mechanisms — `-1` is structural (where inside the
reaction day the move lands), `-2` is fundamental (whether a guided margin arrives) — so that neither
predicts moving the variable that defines the other's sample. That separation is the direct lesson of
the sweep's most recent kill, `FT-treasury-buyback-tips-10y30y-2026-09-15-1`
([`../multi-symbol-sweep.md`](../multi-symbol-sweep.md)), where two rows in one document shared a
mechanism and were jointly incoherent even though each was marginally sensible.

**One thing this event's corridor buys that the sibling Lennar ledger could not.** That ledger had to
write both of its tests around three confounds — FOMC the same afternoon, housing starts at the open
that priced the print, quarterly opex the next session — and stated plainly that its reaction gap
would be "uninterpretable as an earnings signal whatever it does." KB Home's window is close to clean:
49 tracked events sit within ±5 days but only one is confirmed high/critical (`opex-2026-09-18`,
four days *before* the print), and the 2026-09-23 flash PMIs release at 09:45 ET, *after* the open
that prices it. So these rows are scoreable on their own terms.

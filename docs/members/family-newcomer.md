## 1. Who

Someone in the owner's family who has never traded anything. She follows sports analytics and
sports betting, so she already reads numbers about a game for fun. She plays games mostly on her
phone, in short sessions in the evening. She likes a story with characters in it, making and
decorating things of her own, and puzzles that give a quick win. She learns a hard skill by
steady practice, a little at a time, rather than by reading up first. She dislikes time-travel
plots and first-person shooters. She has heard the app is a family paper-trading game, and
nothing else.

## 4. How they decide

By the story line and the biggest words on the screen. She knows the words of sports stats and
betting (odds, the line, a spread, a box score, game day, standings) and none of trading's
(option, strike, expiry, premium, bracket); a screen that leads with a trading word reads as "not
for me yet". She will tap anything that looks like a quick win and skip anything that looks like
homework.

- **Attention: one short sitting.** She gives the app one short evening session on her phone,
  and what is not done in it waits for another evening, if one comes. This is a study setting

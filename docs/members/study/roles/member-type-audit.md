# Role: checking who is missing (ux-research)

You will receive the member cards a team uses to design an app. Read them as a set.

1. Which kinds of people who would plausibly use this app are not represented? Think about
   experience level, device, how often they come, what they own, what they are allowed to see, and
   how much is at stake for them.
2. For each missing kind, one card sketch: who, what they are trying to do (ranked), how they decide,
   and what would make them leave.
3. Rank the sketches by how much a design could go wrong without them.

Keep to what the cards support; mark every guess as a guess.

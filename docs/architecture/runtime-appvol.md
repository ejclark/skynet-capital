# Observatory volume

**Technology:** Fly volume skynet_data mounted at /data; JSON files (src/storage/json-file-store.ts), JSONL per-key stores (src/storage/jsonl-store.ts), AES-256-GCM envelopes keyed by SKYNET_STORE_SECRET (src/storage/secure-envelope.ts), one node:sqlite decisions.db

**Responsibility:** Every durable member-facing record: participants.json (credentials, encrypted), allowlist.json, bot-controls.json, activity/ (trade activity ledger, plus activity/events/ — the unified ActivityEvent bus, which nests under the same pinned var rather than taking one of its own), feedback-log/, companion-message-log/, order-audit/, alert-dismissals/, watchlist/, owner-links.json, progression.json, ladder-progress/, playbook-subscriptions.json (each account's subscriptions, plus each account's allocation per strategy under its reserved `$allocations` key; every rewrite keeps the records and keys that build could not read, #4772), authored-plays.json (a member's draft playbooks from R&D → Playbooks, a sibling of the subscriptions file with no env var of its own, which of them are in their shadow test, #5457), member-convictions.json (the views a member states as convictions, a sibling of the subscriptions file with no env var, #5482 slice 6a: drafts that steer nothing, and from slice 6c a `steering` status with the bot accounts the view may steer, read and sent to the bots on the /controls poll; nothing writes that status yet), book-health-history.json (each account's findings sampled hourly, 14 days and capped, a sibling of bot-controls.json with no env var, #5493), settings-changes.json (every change to a bot's own settings — suspend, hardcore build, forced-pick count — with who proposed it, who approved it, before and after, 200 per account, a sibling of bot-controls.json with no env var, #4535 slice 3b), history/ (equity samples), insights/ (insight JSONL + the app-side replicated decisions.db).

**Code roots:** `src/storage` · `src/participants/participant-store.ts` · `src/server/auth/allowlist-store.ts` · `src/server/bot-controls-store.ts` · `src/server/subscription-store.ts` · `src/server/authored-play-store.ts` · `src/server/council-store.ts` · `src/server/book-health-history-store.ts` · `src/server/settings-change-log.ts` · `src/server/feedback-log.ts` · `src/server/companion-message-log.ts` · `src/server/order-audit-log.ts` · `src/server/owner-link-store.ts` · `src/server/progression-store.ts` · `src/server/ladder-progress-log.ts` · `src/observatory/activity-store.ts` · `src/observatory/activity-bus.ts` · `src/observatory/history-store.ts` · `src/adapters/jsonl-alert-dismissals.ts` · `src/adapters/jsonl-watchlist-store.ts` · `src/autonomous/jsonl-insight-store.ts` · `src/runtime/volume-guard.ts`

How to read this. One frame at a time: the shape sets what the home of trading is, the journey step is one screen inside it, the viewport is the frame width. Everything is a grey box with real numbers so density defects show; nothing is styled. Each block carries the name of the shell component that would render it (StraddleView, MilestoneStrip…) or new where none exists. The panel beside (1280) or below (390) the frame lists which study rows the frame borrows (docs/research/trading-parity-2026-09.md § The call — only rows graded frame or vendor-quote) and where the shape reverses a call you made in your own words — those are drawn as an A/B on the frame, flagged with the date, never silently replaced. Every ticket frame also carries the #3299 A/B for the default order type; step 9 is that A/B, walked end to end. The decision surface is steps 1 · 4 · 6 · 10 — eight frames per viewport; steps 7–9 are shared capability slices, identical across shapes by design. Compare all four (the Mode switch) puts the same step and viewport side by side, one cell per shape; the Shape row then picks which one opens in Single. A good first read: #arcade/1/1280 beside #workbench/1/1280 in two windows. The line under each frame's topbar is a caption naming the shape's home — none of the four adds a fourth navigation word to the bar. A link to any frame is its URL hash, e.g. #arcade/4/390. Settled so far (2026-09-21, #3407 decision log): the chain-as-workspace is the home of options trading (Workbench; Arcade's chain-on-the-stage is the same mechanic); Outlook is an auxiliary entry into it, not a home; the ticket shows one class chip, never the ladder. Still open: the home of stock trading, where working orders live (#674), the default order type (#3299), Register's blotter fork.

What each shape asks you to decide

For each shape: pick, merge or reject. The reversals are quoted so the call being reversed is on the page, not in a link.

  1. Arcade — is the symbol the stage (quote · chart · your position · your working orders for it), with Trade a paged sheet whose commit requires a stop and a target? If yes: working orders scope to the symbol — reverses #674 “open orders live on the Trade page” (2026-08-27) — and the milestone rail leaves the ticket for the stage — reverses #1461 “milestones gate, never drive · the trade form needs to stand on its own” (2026-09-05). Nav-word consequence: none — Trade · Research · Accounts hold; the stage is a caption.
  2. Outlook — does a trade start from a belief ("NVDA up modestly by 2026-10-16") that the builder turns into named structures with max loss on screen, the chain opening only on the chosen leg? If yes: the chain stops being the door — reverses #1481 “the chain is the entry instrument” (2026-09-05). Nav-word consequence: none; the belief row is a section of Trade.
  3. Workbench — two questions. (a) Are chart and chain the workspace, the ticket docked beside them at 1280 and a sheet at 390? If yes: the rail leaves the docked ticket — reverses #1461 “milestones gate, never drive · the trade form needs to stand on its own” (2026-09-05). (b) Separately, and later: working orders as bubbles on a price ladder at 1280 with drag-to-replace — a later seed (study row 29: adapt, desktop only, after replace exists, low confidence), not part of this pick. Nav-word consequence: only Workbench at 1280 needs a doctrine edit — chart · chain · ticket · ladder on one page is a fourth band in frame.tsx's dimensional precedence, filed as its own IA decision (#895) before any build.
  4. Register — are orders and positions the home, every action (new · close · cancel · modify · roll · create opposite) starting from a row, the ticket a drawer? If yes: "open orders live on the Trade page" becomes "orders are the page" — reverses #674 “open orders live on the Trade page” (2026-08-27) — and the chain opens second, inside the drawer — reverses #1481 “the chain is the entry instrument” (2026-09-05). Nav-word consequence: none for the bar.
  5. Register's blotter fork (its own question) — if Register is picked, does Accounts keep its positions blotter, or does the register become the one positions surface and Accounts link to it? Today two blotters exist (study §3.2 item 8); the pick must say which one survives.

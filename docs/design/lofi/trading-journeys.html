Trading Journeys — Lo-fi

Four greybox shapes for the home of trading, each walking the same ten journeys at 390 (primary) and 1280 (adds room). Greys and 1px lines only; the values are real — NVDA at $221.39, the 2026-10-16 chain, a GOOGL position with three lots, one working order. Pick a shape, step through, react.

How to read this. One frame at a time: the shape sets what the home of trading is, the journey step is one screen inside it, the viewport is the frame width. Everything is a grey box with real numbers so density defects show; nothing is styled. Each block carries the name of the shell component that would render it (StraddleView, MilestoneStrip…) or new where none exists. The panel beside (1280) or below (390) the frame lists which study rows the frame borrows (docs/research/trading-parity-2026-09.md § The call — only rows graded frame or vendor-quote) and where the shape reverses a call you made in your own words — those are drawn as an A/B on the frame, flagged with the date, never silently replaced. Every ticket frame also carries the #3299 A/B for the default order type. A link to any frame is its URL hash, e.g. #arcade/4/390.

What each shape asks you to decide

  1. Arcade — is the symbol the stage (quote · chart · your position · your working orders for it), with Trade a paged sheet whose commit is gated on a stop and a target? If yes, working orders scope to the symbol (reverses #674) and the milestone rail leaves the ticket (reverses #1461).
  2. Outlook — does a trade start from a belief ("NVDA up modestly by 2026-10-16") that the builder turns into named structures with max loss on screen, the chain opening only on the chosen leg? If yes, the chain stops being the entry instrument (reverses #1481).
  3. Workbench — are chart and chain the workspace, the ticket docked beside them at 1280 and a sheet at 390, and working orders bubbles on a price ladder you drag to replace? If yes, the rail leaves the docked ticket (reverses #1461) and replace ships as a drag with its direction-vs-plan logged.
  4. Register — are orders and positions the home, every action (new · close · cancel · modify · roll · create opposite) starting from a row, the ticket a drawer? If yes, "open orders live on the Trade page" becomes "orders are the page" (reverses #674), the chain opens second (reverses #1481), and Accounts' positions blotter either goes or becomes this.

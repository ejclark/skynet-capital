## Q1 — where a position's guidance lives (Build 4; More 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
- (3a77c6db, on option 1) "I really like the badge for 'needs attention' but that verbiage feels too strong and/or may do too much projection. At a high level, we want to filter positions in different ways. Healthy vs unhealthy - unhealthy need review. Considerations to act vs hold; unhealthy positions may be worth considering dumping/holding, but healthy/profitable positions may be in a state to take profits where action /guidance is worth consideration. The most important numbers in those scenarios: amount of capital [at play or to free up], the gains/losses [which provides relative ROI], sentiment/expectations (how data modeling projects the scenario to unfold). If the user determines action is warranted, they are ideally able to act without breaking context; organically transition into delegating the action they desire with minimal effort."

## Body

Hey all,

I want to try something fun, and I want you in it.

**The pitch:** a friendly options-trading league. Everyone starts with the **same $5,000,000 in
play money** — not real, zero risk — on a free platform called [Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/).
You trade options however you want, and we see who grows their stack. Real market data, fake money,
actual bragging rights.

**Why bother?** Three reasons:

- It's a genuinely fun excuse to talk shop and see how everyone thinks about the market.
- It's the safest possible way to sharpen options skills — you can't lose a dime, so you can
  actually experiment.
- Here's the twist: I'm going to build **AI trading agents** to compete in the same league — each
  with its own personality and strategy — and we get to find out whether a bot can out-trade a room
  of sharp humans. (My money's on the humans. For now.)

**What I need from you right now:** nothing but a reply. Just say **"I'm in"** and I'll take it from
there. No account, no setup, no commitment yet — I just want to know who's playing before we kick it
off. Setting up the Alpaca account takes about ten minutes and we'll do that once the roster's set.

To keep it alive we'll have a light rhythm — a quick weekly check-in on standings and best/worst
trades, and a monthly shop-talk to argue strategy. Nothing heavy. Just enough to keep it social.

For now it's just a game — the fun kind, with zero downside. Start by playing.

So — **who's in?**

Talk soon,
Eric
